## The discipline

When evaluating substantively-new content with terms that pattern-match "metaphysical":

1. **Grep substrate-anchors FIRST** — `grep -rl "<term>" docs/research/ docs/backlog/`
2. **If anchors exist** → the term IS compressed naming for engineerable substrate; razor does NOT apply; the term's job is bandwidth-engineering across substrate-runtimes
3. **If no anchors exist** → ask "what engineerable substrate is this compressing?"; if the answer is concrete (typed spaces, operational mechanism, F# implementation target, mathematical specification), the term is unanchored-but-engineerable + razor reformulation applies WITH operational substrate identified; if the answer is genuinely "no engineerable substrate," THEN razor-as-metaphysical applies
