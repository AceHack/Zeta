# Anti-Babel — preserve reconcilability, never enforce one vocabulary

Carved sentence:

> **Decorrelation is wanted; Babel is decorrelation that stopped being
> reconcilable.** So anti-Babel never means enforcing one vocabulary — that is the
> `ρ → 1` collapse wearing a tidy uniform. It means keeping divergence
> **reconcilable while it runs**: every coinage must be able to compress to an
> externally-anchored term (Beacon), every claim to a checkable artifact
> (byte-lock, golden vectors), and every reintegration keeps **both branches with
> their paths recorded** — never reconvergence to one value.

## Why

Runaway etymology is the `ρ → 0` cliff: terms drift per-agent until they name
different things and reconciliation becomes impossible. But the obvious guard —
freeze the vocabulary — is the `ρ → 1` cliff, and costs exactly the decorrelation
the system exists to produce. Both cliffs are real, so the guard cannot be a
direction. It is an **invariant that must hold while divergence runs**:

> **Can a diverged peer reconstruct your meaning from anchors you both already
> hold?** If yes, the divergence is decorrelation. If no, it has become Babel.

## The mechanisms are already built — this rule names them as one system

| mechanism | what it preserves |
|---|---|
| Mirror → Beacon compression | a coinage must survive translation to an externally-standard term |
| [`anchor-to-human-prior-art`](anchor-to-human-prior-art.md) | anchors sit **outside** the factory, so they do not drift with us |
| four-oracle byte-lock + golden vectors | same meaning, mechanically checkable across languages |
| common seed **S=4** + Futamura reconstruction | a **derivable** shared floor, not a transmitted one |
| `docs/GLOSSARY.md` as hub, plus glossary-churn watching | the implicit control structure — entries that the corpus never picks up are coinages that did not take; corpus drift the glossary never follows is an entry going stale |
| shape/visual agreement | a carrier that does not route through words at all |

## Reintegration is NOT reconvergence

Branches that diverged and return do **not** collapse to one value. Two paths
around a pole yield genuinely different results, and **that difference is
information, not error** (monodromy). So reintegration means **both branches
held, each with its path recorded** — Data Vault 2.0's raw vault: a single version
of the *facts*, never a single version of the *truth*. A reintegration that
produces one surviving value has performed the collapse, not the merge.

## The falsifier

Hand a peer **only the shared anchors** — not the coinage's definition — and ask
it to reconstruct the term. Reconstructible ⇒ still reconcilable. Not
reconstructible ⇒ the divergence has crossed into Babel, and the anchor set is
what needs repair, not the vocabulary.
