# Only the irreducible is primitive — generate the rest; the generator IS the ECC

Carved sentence:

> Only the **irreducible** is primitive. If a thing can be **decomposed or
> generalized**, you do not hardcode it — you **generate** it from the irreducible
> generator (the **free object**: the free monoidal category / operad at the top,
> the free algebra as its algebraic shadow). Generators read the *free interface*;
> every structured special case — Clifford, E8, a Lie algebra — is an **earned
> quotient** obtained by declaring its relations. This is **Rodney's Razor
> compression applied to the foundation itself**. And the highest-value generator
> **IS an error-correcting code**: the one object that generates the structures
> also **corrects their drift across space (the N-oracle byte-lock) and time (DST
> replay / versions)** — generation and error-correction are dual, and
> `gen(gen) == gen` is *both*.

## Why

Keeps the substrate **weight-free at the root**: there is no committed special
case to capture (capture = weight, manifesto §3), only the free generator is
primitive and everything else is derived and therefore *checkable*. The
generator-as-ECC is why drift cannot accumulate — **regenerating from the
irreducible IS the correction** (an adinkra carries Gates' doubly-even self-dual
codes; Cayley–Dickson is the doubling generator). So the same act that produces
the 6-language structure also detects and repairs its divergence: build = verify.
This is the algebra-level statement of `interfaces-free-classes-earned` (the free
interface is free; the class/quotient is earned) and of `gen/` (generate from the
free object, not from a frozen special case).
