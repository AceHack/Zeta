# same/grey — gray

**Asserts:** **grey** and **gray** are the **same** — the same colour/register, different spelling
(British **grey** / American **gray**). The canonical `same/x-y` pair (the first real one): a **byte-
different, same-referent** sameness.

- **x:** `grey` (canonical spelling; `grey/`)
- **y:** `gray` (American-spelling alias; `gray/`)
- **kind of same:** **same-referent** (one colour, two spellings) — note this is the **provable/bit-aware**
  kind (you *can* show they denote the same colour), NOT the encrypted-null unprovable kind. Byte-different
  (g-r-e-y vs g-r-a-y), referent-identical. (Culture/spelling variant; ties to culture-invariant ordinal —
  byte order differs, referent is one.)
- **shared cause:** one colour concept; English spelling divergence (Commonwealth vs US).
- **established:** 2026-06-10 · Aaron ("same/grey-gray") · the grey/gray disambiguation.
- **falsifiable?:** yes here (unlike encrypted-null sameness) — they provably co-refer; the one caveat is
  **gray (Gy)** the SI radiation-dose unit, a *different* meaning excluded from this pair (see `gray/`).

**Carved sentence:** grey and gray are one colour spelled two ways — canonical `grey`, alias `gray`,
same referent.
