## Prior art — the letter-minimization has a human lineage (Aaron asked; anchor it)

The individual shapes anchor their fixed-points (Kleene, Banach, Tarski, CRDTs, Friston…). The **method
itself** — a minimal, finite, *lettered* catalog of the shapes a system settles into or changes through —
is one of the deep threads of 20th-century mathematics, and the catalog should stand on it:

- **Poincaré** — classification of the singular points of a dynamical system (node / saddle / focus /
  centre), *Mémoire sur les courbes définies par une équation différentielle* (1881–86). The original
  finite taxonomy of fixed-point shapes.
- **Andronov / Hopf** — structural stability + the bifurcation classification (saddle-node, transcritical,
  pitchfork, Hopf): the minimal catalog of *qualitative changes* (Andronov 1937; Hopf 1942).
- **Thom — catastrophe theory** — *proved* there are exactly **seven** elementary catastrophes up to
  codimension 4 (fold, cusp, swallowtail, butterfly, hyperbolic/elliptic/parabolic umbilic), *Stabilité
  structurelle et morphogénèse* (1972). A minimal finite named catalog — and note the count: **seven, like
  A–F + D⁰.**
- **Arnold — the ADE classification of simple singularities** (`A_n, D_n, E_6, E_7, E_8`). *The* canonical
  **letter-minimization** in mathematics; it recurs across Lie algebras, Dynkin diagrams, quivers, the
  McKay correspondence. Its **E₈ is the same E₈** as the `AdinkraCode`/Clifford/E8 privacy stack.
