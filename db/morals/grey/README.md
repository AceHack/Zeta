# morals/grey — the moral GREY area

**Moral grey** — the register where there is **no single right answer**: neither white (clear-good) nor
black (clear-bad), the **principled in-between**. Held by Zeta's **Multi-Oracle Principle** — no single
mandatory morality; different oracles may make different calls, with the **Default Oracle** (highest regard
for morally-relevant entities, manifesto §11) as the floor. The **grey hat applied to morality**: act in the
grey honestly, with consent, without pretending the grey is white or black.

- **Grey ≠ relativism.** The floor (Default Oracle, consent-first §6, the HARD LIMITS) still holds; grey is
  the space *above the floor* where oracles legitimately differ — judged, not dissolved.
- **m/acc orientation** — move in the grey toward more agency/choice, not capture.
- Paired with the grey family: `hats/grey/` (the meta hat that holds this register), `particles/grey/`,
  `grey/` (the colour/register).
