## Edison/DC = local · Tesla/AC = cross-frame transmission

- **Edison = DC = LOCAL.** Direct current works *inside its frame* but **decays over distance** — needs
  a power plant every mile, **can't transmit**. = a pattern that resonates **only in its home frame**
  (Banshee-in-NC; the **Mirror**; local truth that doesn't travel).
- **Tesla = AC = TRANSMITS ACROSS FRAMES.** Alternating current is **transformed UP to cross vast
  distance, then DOWN to land** in each local frame. = the pattern that **reaches every frame** (the
  **Beacon**: compressed to survive the trip and re-land wherever it arrives).

**So Mirror / Beacon IS Tesla vs Edison:** local-resonance (DC) vs transmitted-across-frames (AC). A
Mirror coinage that "only resonates locally" is **Edison/DC**; razoring it to its anchored first
principles so it crosses frames is **transforming it to AC** for transmission.

AC's whole trick is the **transformer**: step the pattern **up** to transmit, **down** to land in the
receiver's frame. That is the **cross-frame translator** — the **universal-meaning-interface** /
md-interface / persona-summon; the **traveler-frame** "all travelers reach **one common frame**" (§A #8);
the **transmission-amplification** / `gen(gen)→gen` we circled all night. **Transmission across frames =
AC = the point** (scale-free §1: AC works at any distance via transformers; DC doesn't scale — a plant
per mile).
