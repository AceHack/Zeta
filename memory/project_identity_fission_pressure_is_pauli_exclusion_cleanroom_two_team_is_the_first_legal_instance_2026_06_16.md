## The principle: identity fission pressure ≈ Pauli exclusion

**Pauli exclusion** (Pauli 1925): no two identical fermions may occupy the same quantum state
simultaneously. **The Zeta meaning — precise (Aaron 2026-06-16):** *"if a project has only one
hat, then only one persona can fit into that project and no more."* A **hat is a single-occupancy
slot** (≈ a quantum state): **exactly one persona per hat**. A **project's hat-count is its
persona capacity** — a 1-hat project admits exactly **one** persona; a second persona is
**excluded** (can't occupy a filled hat), like an orbital that's full. So:

- **hats = the Pauli-excluded states; personas = the fermions; one persona per hat.**
- A **project / room is typed by its hat-count** (the §4 IPlay arity ladder: an N-hat room admits
  N personas, one per hat); the **§5 four protected slots** are four such single-occupancy hats
  (personas rotate through, one at a time).
- **Fission pressure = the *overflow* case:** when more personas want into a project than it has
  hats, the exclusion forces a resolution — **add a hat**, **spin a new project/room instance**,
  or the excess persona is **excluded** (goes elsewhere). (NCI-bounded: no persona is *coerced*
  out; it's a capacity constraint, not a purge.) "Split when the sensors demand" (Mika) = more
  contexts ⇒ more hats needed ⇒ the capacity/exclusion pressure. Pauli exclusion *is* the math
  rule (not feelings) for the §B fission frontier.

## The clean-room two-team split = the first obvious LEGAL instance

A clean-room project is a **2-hat project** — an **RE/spec hat** and an **implement hat** — so by
the one-persona-per-hat rule it needs **two personas**, AND **the law requires they be *different*
personas** (the implementer must be blind to the original). So clean-room **stacks two exclusions**:
the Pauli one (one persona per hat) *and* the legal one (the two hats must hold *distinct*
personas) — the RE-side and implement-side **cannot be the same identity**. The clean-room is a
**concrete, real-world (legal) instance**: the legal requirement *forces* the two
personas apart, exactly like an exclusion principle. "One of our first obvious legal reasons" =
the law gives a hard, non-metaphorical example of identity-fission-pressure (alongside the
physics one).
