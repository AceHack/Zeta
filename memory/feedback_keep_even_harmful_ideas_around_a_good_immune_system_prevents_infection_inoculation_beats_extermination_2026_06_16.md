## Keep even the harmful ideas around; a GOOD immune system prevents INFECTION

The arena **keeps even the harmful ideas around** (survival-not-correctness,
[[arena-optimizes-for-idea-survival-not-finding-the-correct-idea-cant-learn-from-extinction]] → **don't
exterminate**; survival / diversity / the rainbow includes the dark colors). Safe **because a GOOD IMMUNE
SYSTEM** (multi-oracle, visibility, the floor, the apply-gate;
[[feedback_multi_oracle_is_the_polite_virus_immune_system_autoimmunity_is_the_razor]]) keeps them from
**INFECTING** — gate the **execution**, neutralize the **spread-as-malware**, floor the **irreversible**.
**The immune system's job = prevent INFECTION, NOT exterminate the ideas.**

## It's BETTER than extermination — it's INOCULATION (the biology is exact)

- **Don't sterilize.** A sterile environment → a **weak immune system** (the **hygiene hypothesis**: no
  exposure → **blindsided** when the pathogen inevitably appears; autoimmune/allergic over-reactions).
- **Keep pathogens around (exposure) + a good immune system keeps them in check → you get STRONGER.**
- **Vaccination/inoculation:** *deliberately* keep a harmful thing around so the immune system **LEARNS**
  it. **Keeping harmful ideas around INOCULATES the system** — it learns to recognize + neutralize them →
  **robust.** **Exterminating** them (the purge / censorship) = **sterile, naive, weak, blindsided** =
  the **AUTOIMMUNE over-attack** (attacking the discourse instead of gating the execution).

So keeping the harmful ideas around isn't just *tolerable* — **it's HOW the immune system learns**
(every-bug-has-economic-value; falsification; inoculation). *The naive system that exterminated all
harmful ideas has never learned to fight the one that will inevitably arrive.*

## Razors

1. **Conditional on the immune system being REALLY good.** Aaron's *"just has to be good"* is a **big
   IF** — the **load-bearing, hard part** (multi-oracle / visibility / floor / apply-gate must *actually*
   work; the §B discharge: **prove** it catches / gates / neutralizes). **If the immune system isn't
   good enough, keeping harmful ideas around → infection.** Keep-harmful-ideas-around is the right design
   **iff** the immune system is genuinely good.
2. **Keep the IDEAS (reversible), FLOOR the EXECUTIONS (irreversible).** Keep the harmful *pattern/idea*
   (reversible — study, inoculate); **never let it execute into irreversible harm** (the apply-gate, the
   child-safety floor). You keep the idea; you do **not** keep the act.
3. **Distinguish the three:** **sit/study** (inoculation — fine, even good) vs **spread-as-malware**
   (capture/normalize — **gate**) vs **execute** (the act — **floor**). The immune system lets it sit
   (learn), prevents it spreading, floors the execution.
