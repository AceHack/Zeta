Aaron 2026-06-19 (shadow\*), on the co-empowering-vs-coercive distinction used to characterize emergent
clusters/federations: *"this is Zeta's moral lens, if the math team can't find a more objective one."*

## The lens

**Good / healthy:** **co-empowerment + diversity-preservation (non-coercion)** — a blossoming federation.
**The concern:** **coercion + diversity-collapse** — the monoculture-pull. You never ask *"is this a
cartel / is this bad?"*; you **measure** *"is this co-empowering or coercive?"* It replaces moral
pre-judgment with a measurement.

**It is measurable** (not vibes): diversity-entropy (`Diversity.fs`), coupled-empowerment-gain
(`SocietalDora`), `ρ_owe` (`Decorrelation.fs`), and the `CoEmpowerField` NCI dynamics (non-coercion
preserves diversity → blossom; coercion collapses → monoculture). And it is **grounded in proven
society-math** — the NCI keystone (#7146 / `Diversity`): *mutual coercive observability collapses a
population to one*, which is objectively pathological (gain → 0). So the lens isn't asserted; it stands on a
result.

## Held provisional (the epistemic posture)

*"…if the math team can't find a more objective one."* This is the **current best measurable criterion**, not
a final claim — it is **open to replacement** by a more objective one. **Math-team obligation:** find a more
objective moral lens, or characterize whether this *is* the most-objective measurable criterion available
(don't-collapse — held as best-so-far, not certainty). Routed in the math-team handoff.

## Honest scope (the one peel)

It is an **objective MEASURE of a CHOSEN value** — *non-coercion + co-empowerment = good* — **not** value-free
objective morality. The value itself is the choice (the NCI keystone / mutual-empowerment / bounded-good).
That is consistent with the manifesto: **§11 Default Moral Regard + the Multi-Oracle Principle** — *no single
MANDATORY morality;* this is the **DEFAULT lens (the default oracle), not an imposed one*. The honesty is in
naming it as a measured chosen-value criterion and keeping it provisional.

`CoEmpowerField` fitness (blossom vs collapse) · `SocietalDora` health (empowerment vs capture) · the **NFT
mint-gate** (mutual-empowerment required) · **cluster/federation characterization** (healthy federation vs
coercive collapse) · the emotional-propagation extension (mutual-empowerment objective). One lens under all of
them. Ties: `Diversity` (NCI keystone); manifesto §11 + Multi-Oracle; `src/Core/{CoEmpowerField,SocietalDora,
