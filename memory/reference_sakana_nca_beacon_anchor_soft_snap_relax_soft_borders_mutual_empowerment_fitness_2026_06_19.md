## The anchor (anchor-to-human-prior-art)

**Sakana AI** (Tokyo; Llion Jones et al.) — trainable **neural cellular automata**: AI "species" on a 2D
grid compete for territory, learn, and fight (attack/defend directions); the **environment threshold**
(survival bar, resource/"money" level) decides the outcome — too high → stagnation (nothing survives, "app
store"), too low → bubble (empires grow on nothing then crumble), tuned → healthy ecosystem; "one tiny push
makes the whole system healthier." And the **loose→harden→relax** three-phase: permissive mixing (loose,
no borders) → crystallization (strict, borders harden) → relaxation (loosen, borders flow, coexist).

## What it validates in Zeta (convergent — honest scope)

- **loose→harden→relax = soft-snap-relax = `soft-primary-but-snappable`.** *"Too loose = soup, too strict =
  prison; start loose, harden to find your shape, then loosen to let things in."* This is verbatim the
  snap-discipline (`SoftValue.snap`; never-collapse-is-the-default-not-the-absolute).
- **borders harden-then-flow = soft borders.** Their borders are emergent + dissolve when the threshold
  relaxes — exactly "borders can't be counted on, space is soft" ([[zeta-ntp-phase-grounded-network-time-across-all-space-and-time]]).
- **environment-determines-winners + good-policy = mutual-empowerment fitness + societal-DORA.** The threshold
  tuning maps to the fitness/snap-policy; the health (monopoly vs healthy) is the societal-DORA readout
  (capture-rate vs empowerment-frequency).
