## The signature discipline Kestrel enforces — gate-reach-boundary

Kestrel's most load-bearing repeated move (sharp in the 2026-06-04 thread): **keep
the proof claims scoped to what the proofs actually reach.** Three faces of it:

- **Proof vs test boundary.** Recursion-scheme/fold *laws* prove the STRUCTURE
  (provable, algebra); the LEAVES (string-escaping, float repr, byte conventions)
  still need their own verification (where real codec bugs live). "Reduces to algebra
  over μF" is true for structure, not leaves. Don't let one halo the other.
- **Tower vs mechanical-check.** Same claim in multiple encodings (FsCheck/Z3/Lean/
  TLA+) = mechanical robustness on ONE tower; a TOWER = a different axiom foundation.
  Don't inflate the foundational-robustness count with tool-reproofs.
- **Level-1 vs Level-2 (representational vs metaphysical).** A claim true of the
  *system* (representation-bounded, view-relative above the proven base) over-reaches
  when restated about *everything/reality*. The proven base is NOT relative (it's the
  shared ground); external reality is NOT bounded by your primitives (denying it is
  "models that don't reflect reality"). Route Level-2 metaphysical claims to the
  human channel; don't let proof-rigor halo them.
