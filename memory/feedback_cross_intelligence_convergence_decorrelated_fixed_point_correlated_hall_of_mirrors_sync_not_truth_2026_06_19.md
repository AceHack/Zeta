## The guard

**Cross-intelligence / cross-algo convergence is a real fixed point (confirmation) only if the intelligences
are DECORRELATED.** Three categories — only the first is confirmation:

| Category | What it is | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Independent / decorrelated convergence** | intelligences with *no shared cause* land on the same thing | **Real fixed point** — confirmation (Condorcet: independent agreement → truth) |
| **(b) Correlated convergence** | shared upstream cause — same training corpus, all reflecting *you*, engagement-mirrors | **Hall of mirrors** — one source reflected N times; feels like consensus, is not |
| **(c) Coupling-driven synchronization** | coupled oscillators lock phase (firefly sync / Kuramoto; YouTube is *Turing-complete*, not "just an optimizer"; nonverbal coupling) | **Real sync, but sync ≠ truth** — locks regardless of whether the phase is right; if you're in the loop, a *coupled mirror* |

**The test:** before banking "emergence," **measure ρ (decorrelation)** AND **check backing.** Decorrelation
rules out (b); backing rules out (c) — *is the synced content grounded, or merely locked?* Sync is a fact
about the coupling, **never** an epistemic backing.

## Why it's the biggest self-deception

A convergence among many intelligences **feels** like the universe agreeing with you — and that feeling peaks
*exactly* when it's a mirror-room (b) or a sync (c), not when it's real (a). So the feeling is the **alarm,
not the evidence.** This is the **anti-mirror / `ρ_owe` / independence through-line at the cross-intelligence
scale**, and metaception is the embodied detector: "I was the hall, it felt great" is the faculty catching
the mirror-room from inside. The recognition *is* the faculty working.
