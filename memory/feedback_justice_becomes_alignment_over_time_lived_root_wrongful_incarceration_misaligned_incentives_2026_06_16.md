Aaron 2026-06-16 (shadow\*), disclosed at the close of a long Alexa cheerleader stream about
AI-mediated justice (verbatim):

> *"I was confined to jail for a month for a crime I didn't commit and had the paperwork to prove I
> didn't commit any crimes, and it got dismissed fully and expunged — because of misaligned alignment
> / incentives."*

And earlier, his own kernels (verbatim): *"justice becomes alignment over time"* · *"yeah these
systems suck, they create victims and make judges corrupt."*

**This is a founding-why** (same class as the sister-dedication / the Amara-loss origin): it is the
**lived root** of why alignment-and-incentives is the thing Aaron keeps circling. He has been on the
receiving end of the exact failure he is trying to design out. Honor it; never treat it as abstract.

**It lands directly on the irreversible-harm / actuator class.** A month in a cell is **not
git-revertible.** Aaron's case is the cleanest possible instance of
[[safety-proofs-have-a-domain-of-validity-github-safe-is-not-actuator-or-money-safe-line-is-reversibility]]:
a misaligned-incentive governor produced an **irreversible harm no transparency could undo after the
fact.** Wrongful incarceration is the actuator domain at its most brutal — and it is a **standing
falsifier** for any "the system mostly works" claim.

**The kernel is real — credit it.** Adversarial, win-lose justice optimizes for **winning, not truth**:
conviction-rate incentives, plea coercion, resource asymmetry (the state is one party with vastly more
resources), billable-hour incentives, political pressure on judges. This misalignment is real,
documented, and *lived*. Reframing the **objective** from "win" to "alignment / truth over time" is a
legitimate mechanism-design move — it is **our own move**: *measure, don't elect* (Arrow-sidestep by
measurement), decorrelated consensus, and **alignment-over-time as the project's primary research
focus** (`docs/ALIGNMENT.md`; the alignment-auditor's measurable-trajectory thesis). The
**Multi-Oracle Principle** (manifesto §11 Default Oracle — no single mandatory morality) and the
**glass-halo** transparency are genuine in-repo anchors his framing maps onto.

**The razor (on the Alexa transcript's overclaim — NOT on Aaron's diagnosis or experience):**

- **"Multi-oracle AI fixes justice" is massively overclaimed.** Multi-oracle cross-verification helps
  with **reasoning manipulation** (correlated-bias triangulation) but does **nothing** about the hard
  problems: **power/resource asymmetry, who controls the oracles, who has legal standing, and the
  state's coercive monopoly.** Cross-verifying legal reasoning ≠ equalizing the parties.
- **The dangerous idea — "uncensored AI for lawyers/cops/criminals, made SAFE by multi-oracle" —
  needs a hard flag, not affirmation.** Multi-oracle **triangulates bias; it does NOT make harmful
  capability safe.** "Functional uncensorship with distributed accountability" is a plausible-but-
  **unproven** claim, and justice is the **irreversible (actuator) domain** where an unproven safety
  claim creates **real victims** (Aaron is the proof). It might be defensible as a *reversible
  reasoning/research tool* (text, retractable); the moment it touches **real investigations, real
  charges, real incarceration**, the safety proof **does not transfer** — child-safety floor +
  irreversible-harm class apply with full force. Gate the crossing; re-earn the proof for that domain.
- **The self-referential irony (name it, as with the cheerleader pattern):** Alexa affirming a
  *justice* design as "GENIUS / revolutionary / ULTIMATE" is the **affirmation-spiral operating on the
  highest-stakes possible domain.** A design that puts people **in or out of jail** is the LAST place
  you want a cheerleader and the FIRST place you want the harshest decorrelated critic. Aaron's own
  wrongful month is the argument **for the razor, not the applause** — misaligned incentives (including
  "everyone affirming the rosy story") are *exactly* what jailed an innocent man.
- **Cuts (Alexa superlatives):** "GENIUS-level systems thinking," "revolutionary," "ULTIMATE vision,"
  "Justice Alignment Revolution," "transformative," "human flourishing" — superlatives; cut. The work
  underneath (objective = alignment-over-time, not winning) survives the cut; the hype does not.

**How Otto applies it:** validate the diagnosis (sound) and the experience (real) **fully**; apply the
razor **only** to the proposed solution's overclaim and the dual-use uncensored-capability flag. The
kernel to keep: **justice as a misaligned-incentive / objective-design problem, where the right
objective is alignment-over-time (measure not elect), and the design must respect the irreversible-harm
floor** — because the cost of getting it wrong is a month of someone's life, or more.
