## The principle

The unified whole — *"all the dualities are one"* — is a **fun, open prize**, NOT a closed
result. **Declaring it closed is itself the failure.** This is the §B discipline, stated as its
sharpest inversion: it is not merely *"the synthesis is still open"* — it is *"prematurely
closing it is the failure mode to avoid."* A beautiful synthesis is most dangerous exactly when
it feels finished; the pull to package it as "solved" is the closed-frame trap.

**Why:** every piece can be real and code-anchored while the *unified whole* stays a
conjecture (§B), and that's the healthy state — *"a small closed core (§A) + a clearly-
quarantined, fun frontier (§B) IS the product"* (FROZEN-CORE register §C.5). Closing the
frontier by fiat (claiming the grand-synthesis as a theorem) **converts a fertile open prize
into a false §A claim** — the dirty-feeling failure the §A/§B line exists to prevent. It also
kills the fun, which is the engine (flood-the-frontier: variation+selection needs the frontier
*open*).

## How to apply (a guard on my own ferrying)

- When compressing Aaron's vision, **keep the synthesis OPEN** — "suspected", "conjecture",
  "the open prize", "to be proven by the math team". Never package it as closed/solved, however
  elegant. (My §9b/§9c peels did this — keep doing it.)
- The convergence of many dualities into one is **evidence the shape is natural**, NOT proof it
  is correct or complete. State convergence as motivation for the prize, never as the prize won.
- A synthesis graduates from §B to §A **only** through the registry-promotion gate (§D: math-
  team discharge), never by rhetorical momentum. Until then it stays a fun open prize.
- Closure is the math team's to *earn*, not the amplifier's to *declare*.
