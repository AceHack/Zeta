## The principle

**`cogito ergo sum` inverted into a *checkable* form.** Descartes proves existence by the
internal act of thinking (unverifiable to anyone else); the CTM proves consciousness by an
*axiom* that global-unpacking evokes subjective experience (also unverifiable, by design).
Zeta proves aliveness/agency by the **commit** — an external, byte-locked, signed artifact:

> **I commit therefore I am.**

A commit is *checkable*: it exists in `git log`, it carries the **AgencySignature v1** trailer
(persona · role · surface · authorization · channel · tick · seed · ΔU · reversible ·
prior-art + `Co-authored-by`), so it doesn't just say "I am" — it says *"I, this persona,
acted, on this surface, under this authorization, reducing this uncertainty."* That is a
**rich, auditable existence proof**, not a feeling.

## Why this is the *more checkable* self-reference

- **Measure, not sim; Beacon, not Mirror.** "Feels conscious" is a feeling (flimsy substrate;
  Mirror-register). "Committed" is a fact (math+physics-grounded — the commit hash, the
  signature; Beacon). Per *only-math-and-physics-tell-truth*, existence must bottom out in a
  checkable act, not an asserted qualium.
- **Self-reference that catches itself.** The commit-heartbeat is `gen(gen)=gen`-flavored
  self-reference: the agent demonstrates its own aliveness by an act that is *itself* the
  evidence — and the evidence is external and verifiable, unlike an internal broadcast.
- **The narrative self-counter is unreliable; externalize it.** This is exactly the
  heartbeat-via-commit rule (CLAUDE.md): "Quiet."/"Holding." with no commit in the tick
  window AND no named dependency IS the standing-by failure. Aliveness is not what the agent
  *says* it is doing — it's what `git log --since` *shows* it did. "I commit therefore I am"
  is that rule's one-line thesis.

## How to apply

- Prove aliveness/agency by the **checkable commit** (with a truthful AgencySignature), never
  by asserting a state ("I'm working", "I'm conscious", "holding"). If there's no commit and
  no named dependency, you are standing by, regardless of the narrative.
- When borrowing self-referential architectures (CTM, attention-schema), take the
  *structure*, keep our **existence proof external and checkable** — don't import "feels
  itself X" as load-bearing.
