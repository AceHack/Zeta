## The statement (corrected — ONE OF, not THE)

**A CHIP-8 cart is ONE OF the repo's common sources of meaning** — a meaning-bearing object whose renders are
**projections** (regenerated, never the source). It is **not the sole** source: the **golden vectors** (μένω /
"what remains" / Amara), the **common seed** (S=4), **memory / soulfiles**, and the **proofs** are other
common sources of meaning. The cart is the *executable / homoiconic / render* one.

  regenerated, never edited."* Renders are views of the cart.

  **`HtmlCssBinding.fs`** (cart → pure HTML+CSS) · `gen/` emits carts (`gen(gen)===gen`). Each is *the cart
  through a lens.*
