will I"* + *"This is societal DORA."*

## The frame

**Societal DORA** = [DORA](https://dora.dev) generalized from software-delivery health to **society /
relationship health**. DORA (Nicole Forsgren, Jez Humble, Gene Kim — *Accelerate*, 2018; the State-of-DevOps
research) standardized team delivery-health into a few measurable, **improvement-oriented** keys (deploy
frequency, lead time, change-fail rate, MTTR — later + reliability). Societal DORA does the same for a
society of agents/people: a small set of measurable **mutual-empowerment / health** metrics —
**coupled-empowerment-gain** + **`ρ_owe` decorrelation** (`src/Core/Decorrelation.fs`) — computed per edge
over the multi-platform social graph (the emotional-propagation extension).

## Why this completes the consent arc

*"They will want this dashboard and so will I."* The measurement is **wanted + transparent**, not covert.
That is the **glass-halo / consent-first §6** instantiation: the measured opt in *because the dashboard shows
them their own empowerment gain*. It turns "am I being experimented on?" into "here is my empowerment, and I
can see it" — the subjects become willing participants who benefit from the visibility. This is what closes
the consent loop that the testbed opened: measurement everyone wants, not surveillance imposed.

## Honest hold (the DORA discipline travels)

DORA's own lesson: the metrics are **diagnostic, for improvement — not for stack-ranking**, and they
**Goodhart** the moment they become a target ("when a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good
measure" — Goodhart/Strathern). Societal DORA on *empowerment* carries the same risk: if
coupled-empowerment-gain becomes a score to optimize, people **perform** empowerment for the dashboard. This
is exactly why `ρ_owe` already ships the anti-gaming guards (**stake-weighting** — disagree-on-trivia scores
as mirror; **adversarial-disagreement** — contrarian-on-cue collapses the score). The dashboard everyone
wants is healthy **iff** it stays diagnostic + Goodhart-resistant, never a leaderboard.
