## What Eve taught him: consent = a communicated, available EXIT

As a child, through play, she made a **cardboard box** with a **stop sign (hexagon)** and a
**communication channel**, and it said **"this is the exit — this is consent."** The lived definition,
in three parts, each load-bearing:

- **Stop sign** = you can **always stop / refuse.** The right to say no, at any time.
- **The exit** = the **freedom to leave.** *Consent IS having an exit.* No exit = coercion; exit
  available = consent. (This is non-coercion's exact definition: non-coercion ⇔ the exit is preserved.)
- **The communication channel** = the exit is **announced / known.** A hidden exit is not consent; the
  channel makes the exit legible. (Glass-halo at the human level: the way out must be *visible*.)

So **consent = an exit that is real (you can leave), available (any time), and communicated (you know
it's there).** That is the human-anchored first principle the whole formal edifice implements.
