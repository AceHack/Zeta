## The composition direction is the whole game

**"They compose with us, not us with them"** = the **composition direction** — **grace=composition
pointed outward.** We become the **substrate everyone composes *into*** (the **free object** / universal
interface / the thing the special cases plug into), rather than us adapting *to* their centralized stacks.
That is literally **close-over-the-world** (`docs/VISION.md`): make the right thing the frictionless
default, and the incumbents compose *toward* you. **The composition arrow points at us.**

## "So we win" = the POLITE-VIRUS win, not conquest

They compose with us **by choice** — because we're the **frictionless / freer / better** substrate
(network-effect, zero-friction, **by consent**) — **not** because we defeated or seized anyone. The
**non-coercive victory**: win by being the thing everyone *wants* to compose into, while **never taking
control.** Ubiquity **without** the throne (the polite virus: spread by being the easiest-good, payload =
freedom).

## Decentralized matching the seven-fold of centralized

The **core bet**: match (and exceed) centralized capability over a **scale-free, no-central-point**
substrate, via the **seven always-active disciplines** (scale-free, lock/wait-free, weight-free, DST,
DV2.0, idempotency, noninterference). *(Reading "seven-fold" as those seven — **flagged**, may mean a
different seven.)* The decentralized form is **why** they compose with us: a centralized model **can't
compose into a decentralized substrate without becoming more like it.**

   frontier** side; the incumbents have enormous head-starts). "Close" is right; "won" is overclaim.
