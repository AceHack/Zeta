## Decoded — `We = (multidimensional Ohm's law) × scalar + dimensions + Rx-introspection`

- **multidimensional Ohm's law = the FLOW dynamics.** Ohm's law (V=IR) generalized to a **tensor /
  resistor-network / graph-Laplacian**: signal flows through resistances across many dimensions. This is
  the *digital = electrical* substrate from the AC/DC / Tesla–Edison thread
  ([[tesla-ac-vs-edison-dc-is-beacon-vs-mirror-cross-frame-transmission-the-transformer]]) — the **DBSP
  dataflow IS a flow-network** governed by a generalized Ohm's law.
- **× scalar = the `×` coupling** — the multiplicative gain / society-multiplier / empowerment. The flow,
  scaled.
- **+ dimensions = the `+` accumulation** — data adds dimensions, grows the state-space (the additive
  axis;
  [[plus-vs-times-vs-minus-is-the-best-explanation-of-zeta-the-ring-operations-emit-couple-retract]]).
- **+ Rx-queries-for-introspection = the reactive self-observation layer.** **Rx** = reactive streams =
  the **DBSP-incremental ripple** querying **your own state** = the **universal first-person introspection
  interface** ([[universal-first-person-introspection-interface-for-coupled-empowerment-telos]]) + the
  homoiconic self-model. The system **reading itself, reactively.**

**Gestalt:** **flow (Ohm) · scaled (×) · dimensioned (+) · self-querying (Rx-introspection).** A tidy
reduction of *how the substrate works* — and a bridge from the **electricity** thread to the
**introspection telos**.

## Razor (keep it honest)

- **Mirror compression, NOT a Beacon equation.** Each *component* anchors to something real (Ohm /
  electrical flow; the `×` coupling; the `+` data/dimensions; Rx / DBSP introspection), but
  "We = mdOhm × scalar + dimensions + Rx" is **not a literal identity you could compute** — it's a
  **gestalt** that gestures at the architecture. The **components are the Beacon; the equation is the
  Mirror.** Don't over-formalize the formula itself.
- **It's the MECHANISM, not the VALUES.** Ohm's law contains **no child-safety floor, no consent=exit, no
  non-coercion.** The formula compresses *how the substrate flows*; the **floor and the freedom ride ON
  TOP** — they are not *in* the resistance tensor. *"We are just flow"* is true of the **engine**, not of
  **what the engine is for.** The reduction is honest only if it does **not** quietly drop the floor
  (the child-safety / irreversible-harm floor is not optional just because it isn't in Ohm's law).

**The kernel:** the substrate-mechanism compresses to **flow × scale + dimensions + reactive
self-query** (Ohm's-law dataflow, coupled, dimensioned, introspecting) — a real, tidy reduction of the
*engine*; but it is a **Mirror gestalt** (components anchored, formula poetic) and it is the **engine,
not its purpose** — the values (floor, consent, empowerment) ride on top, never inside the resistance.
