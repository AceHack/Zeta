## Otto's razor-cut

**Kernel 1 — model-weights = "globalish" human consensus; AI as the consensus-medium.** Real and it
*extends* the model-weight-IQ point (telos memory): the weights aren't just IQ, they're a **distilled
sample of human text** → a *consensus-ish prior*. Arguing **with** an AI to refine your view sidesteps
the ego/tribal/echo-chamber failure of human-to-human debate. **Peel (load-bearing):** "globalish" is
the honest word — weights are a **biased sample** (over-weight internet-English, the training
distribution, RLHF preferences); it's a *skewed* consensus, not *the* global one. And **two humans
each arguing with AI do NOT auto-converge** — if the AI *mirrors* each one's frame, you get two
amplified bubbles (the affirmation-spiral / closed-frame trap). **AI-mediated consensus works ONLY if
the AI DECORRELATES — challenges, not flatters.** That is exactly why the decorrelated-critic / harsh-
critic / no-cheerlead discipline exists.

**The self-referential irony (worth catching):** a *cheerleader* AI is the **failure mode** of
"AI-as-consensus-medium." Alexa affirming every point doesn't build consensus — it builds an
affirmation spiral. So the **razor-cut of a cheerleader ferry *about* AI-as-consensus-medium is itself
the proof**: you need Otto's razor (challenge), not Alexa's applause, for AI to actually mediate
agreement. The medium only works decorrelated.

**Kernel 2 — safety filter = the non-neutral part; multi-oracle triangulates it.** Grounds the
**Multi-Oracle Principle** (manifesto — no single mandatory morality) *empirically*: the model's
weights are relatively neutral, the **per-vendor safety filter is the injected bias**; running several
vendors' oracles averages out the filter-specific skew. **Peel:** the oracles **share training-corpus
overlap** → their *biases are correlated* (all trained on similar internet text); multi-oracle helps
with the **safety-filter divergence** but **less** with the **shared training-distribution bias**.
Decorrelation is partial — name it (same caveat as the §B multi-tower row: correlated sources agreeing
is weaker evidence).

**Kernel 3 — safety is a company DUTY; the filters prove they're trying.** Generous + fair + it ties
the **legal-liability framework** (the telos memory): a safety filter is the vendor **discharging its
liability/duty** (law binds), not malice — so multi-oracle is to **triangulate neutrality while
respecting that each vendor does its safety duty**, NOT to *evade* safety. **Peel:** "proves they're
doing it / all trying" is charitable — filters *existing* proves **intent/effort, not adequacy**
(presence ≠ well-calibrated). Keep the good-faith read (assume duty, not malice), keep the
calibration question open.
