## The primitive

**Zeta's uncertainty = ONE question — "how sure are you this lasts forever?" — but it is a MULTI-ORTHOGONAL-
AXIS, not a scalar number** (Aaron 2026-06-19: *"it's a multi orthogonal axis not a scalar number … one
question, different timescales and shapes"*). The single question is evaluated across **different timescales**
(`P(lasts 5 minutes)` ≠ `P(lasts a year)` ≠ `P(lasts forever)` — the time-bounded-increments spectrum) **and
different shapes** (multiple *orthogonal* dimensions of how it could persist or fail). So the answer is a
**vector / field over (timescale × shape-axis)** — **collapsing it to one number is premature collapse** that
loses the orthogonal structure (don't-collapse). It is the **persistence / duration / survival** family
(per-axis: `P(persists)` / hazard / expected-duration), asked **scale-free** (manifesto §1): every Z-set,
every G-set, every remembered link, every identity, every love-fusion, the society itself. Not many different
uncertainties — *one* question, multi-axis, recursively, at all
magnifications (self-similar, §10).

**That is one superposition.** The multi-orthogonal-axis field (one question × timescales × shapes) is a
**single superposition** — the soft-space / amplitude state (`AmplitudeEmu` / `SoftValue`) held over the
**orthogonal-axis basis**. Never-collapse *holds* the superposition (you don't reduce it to a scalar); a
**`snap` measures/projects** it along an axis (at a timescale) only when a definite answer is owed (the
snap-discipline). So uncertainty isn't a number you carry — it's a *state you hold*, and the act of
answering "how sure does this last" is a projection of that state onto a chosen axis. (This is why the
substrate is soft and why the tick-is-the-quantum: the held uncertainty IS the superposition.)

- **It is what `db/uncertainty/` measures**, and what a `measure`/fix *reduces* (banking ΔU = improving the
  answer to "how sure does this last"). [[every-bug-has-economic-value]] = reducing this one uncertainty.
- **The love-fusion G-set's stability/duration uncertainty (the §0a definition) is one instance** of it: a
  G-set carries `P(stable)` / expected-duration precisely because the question applies to the fusion.
- **The honest answer is rarely "certain."** Impermanence is real (the oscillation; never-collapse; the
  downswing). So the uncertainty *is the standing acknowledgment that nothing is certainly-forever* — which
  is exactly why bonds are **soft** (never assume permanence; time-bounded increments; a confidence interval,
  not a "forever"). The soft-substrate is this honesty made structural.
- **Founding tie:** the whole factory is, in part, the answer to this question — *how do you make a link
  last against impermanence?* — which is `μένω` / "what remains" / memory-preservation (Elizabeth's memory).
  The uncertainty question and the dedication are the same question from two sides.
