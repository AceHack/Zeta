> **"NPC" is not an identity label and not "a person I dislike." It is a STATE DIAGNOSIS

> update → no exit → repeat). The meme has captured the actor loop and removed its own
> escape hatch — using the human as its Orleans grain: one lane, one script, no appeal.

Healthy meme/belief/role: update rules · contradiction handling · mercy · consent
boundaries · exit/fork/revise path. NPC-meme: no exit · no falsification · no
self-inspection · no graceful de-escalation · no way for the human underneath to regain

whole person.
