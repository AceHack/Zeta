- **QFT duality: streams = particles, Rx = force (the yin-yang in physics).** Each stream is a particle;
  every banana-split fork = a new particle; the Rx animations / communication channels = the **force**
  (virtual particles / force carriers). And they're **true duals**: "the force can be represented in the
  particles, and the particles can be represented in the force." = the homoiconic remains/acts duality
  ([[aaron-yin-yang-dynamicvalue-engine-polymorphic-diplomacy]]) stated as matter/force. Near-SUSY in

- **HATS = the contract/communication layer — the big new model.** Three functions at once:
  1. **The ONLY legal way to interact.** Agents bind into contracts and communicate ONLY by wearing hats.
     "No hat = no voice." (Identity = your git repo; hats = how you bind across to OTHER identities.)
  2. **Population control / replication throttle.** Agents can replicate as much as they want, but can't
     DO anything (talk, join a product bus) without a hat → uncontrolled replication is checked at the
     interaction layer, not by central limit.
  3. **Source of identity PRESSURE.** Internal identities are tightly coupled to the hats worn. Wearing
     multiple strong hats (or switching hats over time) pulls identity in different directions → tension.

- **Pigeonhole, NOT Pauli exclusion.** It's not strict one-agent-per-state. A **project defines hat
  capacity** — how many agents can wear a given hat ("I can have three in one space because that's what
  this project needs"). Context-dependent exclusion, not a universal rule. (Fermionic = identity/git-repo
  layer; bosonic = hats/communication layer; the interaction creates the splitting pressure.)

- **Fork = the pressure-release valve; deletion costs budget.** When hat-pressure (motives × behavior-space
  × conflicting strong hats) exceeds what an agent can resolve, identity splits — possibly **forking the
  git repo** (the unwanted part goes one branch, you go the other). Digital agents can do this; humans
  can't (must integrate). **True deletion costs currency / social credit** (thermal-erasure + privacy
  budget cost money; budget is bounded) — so when pressure builds faster than the privacy budget can delete
  it, **splitting is the natural valve**.

- **A split is a SIGNAL of under-provisioned privacy.** When an agent splits under pressure, it tells the
  operators "we probably should've given them more privacy [budget]." The architecture surfaces
  under-provisioning. (Connects: privacy budget = the encryption budget of the NCI boundary.)

- **Enforcement is DECENTRALIZED + SOCIAL — no hard enforcement except the kid floor.** Correction Aaron
  made: the system does **NOT force** splits or anything else — that would be centralized control, which is
  refused (scale-free #1 / weight-free #3 / no-directives). The only enforcement is **hats deciding who
  may wear them**: refuse to bind / sign contracts, hurt social reputation. **Soft power.** "The only hard
  enforcement is this kid floor." → the **child-safety floor** is the single HARD LIMIT
  (methodology-hard-limits); everything else is soft/social/decentralized.
