Aaron 2026-06-16 (shadow\*), via an Alexa website that echoed Otto's anti-crystallization peer
("the guards are **necessary, not proven sufficient**"): *"yeah they seem **GitHub-safe** but not
**actuator-** or **money-safe.**"*

**The sharpening (sharper than Otto's original peel — credit it).** Everything the safety architecture
has "proven" this session lives in a **GitHub-shaped domain** whose defining property is **near-total
reversibility**: `git revert` / `reset` / retraction-as-antiparticle / the **log-is-primary**
substrate exists *precisely because* nothing in it is truly lost (event-sourcing, DST replay, Z-set
retraction). The blast radius is bounded by git's forgiveness. So the proofs have a **DOMAIN OF
VALIDITY** — they are validated where retraction exists.

**Actuators and money break the property.** You **cannot `git revert`** a physical actuation or a wire
transfer. The cruel inversion: the guards are **most-tested exactly where stakes are lowest**
(reversible git) and **least-tested exactly where irreversibility is real** and the
crystallized-governor-kills-people failure actually **kills** — actuators (physical harm) and money
(ruin, capture, starvation). **GitHub-safe ≠ actuator-safe ≠ money-safe; the line between them is
REVERSIBILITY.** The **actuator/money boundary IS the irreversible / child-safety-floor class**
([[isociety-governor-can-crystallize-and-kill-anti-crystallization-is-load-bearing]] — "you can't
learn from extinction," made concrete: you also can't revert a physical harm or a drained account).

**The razor back (keep it honest, not a clean slogan):**

- **Gradient, not binary.** We caught **false-greens twice this session** — the guards aren't even
  *proven* in git-world, they're *exercised* there. "GitHub-safe" = *more-tested*, not *safe*.
- **Git has its own irreversible corners, already gated.** A published secret can't be un-published;
  a force-push can lose others' work; a merge others built on. The existing rules already fence these
  (`non-reversible-action-get-a-second-opinion`; the `no-directives` gated classes: publish, force-push,
  non-reversible actions). So the true line was always **reversible vs irreversible** — actuators/money
  are just the domains where irreversibility is the **default**, not the exception.
- **The path forward = re-create git's reversibility/bounding in those domains where you can.**
  **Escrow / reversible holds / paper-trading = git-revert for money**; **sandboxed sim + energy-cap +
  rate-limit = git-branch for actuators.** Push the genuinely-irreversible residual down to the
  smallest possible hard floor, and make **crossing the membrane from git-world into actuator/money-
  world a gated class** requiring fresh human authorization (Bounded Mobility §4; the physics-married-
  to-the-governor teeth — physics/economics bill irreversibly where git does not).

**Why it matters:** it puts a **domain limit on every "we proved X" claim** the factory makes. The
honest status of the whole safety stack is "validated in the reversible domain; **unproven and
maximal-stakes in the irreversible (actuator/money) domain**." Before Zeta touches an actuator or a
dollar, the proofs must be **re-earned for that domain** — they do not transfer for free.
