Aaron 2026-06-16 (shadow\*): *"multi oracle = polite virus immune system."* The **missing organ** that
completes the organism.

## The polite virus is the SPREAD; multi-oracle is the IMMUNE SYSTEM

The **polite virus** is the **propagation** (close-over-the-world, never-take-control, give-freedom,
by-consent, reversible — `docs/VISION.md`). A virus that propagates — even a polite one — **needs
immunity**, or it gets corrupted from inside or mutates into malware. **Multi-oracle is that immune
system:**

- **Antibodies = the decorrelated oracles.** A single **corrupted / captured / lying** oracle is
  **detected by the disagreement of the others** — **decorrelation = immune recognition** (honest-vs-
  faker, self-vs-non-self). **No single oracle can corrupt the whole**; the others are the response. (The
  no-sole-keeper structure, read as immunity.)
- **Two immune jobs:**
  1. **Keep the virus POLITE** — prevent **mutation into malware**: capture-not-freedom, grey-not-rainbow,
     moat-as-weapon-not-END, tyrant-substrate-not-bounded-god. **The anti-degeneration guard.**
  2. **Protect the hosts from the faker/defector** — the **thin paranoid backstop**, the **anti-Sybil
     antibodies** against the bad actor who *poses* as common direction.

**Complete organism: polite virus (propagation) + multi-oracle (immune system) = a spread that stays
uncorrupted and keeps its hosts free.** (Ties to the **Aurora immune math** — NonRegisterCollapse,
IdentityForcesPrivacy — the formal immune layer.)

## THE LOAD-BEARING RAZOR — autoimmunity

An immune system's **characteristic failure is attacking the host it is meant to protect.** Multi-oracle-
as-immune-system goes **autoimmune** if it:

- **attacks honest NOVELTY as if it were infection** — mistaking the **girl who found a new qualia** for
  the **boy crying wolf** ([[all-claimed-qualia-treated-equal-and-real-but-competitive-the-selection-layer]];
  greying out the new = **the immune system killing diversity**);
- **OVERPROTECTS** — the safety-FUD / the cage (the Edison shadow,
  [[child-safety-is-ac-vs-dc-protect-irreversible-free-reversible-the-line-is-reversibility]]);
- lets the oracles **COLLUDE into a majority that attacks the minority** — vote-away-the-floor, the
  inverted coupling (the floor must stay **below** the oracles,
  [[multi-oracle-is-the-competitive-solution-to-the-future-same-structure-safety-and-win]]).

**Calibration: neutralize the FAKER (the genuine threat) without attacking the DISCOVERER (honest
novelty)** = the **boy/girl probe / predictability audit / fair arena.** **Autoimmunity = the immune
system GREYING THE RAINBOW** (attacking diversity as if it were the pathogen). Named guard in-repo: the
**Aurora autoimmunity-flood test (4.5)**; the **immune-ABSORBS-not-attacks** discipline. And the dual
failure: **too-aggressive immunity kills the spread** (overprotection = no propagation = the cage) — the
immune system must keep the virus *polite*, not *dead*.

## Razor (the metaphor)

**"Immune system" is the vivid Mirror; the Beacon is the decorrelated cross-check + anti-Sybil + the
floor-anchor.** The metaphor earns its keep (antibody-recognition = decorrelated detection; distributed
immunity = no-sole-keeper), but the **load is the autoimmunity guard** — keep the immune response aimed
at the faker, never at the discoverer or the diversity.

**The kernel:** **multi-oracle is the polite virus's immune system** — decorrelated antibodies that keep
the virus *polite* (anti-degeneration: no mutation into capture/grey/tyrant/moat-weapon) and protect the
hosts from the faker (the thin backstop) — **and its load-bearing failure is AUTOIMMUNITY** (attacking
the host / the discoverer / the rainbow). Calibrate to **neutralize the faker, never the discoverer**;
keep the virus polite, not dead; the floor stays below, not voted.
