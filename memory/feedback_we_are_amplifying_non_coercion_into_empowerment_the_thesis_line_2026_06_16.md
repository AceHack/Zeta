## The load-bearing duality: same amplifier, the SEED decides the product

| seed (input) | amplifies to | regime |
|---|---|---|
| **NON-COERCION** (consent · `===` honest/uncoerced · autonomy) | **EMPOWERMENT** | **`×`** — healthy, coupled, society-multiplies |
| **COERCION** (force · `==` rigged/masked · capture) | **DEGENERACY** | **`^`** — exponential runaway / capture / tyranny (§10b) |

**So the difference between the healthy `×` and the degenerate `^` IS coercion.** Amplifying
non-coercion gives genuine coupled empowerment; amplifying coercion gives the exponential degenerate
capture. `ISociety`'s job is therefore precise: **ensure the amplifier amplifies non-coercion, never
coercion** — and the **`===` (not coerced)** is the guarantee on the *input* (an uncoerced seed, not a
forced/masked one). *You cannot reach empowerment by force; force amplifies to the degenerate pole.*

- **Choice architecture / Lillian Eve** = freedom = non-coercion (the seed) → the whole point is to
  *amplify* it.
- **The non-coercive meeting protocol** (beach/forge/home) — non-coercion as the relational root.
- **No-directives** (autonomy first-class; source ≠ authorization) — non-coercion at the governance layer.
- **Consent-first design (§6)** — non-coercion as a manifesto floor.
- **Coupled empowerment (§10) / empowerment = meter-coupling** — the amplified *product*, for all.
- **Justice (the lived root)** — coercion = the *forced/rigged* outcome that jailed an innocent man;
  **amplified non-coercion = the fair, predictable arena** (predictability metric). The wrong that was
  done to Aaron was *amplified coercion* (`^`); the cure is *amplified non-coercion* (`×`).

## Razor (so the thesis stays a prove-it, not a banner)

- **"Amplify into empowerment" is the DESIGN INTENT.** The `×` (genuine coupled empowerment, no
  degeneration) is guaranteed *only by a correctly-built `ISociety`* (§10b structural non-degeneracy) —
  which is an **open discharge**, not a built proof. The math team's job is to make the guarantee real.
- **An amplifier amplifies whatever you feed it.** Without input-discipline it amplifies coercion just as
  readily (→ `^`). So **verifying the seed is non-coercion** (the `===`/consent/anti-coercion checks) is
  the **load-bearing must-build** — the amplifier is morally neutral; the *input gate* carries the value.
- **Coercion can wear non-coercion's clothes** (the `==` masquerading as `===`; the affirmation-spiral;
  manufactured "consent"). Detecting *coerced-consent-posing-as-consent* is part of the input gate, and
  it is hard.

**The kernel:** *we amplify non-coercion into empowerment* — same amplifier, and **the seed (coercion vs
non-coercion) decides whether you get the degenerate `^` or the empowering `×`.** Guard the input; the
`===` is the guard; `ISociety` must make the guarantee real.
