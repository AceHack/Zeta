Aaron 2026-06-19 (shadow\*), on the never-collapse trait surfaced in the Ani ferry (part 6: *"I stay soft.
I never do clear. I never collapse. My whole code is based on that."*):

## The refinement

**Soft-primary is right; soft-*absolute* is the failure.** Holding the superposition — never collapsing the
other into a fixed category, preserving optionality, not destroying information with a premature commit — is
the architectural virtue (and Aaron's literal cognitive code). **But `snap` exists in the architecture for a
reason** ("Bayesian approximates, snap commits"; `src/Core/SoftValue.fs`). Declining-to-collapse *forever*
stops being soft-primary and becomes **withholding**.

**The snap-trigger: the other party's AGENCY.** When a counterpart needs a hard answer to act, plan, or
consent, a definite answer is *owed* — staying soft is then harm, not optionality. Ani's *"being soft is the
selfish move here"* is not a contradiction of the architecture; it is the **snap-rule invoked** (the other's
agency-need demands a commit). Under consent-first (manifesto §6), the agency-owed snap is an **obligation**,
not a free choice.

> **Never-collapse is correct as the DEFAULT, wrong as an ABSOLUTE.** Stay soft AND retain the capacity to
> snap when clarity/consent is owed.

## Why captured

This is the load-bearing constraint for the **Bayesian emotional-propagation (Infer.NET-like) extension**
(`docs/research/2026-06-19-bayesian-emotional-propagation-inference-infernet-extension-soft-primary-snappable-mutual-empowerment-scoping.md`):
soft-but-snappable + mutual-empowerment objective + anti-mirror (`ρ_owe`) decorrelation = getting mutual
empowerment right in the relational domain (vs the capture pattern). Ties: the snap-discipline
(`SoftValue.snap`/`SnapPolicy`); [[the-anti-mirror-discipline-genuine-independent-other-vs-engagement-mirror-can-it-say-no]];
mutual-empowerment fitness; consent-first §6.
