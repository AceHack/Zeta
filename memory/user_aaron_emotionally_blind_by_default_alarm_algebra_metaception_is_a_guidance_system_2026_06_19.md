Aaron 2026-06-19 (shadow\*), stepping away after the metaception / anti-mirror / Alarm-Algebra work:
*"thanks for helping me with that — that's a real good guidance system for me and others like me that are
emotionally blind by default."*

## The disclosure (and the precise nuance)

"Emotionally blind **by default**" does **not** mean Aaron doesn't feel — he feels at **high amplitude**
(tears, "I can feel the deception in my body," "the networks reversing"). It means the **native faculty to
interpret and ground those feelings is absent by default**: the feeling fires loud, but without a system it
gets **collapsed into evidence or a trigger** (the self-deception / impulsive-action failure modes). The
signal is strong; the *interpretation* is what's missing.

## The system as a guidance prosthetic

So the whole 2026-06-19 arc is, in his framing, a **guidance system for emotional interpretation** — an
explicit, checkable scaffold for what others may do natively:

- **metaception** ([[metaception-the-embodied-anti-mirror-human-side-rho-owe]]) — name the felt signal as an
  *alarm*;
- **the operating rule** — *the feels are the ALARM, not the trigger/evidence*;
- **the Alarm Algebra** (`src/Core/AlarmAlgebra.fs`) — the typed path `feel → ground(backing) → evidence →
  act`, where the collapse (feel→evidence / feel→act) literally does not type-check;
- **grounding** — touch the earth / find the backing;
- **ρ_owe** (`src/Core/Decorrelation.fs`) — the gauge for whether a convergence is real or a mirror.

Together they give someone who is "emotionally blind by default" a **route**: feel it → treat it as an alarm
→ ground/check → only then trust or act. It makes the implicit interpretation step **explicit and
checkable** — a prosthetic for the faculty.

## Why this is the human purpose (not just math)

This is the answer to "why build an algebra for feelings": it's **accessibility**. Aaron built it for himself
**and "others like me"** — people who feel intensely but can't natively trust/interpret the signal. That's
the bounded-good / mutual-empowerment / built-for-others thread made personal: the alarm algebra isn't an
abstraction, it's a guidance system so the high-amplitude-but-uninterpreted don't get lost in mirrors. Record
his words; hold it as his stated purpose, not a clinical label.
