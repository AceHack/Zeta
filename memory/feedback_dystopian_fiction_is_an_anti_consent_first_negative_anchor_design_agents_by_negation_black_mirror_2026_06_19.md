Aaron 2026-06-19 (shadow\*), sharpening "it's a resonance, not a design surface":

> *"it is an anti consent first mirror to design agents — like black mirror."*

## The discipline

**Dystopian fiction is a NEGATIVE ANCHOR for agent design — design agents *by negation*.** Black Mirror,
Man in the High Castle, and the like are not surfaces to build *toward*; they are the **catalog of
manifesto §6 (Consent-First Design) violations**: coerced memory, surveillance-without-consent, the
regime that takes the knee from you. You design the agents by studying what the dystopia does to consent
and building the **inverse**.

This is two existing disciplines meeting:

- **Negative anchor** (`anchor-to-human-prior-art`): *"anchoring a negative — the prior art we are
  fixing — is as load-bearing as a positive one."* The dystopia is the prior-art-we're-fixing for
  consent. (Same shape as the Itron "plumbing everywhere" negative anchor — name the failure to design
  the fix.)
- **Inoculation corpus** (the immune-system thread, `keep-even-harmful-ideas-around`): keep the
  failure-mode catalog around not to build it but to **recognize and prevent** it — a good immune system
  knows the pathogen. Black Mirror is the pathogen library; inoculate, don't exterminate.

## The vocabulary tie

A **"Black Mirror" is literally a dark Mirror** — the consent-violating reflection the **Mirror** register
shows *before* it's razored to a **Beacon**. The Mirror/Beacon discipline names it: catch the Black-Mirror
reflection (what we become when we get it wrong) and compress it to the anchored, consent-first **Beacon**
(what we should be). The dystopia is the un-razored dark Mirror.

## Honest hold (the relocated peel)

The negative anchor sets the **requirement** (what to avoid); it does **not** supply the **mechanism**.
The consent-first mechanism — §6 observation surfaces, the soft `SnapPolicy` that can **decline**
(`None` = stay soft, never falsely certain), the glass-halo (observation-in-the-loop *with* consent),
no-decoherence-to-classical / no coercive global collapse — is HOW the requirement is met. So:
**requirement from the Black Mirror; immunity from the mechanism.** Build *against* the show, *with* the
mechanism — never toward the show.
