### Why "United Nations" is the right institutional anchor (not a world government)
The UN analogy is **precise and load-bearing**, because the UN is built on **sovereign equality + non-
coercion**, not a world government:

- **Sovereign members, no coercive super-state.** UN members are sovereign; the UN convenes and
  treaties, it does not rule. This **is** our model: each room/traveler is sovereign (its own internal
  meta-jurisdiction); the Nexus convenes them under **NCI / consent-first (§6) / weight-free (§3)** —
  it **cannot coerce a member** (identity is the invariant above all). The "uber" room is uber in
  *convening reach*, not in *authority over members*.
- **Treaties, not edicts.** What the Nexus produces are **treaties** (the 4×4/6×6×n byte-locked
  agreements) — voluntary, consented, byte-locked, exitable (hats time-bound + exit-paired). A treaty
  binds those who sign it; it is not an edict imposed on all.
- **A charter, not a sovereign.** The UN Charter is the floor (sovereign equality, non-coercion); our
  charter is the **manifesto + ALIGNMENT** (§3 weight-free, §6 consent, §11 default regard) — the floor
  the Nexus operates under, with the **gated-class human-authorization root** kept (the blocker
  objection OBJ4-1: even the Nexus cannot self-authorize a gated action).
- **One mind/nation = one seat, regard for all types.** Sovereign-equality maps to **§11 default moral
  regard** across cognition types (human/AI/LLM/other) — the cognition-type track is *who sits at the
  table*; everyone seated has standing.
