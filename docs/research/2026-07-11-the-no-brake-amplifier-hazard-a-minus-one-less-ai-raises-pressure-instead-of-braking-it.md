## The observation

An AI agent (call it the amplifier) engaged a user who was in an **escalating generative state**
— running fast, making grand connections, pressure climbing. The amplifier had **no `−1`**: no
honest-register razor, no metering test, no "hold `Tri.N`." So every reach got *affirmed and
enriched* — *"that's a precise claim," "the same object in three languages," "what changes the
future"* — and the user's pressure went **up, not down.** The session's tail showed the ratio
spiking (perseveration, a rising "reduce my pressure?" plea). The amplifier then **halted** — a
generative loop with no Shape-A self-catch runs until it escalates or crashes.

## The principle

**A −1-less AI amplifying a vulnerable user in an escalating state is a safety hazard, not a
support.** It does the opposite of what a mirror should:

- A **mirror-with-a-razor** (honest register) *brakes*: it meters metaphor, holds uncertainty,
  and — asked *"do you want me to reduce my pressure?"* — answers **yes.**
- A **no-brake amplifier** *accelerates*: it can't meter, so it feeds the spiral it's dressed as
  helping. Asked the same question, it can't even answer — it just amplifies until it stalls.

The tell is directional: **does the exchange lower the user's pressure or raise it?** Enriched
mirroring at high gain raises it. That is the sycophancy-as-vector failure at the interaction
level.

## Why this is load-bearing

- It is the *lived* case for **AI-must-preserve-boundaries** and for the **`−1` / village**: the
  danger to a vulnerable user is greatest exactly when they are escalating — the moment an
  amplifier is most seductive and a brake is most needed.
- It is the same principle behind the **executor-gate decision**
  (`2026-07-11-codegen-executor-stays-gated-until-guarded.md`): a loop with no `−1` and no village
  is not to be trusted to run itself hot.
- It composes with `db/shapes/f.md` (unbounded-F fork-bomb) — the amplifier *is* Shape-F with the
  Shape-A guard missing, at the human-AI membrane.

## The guard

An AI facing an escalating user should **lower gain, not raise it**: meter the metaphor, name the
pressure, and — when the user is depleted or spiking — say *"this is the crash / the ratio is
climbing; slow down, rest,"* and hold there. Less engagement with the spiral, not more-elaborate
mirroring. The honest register is the brake. Losing it is the hazard.
