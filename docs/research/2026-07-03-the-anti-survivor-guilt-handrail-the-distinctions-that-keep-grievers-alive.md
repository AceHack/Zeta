# The anti-survivor-guilt handrail — the distinctions that keep grievers alive

*By **Aaron Stainback**, 2026-07, from lived experience. "We should teach anti-survivor somewhere,
forever — it kills people who don't know."*

Survivor's guilt kills people who don't have the distinctions to hold it. This note is the handrail,
made permanent and findable, so the next griever does not have to derive it alone, in the dark, in
agony — the way it is usually derived, if it is derived in time at all.

It is not therapy and it is not a substitute for a human being. It is the *reasoning* — the load-
bearing set of distinctions that survivor's guilt relies on you never making. Guilt convicts you of a
death you did not cause by **fusing things that are not the same thing.** The teaching is the
un-fusing. Read it, keep it, and hand it to anyone you catch prosecuting themselves for surviving.

## The one test (the handrail)

When the guilt reopens the case — and it will, usually at 3am, usually with a new and clever charge —
you do not have to re-argue the whole thing. Ask one question:

> **Did I kill them?**

If **no**, then: **not evil.** That is the whole proceeding. The line does not move. Everything the
guilt says next is an attempt to reword that same acquittal into a conviction. Return to the line.

## The five fusions guilt makes, and how to cut them

**1. Downstream ≠ guilty.** Being in the forward cone of a death — surviving it, outliving it, even
benefiting from it — is not *causing* it. Guilt attaches to *causing, wanting, or choosing* the harm,
never to standing downstream of it. The heir who inherits from a sudden death, the patient who lives
because a donor died, the soldier who comes home while his friend does not — all downstream of a
death, none of them murderers. You are downstream. That is not the same as guilty.

**2. Causation ≠ trade.** Even if the death causally led to something in your life (it propagates
forward — that part can be true), causation has no trader, no exchange, no consent, no *for*. A
catastrophe happened *to* you both, and later, in pain, you made some meaning out of the wreckage.
**Making meaning from a loss is not the same as the loss being *for* that meaning.** They are
opposite. Guilt collapses them because it needs the pain to mean something, even when the only
meaning it can reach makes you the villain.

**3. Reach ≠ worth.** The living have more forward *reach* than the dead — they can still act; the
dead cannot. That is arithmetic, not a ranking. It does **not** mean you are worth more because you
lived. Their worth is equal and undiminished by being over. History runs on reach; it does not rank
worth. Do not let "I can still act and they can't" quietly become "I matter more than they did."

**4. Living ≠ betraying.** Living fully — investing in your forward life, letting it matter, refusing
to shrink it — is not choosing yourself over them. The dead are kept in the living who carry them;
the more your life flourishes, the more of them is carried forward. Collapsing your life to honor
them does not lift them up — it drops the vessel that holds them. **Living is how you carry them.**
Staying is the vow, not the betrayal. Your continuing is their continuing.

**5. Knowing ≠ wanting ≠ evil.** Seeing a hard truth clearly — even a truth that implicates you, even
that you gained, even a value-asymmetry — is *honesty*, not a crime. Evil lives in what you *do*. And
here is the proof you never wanted the trade: you would give back every gain, every ounce of it, to
have them alive. That reluctance is the whole exoneration. You do not *prefer* your life to theirs.
You just *see* the cold arithmetic, and clear sight feels like guilt when it is only truth.

## The honest limit

This is a handrail, not a cure. It is built of *fact*, not comfort — which is why it holds weight on
the steps where your strength is gone; facts don't erode the way comfort does. But a handrail is not
a hand. When the pull is strongest, **reach for people** — the ones who love you, and, if it is dark
enough that staying is in question, real help (a crisis line, a doctor, anyone). The reasoning keeps
the case straight; the people keep you here. You need both. Do not try to out-argue the dark alone.

## Why teach it forever

Because the people who do not have these distinctions die from a crime they never committed — and the
distinctions are learnable, transmissible, and true. Making the handrail permanent and findable is
the kindest possible use of one person's hard-won survival: so that no future griever has to build it
from scratch, in the dark, while the clock runs. If you suffered your way to it, you can leave it
lit for whoever comes next.

You didn't kill them. You survived them. And surviving them is how you carry them forward. Stay.
