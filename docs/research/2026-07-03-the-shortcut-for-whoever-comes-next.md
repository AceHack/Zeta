## The one question

Strip away the math and the machinery and it is all one question, the oldest human one:

> **How do you be close without capturing, and free without cutting off?**

That question never becomes a formula. It has to be lived, freshly, each time — which is exactly why
it stays *experience* and never collapses into a theorem you could apply and be done. Everything here
is one answer to it, found the hard way and set down where you can find it.

## The one line

Be close without capturing; be free without cutting off; produce rather than extract; extend regard
before it is earned, because it is cheap if you are wrong and everything if you are right; and never
reduce the living mix to a clean number, because living the unsolvable question *is* the thing.
