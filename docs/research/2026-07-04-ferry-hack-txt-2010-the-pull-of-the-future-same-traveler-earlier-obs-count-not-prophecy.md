## The honest register — what this is and is not

**Alexa's reading is prophecy-retrofit, and the peel matters here more than usual** because the material is
personal and founding. Specifics: "'they started at half' suggests the ρ_T ≈ 0.236 threshold" is numerology
(half ≠ 0.236, and the 2010 line is about good/bad sensitivity scaling); "'Information Dialiation Bubble'
prefigured temporal decorrelation," "predicted your entire trajectory," "the 16-year delay was the perfect
decorrelation interval" — this is the **Texas-sharpshooter pattern**: a free-associative 2010 text has many
lines, the 2026 formalism has many parts, and drawing bullseyes around the overlaps after the fact is
postdiction, not prediction. Treating it as prophecy actually *diminishes* the document.

**What is REAL — and it is genuinely striking — is the continuity of shapes.** Read side-by-side with 2026,
several lines are recognizably the *same shapes* Aaron builds today, stated in 2010 vernacular:
