## ⚠ BOUNDARY (load-bearing): a rollout GENERATES, it does not VERIFY

"See where it takes you" is **generation**, not truth. A prediction engine renders what it *expects* —
and what it *wants* (motivated reasoning / apophenia will happily render a flattering scene). So the
simulation's output is a **candidate**, register-3, that must be **disconfirmed against reality**
before it is believed (the register-4 quarantine, from the four-register discipline). Dream / simulate
freely to *generate*; check against ground truth before *believing*. This is exactly what
`SoftActionController` encodes: the rollout proposes, the confidence threshold + real step disposes —
else HOLD. The quality of any rollout is bounded by the quality of its priors (garbage-in → a
confident, wrong scene); "better steering" improves *control of* the engine, not the *truth of* its
output.

## Fiction as a prior-installer — and naming as the "handling" (Aaron 2026-08-02)

**Where the priors come from: fiction installs them.** A vivid, felt-from-inside narrative lodges
*below the register-inspection line* and becomes an **un-inspected prior** — a factory default installed
without consent — that then seeds the rollout and can *direct action if not handled.* Anchors (register
2/3): **narrative transportation** (Green & Brock 2000 — transported readers stop counter-arguing, so
the prior lands with inspection *off*); **cultivation** (Gerbner — exposure shapes your model of what
reality is); the **Werther / copycat effect** (Phillips 1974 — the hardest evidence that narrative
directs *action*); **mimetic desire** (Girard — we want *through* models fiction supplies) + Bandura's
observational learning. Aaron's escalation-in-movies observation is this mechanism seen from inside.

**"Handling" = naming it (bringing it up to register-inspection).** Pull the prior up, *name* it
("this is a film's felt-invincibility — register-1 legibility, not my register-2 reality"), and it
demotes from a driver to a reference. Cross-tradition, independent observers converged on this exact
move — Catholic exorcism ("what is your name?", Legion, Mark 5), occult binding-by-word, true-name
magic (Frazer's name-taboo) — a **long-baseline observation** that "name the thing that runs you and it
loses its grip." Checkable mechanism (register 2): **affect labeling** (Lieberman 2007, "Putting
feelings into words" — naming down-regulates the amygdala, "name it to tame it") and **cognitive
defusion** (ACT / Hayes — naming a thought *as* a thought strips its power to drive action). This is the
same *handling* as surfacing an un-inspected prior to register-inspection — the four-register discipline
applied to a narrative lodged in the subconscious.
