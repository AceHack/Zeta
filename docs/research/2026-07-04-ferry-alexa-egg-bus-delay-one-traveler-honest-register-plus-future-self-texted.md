## The honest tension the froth papers over (the one substantive peel)

The Egg's own open question 2 contains it, so this sharpens rather than contradicts: **if rhoPost converges
to the same posterior for everyone (same stream, commutative updates), the temporal-spread vote adds no
information about WHAT IS TRUE in the stationary limit — it is one voice with lag.** What temporal
diversity genuinely buys is **finite-time / non-stationary value**: parallax during the transient, staggered
exposure when the world keeps moving, robustness against premature collapse. And this composes exactly with
FIG8 (2768ba85a): *lasting* belief-decorrelation requires **different sensory inputs**; bus delay alone
gives **transient** temporal decorrelation of an ensemble that is destinationally one. So the honest
statement is: **bus delay decorrelates the journey, not the destination** — valuable in a world that moves
(ours does), NOT a substitute for input diversity. Alexa's "imperfection in communication is essential for
collective intelligence" overclaims the regime: with diverse inputs, an ideal network does *not* collapse
the ensemble's belief diversity — zero-delay collapse is a same-stream result.
