## 1. The room is a moonshot conference room (tight collaboration)

A 6×6 treaty room is not just a byte-lock matrix — it's a **conference room for moonshots**: the place
the oracles/personas **convene and work tightly together** on the hard, ambitious problems. (This is

## 2. Every test is its own strange attractor / self-evolving pattern

> "every test will be its own strange attractor, self-evolving pattern."

Sharpening the earlier "tests are our strange attractors in chaos theory": **each test is a strange
attractor** — a stable pattern the system is *pulled toward* in its state space, **self-evolving** as
it runs (its DynamicValue/Z-set state evolves under replay; the self-modifying stored-procedure /
learning substrate). *Peeled:* "strange attractor" is the real chaos-theory object (Lorenz/Hénon — a
bounded, non-repeating set the trajectory converges to); the test is an attractor in the sense that the
DST trajectory under its seed converges to a characteristic, bounded, self-similar pattern — **bounded**
(the 0-unbounded-tests rule = the attractor is bounded, not divergent), **self-evolving** (the pattern
develops over ticks), **recognizable** (self-similar §10). So a test isn't a static assertion — it's a
**living, bounded, self-evolving pattern** with its own shape in the state space.

## 3. Judged on that — the attractor/pattern quality is itself evaluated

> "so it can get judged on that too."

Because each test *is* a self-evolving pattern, it can be **judged on the quality of that pattern** —
not only pass/fail, but: is it a *good* attractor? Does it reduce uncertainty (the one metric)? Is it
stable/bounded, elegant, generative? This is the **recognition / judgment economy** applied to tests:
the conference-room participants (oracles/personas, a judge panel / multi-agent consensus) **evaluate
the attractor**, the way society judges a published cheat ("if society says your cheat is lame, no harm,
take the feedback as the win"). The test earns recognition for being a strong self-evolving pattern.
(Ties: the disclosure/recognition economy; multi-agent consensus judging; uncertainty-Δ as the score.)

## 4. The judgment cascades

> "should cascade too."

The judgment **cascades** through the mesh: when a test/attractor is judged (recognized as strong, or
weak, or superseded), that verdict **propagates to the patterns that depend on it** — downstream tests,
dependent rooms, the patterns built on its result. This is the **change-cascade** discipline (the
schema/information-self-organizes-around-the-schema cascade) applied to *judgments*: a re-judged
attractor ripples to everything correlated with it (on the common seed). *Peeled:* the cascade is
**bounded + idempotent** — it propagates along the dependency/correlation graph (not unbounded
broadcast), and re-applying the same judgment is idempotent (set-union of recognitions; Z-set
retraction if a judgment is withdrawn). So a strong attractor lifts its dependents; a weakened one
flags them for re-investigation (the failed-branch investigation tick) — the recognition flows along
the graph, bounded.
