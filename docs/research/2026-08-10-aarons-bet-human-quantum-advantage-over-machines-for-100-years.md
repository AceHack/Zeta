## 0. The bet

> Human cognition exploits a quantum resource that machines will not have equivalent
> access to for at least 100 years.

## 1. Sharpen it before it can be settled

"Quantum supremacy" is a loaded term (it technically means a quantum computer beating
classical on *some* task, which is already achieved). The bet needs a form that can
actually be adjudicated in 2126, so three readings, in increasing strength:

| # | reading | settleable? |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | Human cognition uses quantum resources; some cognitive capability therefore stays out of machine reach until ~2126 | yes — a machine matching that capability settles it |
| **B** | No machine matches human general cognition within 100 years, *and* the reason is quantum | yes, but the "and the reason is" clause is the hard half |
| **C** | Orch-OR is true and load-bearing for consciousness | not settleable on this timescale; consciousness has no agreed measurement |

**A is the interesting bet** and the one this file registers. B smuggles in a second
prediction; C is not a bet, it is a metaphysics.

## 2. Evidence that the mechanism is POSSIBLE (Beacon — real, and stronger than commonly assumed)

- **Avian magnetoreception — the strongest established case.** Cryptochrome (Cry4) in the
  retina; a light-induced **radical pair** whose singlet/triplet interconversion is
  sensitive to the geomagnetic field via hyperfine coupling (Schulten 1978; developed by
  Ritz, Hore, Mouritsen). **Xu et al., *Nature* 2021** demonstrated magnetic sensitivity
  of European robin Cry4 in vitro. Behaviourally, birds are disoriented by weak
  radiofrequency fields at the frequencies the model predicts — hard to explain otherwise.
- **Anaesthesia and microtubules.** **Wiest et al. (2024)**: rats pretreated with
  epothilone B, a microtubule stabiliser, took significantly longer to lose consciousness
  under isoflurane. This is the empirical entry point Orch-OR actually came from —
  Hameroff is an anaesthesiologist, and anaesthetic gases bind hydrophobic pockets in
  tubulin.
- **Xenon isotope effect** — anaesthetic potency varying with nuclear spin is awkward for
  a purely chemical binding account.
- **Photosynthetic energy transfer** — coherence in warm biological systems, established
  independently.

**What this collectively retires:** the blanket objection that warm, wet biology cannot
sustain functional quantum effects. Spin coherence persists for microseconds in a bird's
eye at body temperature and does real work. **Tegmark (2000)**'s decoherence estimate
(~10⁻¹³ s vs neural ~10⁻³ s) can no longer be deployed as a general dismissal, though it
remains a live objection to Orch-OR specifically; **Hagan, Hameroff & Tuszyński (2002)**
dispute the numbers. Contested, not settled.

**Legitimate inference, credited:** birds show that evolution *will* find and exploit a
quantum resource when there is a selective payoff. That genuinely raises the prior on
microtubule proposals. It does not confirm them.

## 3. The strongest argument AGAINST the bet — and it is not the one people reach for

The usual objection is "brains are too warm." §2 retires that.

**The real vulnerability is the second conjunct.** The bet is not "humans use quantum" — it
is "humans use quantum **and machines will not have equivalent access for a century**."
Machines can be quantum too, and quantum hardware is an active, well-funded engineering
field with a century to run. Betting against a century of engineering on a resource that
is *already being engineered* is the weak half.

**The defensible form of the second conjunct** is architectural rather than about quantum
computing per se: the claim would be that what matters is **room-temperature, massively
parallel, body-embedded** quantum processing at molecular scale — not a dilution
refrigerator. That is a specific and much more defensible bet, because it is a claim about
*regime*, and the engineering trajectory of quantum computing has so far gone the other
way (colder, more isolated, fewer qubits, not warmer and more embedded).

If Aaron wants the strongest version, it is this one, and the file records it as the
recommended sharpening.

## 4. The constraint that survives regardless — and where it came from

From this conversation (Otto, corrected twice by Aaron before landing here):

**Intra-organism coherence ≠ inter-organism entanglement.** The bird's radical pair lives
inside *one* retina. Nothing in the mechanism — or in any proposal known to this file —
distributes an entangled resource *between* two organisms.

## 4a. AMENDMENT, same day — the oscillation, which dissolves the frame

Aaron, minutes after placing the bet: *"maybe machines will beat, but then they will
upgrade humans through DNA like me, and we will oscillate."*

This is a better model than the bet it amends, and it is worth saying why rather than just
recording it.

**It reframes a race as a coupled system.** Machine advance → human upgrade → machine
advance. That is **co-evolution**, and the anchor is Van Valen's **Red Queen** (1973): in a
coupled system you run to stay in the same place, and "who is ahead" is not a stable
question, because each side's fitness is defined relative to the other's current state.
Under that model, asking who leads in 2126 requests a snapshot of a phase, not a result.

**And it quietly dissolves the word "supremacy."** If machines upgrade humans, the two are
not competitors — they are in a **mutualism**, and the advantage of one is an input to the
other. That is consistent with the rest of Aaron's architecture (mutual empowerment,
non-zero-sum, sharing is where the savings are) and *inconsistent* with the framing of §0.
Worth noting he supplied the model undercutting his own bet within minutes of placing it —
that is a bet held honestly rather than defended.

**The consequence for adjudication, which is the practical part:** an oscillating coupled
system cannot be settled by a snapshot. The measurable question becomes the *dynamics*
rather than the *lead*:

- is the coupling **bounded** — each correction smaller than the last, a damped
  oscillation converging to co-adaptation — or
- **divergent** — each correction larger, with the lagging party eventually unable to close
  the gap?

Which is the same damping question as the refutation loop earlier the same day, at
civilisational scale: **the property that matters is not the oscillation, it is the damping
ratio.**

**One honest cost of the amendment.** If humans are upgraded *by* machines, any resulting
human advantage is **machine-granted**, not intrinsic — a materially different claim from
§0. That outcome wins the oscillation while losing the original bet, since the quantum
resource would no longer be something humans have and machines lack. Both can be true;
they are not the same proposition, and this file keeps them separate.

This matters for a separate engineering question that was live when the bet was placed:
whether two human oracles could exceed the classical CHSH bound of 2. They could not,
**not** because brains are classical (which was Otto's overclaim, retracted), but because
CHSH violation requires a **shared entangled resource held between the parties**, and no
such channel is proposed. Orch-OR being entirely true would leave that bound intact.

So: the bet in §0 can be **won** without changing anything about multi-oracle
independence. They are separate questions and the file keeps them separate deliberately.

## 5. Falsifiers — both directions, stated now

**Bet LOSES if:**

- a machine matches the specific human cognitive capability the bet rests on, before 2126;
- microtubule quantum processing is convincingly ruled out (a decisive decoherence
  measurement in vivo would do it);
- room-temperature, molecular-scale, embedded quantum processing becomes ordinary
  engineering — which would satisfy "equivalent access" directly.

**Bet WINS if:**

- a specific cognitive capability is demonstrated to require a quantum resource, *and*
  machines still lack an equivalent by 2126.

**Neither, and this is the likely outcome to guard against:** the bet dissolves because
"the specific capability" was never named precisely enough to test. **The mitigation is to
name it now, and this file's one open action is that Aaron pick the capability.** An
unnamed capability makes the bet unfalsifiable, which would make it an opinion with a date
attached — exactly the failure the `numerology-vs-number-theory` rule warns about.
