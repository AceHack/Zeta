## A. Additive entropy floor, stated as an assumption

Let `X_i` be the private innovation attributed to identity `i` during one
fixed admission epoch, `Z` the adversary's information before those innovations,
and `h_i >= 0` an asserted lower bound in bits. Assume, for every positive-
probability conditioning value and every `x_i`,

```text
P(X_i = x_i | Z = z, X_1 = x_1, ..., X_(i-1) = x_(i-1)) <= 2^(-h_i).
```

The chain rule gives `P(X_1=x_1,...,X_n=x_n | Z=z) <= 2^(-sum_i h_i)`.
Thus the joint worst-case conditional min-entropy floor is additive:
`H_inf(X_1,...,X_n | Z) >= sum_i h_i` under precisely this pointwise condition.
The calculation does not establish that a device has the assumed source,
that it cannot lease sources, or that a source belongs to one person.
Public disclosure can reduce secrecy; the bound is for the declared pre-
disclosure `Z`, not automatically for the final public transcript.

For mutually independent fair innovations the premise holds with one bit per
identity. For copies it fails after the first identity. Pairwise independence
is insufficient: `X_1=U, X_2=V, X_3=U XOR V` for independent fair bits gives
three pairwise-independent streams but only two joint bits. Deterministic
expansion or relabeling of one seed supplies no extra information-theoretic
entropy. A computational indistinguishability claim would need a separate
security parameter and adversary model.

Physical-source non-fungibility is a separate resource assumption. The
experiment must never infer a count of independent clocks from a count of
uncollapsed statistical components.

## B. Pairwise multiplier with separate units

Let `V` be the declared claimed-identity set and `E_req` the set of required,
unordered bilateral obligations in an admission epoch. Self-pairs and
unknown endpoints are invalid. Duplicate declarations count once. An edge
means an obligation, not evidence that an interaction occurred. Report
`E_observed` and missing obligations separately; a missing edge is unknown
coverage, not agreement or misconduct.

Let `b_i > 0` be the stipulated acquisition/maintenance work per admitted
source and `r_e >= 0` the stipulated incremental work to maintain and check
an obligation, measured in the SAME declared work unit. Define:

```text
B = sum_i b_i
R = sum_(e in E_req) r_e
M = 1 + R/B
C_model = B * M = B + R
```

`B` is the additive resource floor and `M` is the pairwise multiplier over
it. Entropy bits `h_i`, work `b_i`, verification operations, bytes, and wall
clock are separate columns. Conversion from entropy to work needs an explicit
calibration, which this experiment does not provide. The empty fleet has
`B=R=C_model=0` and multiplier undefined, rather than dividing by zero.

For homogeneous `b_i=b` and `r_e=q`, `M=1+q*|E_req|/(n*b)`. A complete graph
has `n(n-1)/2` obligations, so `C_model` is quadratic for positive constant
`q`; a bounded-degree graph has linear cost. Multiplying `n*b` by all `n^2`
pairs again would double-count the baseline and incorrectly produce cubic
scaling. Neither algebraic factorization nor pair count proves a lower bound
on an adaptive attacker's cost. One controller can generate a coherent fake
complete graph; shared generation, batching, fabricated counterparties,
borrowed entropy, colluding witnesses, and missing honest boundary edges can
invalidate the stipulated incremental costs.

Do not multiply edge acceptance probabilities as if they were independent.
The valid factorization is a chain of conditional probabilities given all
previous accepted constraints. Triangles can share evidence and constraints.

## C. Named invariant on a declared disclosed causal cut

Each immutable receipt has a canonical event ID, ordered actor/counterparty
IDs, interaction ID, observation channel, claim key, signed integer change,
parent event IDs, and a capability-supplied attestation. A local occurrence
adds observer ID, local causal position, and local clock coordinate. Distinct
agents may report different experiences of the same interaction: their actor
and channel remain part of the claim key. The invariant does not force their
beliefs or perceptions to agree.

The caller fixes a finite cut `D` of required event IDs. It is a *declared*
causal cut: all parents of its receipts must also be in `D`. Agreement on who
selected `D`, honesty of disclosure, and admission policy are outside the
kernel. Every report names `D`; agreement on an intersection cannot silently
be promoted to agreement on an undisclosed whole history.

Name the invariant **authenticated causal receipt structure on D**:

```text
I_D = (canonical typed receipt contents on D,
       transitive happens-before relation on D,
       consolidated signed claim map on D).
```

Canonical contents include event IDs, ordered identities, interaction,
channel, claim, signed weight and parent IDs, but exclude attestation encoding,
arrival order, local positions and clock readings. Authentication is checked
against the immutable contents. Different signature encodings do not change
meaning; a changed signer or payload does. A negative weight changes the
consolidated view while keeping the earlier receipt in the causal structure.
Cancellation in the map is not deletion of historical evidence. Integer
weights live in a free abelian group; their minus sign is not a metric signature.

Allowed transports change local coordinates and linearize concurrent events
in a different order while preserving canonical content and causal order.
Positions are unique and parents precede children when both are disclosed;
clock coordinates need no common origin, scale or physical interpretation.
The kernel checks transport validity rather than assuming every relabeling is
valid. It refuses coordinate collisions and causal reversal. It detects cycles
and conflicting authenticated records. Replaying an identical receipt is
idempotent and reported; it cannot create a second interaction or increase
coverage. Missing or unverified records produce explicit unknown coverage.
Authentication rejection is reported separately from an authenticated fork.
A closed, authenticated, non-conflicting pair of views may report only
`consistent on the declared cut`; never `distinct identities` or `same mind`.

### Explicit categorical statement

Fix `D`. Let `G_D` be the groupoid of valid charted copies of one authenticated
receipt structure, with morphisms content-preserving bijections of occurrence
coordinates that preserve causal order. Identity is unchanged coordinates;
composition is ordinary bijection composition; inverse restores coordinates.
The functor `F_D : G_D -> FinPos` forgets chart coordinates, maps an object to
its event poset, and maps a transport to the identity map on canonical event
IDs. It preserves identity and composition by construction.

Separately, claim-key relabelings extend linearly to the free abelian group
`Z^(K)` of finite-support signed claim maps. This extension preserves identity,
composition, addition and negation. Consolidation commutes with relabeling.
The implemented transport fixes canonical claim keys, so its group map is the
identity. These are restricted constructions, not an equivalence of all
memories or a universal compression result.

The normalization theorem follows from its assumptions; it earns no physical
symmetry. The falsifiable implementation obligations are accepting valid
charts, refusing invalid ones, and not hiding conflicting or missing evidence.
There is no metric, interval, boost, invariant propagation speed, dagger,
tensor product or compact structure here. No functor to CQM or Clifford is
claimed. Any later connection must name that additional structure, define its
map, and retain a counterexample that would break the claimed law.

## D. Frozen executable panel and independent replay

Use exact integer/rational arithmetic for structures, entropy enumeration and
workload accounting. Float comparisons occur only for existing statistical
baselines. The kernel is bounded to 128 required events, 256 local occurrences
per view, 128 parents per receipt, 128-character nonempty IDs, and 64 identities
for scaling. Reject malformed or over-budget input as a value. Authentication
is a total caller capability returning accepted, rejected or unavailable.
The fixture verifier recognizes an exact signer/content/token table; it is
not a cryptographic implementation and carries no unforgeability claim.

Freeze the following panels before measurement:

1. A four-event causal diamond, with one assert/retract pair and a second
   actor's distinct report. Enumerate all 24 arrival permutations and all valid
   local topological orders; apply coordinate origins `-1000, 0, 1000` and
   scales `1, 7`. Verify exact `I_D`, inverse and composition laws. Keep a
   causal reversal and a coordinate collision as refused controls.
2. Mutation cases: identical replay, changed payload with old attestation,
   substituted key, two authenticated contents under one event ID, causal
   cycle, missing parent, omitted expected event, unavailable verifier,
   conflicting disclosed common event despite missing other events, same
   interaction with different actor/channel observations, and fabricated
   counterparties whose table-valid receipts are internally consistent.
3. Shared-entropy controls: enumerate all states of three independent fair
   bits, exact copies, inversion, and `(U,V,U XOR V)`. Report joint maximum
   probability, joint min-entropy and pairwise dependence. Check the pointwise
   chain premise exactly; do not claim physical-source measurement.
4. Scaling for `n in {0,1,2,3,4,8,16,32,64}` on empty, complete, path, star
   and cycle graphs (cycle defined only for `n>=3`). Freeze `b=32` and `q=8`
   stipulated work units. Report vertex count, required/observed edges, missing
   edges, `B`, `R`, exact rational `M` and `C_model`; test relabeling and
   duplicate-edge idempotence. Withhold one required edge where one exists.
5. Baselines: signature-only consistency, `AntiSybil.correlation` at `0.8`,
   and `CoordinationSpectrum` raw pair lines on explicit balanced CHSH rounds.
   Include exact replay, inverted replay, zero-correlation deterministic
   shared-seed coding, and identical complete transcripts produced by either
   distinct controllers or one controller. Retain false positives and false
   negatives under each synthetic label. Statistical and receipt inputs are
   different channels; inability to see an attack is a recorded limitation.

The native F# implementation emits a JSON receipt. An independent standard-
library Python implementation regenerates the invariant, exact entropy and
scaling panel from the frozen fixture specification and compares the complete
semantic receipt. It must not import the F# answers as its expected values.
Tests also mutate saved results so a permissive comparison is rejected.
Record elapsed CPU/time and allocated bytes only if measured successfully;
these are local engineering measurements, not the stipulated `B` and `R`.

## Promotion and stopping rule

One complete deterministic panel, all outcomes retained, ends this registered
experiment. Success requires exact cross-language agreement, all listed
structural falsifiers, explicit unknown coverage, and the shared-controller
indistinguishability witness. A failure is a useful result and must be repaired
with a witness or reported unchanged; no tuning until the panel looks good.

Passing earns a research receipt-normalization kernel and an explicit workload
model. Runtime identity admission, monetary pricing, BFT membership, causal
world-model transport, Lorentz symmetry, and Sybil soundness each require a
separate proposal with observable assumptions and an adversary model.
