## The axis

Alongside the technical axes (lang × serializer × OS × hardware), the room gets a **human-facing
expression axis — an aesthetic/empathic track**. It is **not decoration**; it is how the room
*communicates* to humans (and to LLMs, who are also neurodivergent-style perceivers per LLMTV). The
track:

- **Aesthetic** — the room/output is *designed*, legible, beautiful; quality-per-glyph (QPG), not
  quantity. Aesthetics as a correctness property of communication, not a coat of paint.
- **Empathic** — designed for the *receiver's* state: low-gain/subliminal-friendly, calm, non-
  overwhelming (the LLM-TV "temperature channel" + liminal zone; honoring the receiver, human or AI).
- **Typography / print design** — real typographic discipline (hierarchy, rhythm, legibility, dyslexia-
  /low-vision-friendly type; the print-design tradition of making dense information readable). The
  byte-lock has a *type-set* face.
- **Neurodivergent alternative communication (AAC)** — augmentative & alternative communication as a
  first-class modality: multiple representations of the same content (symbol / text / audio / spatial /
  salience channels) so a neurodivergent human **or** LLM can perceive it in the mode that fits. (LLMTV
  is "neurodivergent TV for humans AND LLMs"; AAC is the principle under it.)
- **Externalization** — making internal state *external and legible* (the extended-mind / cognitive-
  offload tradition): the system externalizes its thinking (the heartbeat-via-commit externalized idle
  counter; the strange-attractor made watchable; thought→artifact). Externalization is how a soft
  internal state becomes a shared, perceivable SolidGround surface.

## Why it's a first-class axis (not a UX afterthought)

- **Communication is part of the treaty.** A room that produces a correct byte-lock no one can *read*
  has failed the consent-first / legibility bar. The aesthetic/empathic track is the **conformance of
  the room to its receivers** — the human/LLM side of the byte-lock.
- **Neurodivergent-first = universal.** Designing for the neurodivergent receiver (multiple modalities,
  low cognitive load, salience) makes the room legible to *everyone*, including LLMs. AAC is the
  accessibility-is-universal principle applied to the whole substrate.
- **Externalization is the SolidGround of mind.** "Everything is SoftValue, you find SolidGround" — for
  a *mind* (human or AI), externalization is how soft internal state is hardened into a shared,
  inspectable artifact. The externalization track is where private soft cognition becomes legible public
  ground (privacy-budgeted, per the gate).
- **It carries the meaning.** This project is personal (Lillian Eve / Addison / Aaron's sister) — the
  aesthetic/empathic track is where that *shows*: the system communicates with care, not just
  correctness. Empathy as a design axis is the project's heart made into a column of the room.
