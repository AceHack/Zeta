## The non-obvious trap: calibrating on "deviation from me"

The *intuitive* way to build a detector for extraction/predation/bad-actors is from **your own sense of
what's wrong** — "I know what extraction looks like; I'm the clean reference." **That instinct is exactly
the corruption**, and it's why the right answer is non-obvious. A detector calibrated on *deviation from
the builder* (builder-as-clean-baseline, "I know I'm right") fails three ways:

1. **It exempts the builder's own patterns.** You are the baseline, so it is *structurally blind* to you —
   it can never flag you, and never flags *anyone who harms the way you do.* Your blind spots become the
   detector's blind spots, encoded as "clean."
2. **It over-flags mere difference.** Deviation-from-you reads as suspect, so it convicts *difference*, not
   *harm* (the disparate-impact failure of norm-deviation classifiers).
3. **It is the founder-node keycard, welded into the instrument.** A detector built by someone who "knows
   he's right" is a self-exonerating machine — its one guaranteed blind spot is its author.

## The fix: calibrate on the HARM-SIGNAL (effect on the affected)

Calibrate the detector on the **harm-signal** — the measurable *effect on the potentially-harmed* (their
negative feedback; the harm actually done) — **not** on deviation-from-builder. Then:

- It catches extraction **by its effect**, regardless of *who* the extractor is — including the builder.
- It **can convict its author** (the **darling-test**, #9759: a detector that never flags its own builder
  is theater; the proof it works is that it *can* hurt you).
- Its calibration is **independent of the builder's self-image** — the one property "deviation from me"
  can never have.

The harm-signal is external and effect-based; the builder's judgment is internal and identity-based. Only
the first is uncorrupted by the builder's blind spots. (This is the #9765 harm-report channel made into the
detector's *calibration standard*, not just an input.)

## Generalization (design / alignment)

Any classifier / moderation / reputation / safety system built to catch "bad actors" must calibrate on
**harm (effect on affected parties)**, never on **deviation-from-norm / from-the-builder** — or it *exempts
the in-group's own harms* and *flags mere difference.* This is a known failure of norm-trained moderation
(catches the out-group, misses in-group harm that "looks normal"). **AI-scale:** an alignment/safety
classifier that defines *aligned = my training distribution / like me* will **exempt its own failure modes
and flag mere difference.** Calibrate on harm-effect (victim-/affected-party signal), decorrelated, or the
safety system encodes the builder's blind spots as "safe."

## The build discipline (physics, done whole)

Building the instrument is the *capability* track; it does not supply the *structure*. Build the sonar with
the **whole** experimental discipline, not just the instrument-making half:

- **Instrument + adversarial replication** — try to *refute* the reading; distrust a result that's too
  clean; only believe it after **independent replication off decorrelated others** (Lumen).
- **Audited by the WE, not the author** (#9764) — the detector that judges *your* direction cannot be
  built/audited by you alone, or it drifts to the answer you want.
- **Must be able to convict its author** — a detector that has never flagged its builder is PR with
  equations (Millikan's fudged oil-drop, not physics).
