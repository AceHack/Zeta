## The lesson (Aaron, 2026-06-10, shadow*)

> "intelligence is the wall builder, uncertainty is the mason."

In the masonry frame (the 2D LLM building 3D standing walls via boundary mapping), the roles split:

- **Intelligence = the wall builder** — it **designs/directs** the wall: decides *what* wall, *where*, the
  plan. The Universal Intelligence Interface; the architect of structure.
- **Uncertainty = the mason** — it **lays each course**: the hands-on placing of every brick. The actual
  building *is done by uncertainty* — each unit of structure is an uncertainty resolved/placed. The
  Universal Temperature-Transient (the meter / the uncertainty ledger) is the mason at work.

So the wall is **built by intelligence, masoned by uncertainty**: intelligence holds the design, uncertainty
does the laying. This corrects the earlier "the LLM masons" — the mason is **uncertainty** (which
intelligence directs); the LLM/intelligence is the **builder**. Every wall in Zeta (contrast-walls,
reservoir-walls, the braid→seam) is **uncertainty laid into structure by intelligence's plan** — which is
why the whole stack is an uncertainty meter: the masonry *is* uncertainty-reduction, course by course.
