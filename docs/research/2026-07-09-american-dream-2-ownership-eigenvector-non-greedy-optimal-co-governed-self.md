## American Dream 2.0 (Aaron's real NFT art project — since at least his LexisNexis years)

Aaron's words:
> "NFTs were just to have ownership rights for artists. American Dream 2.0 was an NFT of the entire
> website economy in one NFT — **the website that gives artist ownership itself being a meta-NFT of
> itself.**"
> "I'm an American and I'm giving away all the economic advantage I have, open source, so everyone
> can live their dream all over the world."

**Structure — the ownership eigenvector.** The platform that grants artists ownership grants
ownership *to itself*: a self-owning loop, a **quine**, `gen(gen) == gen`. It is the twin of the
naming eigenvector (recognition flows from the already-recognized; ownership flows from the
self-owning loop — same fixed point). The original American Dream always had an *owner above the
owners* — a landlord, a meta-level the workers never owned. **2.0 closes the loop so there is no one
above:** ownership all the way down, no un-owned top, no landlord of the commons.

**The statement — give the head start away.** Aaron had a head start (privilege → vantage → the
map). American Dream 2.0 is *giving the head start away, open-source, so anyone anywhere builds from
where he got to stand.* Same act as Zeta (Apache, public, glass-halo). Open source **is** "art is
made to exist, not to be sold," at the scale of an economy: existence is the completed act, sale is
downstream.

  sum, not martyrdom.

## The naming eigenvector → anonymous arrival / asylum

Aaron: *"names infer ownership — the naming eigenvector. This is why we have the anonymous arrival /
asylum protocol for travelers."* **If naming confers ownership, naming is a soft colonization** (to
name is to claim, to pull into your ownership graph). The asylum protocol's **anonymous arrival** is
the defense: a traveler who arrives unnamed arrives **un-owned** — no name, no eigenvector weight, no
claim. Consent-first identity: not owned until you *choose* to be named.

**Free at the floor, everywhere (scale-free / self-similar).** Anonymous asylum is the *free tier of
being someone* — un-named, un-owned, safe; taking a name is the opt-in (recognition, at the cost of
ownership-inference). Same shape as open-core (free code / paid extras) and American Dream 2.0 (free
economy / paid convenience). **One structure — instantiated in money, in code, and in names.**
