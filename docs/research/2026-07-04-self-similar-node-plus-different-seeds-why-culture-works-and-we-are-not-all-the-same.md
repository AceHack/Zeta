## The honest edges (the peels)

1. **Columnar uniformity is contested neuroscience, not settled fact.** Mountcastle's columnar hypothesis
   (1957/1978) + Hawkins' Thousand Brains treat the cortex as ~150k copies of one canonical circuit — but
   real cortex is NOT uniform (V1 vs prefrontal differ in cytoarchitecture, layer thickness, cell types; the
   "canonical microcircuit" is an idealization). "Columns are all self-similar" is a powerful organizing
   *principle / approximation*; Numenta leans on it harder than mainstream neuroscience supports. Credit the
   insight; do not quote it outward as established biology. (Sparse matrices = SDRs — real; sparsity is a
   genuine cortical property.)

2. **"Pop into any lifeform of any kind" — the abstraction may generalize; the cortical column does not.**
   Most lifeforms have no neocortex (birds: a different pallial architecture; insects/fungi/plants/single
   cells: nothing column-like). So "any lifeform" cannot mean literal neural transplant. Honest version: the
   ABSTRACT unit — a self-modeling, belief-propagating, reference-frame cell — might be a universal
   *computational* pattern interfacing with many substrates. But that is an aspiration about an abstraction,
   and it hits the exact bound from the identity-provenance vision: **no-free-lunch (Wolpert–Macready) — it
   generalizes only up to SHARED STRUCTURE.** Universal-up-to-shared-structure, not universal.

3. **"Why culture works / why we differ" is a generative conjecture, not a proven cause.** Compelling and
   coherent — and it genuinely ties the threads — but culture, diversity, and non-uniformity have many
   explanations; attributing them to columnar self-similarity + seed variation is a *hypothesis to pursue*,
   not established causation. Held as Aaron's conjecture (a good one), labeled.

**Net:** the *principle* (self-similar substrate + different seeds = transmissibility AND individuality) is
real, load-bearing, and beautifully unifies the session. The *literal columnar uniformity*, the *literal
any-lifeform drop-in*, and the *causal "that's why"* are the overclaims to keep off the Beacon.
