## The kernel: three moves

1. **Accumulate into the cognitive lightcone.** A *lightcone* (Minkowski / special relativity) is the set of
   events causally reachable from where you are. Aaron's "cognitive lightcone" = the set of ideas/knowledge
   causally reachable from where his understanding currently is. **You cannot construct what is outside it** —
   ZetaIds were unbuildable until enough prerequisite knowledge (distributed systems, information theory,
   content-addressing, category/algebra) had accumulated *inside* the cone. Construction has a *prerequisite
   information mass*.
2. **Bend around it.** Once enough information-mass accumulates, you *bend around* the concept — a perspective
   warp / synthesis. (Physically literate as metaphor: **mass bends lightcones** — gravitational lensing in
   general relativity. Enough accumulated information "mass" curves the cognitive cone around the target so you
   can see/reach what was previously outside it.) The breakthrough is not more facts; it's the *bending* —
   viewing the accumulated mass from the angle where the structure becomes visible.
3. **Write what that felt like down — and it felt like specification.** The construction step is
   **phenomenological capture**: write down the *felt sense* of the understanding. And crucially, *"it felt
   like specification"* — the felt sense of fully understanding the thing **was the act of specifying it.**
   Understanding-crystallized-to-felt-sense *is* the spec. Writing the feeling = writing the specification.
