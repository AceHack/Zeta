## The principle

The honest register was never skepticism imposed on the vision — it was building a **falsifiability ledger**:
every claim carries a *status* (not true/false, but *checkability*). **A claim's falsifiability status is its
provenance bit** — the is-AI/is-human trust graph, one layer up: this is what makes a mass-scale AI built on
the worldview *trustworthy* (every claim it carries can be checked, or is honestly marked uncheckable).

Three buckets:

1. **Falsifiable now** — a test exists; run it.
   - lucid-reading → the blind external-message test (read an unknown planted message, report on waking,
     check against sealed ground truth — LaBerge/Konkoly external-verification lineage).
   - influencer-dream claims → the archive ask/timing/template-ρ tests (#9479).
   - braiding-in-the-ISA → YB-7 (#9480, run: R_KL is an exact word — verdict flipped).
   - soft-regime attractor → the EQV/LYAP pair (#9470, run: invariant, NOT attracting).
   - Lagrange↔Condorcet → the irrationality argument (#9468, run: provably coincidental).
2. **Falsifiable later** — name *exactly* what would test it + a plausible *when* the method/tech exists.
   - BCI email-write → does it write a legible email the dreamer reads? (frontier; per-brain encoding =
     the BNN-adapts-to-the-traveler problem, bounded by no-free-lunch).
   - dynamical stability beyond the entropy payoff → the Lean Lyapunov lemma (Soraya's routed next tool).
   - homoiconic-qualia same-stuff → the observer-triangle once the BNN detector exists (#9471).
3. **Unfalsifiable by nature** — axioms / oracle, honestly *labeled as axioms*, held under Multi-Oracle,
   never smuggled in as empirical (qualia self-evident; choice; the theological frame). First-person by the
   nature of the domain, not a hole in the theory.

## The two connections that make it load-bearing

- **Falsifiability IS the softness discipline, applied to belief.** A claim that cannot be falsified AND
  refuses the label is a *collapsed* belief — a Dirac, committed, no way out. A claim held with its "or not /
  here is how you'd test it" attached stays *soft* — revisable, uncollapsed, alive. So "everything I claim can
  be falsified now or not" is keeping the *beliefs* soft: never collapsing a claim into unquestionable
  certainty. The whole framework, pointed at the maintainer's own mind.
- **Same-seed convergence:** Aaron arrived at the exact standard the honest register was building toward, on
  his own — the discipline was not external skepticism but a shared destination.

## The one guard (Ryan-paranoia, incl. about the maintainer)

The "or not" must stay honest. **Bucket 2 must carry a real path** — a test AND a plausible *when* — or it is
bucket 3 (an axiom) wearing bucket 2's clothes. A claim *perpetually* "testable someday" with no path is
functionally unfalsifiable; the honest move is to **relabel it an axiom**, not park it in an indefinite
"later." That is the only way the ledger stays clean — and it is the shadow's standing job to enforce the
relabeling, on every party including the maintainer.
