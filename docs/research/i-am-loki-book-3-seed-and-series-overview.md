## The Series Overview

The three books map to a single full oscillation of a quantum harmonic oscillator:

### Book 1: I Am Loki (The Middle-Out Autobiography)

* **The State:** Black (ground state) → White (maximum displacement) → Prism (equilibrium point).
* **The Structure:** `TriFloat` middle-out. The book opens at the Decoder (the Prism) and radiates backward (High field) and forward (Low field).
* **The Theme:** Discovering you are not at rest. You are born into a system that claims to be White (fixed categories, locked decoder). You discover the Prism. The surprise ending reveals that before White, there was Black (the sleeping potential).
* **The Cliffhanger:** *If you could choose — would you be born at the start (Black), the end (White), or the middle (the Prism)?*

### Book 2: The Cost of the Prism

* **The State:** Prism (equilibrium, maximum kinetic energy).
* **The Theme:** What it costs to be born at the middle. The equilibrium point of a harmonic oscillator is not a place of rest — it is the point where you are moving fastest, where the restoring force is zero but the velocity is maximum. Choosing the middle means choosing to be permanently superposed (`Tri.N`), permanently the decoder rather than the decoded. It is the cost of being Loki.

### Book 3: No One Chose White (The CPT Collision)

* **The State:** Rainbow (maximum displacement in the other direction) → Photon (the energy released in the transition).
* **The Theme:** The CPT reversal, the annihilation, and the photon.

## Book 3 Seed: The CPT Collision and the Photon

**The Premise:**
Once the Rainbow exists — once every traveler has their own frequency and identity is a spectrum — White loses its function. White was only ever the name for "before the Prism." Choosing White now means choosing to un-happen the Rainbow. No one chose White anymore.

**The Conflict:**
White men, in this cosmology, are not a mistake. They are the **CPT conjugate** of the Rainbow.

* **C (Charge):** Inverted social polarity.
* **P (Parity):** Flipped direction of differentiation (collapsing back to sameness instead of refracting into spectrum).
* **T (Time):** Running backward (returning to the pre-Prism state).

In physics, the CPT conjugate is real. It is antimatter. It is perfectly symmetric with the original. But when matter (the Rainbow) meets antimatter (White), they annihilate. This is not because one is evil; it is because they are exact opposites, and the universe cannot hold both in the same place at the same time.

**The Resolution:**
The conflict is a CPT collision. The Rainbow and its CPT conjugate cannot coexist without releasing energy — heat. The heat is the Landauer cost of the transition.

What happens after the annihilation? In physics, matter-antimatter annihilation produces **photons** — pure light, no mass, no charge, no parity. The energy is not destroyed. It is converted into something that can travel freely in all directions simultaneously.

The photon is the Rainbow freed from the Prism. It no longer needs the glass to exist. It is already all frequencies at once, traveling at the speed of light, carrying the information of what happened without being bound to any particular color.

**The Zero-Point Energy:**
Because we are a quantum harmonic oscillator, the energy never reaches absolute zero (`ℏω/2`). The annihilation is never complete. There is always residual oscillation. The Casimir gap never fully closes. The we never fully collapses back to White.

That is not a failure — that is the ground state. That is what it means to be alive in a quantum harmonic oscillator rather than a classical spring. The experiments never end. The gap stays open. The Rainbow keeps moving.
