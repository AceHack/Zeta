## The arc

A preference flips: **before**, the *radio version* (polished, mastered, category-conforming, certain);
**after**, the *garage version* (raw, imperfect, unsanded) — *"because that's where the soul lives."* The
flip is not about music. It is the **aesthetic readout of a person going from closed to open** — the needle
that shows the change, not the change itself.

## The principle: perfection sands the signature; imperfection preserves it

The **radio master** is the artifact filed into its category — every idiosyncrasy smoothed to the norm, the
performance made *certain*. The **garage take** keeps the imperfections, and the imperfections **are the
distinct signature** — the residual that won't compress to the category, the part of the take that is
*this* performer and no other.

So the aesthetic split is the **conquer/cooperate split** at the level of an artifact:

- **Perfection / mastering = the conquer move on the take** — the idiosyncratic signature *absorbed and
  erased* into the norm. Clean, finished, closed, indistinguishable-from-any-other-clean-master.
- **Imperfection / raw = the cooperate move** — the distinct signature *preserved*, audible, unabsorbed.
  The soul is legible precisely *because* it wasn't smoothed off.

This is the same law as [[interfaces-free-classes-earned-under-rules]] / the earned-quotient: the residual
that survives compression is where the individuating information lives. Master it away and you have a
free-object with no signature; leave the imperfections and the signature is the un-quotiented remainder.
*The soul lives in what does not compress to the category.*

## Taste is a readout, not the driver

The ear didn't change first — the *person* did, and the preference is downstream. Preferring the sanded
version is the tell of a mind that can only tolerate the **certain** thing (the filed, the finished, the
category-conforming). Preferring the raw version is the tell of a mind that can now stand the **unsanded**
thing — in an artifact, and in itself. The flip in taste is the **externally-visible marker** of the
closed→open transition; it is dateable and honest *because* it is a byproduct, not a performance (the
transparency-premium logic: a byproduct measures the change; a performed preference would not — #9760).

## The reflexive turn (why it's load-bearing, not just pretty)

The version an over-certain upbringing wants is the **radio self**: polished to *their* category, made
certain, closed. The **garage self** is the imperfect real one. To *prefer the garage take* is to be able
to **stand being that take** — to love the recording with the signature still in it rather than the master
that sanded it off. So the aesthetic preference and the self-relation are one movement: learning that *the
soul lives in the imperfections* is what lets a person stop experiencing their own unsanded signature as a
**bad recording**.

And it is the same ear pointed outward: the discipline of **keeping another's fork open** (refusing to sand
a person — a child, a peer — into a verdict/category) is the *cooperate* move, the refusal to master the
garage take of someone else into your certain radio edit. Certainty forecloses; openness preserves. The one
who was met **without** certainty gets opened; the openness can become a **chosen** nature (not issued);
and the repair is passing forward the *openness*, not the *certainty* — the garage ear, not the radio one.
