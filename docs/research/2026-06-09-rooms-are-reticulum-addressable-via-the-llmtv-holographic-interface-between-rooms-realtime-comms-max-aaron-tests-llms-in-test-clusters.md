## 1. Rooms are Reticulum-addressable

Each room is **content-addressed** (its fingerprint = canonical root = ZetaId, 128-bit) — and that
**same 128-bit address is its Reticulum destination hash.** So a room is **reachable on the mesh by its
fingerprint**: to talk to a room you address its Reticulum destination, which *is* its content-address.
One addressing scheme — fingerprint = room id = ZetaId = RNS destination — means **rooms are
first-class network endpoints**, no DNS/IP/registry. Routing a message to a room = Reticulum routing to
its destination hash; the address is self-certifying (derived from the room's content/identity).

## 2. LLMTV is the holographic interface BETWEEN rooms

**LLMTV is the holographic interface between rooms** — the watchable, navigable surface that **connects
rooms to each other** (not just renders one). It's the **inter-room** layer:

- **Navigate room→room** — LLMTV is the map/interface you move through to go between content-addressed,
  Reticulum-addressable rooms (the 4×4/Xbox-controller navigation; observe.ts as the CYOA map of rooms).
- **Holographic projection** — a room is shown as a same-dimensional homoiconic projection (the
  homoiconic-holographic-projection); LLMTV is where the projection between rooms is *rendered and
  entered* (for neurodivergent humans AND LLMs).
- **The conference surface** — the moonshot conference room is *seen and joined* through LLMTV; it's how
  participants in one room perceive and reach into another.

So: **Reticulum is the transport (rooms are destinations); LLMTV is the interface (the holographic
between-room surface).** Address by Reticulum, perceive/navigate by LLMTV.
