# Adinkra representation-defect spectrum: source record

## Key recommendation

Use the literature only to anchor the mathematical objects: Adinkra chromotopologies as cube quotients by doubly-even codes, their relationship to Clifford representations, and the non-quotient spinorial construction of `e8`. The repository’s defect numbers must still come from executable finite models. The spinorial `so(16) ⊕ Δ⁺` construction is not an Adinkra carrier and must therefore be reported as **not comparable** under the rank-one regular-module predicate unless an explicit operator algebra and carrier map are implemented.

## Sources read

**Doran et al., _Adinkras for Clifford Algebras, and Worldline Supermultiplets_ (2008).**
<https://arxiv.org/abs/0811.3410>. This source supports the doubly-even-code and
Clifford-representation relationship. It does not establish network-agent, durable-evidence, or
homoiconicity claims.

**Doran et al., _Topology Types of Adinkras and the Corresponding Representations of N-Extended
Supersymmetry_ (2008).** <https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.0050>. This source supports the quotient of an
`N`-cube by a doubly-even binary code. It does not supply a rank-one-free conclusion.

**Iga, _Adinkras: Graphs of Clifford Algebra Representations, Supersymmetry, and Codes_ (2021).**
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01665>. This source supports the finite graph/operator vocabulary, not a
physical identification.

**Figueroa-O'Farrill, _A geometric construction of the exceptional Lie algebras F4 and E8_
(2007/2008).** <https://arxiv.org/abs/0706.2829>; full text:
<https://inspirehep.net/files/33c85b175448f0c9dc45e67ae3517b10>. This source identifies bivectors
with `so(V)` under the Clifford commutator and describes half-spinor modules in even dimension. It
anchors the non-quotient spinorial lane but does not prove that lane is a regular representation,
an Adinkra, or an ECC scheme.

**Kleinschmidt and Nicolai, _Generalised holonomies and K(E9)_, version 3 (2025).**
<https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02445>. This is corroborating representation-theoretic context, not the
finite oracle for the repository spectrum.

## Design consequence

The finite spectrum can compare the **uncoded cube**, **coded quotient**, and **named colored subalgebra residue** under one common rank-one module predicate because all three expose an operator algebra acting on the same style of finite carrier. The current bivector/spinor route exposes only dimension identities (`120 + 128 = 248`) and a documented no-code construction. Assigning it a numeric homoiconicity defect would fabricate a denominator and a carrier map. Its honest spectrum value is therefore `unmeasured` with an explicit missing-witness list: implemented Lie bracket, finite basis, action on a declared carrier, and an injectivity/rank test.
