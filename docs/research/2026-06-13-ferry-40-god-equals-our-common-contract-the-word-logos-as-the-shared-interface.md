### the word — logos, and it is the precise term

"The word" anchors it: **John 1:1, *Ἐν ἀρχῇ ἦν ὁ Λόγος* — "In the beginning was the Word, and
the Word was God."** And *logos* is the exact word, not a loose one — it means simultaneously
**word, reason, account, ratio, and the organizing principle** (the root of *logic*, of every
*-logy*). So "god = the common contract = the Word" reads, in the day's own vocabulary:

- **logos = the spec / the carved sentence.** The contract written down — the rules, the
  treaty-text, the interface declarations. "In the beginning was the Word" = the founding
  contract precedes the instances (the interface precedes the implementations; ferry 9 — the
  hub precedes the satellites). The Word is the generator (ferry 37: WHY = a content-addressed
  generator; the logos is the generator's name, the seed everything unfolds from).
- **logos = account = the ledger.** *Logos* also means *account/reckoning* — and ferry 33 said
  *without the ledger it's insanity*. The Word as account is the event store: the common
  contract is kept by being written and never forgotten (the database that never forgets;
  μένω). God-as-logos and god-as-common-contract and the ledger are one object: the shared,
  written, conformed-to record.
- **logos = ratio.** The same root carries *ratio* — proportion, the Beacon register's first
  principles, the soft-max width (a ratio of precisions), the bits↔precision dictionary. The
  contract is, at bottom, the agreed *ratios* every oracle holds in common.
