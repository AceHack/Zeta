## The peel — the real science, and the sharpening

### The claim's true core: male coalitionary violence is evolved

"Males kill others to meet objectives" is a real, documented evolutionary pattern, not edgelord
over-generalization — **male coalitionary aggression / intergroup lethal violence** (Wrangham &
Peterson 1996, *Demonic Males: Apes and the Origins of Human Violence*; chimpanzee intergroup
killing; male-male competition for resources/status/mates). The first killing in the file's own
mythic register is exactly this: **Cain kills Abel** (ferry 23) — brother-brother, the male −1,
violence-to-meet-an-objective as the founding fault. The men carry the stain (the naming-legacy
file: the −1 came down the father-line); this is that stain at species scale.

### The brake — and "dick" is the Mirror term for the real mechanism: sexual selection / self-domestication

"It's dick slowed that down" = the reproductive drive braking the killing drive — and the
Beacon mechanism is **self-domestication** (Wrangham 2019, *The Goodness Paradox*): humans
selected *against* reactive aggression — the hot, kill-to-meet-objective kind — over evolutionary
time (selection against bullies; cooperation; **female mate choice**), reducing it markedly
while keeping the species' capacity for planned action. The sharpening that lands on tonight's
thread: **the brake is not "dick" — it is the women.** Sexual selection runs through *female
choice*; pair-bonding reduces male-male lethal conflict; mate choice that favors the less-murderous
male is the selective pressure that domesticated the species. So the same force named all night —
the women — is the brake on the male −1: not only the forgiveness-shape that lets the thread
continue past faults (ferry/lineage addendum 13), but the **domesticating selection** that slowed
the killing in the first place. "I learned by observing women not men" (lineage addendum 15) has
a species-scale twin: the species itself learned (was selected) toward the women's brake. The
women slowed the killing — by choosing.

### Why it's hopeful, not deterministic

The load-bearing, non-grim reading: self-domestication proves the violence is **modifiable** —
the species already braked its own worst trait substantially. It is not destiny; it is a −1 with
a documented, evolved +1 appended (the same retraction-not-erasure / grace-shape the whole night
runs on: the violence isn't erased from the species, it's *held and counter-selected*). The
killing slowed because the species, through the women's choosing, appended a brake.

## Bounds (prominent, because the crude framing invites bio-determinism — which is wrong)

- "Males naturally kill to meet objectives" is **partial and context-dependent**, NOT
  deterministic and NOT all males: coalitionary lethal violence is real and evolved, but
  expression is hugely context- and culture-dependent, and self-domestication shows the trait is
  *reducible* (the central finding). Do not read this as "men are killers" bio-determinism — the
  science says the opposite is also true and ongoing: the species braked itself.
- "Dick slowed it down" is **Mirror-crude** for the real mechanism (sexual selection / female
  choice / pair-bonding / self-domestication). The Beacon term is self-domestication; the brake's
  agent is female choice, not the drive per se.
- This is evolutionary biology (a real, contested, active field), used as the species-scale
  instance of the file's Cain/men-stain/women-grace thread — not a claim about any individual,
  and explicitly not bio-determinist licence. Rung-appropriate: the pattern is real; the strong
  universal reading is declined.
