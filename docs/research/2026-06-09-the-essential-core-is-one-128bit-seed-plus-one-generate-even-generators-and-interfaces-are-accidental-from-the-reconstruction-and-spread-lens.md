## The cut: essential vs accidental (Brooks; Rodney's Razor)

Pick the lens **reconstruction + spread-with-0-friction**. Under *that* lens:

- **ESSENTIAL (irreducible):** a **single 128-bit seed** + a **single simple
  `generate`** operation. That is the whole minimal description — the Kolmogorov
  core. From it, everything unfolds.
- **ACCIDENTAL (unfolded, derivable, not the essence):** the **generators**, the
  **interfaces**, the code, docs, governance, proofs, hardware. Each is real and
  useful as intermediate scaffolding, but **none is necessary to *hold*** — all of
  it regenerates from `generate(seed)`. *"Even the generators can be generated"*
  (the generator-of-generators bottoms out at the seed); *"even all the interfaces
  are not necessary complexity"* (they too are output of `generate`, not input).

So the tower we built this session — interfaces → generators → ace → code/docs/
proofs/hardware — is, from the reconstruction lens, **one expression**:
`generate(seed)`. Everything else is the unfolding, and **"there is a lot of
accidental in here"** — much of the apparatus is accidental complexity to be
razored against this essential core.

## Reconciling with "the interfaces are the valuable thing"

Not a contradiction — a **lens shift** (Mirror/Beacon-style):

- **Operational lens (day to day):** the interfaces are the valuable thing you own,
  protect, and trust against — because you live in the unfolded world and act
  through them.
- **Reconstruction + spread lens (essence):** the interfaces are *accidental* —
  derivable from `generate(seed)`. What you must truly preserve to reconstruct or
  spread is **just the seed + the generate**.

Both hold. The interfaces are the valuable *operational* surface; the seed+generate
is the valuable *essential* core. The razor says: protect the seed absolutely; treat
everything above it as regenerable, and cut accidental complexity ruthlessly against
"could `generate(seed)` reproduce this?"

## Why 0-friction demands this

SuperFluid (0-friction) spread needs the **smallest possible thing to move**: a
128-bit seed travels with zero friction; a whole apparatus does not. If the seed +
a simple generate reconstructs everything at the destination, then **spread = ship
128 bits**. Accidental complexity is friction; the essential core is frictionless.
This is why the close-over / regeneration story bottoms out here: the *point* of
making everything regenerable was to shrink the thing-that-must-travel to the seed.
