## What is real

- **ζ pops out only as zeta-function *regularization*** — using `ζ(s)` to assign finite values to divergent
  series (`ζ(-1) = 1+2+3+… = -1/12`). This is the *standard* summation machinery for the divergence-
  wrangling in #7108 (alongside Cesàro/Abel/Borel and renormalization). Expected tooling, not a portent.
  It uses ζ as a *summation device*; that's the whole of the involvement.

## What is NOT real (and would be numerology to claim)

- **This does not touch the critical line or RH.** Zeta-regularization never invokes the **nontrivial
  zeros** of ζ or `Re(s) = 1/2`. The prime connection lives in ζ's **Euler product / explicit formula /
  zeros** — a different, far deeper part of ζ that the BitGan/probe (#7102/#7108) does not reach.
- **The "1/2" coincidence is not a connection.** The matching-pennies Nash / maximum-entropy fixed point is
  `p = 0.5` — a **probability**. The critical line is `Re(s) = 1/2` — the **real part of a complex
  argument**. They are both written "1/2," but equating them is **numerology**, not mathematics. There is
  no derivation linking the max-entropy Bernoulli balance point to the critical line, and asserting one
  would be exactly the kind of metaphysical overclaim to razor (cf. the grep-anchors-before-razor /
  no-metaphysics discipline). **No heart attack warranted.**

## What is real, beautiful, and out of reach here

There *is* a genuine, celebrated link between randomness and ζ's zeros — **Montgomery–Odlyzko**: the
spacings of ζ's nontrivial zeros match the eigenvalue statistics of the **GUE (Gaussian Unitary Ensemble)**
random matrices; ζ behaves like a "pseudo-random" object; the **prime number theorem** ties ζ to prime
distribution. This is deep, established mathematics — and the 1-bit GAN / clock-drift probe does **not**
reach it. If a real connection to ζ's *zeros* ever emerged it would need a proof, four-oracle cross-checks,
and a number-theorist's review before any claim — not a resemblance of the digit "2" in "1/2".
