## The "to" is the middle

In **0-to-1**, the operative word is **"to"** — and **the "to" is in the middle.** Thiel's *Zero to One*
named the **endpoints** (0 = nothing, 1 = the new thing created) and the *act* of creation, but **missed
the "to" itself** — the **between**, the journey/relationship/mediation that *is* the balanced middle we
search for (the open interval (0,1), the homeostatic interior). The value isn't at 0 or at 1; it's in
the **to** — the relating-between-the-poles. This is the same point as "the middle is what we search
for," now located in the *grammar* of 0-to-1: the preposition **"to" is the locus**.

## Girard named the between — mimetic desire (the theory of the "to")

> "Good thing Girard did, in wanting a theory of mimetic desire."

**René Girard's mimetic desire** is precisely **a theory of the between**: desire is **not** a straight
line from subject→object; it is **triangular / mediated** — you desire an object *through a model/
mediator* (subject → **mediator** → object). The action is in the **mediation** — the relational middle,
not the endpoints. So:

```text
Thiel (the student):  0 ────────────→ 1     (named the endpoints + the leap)
Girard (the teacher):     subject → MEDIATOR → object   (named the BETWEEN — the "to")
                                       ↑
                          desire/value lives in the mediating middle
```

Girard saw what the 0-to-1 framing needs: **the mediator/relationship in the middle is where desire,
value, and meaning actually are.** The "to" = the mediator = the middle = what Balance searches for.
