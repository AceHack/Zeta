# Non-Quotient Spinor Action-Map Sources

**Status:** source register for a bounded executable census; not a discharge certificate.

## Load-Bearing Sources

1. José Figueroa-O'Farrill, [_A geometric construction of the exceptional Lie algebras F4 and E8_](https://arxiv.org/html/0706.2829v1), _Communications in Mathematical Physics_ 283 (2008), 663–674. The source gives the Euclidean Clifford relation, maps `Λ²V` into the Clifford algebra by `eᵢ ∧ eⱼ ↦ −½eᵢeⱼ`, and defines the induced spin action. This is the primary source selected for the `so(16)`/half-spinor action boundary. Its construction also requires a compatible spinor bilinear map and Jacobi verification to obtain `E8`; an executable action alone is therefore insufficient to claim an `E8` bracket.

2. Stefan Floerchinger, [_Real Clifford algebras and their spinors for relativistic fermions_](https://arxiv.org/html/1908.02235v1), arXiv:1908.02235 (2019). The review defines a real Clifford algebra through the anticommutator relation and treats spinors as minimal or quasi-minimal ideals, separating the algebra from any particular matrix realization. It anchors the requirement that a claimed action map state its basis, scalar field, bilinear form, and representation rather than merely quote dimensions.

3. Skip Garibaldi and Robert Guralnick with Alexander Premet, [_Spinors and essential dimension_](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.00590), _Compositio Mathematica_ 153 (2017), 535–556. For dimensions divisible by four, the source states that the natural spinor action splits into two inequivalent half-spin representations. It anchors a chiral-lane distinction; it does not justify assigning a numerical defect to an unconstructed action map.

4. Pierre-Philippe Dechant, [_The birth of E8 out of the spinors of the icosahedron_](https://royalsocietypublishing.org/rspa/article/472/2185/20150504/57684/The-birth-of-E8-out-of-the-spinors-of-the), _Proceedings of the Royal Society A_ 472 (2016), 20150504. This paper supplies a distinct `Cl(3)` spinorial route to the `E8` root system. It is retained as a **non-equivalence control**: a root-set construction from `Cl(3)` is not automatically an `so(16)` half-spin action, nor an Adinkra representation lane.

## Research Boundary

The next executable artifact may report only finite properties of a declared map—for example, dimensions, skew-adjointness checks, brackets, Jacobi residuals, kernel, image, and independently reproduced basis products. It must refuse a regular-representation or homoiconicity score until it contains an explicit action map and falsifiers capable of failing.
