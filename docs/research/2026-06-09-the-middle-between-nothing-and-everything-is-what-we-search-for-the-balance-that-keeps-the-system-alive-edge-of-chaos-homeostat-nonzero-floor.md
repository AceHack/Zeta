## The middle is the objective

The traveler-frame spans **⊥ = Nothing (∅ / zero / D⁰, the void / heat-death)** to **⊤ = Everything
(the totality / unbounded generative-expansion)** — both are travelers (the resolved paradox). **What we
search for is neither pole — it is the *middle between them*, the balance that keeps the system alive.**
This restates the system's actual objective geometrically: **not maximize, not minimize — balance at the
productive middle.**

```text
⊥  Nothing / ∅ / D⁰        — collapse / heat-death (AVOID)        |
                                                                  |  THE MIDDLE
   ...the balanced, living, bounded middle we search for...  <----+  (the target:
                                                                  |   homeostatic
⊤  Everything / unbounded-F — runaway / fork-bomb (AVOID)         |   set-point)
```

Both extremes are death of a different kind: **⊥ = collapse to nothing** (D⁰ heat-death, diversity→1,
identity-collapse); **⊤ = runaway to everything** (unbounded expansion, the fork-bomb, infinite
recursion, noise/chaos). The system is **alive only in the middle** — bounded, plural, balanced.
