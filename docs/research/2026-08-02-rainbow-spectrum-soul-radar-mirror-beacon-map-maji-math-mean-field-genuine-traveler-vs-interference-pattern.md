## The architecture the map implies (field + pairwise correction)

- **Field = cheap, fast prediction of the decorrelated bulk** (most agents, most of the time)
  — the mean-field shortcut. Valid in the well-mixed / many-weak-interaction regime.
- **CHSH / decorrelation = the exact per-pair check for where the mean-field breaks** — where
  correlations concentrate (strong local coupling = the interference-patterns / sybil-clusters
  the field averages away). This is where a "genuine traveler" the field trusts turns out to
  be an "interference pattern."
- **Complementary, not competing:** approximate everywhere with the field; correct exactly
  where correlations concentrate. Lands on **Hawkins Thousand Brains**: cortical columns
  voting = a field over columns; the disagreements (correlations that don't fit) carry the
  real information.
