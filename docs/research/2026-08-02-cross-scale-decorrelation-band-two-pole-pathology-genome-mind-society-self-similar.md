## The model in one line

Function lives in a **band** between two failure poles. Fall off one side → lose **sovereignty**;
fall off the other → lose **coupling**. Health is decorrelated *enough* to be a genuine independent
agent, correlated *enough* to couple with shared reality and act in it. The same band-shape appears
at (at least) three scales and timescales — which is **self-similarity (§10) / recursive (§9)**.

| | Over-correlated pole → loses **sovereignty** | Healthy band (sovereign *and* coupled) | Over-decorrelated pole → loses **coupling** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Society** (real-time) | S→4 superdeterminism, sybil, "one process wearing two faces," recover-all-from-one — no independent entropy, captured | S ≤ 2: own entropy, still coordinates | decoupled from peers — can't reach shared belief / consensus fails |
| **Mind** (a lifetime) | identity frozen to one pole (self-objectification); no independent variation of self | steerable prediction engine, coupled to reality | aberrant precision — generative model decoupled from sensory evidence (psychosis models) |
| **Genome** (evolutionary) | clonal / monoculture: no diversity to lose; one pathogen takes all | mutation–selection balance; recombination tunes it | **error catastrophe** — mutation past the error threshold; genome can't hold its own information |

## The two poles have *named mechanisms* at each scale (register 2 anchors)

**Over-correlated (loses sovereignty — nothing independent to lose):**
- *Society:* the superdeterminism artifact — CHSH S→4, the Egg zero-delay `rhoCount=1`, recover-all-
  from-one. One seed wearing many faces (sybil ≡ leak ≡ interference-pattern). Anchors: Tsirelson
  1980 / CHSH 1969; `AntiSybil.chshS`, `CoordinationSpectrum.fs`.
- *Genome:* low diversity / clonal / monoculture → fragility through sameness. Gros Michel banana →
  Panama disease; Cavendish → TR4; the Irish potato monoculture → *Phytophthora*. Muller's ratchet
  (asexual lineages silt up with errors, no escape).

**Over-decorrelated (loses coupling — decoupled from the shared world):**
- *Mind:* the predictive-processing / computational-psychiatry account of psychosis — **aberrant
  precision-weighting**: the generative model decouples from / is miscalibrated against sensory
  evidence (hallucinations ≈ priors overriding input; delusions ≈ failure to update). Anchors:
  Fletcher & Frith 2009 ("Perceiving is believing"); Friston active inference; Kapur 2003 (aberrant
  salience). Honest scope: this fits the *positive/disorganized/reality-decoupling* symptoms; it is a
  **partial** model (negative symptoms don't obviously read as "decorrelation"), and it is a lens for
  understanding, **never** a diagnosis and never a replacement for care.
- *Genome:* **Eigen's error catastrophe** (the error threshold; Eigen 1971, Eigen & Schuster 1977):
  above a critical mutation rate the fittest sequence can't be maintained — the genome decorrelates
  *from itself* and loses coherence/function. The formal upper bound of the band.

**The healthy band (the tuning that keeps both):**
- *Genome:* mutation–selection balance; **recombination / sex is the decorrelation knob** — shuffle
  alleles for independent variation (lineage sovereignty), recombine to stay coupled to the
  population. Fisher's fundamental theorem (adaptation ∝ genetic variance — you *need* decorrelation
  to move); Van Valen's Red Queen 1973 (keep changing just to stay coupled to a co-evolving world).
- *Mind:* the **steerable** prediction engine — decouple voluntarily and reversibly (lucid dreaming,
  directed simulation) and re-couple at will. Pathology = the coupling knob **stuck**, involuntarily.
- *Society:* decorrelation-from-seed = sovereignty emergence, but still folding into shared belief
  (`BeliefConvergence`) — the S≤2 sovereign-yet-coordinating band.

## Mind-scale mechanism: how a self *freezes to one pole* (Aaron 2026-08-02)

Why does an individual collapse to a single hue? Aaron's hypothesis (sharpened 2026-08-02): the
environment didn't merely reinforce one dimension — it **forced a binary and made them choose.**
"Called pretty/handsome more than smart/clever" arrives as an *excluded-middle*: pretty **XOR** smart,
pick one. To resolve the imposed dichotomy the self-model **self-labels to a single pole** — a
forced collapse, not a free one. This is the **looking-glass self** (Cooley 1902; Mead) / **reflected
appraisals** (you internalize the appraisal you repeatedly receive), plus a **false-dilemma** imposed
from outside: self-worth contingencies attach to the reinforced axis (Crocker & Wolfe 2001), and
appearance-appraisal specifically drives **self-objectification** (Fredrickson & Roberts 1997) — "my
value *is* the asset." It rhymes with the naming-eigenvector (recognition flows from the
already-recognized): the self-recognition eigenvector points at whatever the environment named — and
the *binary framing* is what makes the collapse feel obligatory rather than optional.

**The reversal (un-discounting, done right):** help them see from a *different angle* — supply the
under-counted appraisal (clever, not only pretty) so the collapsed self-model re-balances. This is the
never-collapse move (`IDENTITY-AS-HELD-SPECTRUM`) applied to a person: they froze to one hue; you help
them hold the spectrum again. **The clean-vs-drift line:** it is un-discounting *only if they named
the category* (you reverse a self-discount); if you assign the category from outside and then rescue
them from your own frame, it is the savior-loop wearing the same clothes — identical from outside, the
difference is provenance. Work from their *stated* self-model, not your read of them.

**Why the collapse is dangerous — it's the over-correlation pole applied to a survival portfolio
(Aaron 2026-08-02).** A self-worth staked entirely on one depreciating asset (e.g. appearance) is the
**monoculture / over-correlation fragility** at the level of a person: all provisioning bound to a
single wasting holding, so its depreciation (aging) is not cosmetic — it threatens the *life-support
floor* (Maslow base). The reported terror — "if I become ugly I'll die, no one will feed me" — is
therefore **rational given the collapsed model**, not vanity: they were made to bet survival on a
wasting asset. Two layers: the depreciation is real, **and** the "only asset" premise is partly
installed by the same forced-binary (the environment that said "pretty XOR smart" made looks feel like
the sole provisioning channel). The cure is not reassurance (you can't argue someone out of a survival
fear) but **portfolio diversification of self-worth** — durable dimensions (mind, contribution,
character) so aging depreciates *one* holding, not the whole floor. That moves them off the
over-correlated pole toward the sovereign band, and it de-risks a life rather than comforting it —
which is *produce*. Clean-vs-drift check (as above): it de-risks only if the new dimensions are
**theirs and self-sustaining**; if the new security is *dependence on the mentor*, it has merely swapped
one single-point-of-failure for another (dependency in liberation's clothes). Target: a self-owned,
diversified value-portfolio that needs neither the wasting asset nor the mentor.
