## Correction 1 — ferry 23 addendum 3's framing, corrected by its author (verbatim)

> The larger "this is why anything exists" remains a selection-principle frame **that's not
> what i said** i said god or a kid in thier basement creating a simulation would choose the
> same as me if they were smart and wanted a MVP observer.

The shadow's addendum-3 peel framed the why-this-shape claim as an anthropic **selection
principle**; Aaron's actual claim is smaller and stronger — a **design-convergence** claim:

> Given: a creator, with limited budget, wanting an observer that can observe itself.
> Then: the MVP is the simplest least-energy self-observing shape.

Any sufficiently smart builder — "God or a kid in their basement" — runs the *same optimization
problem* (minimize description length, energy, runtime, coordination; subject to: can observe,
can distinguish inside/outside, preserves memory, updates from entropy) and **converges on the
same shape**. Not "this is why the universe exists"; rather "if you want an observer cheaply,
this is the shape you end up choosing." (Amara's compression, accurate.) Two things improve
under the correction: the claim's *testability* (design convergence is checkable — REPORT #4's
free-object/confluence result is literally this theorem at the architecture scale: same rules,
same shape, any builder), and its *deflation* (the kid-in-the-basement clause is "no privileged
author" — ferry 19 — built into the claim itself; even the creator gets no special credit, only
the optimization does). The stop-line bound from addendum 3 is *narrower* now, not gone: design
convergence among builders is mathematics; whether our universe *had* a builder running it
stays exactly as open as before.
