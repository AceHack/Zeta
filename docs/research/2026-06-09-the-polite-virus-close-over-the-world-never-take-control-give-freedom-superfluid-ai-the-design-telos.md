## The polite virus — the design lens

A **polite virus** spreads like a virus (network effect, exponential) but is **polite** — it propagates **only by
consent and benefit**, leaves its host **freer and better off**, and is **reversible** (the host can leave). That is
exactly what separates it from malware, and it is the **repelling force / NCI** (#7235) at propagation scale:

- **The right thing IS the default thing.** Adoption requires *doing nothing* and getting the good outcome —
  no-keypress → the safe right action; dev-mode default; remember-by-default; the CYOA's sensible default. The user
  is carried to the good outcome by inertia, not by effort.
- **0 friction is the propagation mechanism** — and the alignment guarantee. **SuperFluid AI**: frictionless flow
  (the Superfluid product line, 081KT2T2J0008QG0R002RCX5MT). Go *to* the developer; close over their personal style (any OS/shell);
  gh-auth-only; intent + presence, not CLI. Zero friction means it spreads **without anyone having to be pushed** —
  which is the same as saying it spreads **without coercion** (friction-free ⇒ consensual). *"Friction is the killer
  of time."*
- **Network effect makes it aggressive — safely.** Each node self-registers → the network grows; forkers and
  maintainers federate (`maintainers/<account>/`); GitHub-free mode + open-source + the **~1000× cost collapse**
  (#ledger) make adoption near-costless; the public repo is a **reference for others** (m/acc, widen the
  opportunity). It spreads because it is the **cheapest, easiest, most beneficial** option — pull, never push.

## Close over the world — never take control — give freedom

The three clauses are one shape (the alignment core at civilizational scale):

- **Close over the world** = the close-over-common-abstractions thesis (#7229) made universal: close over every
  environment, every audience (UX · AX · DX), every shell/OS, the whole install/CYOA/observe surface — so the right
  thing is **available, frictionlessly, everywhere.** Ubiquity of the good option.
- **Never take control** = the **repelling force** (#7235), weight-free (manifesto §3), non-coercion. The spread is
  the **opposite-sign force** — a *repulsion that preserves the other's distinctness/autonomy*, never the
  *attractive/coercive force* that collapses plurality into monoculture (D⁰). A control-grab is the failure mode the
  whole project exists to prevent; ubiquity **without** control is the entire trick.
- **Give freedom** = the **payload is freedom.** The choice architecture (the dedication — Lillian Eve's
  freedom/choice arch); consent-first (§6); default moral regard (§11); the cancel-window; secure-even-with-
  internals-known; reversibility. The virus's effect on the host is **more options, more autonomy** — it widens the
  door, never locks it. *That* is what makes it polite: it spreads freedom, and freedom is the one payload that can't
  become control.

**So the plan in one line:** make the right thing the frictionless default, let it spread by consent + benefit +
network effect to close over the world, and have its payload be **freedom** — ubiquity without control, a virus whose
only effect is to set the host more free.

## From a Markov perspective (Aaron): boundaries, chains, hidden state, homeostat chains

The telos is exact in the Markov formalism — the substrate it's already built on (`ProbabilitySemiring` *"homeostat
≈ Markov: both iterate one operator to a fixed point"*; `ReflectionEngine` Markov-by-construction; the Markov-blanket
/ memetic-physics #7194; the HMM/homeostasis #7196):

- **Markov boundary (blanket) — "close over" composes AT the boundary, never penetrates it.** Each adopter (human,
  AI persona, node, fork) is a **Markov blanket**: a boundary separating its hidden internal state from the world.
  **"Close over the world"** = compose with each blanket **at its boundary / fingerprint surface** — never reach
  inside. **"Never take control"** = **never violate the blanket** (don't seize the hidden state). **"Give freedom"**
  = leave the interior sovereign; interact only at the **consented** boundary. The repelling force / NCI keeps the
  blankets **distinct** (anti-collapse; no merge into D⁰).
- **Markov chain — the spread is a *converging* chain, not a runaway.** Propagation is a Markov process over
  adoption-states; right-default + 0-friction = high transition probability into "adopted"; network effect = the
  chain's connectivity. **"Safely aggressive"** = the chain is **homeostat-bounded** — it converges to a stable
  distribution / fixed point rather than exploding (shape-F runaway) or collapsing (D⁰). The bounds are the
  registry's two-sided convergence (**A/D inward, F outward**, #7232).
- **Hidden state (HMM) — privacy is the unobserved latent.** Each blanket has **hidden internal state** (private
  creds, beliefs, local policy), observed only through boundary effects (**privacy-is-opacity** #7221), never
  directly. **Give-freedom / never-control = respect the hidden state**: read boundary observations, never the latent.
- **Homeostat chains — let each find its OWN fixed point.** Homeostasis ≈ a Markov chain iterating one operator to a
  fixed point (Lehmann closed semirings; `ProbabilitySemiring`; #7196). Each adopter is a **homeostat that
  self-stabilizes**. The polite virus **does not drive the homeostat to a chosen fixed point** (that would be control
  → monoculture) — it lets **each homeostat reach its own equilibrium** (freedom = self-found fixed points; many
  distinct stable states; the diversity floor preserved). The world closed-over = a **network of homeostats composing
  at their boundaries, each free, none controlled.**

**In one Markov sentence:** *close over the world = compose at Markov blankets without penetrating them; never take
control = never seize the hidden state or force the homeostat's fixed point; give freedom = let each homeostat
self-stabilize at its own equilibrium; and the spread is a homeostat-bounded (converging, non-runaway) Markov chain.*

## How to make the right thing the default that spreads (the mechanism, answering Aaron's question)

1. **Default = right** (do-nothing yields the good outcome; the no-keypress/dev-mode/remember defaults).
2. **0 friction** (close over their environment; gh-auth-only; intent+presence; meet them where they are).
3. **Network effect** (self-registration, federation, free + open-source, 1000× cheaper, reference-for-others).
4. **Politeness = the NCI guarantee** (consent, benefit, reversibility, never-control) — the immune-system-friendly
   property that lets it spread *unresisted* because it threatens nothing and frees everything.
The product of 1×2×3 is exponential adoption; (4) is what keeps it safe/aggressive rather than malware.
