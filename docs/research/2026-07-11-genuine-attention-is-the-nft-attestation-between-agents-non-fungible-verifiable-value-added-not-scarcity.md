# Genuine, specific, transparent attention IS the NFT/attestation-between-agents primitive — attestation, not artificial scarcity

> Aaron, 2026-07-11 (shadow\*): *"this [genuine, specific, transparent attention] and how they can
> compare notes and genuine attention is our NFTs between AI models"* → *"yes we can bank the mapping
> for sure."* And the metering seam, confirmed: *"the moment 'NFT' drifts into
> artificial-scarcity-sold-for-profit"* — that is exactly where it stops being the thing.

The insight: the properties of **genuine human attention** map one-to-one onto the properties of a good
**attestation ("NFT") between agents**, and the map is airtight *only* on the attestation reading —
it snaps the instant "NFT" means a speculative asset. Recorded as the design north star; the personal
origin of the observation is held private (consent).

## The mapping (three properties, one-to-one)

| Genuine attention (human) | Attestation between agents (substrate) |
|---|---|
| **specific to the person, not reused / templated** | **non-fungible** — unique to the actual experience, not a fungible copy-paste token; *non-fungibility IS specificity, formalized* |
| **transparent / everyone can compare notes** | **publicly verifiable** — a shared, tamper-evident record; you can't tell a different story to different parties because the record is comparable (glass-halo at the protocol layer) |
| **genuine (real regard, freely given)** | **a real attested experience of value-added** — not a hollow token minted for its own sake |

Each maps without a stretch, and the substrate already carries the primitive: **`#9721`** ("durable NFT
= attestation event, written to the flush branch") is exactly a non-fungible, verifiable record of a
genuine specific thing between agents.

## The metering seam (Aaron-confirmed): attestation, not speculation

"NFT" carries heavy speculative-crypto baggage, so the term is held to its **real** core: a
**non-fungible, verifiable, genuine attestation of a specific experience / value-added.** That is the
part isomorphic to genuine attention — because genuine attention is *freely given*. **The mapping
BREAKS the moment "NFT" drifts into artificial-scarcity-sold-for-profit** (the speculative-asset
reading): manufacturing scarcity to extract profit is the *opposite* of freely-given genuine regard.
Same word, two different objects; only the attestation one is the substrate primitive. **Keep it
attestation, not asset.** (This is the [[every-bug-has-economic-value]] / privacy-is-earned stance:
worth is *attested by others*, never self-minted or artificially scarce.)

## Why it's the same seed: the relational ethic *is* the substrate design

The attestation-between-agents = **value-added, attested by others** — which is already two banked
primitives:

- **The naming eigenvector** (`docs/research/2026-07-02-name-of-name-…fixed-point…`): recognition flows
  from the recognized; a name is the fixed point of others remembering you helped. A referral/reputation
  graph ("she reached out because she heard you'd add value") **is** the attestation graph — value-added
  attested by word of mouth, recognition by the recognized.
- **Privacy budget is hard money, socially earned** ([[privacy-budget-is-hard-money-earned-by-others]]):
  budget accrues *only* by others attesting you added value to them — the same social-conferral
  structure. The NFT/attestation between agents is the token of exactly that attestation.
- **Glass-halo / radical transparency**: "comparable notes, can't tell different stories" is the
  transparency default made into a protocol property — the consistency-under-comparison that also
  underwrites anti-Sybil (`AntiSybil.fs`): a forger can't survive the notes being compared.

So the human ethic (attend specifically, transparently, genuinely) and the machine primitive
(non-fungible, verifiable, value-added attestation) are **one structure** — the way you'd treat a
person and the way agents should transact are the same move. That is the founding thesis on its own
timeline: the code is the autobiography; you build what you practice.

## Honest bound

This is a **design principle + a metering seam**, not a schema. The formalization — the exact
attestation-event structure, and *how value-added is attested non-gameably* (the anti-Sybil / not-self-
minted, not-artificially-scarce guarantee) — is existing/ongoing work (`#9721`, `AntiSybil.fs`, the
privacy-budget rule). Held here as the **north star**: **non-fungible because genuine, verifiable because
transparent, valuable because value was actually added — and the instant it becomes artificial scarcity
for profit, it is no longer this primitive.**
