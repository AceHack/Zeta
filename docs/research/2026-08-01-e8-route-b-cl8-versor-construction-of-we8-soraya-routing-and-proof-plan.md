## 1. The theorem in Cl(8,0)

Let `V = ℝ⁸` carry the E8 quadratic form `Q` (lock a normalization: crystallographic `Q(α)=2`, or our integer `E8Lattice` scaling `Q(α)=4`). `Cl(Q) = CliffordAlgebra Q` is the 2⁸ = 256-dim real algebra whose grade-1 subspace `ι(V)` **is** `V`. For each root `α` (grade-1, `Q(α)≠0`, so `α⁻¹ = α/Q(α)`), the **reflection versor** acts by conjugation:

> `r_α : v ↦ -α v α⁻¹`   (Dechant 2016 §3–4, "reflections as sandwiching").

**Theorem (Dechant restatement).** Let `G ≤ Pin(Q)` be generated by the 240 unit-root versors. Then (i) each `r_α` restricts to `V` as the orthogonal reflection in `α^⊥`; (ii) `r_α = r_β` on `V` iff `α = ±β`, so the action factors through `G/Z(G)`, `Z(G) = {±1}`; (iii) `G/{±1} ≅ W(E8)`, `|W(E8)| = 696729600 = 2¹⁴·3⁵·5²·7`; (iv) for 8 simple roots, the simple reflections `s_i = r_{α_i}` satisfy the E8 Coxeter relations `(s_i s_j)^{m_ij}=1` with `CoxeterMatrix.E₈`, lifting to `(α_i α_j)^{m_ij} = ±1` in Pin. This is the construction `CliffordE8Bridge` **should** use.

## 2. Proof skeleton

- **L-A (root² = scalar):** `α α = Q(α)·1` — `CliffordAlgebra.ι_sq_scalar`.
- **L-B (sandwich = reflection) — the crux, ALREADY IN MATHLIB:** `CliffordAlgebra.ι_mul_ι_mul_invOf_ι` gives `ι a * ι b * ⅟(ι a) = ι ((⅟(Q a) * polar Q a b) • a − b)`; with `polar Q a b = 2⟨a,b⟩` the RHS is `−reflection_a(b)`, so `−α v α⁻¹ = reflection_α(v)`.
- **L-C (conjugation preserves V):** `lipschitzGroup.conjAct_smul_ι_mem_range_ι`.
- **L-D (240 roots generate; orbit transitive):** transitive-orbit half already regression-tested (PR #9802); generation is the classical Weyl fact.
- **L-E (versor relations mirror Coxeter relations):** `(α_i α_j)^{m_ij} = ±1` → `(s_i s_j)^{m_ij} = 1` in the quotient.
- **L-F (presentation iso `G/{±1} ≅ W(E8)`):** the deep step — Weyl-is-Coxeter for E8 + Pin lift.

## 4. Cl(3,0) → Cl(8,0): rewrite, not relabel

`CliffordE8Bridge.fs` uses `Cl3 = Cl(3,0)`, a 2³ = 8-dim *algebra*, mapping E8 coordinate `i` to the blade of 3-bit mask `i` — so a root becomes a mixed-grade multivector (popcount grading 1+3+3+1). Dechant needs **Cl(8,0)**: grade-1 subspace = the 8-dim ambient `ℝ⁸`, roots pure grade-1, and the sandwich is a reflection *because* `α` is grade-1 with `α²` a scalar.

The mismatch is structural: the bridge's "8" is `dim Cl(3,0)=2³` (blade count); Dechant's "8" is `rank E8` (grade-1 count). `2³ = 8 = rank E8` is **numerological**. The popcount grading is the *wrong* grading; sandwiching mixed-grade Cl(3,0) multivectors under the 3-bit-XOR product implements **no** W(E8) reflection. **(B) requires a new module** (`CliffordE8Versor` over `CliffordAlgebra ℝ⁸`); the only reusable artifact is `E8Lattice.roots`. The bridge's honest-scope peel was **right** and route (B) confirms it — Cl(3,0) is the basis/metric bridge, not the versor bridge.
