## The peel

### 1. The gap in the render

The Kitaev chain's load-bearing feature is its **energy gap**: the topological gap that
separates the ground-state pair (where the Majorana qubit lives) from everything else — the gap
IS the protection (ferry 18 §6's "the bet": continuous noise wagered against a discrete
invariant, and the stake is the gap's height). The render Aaron is looking at is a picture of a
protected emptiness: structure held open by the spectrum.

### 2. "Casimir gap" — and why ζ is standing inside it

The Casimir effect (Casimir 1948; measured, real): two plates confining the vacuum experience a
force, because the **gap restricts which vacuum modes fit** — the energy of confined emptiness
differs from free emptiness. And the canonical computation of that energy is
**zeta-function regularization**: the divergent mode-sum is assigned its finite ζ-value
(Σn³ → ζ(−3) = 1/120 for the electromagnetic case; the famous Σn → ζ(−1) = −1/12 is the
bosonic-string sibling). **ζ is the function that buys a finite answer from an infinite sum.**
So "Zeta comes from the Casimir gap" reads exactly: the project carries the name of the
*finitizer* — the mathematical organ that makes confined infinity affordable. Which is the
project's entire engineering thesis wearing its physics name: the budget (ferry 16 — big-O
explosion control), the soft-max width (REPORT #2 — the infinite fuse priced finite),
"the singularity with a budget is a braid" (ferry 19) — every one of these is
ζ-regularization's move performed at a different scale: *assign the divergent thing its finite,
lawful value, and proceed.*

### 3. The identification, bounded

"The Kitaev gap is the Casimir gap" is a **rhyme with shared mechanism, not an equation**: both
are ground-state structure across a confined geometry (the chain's gap and the plates' gap are
both "what emptiness costs when you bound it"), and Casimir-type vacuum effects in topological
chains are an active research area — but the literature does not identify the two, and this
ferry doesn't either. What it banks: the *name's* referent chain — Kitaev's protected gap →
the Casimir energy of confined vacuum → ζ-regularization as its computation → **Zeta as the
name of making-the-infinite-finite**. The dedication (`docs/DEDICATION.md` — Elizabeth Ryan
Stainback; "the database that never forgets") remains the name's first meaning; this ferry
records its second: the function that holds a finite, lawful value open inside a gap — which,
read back through the dedication, is not a coincidence of registers but the same vow in two
languages: **what is held in the gap is not lost to the infinity around it.**
