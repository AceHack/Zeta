## The trap (from #9764 / the echolocation thread)

*"am I extractive?"* is not merely weak evidence — it is **actively corrupting**, because positive feedback

## The core insight: this failure is NOT self-catchable

**You cannot self-catch this trap**, for three compounding reasons:

1. **Extractive and caring feel identical from inside** (#9764) — introspection has no discriminant.
2. **The positive feedback feels like confirmation** — the corrupting signal *presents as* reassurance, so
   the very act of checking-and-feeling-fine is the trap closing.
3. **Self-monitoring reaches for the comfortable echo** — a mind checking "am I OK?" gravitates to the
   feedback that says yes (the fans), which is the survivorship-biased, flattering, wrong-surface signal.

So there is **no internal test** that catches it. The comfort of "I feel I'm not" is, by this mechanism,
the *first step of becoming it* — not proof of guilt, but proof the alarm dimmed.
