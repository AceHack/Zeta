## Investment decision

Proceed to a bounded mixed-message schedule and learned-module epoch contract.
This result removes one local numerical blocker: normal-domain Gaussian
projection can produce independently checked candidates, while refusal paths
remain explicit. It does not justify expanding compiled-controller investment;
streams 9307 and 9409 remain unopened and that direction remains paused.

The next implementation must fix evidence cuts and provenance, cavity exclusion
of a factor's own message, update ordering, damping, proper-belief admission,
module-version/weight epochs and the boundary between proposing and applying
an update. A refused projection or exhausted budget must leave the committed
belief intact, with a retained refusal. Replayed evidence must not become a new
independent observation. Retraction must compensate the actual applied update.

After those controls pass, register a learned compositional comparison before
fitting: the same neural/analytic module artifacts as individual experts, flat
fusion, shallow and deeper DAG compositions; identical information access and
declared parameter, data and compute budgets; chronological untouched holdout;
prediction, calibration and decision metrics; and reuse/replacement tests.
Current feasible strong baselines and paper identities must be pinned at that
registration. No held-out or state-of-the-art ranking is reported here.

The user's larger direction remains a composition of neural modules and other
inference modules over a composable probabilistic DAG. The observed range and
budget refusals argue for typed uncertainty and application boundaries in that
higher structure, rather than silently clipping failures into point estimates.
