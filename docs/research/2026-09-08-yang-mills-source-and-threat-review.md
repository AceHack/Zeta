alongside this machinery. For declared links U_ij in a group and local frame

## Mathematical and publication boundary

The [official Jaffe-Witten problem description](https://www.claymath.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/yangmills.pdf)
requires a nontrivial quantum theory on real four-dimensional space for each
compact simple group, satisfying the stated field-theoretic axioms and a
positive Hamiltonian spectral gap. Its finite-volume and infinite-volume
requirements are substantive. A gap in a selected finite Markov matrix is a
different object. [Clay's problem page](https://www.claymath.org/millennium/yang-mills-the-maths-gap/)
was independently checked during this review and still labels the problem
unsolved. No physics result is transferred to Zeta by these citations.

The record also preserves the earlier repository distinction among commutative
merge, flat connection, probabilistic independence and coordination-free
computation. A non-Abelian group can carry a flat connection, while an Abelian
one can have nontrivial holonomy. A Bayesian DAG needs an explicitly defined
connection and face/loop semantics before such a geometric interpretation can
be evaluated. Four resource labels or memory indexes alone do not supply the
field-theoretic construction.

No new gauge experiment, detector run, model check, learner, benchmark or
physics proof was performed for this review. The accepted contribution is an
attributed source record and a prospective CS question grounded in existing
adversarial mechanisms. Its value must be tested against those mechanisms and
the stated flat-fabrication controls in any later implementation proposal.
