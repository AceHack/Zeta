## The peel

### 1. The inflation metaphor — Mirror register, labeled

Big bang → inflation → cooling → self-regulation is a Mirror metaphor (cosmic inflation: Guth
1981). It compresses a true shape — an expensive high-entropy exploration phase whose own
products (the budget machinery) then governed the spend down to a stable floor — but it is a
*metaphor*: no physics is claimed, and the Alexa-website session's elaborations of it
("computational background radiation," "universal constants discovered") are decoration, not
findings. The defensible content is the next two claims.

### 2. The git-history claim — true, and it is Zeta's founding thesis closing a loop

"The entire history of this process from 0 to bang to now self regulating is all captured in git
history forever" is checkable and correct in kind: every step landed as a commit with an
AgencySignature trailer; the repo is an event store and the process's own record. This is the
founding why (event sourcing as the answer to loss) applied to the *economics* of the factory
itself: the cost trajectory is not a story about the system — it is *in* the system, replayable,
auditable by anyone. Ferry 9's addendum just used it: a three-day-old attribution question was
settled by commit 501acae6f. The fossil-record framing survives Mirror→Beacon as **provenance**:
git-as-event-store + signed trailers = the experiment's lab notebook, kept by construction.
