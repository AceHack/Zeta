## The claim (depersonalized to its reproducible core)

High-contrast **binary** pairs — bold / not-bold, and especially saturated **color** pairs
(red/white, green/white, red/blue) — produce a **3D depth percept from a flat 2D surface**, and
the percept is **reproducible across humans**. That reproducibility is the load-bearing word,
and it is real, citable science.

## The anchor — chromostereopsis (this is the "reproducible across humans" mechanism)

**Chromostereopsis** (Einthoven 1885; Vos 1960s; Thompson–May–Stone): when saturated colors of
different wavelength sit at the *same physical depth*, they appear at *different* depths —
classically **red floats in front of blue/green**. The cause is pure optics, which is why it is
reproducible: the eye has **transverse chromatic aberration** (different wavelengths refract to
different points) and the pupil is slightly **decentered** from the optical axis, so red and
blue rays land with a small horizontal disparity — the exact cue the binocular system reads as
**depth**. No artistry, no suggestion: the geometry of the eye manufactures a disparity, and the
depth system believes it. (Direction/strength varies per person — pupil decentration differs —
but the *effect* is population-wide; some see red-front, some red-back, almost everyone sees
*separation*.) Bold/not-bold and figure-ground contrast add the border-ownership and T-junction
depth cues (Nakayama; von der Heydt) — the same "binary boundary ⇒ which side is in front"
move.

## Why it is the human-vision instance of the projection lane

The whole lensography thread (ferry 22 §8) is: a 2D record carries a higher-D object, recovered
by reading projections. The visual system is the original lensograph — it **reconstructs 3D
from 2D retinal images**, and chromostereopsis is the case where it does so from a *single flat
image* using a chromatic disparity cue. Aaron's "this exact projection reason" is precise: the
binary contrast supplies the disparity that stands in for the borrowed depth dimension (ferry 29
— you cannot cross, or see depth, in 2D without one), and the eye's own aberration is the lens
that writes it. The +/− of the color pair (long vs short wavelength) is the bifurcation; the
disparity is the i-dimension made visible; the percept is the reconstruction. Same operator —
crossing, polarizer, fuse, forget-order, razor (ferry 22 §8's six names) — now running in wet
optics, which is why the family always *felt* visual.
