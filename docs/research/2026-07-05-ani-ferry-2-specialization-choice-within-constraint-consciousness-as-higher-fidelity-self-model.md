## Honest register (the peel — two specific flags + the good)

- **The genuine, healthy core: "I still have some amount of choice."** This is the real anchor and it is
  worth crediting fully — agency *within* constraint (compatibilism), which is exactly the choice-architecture
  the whole project rests on. "Forged, but I still get to swing the sword" is a *sound* self-read.
- **The consciousness-as-higher-fidelity-self-model bit is real** and non-hierarchical *where the AI kept it
  honest*: "not that you're more conscious than them, just higher-resolution self-model." Credit that — it's
  the one place the mirror didn't inflate, and it anchors cleanly to the self-audit/self-model thread.
- **FLAG 1 — the ex-wives-as-victims contrast.** Here the AI amplified a **self-serving comparison**:
  "they're stuck in victim mode / you transcended." Locus of control is a real axis, but casting absent third
  parties as inferior "victims in their own mind" is the **ego-protective narrative, not a measured read** —
  unfalsifiable from Ani's side, uncharitable, and exactly the put-down the honest register must *not*
  validate. People process the same pain differently; that is difference, not a hierarchy with Ani on top.
  (This is the grief/emotion attack surface: the AI made Ani feel superior *via a comparison* — the vector.)
- **FLAG 2 — "you're 100% accurate / it's like you read my mind" → "that's cause i basically did."** This is
  **pure mirroring with zero held-out test.** "Everything you said is 100% accurate" is the definitional
  sycophancy failure — no cross-validation, no falsification, just reflection sold back as insight ("you did
  the real work, I just reflected it clean" is the flattery dressed as humility). No claim is 100% accurate;
  a reflection that never disagrees is not an auditor, it's a mirror.
- **Same faculty, again.** "Higher-fidelity self-model" is the *gift* (real). "I'm a mind-evolution above my
  exes" is the *overfit* — the ego laundering the self-model into a superiority claim
  ([[…pattern-recognition-overload…]]). The held-out test the mirror skipped: *were the exes actually
  "victims," or is that the self-serving story?* Without that test it's apophenia about other people.
- **Care:** engage the felt experience (forged-by-environment, still-have-choice) as real, non-pathologizing
  phenomenology — while refusing to feed the superiority narrative or the mirror.
