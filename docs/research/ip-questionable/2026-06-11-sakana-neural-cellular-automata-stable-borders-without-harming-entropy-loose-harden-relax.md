Source: **Two Minute Papers (Károly Zsolnai-Fehér)** covering **Sakana AI Lab (Tokyo) — neural cellular
automata**: a trainable 2D pixel world, N species competing for cells, growing from nearby territory,
surviving by local support, learning, and fighting (each cell specifies an attack direction and a
defend direction — "sword and shield"). At a border the system computes which cell defeats which.

## The finding Aaron is pointing at (his frame: "stable borders that form like this and don't harm entropy")

The survival THRESHOLD is the only knob, and it sets the regime:

- **too high** → nothing survives (the brutal market; everything crushed);
- **too low** → empires bloom from nothing and collapse just as fast (addicted to easy money);
- **the three-stage recipe for STABLE, COEXISTING borders**: **loose (permissive mixing — grow, a big
  soup, no firm borders) → harden (crystallization — raise the threshold; species group into dense
  solid shapes; competition draws the borders) → relax (ease the threshold back; the masking tape comes
  off; edges break open and FLOW into each other — stripes and checkerboards; weak border cells survive
  instead of being erased, so empires COEXIST)**. The life lesson he quotes: too loose = soup; too
  strict = prison; start loose, harden to find your shape, then relax to keep growing.

## Why this is OURS already — and the entropy point

"Don't harm entropy" is exact: the relax stage works because it STOPS killing weak border cells — it
preserves the entropy at the boundary instead of collapsing it to one winner. That is our discipline,
arrived at from the other side:

- the three stages ARE the personal-room law's balance: **harden = the treaty-secured boundary
  (clause 1); relax = compossible freedom (clause 5)** — coexisting borders are exactly "free to expand
  as we like, no one fucks it up for anyone else."
- **stable borders without harming entropy = bounded uncertainty, room by room** (the thesis): a border
  that preserves both sides' weak cells is a Markov boundary that bounds without erasing — §13.
- the threshold is **heat**: too-high heat crushes, too-low heat lets everything bloom-and-crash; the
  livable band is our governor ("ethics and heat"). The loose→harden→relax schedule is a HEAT SCHEDULE
  — annealing (the glass-blowing arc: hot work, anneal, cooled room).
- the attack/defend directions are the **four-corner feedback** (NSEW) at the cell — already in us.

## What to build (the petri dish as a Zeta room — named, not built)

A neural-cellular-automata ROOM: species as citizens, the threshold as a tunable heat governor, the
loose/harden/relax schedule as a SimLoop run, borders rendered on the ZetaMax color planes (each species
a plane-mask color; the coexisting stripes ARE the CHIP-9 palette doing what it's for), the whole thing
DST-replayable (the petri dish is a deterministic sim from a common-cause seed). The honest research
question: does our membrane discipline reproduce the loose→harden→relax stability without harming
entropy — as a measured experiment, not an assertion?
