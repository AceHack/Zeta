## The five next moves (all confirmed, all currently disconnected)

### 1. Reticulum transport integration (code, not math)

**What:** Wire Reticulum's `Link` / `Interface` abstractions to `reticulumRoutingMatrix`.
The delay-decorrelation theorem assumes a `latencyMap: Map<(string*string), float>`.
In production this comes from Reticulum link quality measurements.

**Connection points:** Feeds into item 2 (information value needs real latency to price),
item 4 (Thousand Brains columns need a physical transport), and item 5 (settlement needs a network).

**Status:** Math is done (§A #18). Integration is wiring.

---

### 2. Information Value formalization

**What:** `InformationValue.fs` — the economic unit of the attention economy. The delta of
what's known vs. unknown (KL divergence between prior and posterior). Already implicit in
the attention router (KL × alignment). Needs to be named as a first-class primitive with
its own tests. Asks and bids would be denominated in information-value units.

**Connection points:** Feeds into item 5 (token economics denominated in IV), item 1 (IV depends
on latency for pricing), and item 4 (Thousand Brains columns "vote" with IV as the currency).

**Status:** Emergent from existing math. Needs formalization.

---

### 3. Futamura checkpoint / partial evaluator integration

**What:** PR #9330 shipped — "one mix, target knob (Code | Circuit), meaning provably invariant."
Connect the specializer to the society layer: specialize an agent's inference graph at compile
time based on its known memory graph topology. A partially-evaluated agent is faster because
its routing matrix is pre-computed for its known neighborhood.

**Connection points:** Feeds into item 1 (specialized routing for known Reticulum topology),
and item 4 (Thousand Brains columns can be specialized per-region).

**Status:** Futamura landed (Otto). Integration to society layer is future work.

---

### 4. Thousand Brains wiring

**What:** The writer-actor-routing model ("cells voting = thousand-brains lattice") is referenced
in the A-method note but isn't formalized as code. `LocalConsensus` is the seed — but the full
Thousand Brains picture has columns that vote on object identity, with lateral connections
propagating evidence. Maps directly to entangled-subgraph consensus.

**Connection points:** Feeds into #2 (columns vote with IV), #1 (lateral connections = Reticulum
links), #5 (column reputation = market participation history).

**Status:** Conceptual mapping exists. Needs formalization.

---

### 5. Web3 / token economics layer

**What:** `AskBidClearing` is a skeleton. The real attention economy needs: denominated units
(information-value tokens?), settlement mechanics, reputation accrual from successful market
participation, and chain targeting.

**Connection points:** Feeds into item 2 (IV as denomination), item 1 (settlement over Reticulum),
and item 4 (reputation from Thousand Brains column accuracy).

**Status:** Skeleton exists (§A #19). Needs economics design.
