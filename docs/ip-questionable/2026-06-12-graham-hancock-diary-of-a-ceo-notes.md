## Key passages (Aaron's ferry, preserved; timestamps approximate)

0:23 "I'm here to communicate about the possibility of a major forgotten episode in Human Story."
3:05 "That's why I refer to us as a species with amnesia. When I use that phrase, I need to give
credit to Emanuel Velikovsky who wrote a book called Mankind in Amnesia."
5:05 "This is the memory banks of our species. This is the record, the only record we have of a
period before 6,000 years ago. And we shouldn't despise it and scorn it as primitive
superstition. We should say, what can we find in here that we can coordinate with scientific
facts that we're aware of?"
5:39 Hamlet's Mill — Giorgio de Santillana (MIT, history of science) & Hertha von Dechend:
numbers + imagery encoded in world myths deriving from the precession of the equinoxes
(72; 25,920; 43,200).
7:02 The Younger Dryas impact hypothesis; 12,800 yrs BP; the black boundary layer (soot,
nanodiamonds, microspherules, platinum, iridium).
20:18 Definition of civilization: "the willing organization or the unwilling organization of
labor" — Göbekli Tepe (11,600 BP, hunter-gatherers, T-megaliths, astronomical alignments).
41:46 The Great Pyramid: 1:43,200 scale to the Earth's polar radius + equatorial circumference;
aligned to true north within 3 arc-minutes; latitude 30.
50:10 The abgallu (Sumer) / followers of Horus (Egypt): sages carrying pre-flood knowledge
through the cataclysm as advisers to the earliest historical kings; knowledge embedded in
stories + numbers.
31:04 "We're that lost civilization and we don't need a comet … if we're so psychically messed
up as a species, we'll probably end up doing it to ourselves."
1:51:02 "I don't ever want to hear the words 'trust the science.' The words for me are
'investigate the science' … One of the fundamental ethics of science is not to trust the
science, it's to question and challenge the science."
1:53:35 Books: The Sign and the Seal; Fingerprints of the Gods; Keeper of Genesis (w/ Bauval);
Underworld; Supernatural/Visionary.
