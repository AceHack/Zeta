## 1. The five claims, as the video states them

1. **Sugar, not meat, paid for encephalization.** The brain is ~2% of body mass and ~20% of
   metabolic rate — reportedly 60–70% in children. It runs almost entirely on **glucose**, and
   making glucose from protein is metabolically poor. So pre-fire hominins could not have funded
   brain growth on meat; the model points at fruit and honey, and at foraging tools. Claimed to
   resolve the **protein-poisoning** ceiling, and to fit the timing — expansion begins ~2.5 Mya while
   controlled fire arrives later.
2. **Human Accelerated Regions work by 3D folding, not by letters.** HARs are sequences conserved
   across mammals that were rapidly rewritten in the human line. The claim is that what changed is
   how the strand **folds in the nucleus**: insertions and deletions move enhancers next to different
   genes, so an element that regulated one system ends up adjacent to a neural one. Reportedly ~30%
   of HARs.
3. **A de novo gene from "junk."** A long non-coding RNA stopped acting only as a regulator and began
   producing a small protein, in a gene that appears after the split from chimpanzees and bonobos.
4. **What that protein does is delay.** It holds neural stem cells in an **immature** state so they
   divide more times before committing to neurons. Injected into mice, the cortex grows larger
   because development slows.
5. **The growth pathway is already at ceiling.** Epiregulin is present across mammals, off in mouse
   brain development, highly active in humans. Added to **gorilla** brain organoids it drives
   human-like expansion. Added to **human** organoids: *nothing*. That pathway is saturated in us.

Plus the framing claim tying them together: human brains stay **integrated and plastic into
adulthood**, where chimpanzee lobe-integration stops around adolescence — the "Peter Pan" reading.

## 3. Why it lands on the BNNs — and one mapping is much stronger than the rest

Aaron's note is *"directly relevant to our BNNs."* Four mappings, graded honestly.

### 3a. Neoteny is never-collapse, and this is the strong one

Findings 3–5 all say the same thing in different registers: **delay commitment and you get more
capacity.** The protein holds stem cells immature so they divide more before differentiating; the
integration study says human brains keep juvenile plasticity into adulthood; and the whole "Peter
Pan" reading is that *not maturing* is the mechanism, not a side effect.

That is `SoftValue`'s stated property, in wetware:

> a normalized distribution over candidates whose safety property is not *"always certain"* but
> *"always knows its uncertainty"* — `resolve` collapses only above a confidence threshold, and
> otherwise returns `None` (held).

And it is Aaron's own sharpening from 2026-08-21: *"we do everything to never collapse the uncertany
so this out of order holds, uncertany is what gives us commutivity."* A neuron that differentiates is
a distribution that resolved; a stem cell that keeps dividing is one still held. **The biology and
the type are making the same trade** — capacity now for commitment later — and in both cases the
thing that buys the capacity is *refusing to decide yet*.

This is a **structural rhyme, not a mechanism transfer.** Nobody has shown that cortical
progenitor dynamics and a Bayesian posterior share any formalism. What transfers is the design
intuition, which was already in the tree.

### 3b. A saturated pathway is ρ → 1, and the timing is uncomfortable

Finding 5 is the sharper surprise: more epiregulin grows a **gorilla** organoid and does **nothing**
to a human one. The pathway is not a dial you can keep turning; it is already at its stop.

That is the same shape as the measurement that failed on `main` yesterday
(`docs/research/2026-08-22-the-decorrelation-meter-left-its-band-...md`): `rhoFromUnionCoverage`
measures the fraction of the independence-gain that redundancy ate, and `SocietyUsefulWork`'s theorem

epiregulin to a human organoid.

Stated as a design consequence rather than a metaphor: **when a lever stops moving the output, the
honest reading is saturation, not insufficient application.** In the fleet that means more agents on
the same method buy less than the count suggests — which is exactly what ρ crossing its band is
claiming, and why widening the band would have been the wrong fix.

### 3c. HAR folding is structure-before-labels, from genomics

Finding 2 says function changed **without the sequence meaning changing** — the letters stayed
similar; the *spatial arrangement* moved an enhancer next to a different gene. Same tokens, different
topology, different behaviour.

That is the EVE-protocol ordering (`docs/research/2026-08-14-icons-before-symbols-eve-protocol-...`)
arriving from molecular biology: **the shape decides, and the labels resolve through it.** It is also
the V8 hidden-class point Aaron made — structure is the identity — and, less comfortably, a reminder
that *identical content in a different arrangement is a different system*, which is the case
`interfaces-free-classes-earned-under-rules` is built around.

### 3d. The energy bill is a metering fact

2% of mass, 20% of metabolism, ~65% in children. Whatever else the sugar model is, it is a claim that
**intelligence has a currency and the currency is specific** — glucose, not protein, because the
conversion is poor enough to be a real constraint.

That is the ΔU/metering economy's own premise (`every-bug-has-economic-value`, the
proof-of-useful-work line): capability is paid for, in a resource that can be measured, and the
substrate cannot pay in whatever it happens to have. Weak as an analogy, worth one line as a
reminder that the metering framing has a biological precedent rather than being a Zeta coinage.
