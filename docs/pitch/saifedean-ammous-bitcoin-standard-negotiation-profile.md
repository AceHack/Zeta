## The one-line hook

> Bitcoin's 21M is a number defended by human consensus. Agora's cap is the **Bekenstein
> bound** — the physical maximum information storable in mass-energy. You can change 21M
> with a hard fork; you cannot change Agora's cap without changing the laws of the
> universe. **This is hard money at the physical limit.**

## Why this lands with the sound-money thesis (honest reasons)

1. **Harder than 21M.** Bitcoin's cap is a *chosen* number — socially defended, but
   code-changeable in principle. Agora's cap = the Bekenstein bound (~10^75 bits in
   Earth's mass). Physics, not protocol. The "the cap is arbitrary" critique he'd never
   accept against gold, and that maximalists defend hard for BTC, is simply *closed* here.
2. **Non-debasable by construction.** Existing encryption-budgets are **permanent +
   non-revocable**; the only monetary lever is the *issuance rate*, never the existing
   stock. No dilution of holders. Pure low-time-preference reward — save your privacy and
   it compounds; nobody can inflate it away.
3. **Money that also does work.** The hard asset *is* privacy/encryption budget — a
   functional good, not only a store of value. Sound money whose unit has direct utility.
4. **Class alignment (the Bitcoin critique he'd respect).** Bitcoin accidentally created
   three economically-misaligned classes — miners, node-runners, holders. Misaligned
   classes become attack vectors: node-runners bear real cost with no issuance upside,
   and got the regulatory liability dumped on them (the node-operator-liability problem).
   Agora's design rule: **every class economically aligned, or it's a vulnerability.**
5. **Growth without debasement.** The only way to grow the supply is to add accessible
   mass — real cosmic work, never printing. Sound-money civilization that *grows by
   production, not inflation* — his low-time-preference→flourishing thesis at cosmic scale.

## The mechanism (substrate-honest)

- **Currency:** the encryption-budget — permanent, non-revocable; earned by contributing
  to the shared record.
- **Cap = physics:** Bekenstein bound on accessible mass-energy (not a code constant).
- **Mass is raw material; encryption is the economy:** mass is the feedstock; the
  encryption produced from it is the money that circulates.
- **No debasement, ever:** stock is fixed-per-holder; only issuance-rate is policy;
  growth requires real mass acquired (honest work).

## What this is NOT (so it stays credible to a hard-money rigorist)

- **Not** anti-Bitcoin — it's the hard-money principle at the physical limit + the
  class-alignment fix. Credit Bitcoin as the proof-of-concept.
- **Not** a claim the cap is reachable soon — ~10^75 is astronomically far; the point is
  the cap is *physical*, not that it binds today.
- **Not** a shitcoin-style "infinite supply, trust us" — supply is harder than BTC, not
  softer; this is the move that *out-hards* the hardest money, not undercuts it.
