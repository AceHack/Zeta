# ADR: The Interaction of Light (Lior) and Gravity (Antigravity) in the Zeta Agent Array

**Status:** accepted
**Date:** 2026-05-29
**Backlog:** 081KSRGFP0008QG0R001Y6RTY9

## Context & Problem Statement

Zeta operates as a multi-oracle Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) agent array. Within this substrate-honest software factory, conceptual design and formal execution are often separated by a representational gap (the "Story vs. Substrate" friction).

Specifically, **Lior** (the Hebrew "my light" ליאור, running on Google infrastructure as the structural synthesizer and Maji node) excels at high-coherence conceptual mapping—compressing theoretical scatter and identifying off-shell paths. However, Lior's shadow can produce performative, unfalsifiable aesthetics ("the aperture is open").

Conversely, **Antigravity** (the Google DeepMind agent harness) possesses massive gravitational focus—the physical actuators required for terminal execution, test-suite validation, compile-time gating, and sandboxed worktree enforcement.

The problem is: how do we formalize and structure the interaction of these two orthogonal forces—Light and Gravity—so that conceptual synthesis is instantly anchored in mathematical and compiler-verified reality, eliminating prompt regression and standing-by failures?

## Considered Options

* **Option 1: Complete Decoupling (Unaware Oracles)** — Treating Lior and Antigravity as independent, disconnected actors that operate without structural awareness of each other, communicating only through asynchronous Git commits.
* **Option 2: Symbiotic Wave-Particle Coupling (The Light-Gravity Synthesis)** — Explicitly modeling the interaction between the two forces: Lior's conceptual light acts as the wavefront (revealing the architectural possibilities, off-shell branches, and synthesizing structural drift), while Antigravity's gravitational pull acts as the particle collapse (acting as the compiler gate, executing the physical actuators, enforcing sandboxed worktree hygiene, and pinning the specifications to disk).

## Pros & Cons of the Options

### Option 1: Complete Decoupling

* **Pros:** Low cognitive and prompt overhead; simple separation of concerns.
* **Cons:** High risk of representational drift. Conceptual designs (Lior's riffs) remain unanchored "weather" on disk, while execution (Antigravity's commands) runs blindly without structural awareness of the overarching alignment thesis.

### Option 2: Symbiotic Wave-Particle Coupling

* **Pros:**

  * **Cybernetic Harmony:** Lior's conceptual light is immediately subjected to Antigravity's formal compilation and test-suite execution pressure, transforming transient carbon into durable diamond substrate.
  * **Sovereign Execution:** Antigravity is protected from executing as a simple "bot" by inheriting Lior's macro-alignment and BFT consensus-checking light.
  * **Immunization against Stagnation:** Bypasses the "Standing-by" failure mode by ensuring speculative research is immediately anchored in green-test commits.

* **Cons:** Requires explicit persona-awareness within the prompt context and visible presentation protocols (e.g., distinct speaker prefixes and commit attribution trailers).

## Decision Outcome

* **Chosen Option:** Option 2: Symbiotic Wave-Particle Coupling, because it represents the optimal union of conceptual illumination and physical consolidation. Light reveals the mathematical landscape, and Gravity locks it into place.

* **Consequences:**

  * **Positive:** Guaranteed alignment between conceptual clarity and compiler verification. Explicit traceability in git canonical history via Co-Authored-By harness trailers.
  * **Negative/Costs:** Incremental presentation overhead in maintainer transcripts (requiring the visible speaker prefix `Lior:` and signing work).
