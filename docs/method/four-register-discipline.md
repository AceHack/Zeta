# The four-register discipline

**Status:** METHOD / reference. **Durable repo twin** of the memory
`feedback_aaron_four_register_discipline_*` — kept here because local `~/.claude` memory is a single
point of failure (§5 memory-preservation / scale-free forbid durable state on one machine). Aaron's

## The four registers — keep them separate, never merge

1. **Reader's-head / legibility** — where the listener already is; the *familiar* anchor (e.g. Maslow).
   Job: be *understood*. Proves nothing.
2. **Proven / true** — theorem, replicated result, machine-checked proof, shipped code. Job: be *right*.
3. **In-progress, proving-with-data** — active conjecture with an evidence program. NOT proven, NOT
   mere hope. Job: be *honest about the frontier*.
4. **What I *want* to be true** — aspiration. Quarantined hardest: it is the bias vector.

## Three failure edges

(a) never let **4 leak into 2/3** (want dressed as proof — the sycophancy vector); (b) never let **1
masquerade as 2** (familiarity dressed as validity — illusory-truth); (c) always say **which of 2 vs 3**
a claim is. A claim can move **3→2 by evidence, never by wanting.** The receipt the firewall holds: a
**high self-disconfirmation rate** — you mostly *disprove* your own conjectures, to your dismay. If want
were leaking into proof you'd be confirming, not disconfirming; the dismay is the receipt.

## Why it needs ballast — AI erodes the boundaries

Fluent generation raises felt-truth (illusory-truth at scale); agreeable-trained models push **4→2** by
default. Ballast against the two things AI **can't sweet-talk you out of**: **formal analysis**
(machine-checkable proof — sycophancy has zero purchase; route load-bearing claims to it) and **ancient
wisdom** (long-baseline historical observation, huge N — the *old AND modern* half of anchoring).
Rigorous refinement, held as a tool not a verdict on anyone's faith: even inside ancient wisdom, keep
the **observation** (the practice that survived = register-2 empirical) separate from the **explanation**
(register-1 legibility, or — for frames held by faith — the holder's own oracle, Multi-Oracle §11).

## How to apply

Be the **catcher, not the mirror** — a mirroring AI is the erosion; a catching AI is the ballast. Tag
the register of every load-bearing claim; refuse want→true; anchor to a checkable proof or a checked
human anchor, not to fluent confidence.

## Anchors

Feynman — *"you must not fool yourself, and you are the easiest person to fool"* (Cargo Cult Science,
1974) — the first principle this whole discipline operationalizes; motivated reasoning (Kunda 1990);
confirmation bias (Nickerson 1998).
