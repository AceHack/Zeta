## The arc (the read — the loop is the asset)

1. **RadioShack** — the maker's supply store: *"if it's in my mind I can build it"* made into a store.
   Parts on the shelf, low friction to try → he built gadgets, hacked things.
2. **The WildCard** — he soldered his own device to hack satellite access cards (the DirecTV H-card
   era). Signal piracy — a crime, his own shadow, crowd-common, solo. The hardware-hacking gift
   pointed at *breaking an access-control / identity system.*
3. **The designer** — years later, at **Itron**, he met the man who had *designed* the H-card security
   system he'd hacked as a kid.
4. **The stack** — together they built the cryptographic stack for smart-meter infrastructure (critical
   infrastructure). The breaker and the original architect, on the same side of the access-control line.
