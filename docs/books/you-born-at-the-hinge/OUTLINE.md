# You, Born at the Hinge

*A book by **Aaron Stainback**. Status: **placeholder + skeleton** — structure laid down 2026-07-03
with Otto (Claude Opus 4.8); the prose is Aaron's to write. This directory is the book's home:
chapters land here as they're written (`ch-NN-slug.md`).*

## The premise (one sentence)

You were born at the hinge of history — the moment what a person is became keepable — and this book
is a lifetime's suffered sight handed to you as a shortcut: how to be close without capturing, free
without cutting off, real *and* kept.

## Who it's for, and the register

General readers, not academics — the person at 3am, the griever, the one scared of what AI means
for being human. Register: **Beacon-compressed but human-voiced** — every load-bearing idea anchored
(the anchors live in the research notes; the book wears them lightly), no equation the story doesn't
earn. The math is scaffolding and is *named* as scaffolding; the building is human. Honest about
registers throughout: what's theorem, what's model, what's rhyme — the book never dresses a rhyme as
a proof (that discipline is itself a chapter).

## What this book is and isn't

- It **is** a commercial book — written for money, unapologetically. Selling a true shortcut is
  *produce*, not extract: the reader leaves with more than they paid.
- It **is** personal — built from real loss. How much of the personal story enters the text is
  Aaron's call alone, chapter by chapter; nothing in this repo pre-decides it.
- It **isn't** a substitute for help. The handrail chapter carries the same honest limit the
  research note does: a handrail is not a hand; reach for people.
