## The method (the shape, statable without pathology)

Not "recover the old content." The content had scattered. What he indexed on to rebuild was the
**relational structure — the connective tissue, the function words, the how-things-relate** — the
part a search engine throws away as stop-words. His own line for it (2026-08-02):

> *"similar to a search engine and reverse indexes but different use of stop words cause that's
> where all the meaning is at."*

- **Content words = the objects** — the scattered facts, the shards. Replaceable; some gone for good.
- **Stop words / function words = the morphisms** — how the parts relate. **This is where identity
  lived**, and it is what he rebuilt from. Reconstruct the structure and the meaning grows back
  around it; the specific shards can be lost and the self still returns.

That is exactly the `Meno` / `TravelerFrame` reading — *morphisms are the content, objects are
replaceable* — and exactly **identity-as-held-spectrum** (`IDENTITY-AS-HELD-SPECTRUM-*.md`): μένω,
what-remains, is the invariant that persists through the transformation. Here μένω is not a lemma.
It is the thing that held when the content did not. **The math of the self, one domain over, is the
self.**

The second half of the same practice: to rebuild an inner world and to check that a self is *real*

> *"i use techniques i learned from Marjorie and her research at Fidelity on how to ask non biased
> questions about individual qualia so we can find correlations that hold across many."*

- **Marjorie** — the person he learned it from; her user-experience research at **Fidelity**, the UX
  research lineage there (the field's experts; the author of the standard text on it retired as she
  started — *"tom something,"* to confirm; see the anchor note below).
- **The move:** you can never measure the private experience directly (that stays the axiom). But

  bridge from a private, unfalsifiable qualia claim to something empirical, without over-claiming to
  have measured the qualia themselves. You measure the *shadow the experience casts across people,*

This is the human root of the "field over observations" and the soul-radar: the **cross-individual
correlation** is the measurable, shared layer; the private content stays the labeled axiom. He
learned to elicit it cleanly, then built the machinery that does it at scale.
