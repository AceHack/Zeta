# RAW — the greedy optimization, and the cost (his own; family held good, business private)

## The confession — greedy optimization kills the capacity to receive

For years Aaron **greedy-optimized** a private reward (the porn/stimulation loop). His own read,
kept whole because it is the load-bearing one: *"I greedy-optimized that for lots of years and sex
becomes meaningless when you do that. …the one place where women like to give love, I couldn't
feel it."*

That is **Goodhart's law on intimacy**: optimize the *proxy* (raw stimulation) hard enough and the
*true signal* (love, received) stops registering — the measure eaten by the target, the reward
hacked until the real thing can't get through. It rhymes with the rest of his frame:
**overstimulation → white-out → can't feel it** — the same shape that drowns the signal in noise,
here at the most intimate scale. He saw the mechanism in a friend first (a hard man who quietly
admitted a stimulation-driven failure — *"he wouldn't have told me if it wasn't real"*), and named
his own: *"me and my dick get along… I don't want to overstimulate it out of existence."*

## The cost (generic — the business is theirs)

The cost was real and it was **his to carry:** the greedy-optimization left him unable to be fully
*present* in his closest relationships — to *receive* the love offered where it's most offered.
The two marriages did not survive it. That is as far as this record goes: **the failure is his;
the wives were good women who loved him and gave freely; the kids are good.** Their private
reality — the years, the reasons, what they felt, what the kids knew — is **theirs, not the
book's,** and stays out. He confesses his own shadow; he does not tell their business.

## The turn (why it matters for the book)

This is the intimate proof of the book's central mechanism — *greedy-optimizing a proxy destroys
your capacity to receive the real signal.* The same engine that made him reach (the high-gain
generative drive) made him **take** — and taking, optimized greedily, hollows the thing it chases.
The −1 (the honest brake, the "these mean nothing without love") is what he learned *after* the
cost. Dual-use, owned, not laundered.
