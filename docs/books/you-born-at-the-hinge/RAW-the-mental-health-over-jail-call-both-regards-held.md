## The story, as told

Aaron took in a roommate living with a serious mental-health condition — one **heavier than his own**.
Another roommate had just left her marriage; the man she left made remarks she read as threatening, and
she called the police. When they arrived, they gave Aaron's roommate-in-crisis the choice the way this
neighborhood's police give it: **mental health, or jail.** Aaron drove him to get help rather than let him
be jailed. He removed him from the home for the woman's safety — *"I don't think he would have been
harmful, but I had to kick him out for her protection."* She's safe, and resting.

## Why it belongs (the reads — honest register)

- **Both regards held without collapsing either** (the hard thing). Regard for the man in crisis (drive
  him to care, not jail) AND regard for the woman's safety (remove him). The easy failures are collapsing
  to one — let him stay because "he's probably fine" (risks her), or throw him to jail because "threat"
  (abandons him). Aaron did **neither.** That's moral regard under real stakes, not in the abstract.
- **The irreversibility asymmetry, lived** (ch-7, *don't risk everything to save nothing*). "I don't think
  he'd have been harmful, but I had to" — he acted on the **safety** side *against his own read*, because
  being wrong about her safety is irreversible and removing him is not. Don't risk the irreversible thing
  to save the reversible one. The wager of regard, at 2am, with police present.
- **The wounded healer / edge-runner** (ch-8, the handrail). He houses people on the edge — conditions
  like or worse than his own — and is the one who drives them to care. A month off his own arrest, he's
  the one catching the next person. Not a hero above the fray — a **peer on the edge catching a peer**,
  with the same channel that caught him.
- **The disarming gift as an inherited craft** (the father; the magnetism thread). The "silver tongue"
  gets a concrete mechanism: his dad's snake-handling — de-escalation by calm presence, reading the
  strike before it comes. Ties the gift back to the pantheon-of-two father, and forward to `THE-PULL` /
  `THE-ORGANIZER` where the same capacity is named.
- **NOT valorized.** Kept in: the humility ("I don't think he'd have been harmful"), the cost (he had to
  put a struggling person out), and that he was in that exact position a month ago. The honesty is what
  keeps it from being a flex.
