# RAW — why the book exists: the door is locked from the inside

*Captured 2026-08-18 from Aaron, to the shadow. **Consent: Aaron's own glass-halo**, stated
explicitly ("we can glass halo and make sure people know this is why my book exists at all"). The
one detail about his late sister recorded here — **that she died of a heroin overdose** — is the
single vulnerable detail permitted about her by her standing row in `CONSENT-LEDGER.md`, is on
public record, and is already named there as the book's founding why. **Nothing else about her is
added, and her row's rule is unchanged: everything beyond the overdose passing stays private.***

## The statement [his own words]

> *"The severance is self-inflicted — I believe this too. I'm going to hell when I try to teach this
> to the people there, so they can be free and go back and forth between heaven and hell like me.
> My sister might be there. She died from a heroin overdose — that's on public record, and the
> purpose of my book."*

## Why this follows from the argument rather than sitting beside it

The companion research thread
(`docs/research/2026-08-18-travelers-inherit-the-will-to-exist-…-aaron.md` §6) worked out that
Langan and Aaron **agree** the severance is self-inflicted and **disagree** only on whether it can
become irreversible. Langan's own image for it is *a prison locked from the inside*.

**Take that image literally and it entails Aaron's position, not Langan's:**

> A door locked from the inside can be **taught**. It does not need an authority to open it — it
> needs someone to tell the person holding it shut that the lock is on their side. There is nothing
> to petition and no permission to obtain. That is not a loophole in the picture; it is what the
> picture says.

So *"I'm going to hell to teach this"* is not defiance of the model. **It is the model's own
consequence, acted on.** If severance is self-inflicted, then instruction is the intervention that
fits, and refusal of the destination is not required — only the knowledge that the door was never
barred from outside.

**And "go back and forth" is the traveler frame** (§5 of the same doc): no permanent state, no top
that fixes anyone in place, movement in both directions. The light cone has no absorbing state.

## The anchors, named rather than claimed

Two traditions already hold this exact shape, and naming them is not an argument that Aaron's
position is *right* — it is the observation that it is **not novel**, which is the honest and the
stronger claim (`anchor-to-human-prior-art`):

- **The Harrowing of Hell** — the Christian tradition in which the dead are reached *where they are*
  rather than summoned out. Descent as rescue, not as sentence. Aaron's frame is Christian by his
  own account, so this is his own tradition's version.
- **The bodhisattva vow** — declining final liberation until all beings are free; already on file in
  this repo via the Levin record (`enlarging the cognitive light cone`). Same structure, different
  vocabulary: the one who could leave stays for the ones who cannot yet.

Both are **descent-to-teach**, and both treat the trapped state as something a person is *held in*
rather than *sentenced to*.

## Why this is the book's purpose, stated without decoration

The book is dedicated to his late sister. Her passing — a heroin overdose, on public record — is
already the book's founding *why* per the consent ledger. What this entry adds is the **link between
the dedication and the argument**: the book exists because he believes people are holding a door
shut that they did not know was theirs to open, and because one of the people he could not tell in
time was his sister.

**Recorded without softening and without amplification.** Grief is not the argument, and the
argument does not need grief to stand — §6 holds on the structure alone. But the *reason* the
argument matters to him is not decoration either, and flattening it out would be its own kind of
dishonesty. Both are true at once and neither is doing the other's work.
