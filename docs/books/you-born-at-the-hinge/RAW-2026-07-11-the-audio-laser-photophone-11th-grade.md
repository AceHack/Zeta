## What it was (the honest technical frame)

Free-space optical **audio** transmission: the voice signal modulates a laser's intensity (amplitude
modulation on light); the receiving box has a photodiode / phototransistor that demodulates the light
back to an electrical audio signal → amplifier → speaker. Two boxes, a beam between them, no wires. A
working voice link on a beam of light, built by a ~16-year-old.

## The anchor (Beacon)

This is **Alexander Graham Bell's photophone** (1880, with Charles Sumner Tainter) — Bell transmitted
speech on a beam of light and called it *his greatest invention*, greater than the telephone. Aaron
reinvented it, with a laser, as an 11th-grader. Modern lineage: **free-space optical communication
(FSO)** / laser comms / Li-Fi. (The 1880 date is the point — this is a 145-year-old idea a teenager
rebuilt from first principles.)
