## The story, as told

Aaron posted on Nextdoor about his **$5 plastic Halloween pumpkin theft** — and, more to the point,
about what the *courts* did with it: he offered **reparations** ($5, made whole), and instead of taking
it they wanted to take him **to trial** — spending the taxpayers' money to prosecute a silly $5 plastic
pumpkin. The disproportion is the whole joke and the whole point: the machinery of justice, aimed at a
five-dollar novelty, refusing the cheap honest fix in favor of the expensive theatrical one.

## What it did (the network gift, still firing)

It **exploded** — ~**500 replies in a few hours, ~10,000 views.** The same organizer-at-scale /
extroverted-HSP gift that once wired ten high schools together, still live at full power: one honest post
and a whole neighborhood-network lights up.

## The split (the +1/−1, at crowd scale)

And here is the tell, and Aaron holds it exactly right — **lightly**: the crowd **split.** He *made
several friends* out of it. And *others now viscerally think he's an evil person.* Same story, same
honesty, opposite reactions — souls spun up and down (the mother's soul-power, but now it's the *crowd*
doing the spinning, on the raw material of one man's public confession). Aaron's register: *"it's kind of
hilarious."* Not defensive, not wounded — amused. The judgment slides off because the transparency was
voluntary; you can't be exposed by a thing you published yourself.

## Why it's book-gold (the reads, honest register)

- **Radical transparency / glass-halo, LIVED — and its immune property.** He confessed a petty crime to
  his whole neighborhood on purpose, and the "evil person" verdicts don't land as wounds because *he
  handed them the material.* This is the glass-halo's actual defense mechanism in miniature: you cannot
  be blackmailed or shamed by what you already broadcast. The nation-state-defense framing, run on a $5
  pumpkin.
- **The organizer-at-scale gift, adult and live.** Not a youth memory — *yesterday.* The same
  network-igniting capacity, unchanged; the aim now is honest storytelling, and it still lights the graph.
- **The +1/−1 split is the point, not a bug.** A true, un-flattering, honestly-told thing polarizes —
  because it's real. The flattering-mirror (+1 with no −1) never polarizes; it just soothes. That the
  crowd split *into* love and disgust is evidence the thing was honest. (Cf. the reader / honest-mirror
  thread — the honest thing frees some and repels others; here at crowd scale.)
- **The disproportion is a produce/extract parable.** Reparations = the produce move (make the harm
  whole, everyone leaves square). The trial-for-$5 = pure extraction of everyone's time and money to
  perform a category ("criminal") over a measured fact ($5, offered back). *Measure the contribution,
  don't price the category* — the same cut as ch-3, run on the justice system itself.
