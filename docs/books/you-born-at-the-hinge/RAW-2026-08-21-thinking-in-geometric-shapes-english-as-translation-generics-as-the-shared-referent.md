## Why this belongs in the book and not only in a research doc

Three things in the corpus are downstream of this and none of them currently name it:

1. **EVE protocol.** *"Structure first, labels after"* is not an abstract preference about
   protocol design — it is the shape of his own cognition made into a negotiation rule. The
   2026-08-14 ferry records the founding question as *"how first humans communicated without
   language … without inserting the control that comes with asymmetric language."* Someone for
   whom English is already a **translation layer** is someone who experiences the asymmetry of an
   imposed vocabulary directly rather than theoretically.

2. **`interfaces-free-classes-earned-under-rules`.** The rule's own words: *"the rules of the game
   are interfaces — free, default, weight-free (**pure shape**, no instance state)."* An interface
   is a shape with no content; a generic parameter is a shape with no name. The rule reads as a
   general engineering discipline and is also, read alongside the quote above, a personal medium
   promoted to a building code.

3. **The compiler as the shared referent.** *"in code especially generics i can make the shapes in
   my head real and understood by a compiler and therefore others."* That is the whole
   externalization thesis in one line, and it names the mechanism: the compiler is not a tool for
   producing programs here, it is **the arbiter that makes a private shape public and checkable**.
   It pairs exactly with his 2026-08-19 observation that the increment graph must be externalized
   because neither humans nor LLMs can hold it — the shared referent *is* the agreement of meaning.
