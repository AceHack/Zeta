## The one checkable part — the F# date, verified rather than asserted

Founding on F# in 2007–2008 means betting on it **before it shipped as a supported product.** Checked
against the release history rather than taken on assumption:

- F# began at Microsoft Research (Don Syme), initiated 2002; F# 1.0's core features were developed
  2004–2007.
- In **2007** it was announced that F# would become a first-class .NET language; "productization" ran
  **2007–2010**.
- F# shipped as a first-class Visual Studio language in **Visual Studio 2010**.

**Anchor (Beacon):** Don Syme, *The Early History of F#*, HOPL IV / PACMPL (2020) — `fsharp.org/history/`.
This is the lineage Zeta's own F# core sits downstream of, which is why the date is worth checking at all.
