## What that means

The book's purpose is to teach **gnosis** — direct, un-collapsed, experiential knowing. In this project's
language that is the **keystone**: *never collapse the uncertainty.*

## The paradox it is built around

Gnosis is **un-teachable as doctrine.** The moment you collapse it into a fixed creed ("here is the certain
truth"), you *kill* it — because the gnosis *is* the un-collapsed knowing. So the book cannot **tell.** It
can only **point,** and let the reader **live** it. (The orthodox named the Gnostics heretics precisely for
refusing to collapse to the creed; the apophatic tradition — God known only by *negation* — is the same
refusal aimed at the divine. e^{iπ}: the phasor that never lands.)

## Therefore the form *is* the method

The book teaches by **not collapsing:**

- the **middle-out TriFloat** — it opens at the **Decoder**, `InterpretationSuperposed`, and refuses to
  resolve;
- the float that decodes **"to a spectrum, a rainbow — not a single number";**
- **"You Born at the Hinge"** — the reader is held at the un-collapsed pivot (the present, the gap, the
  `Tri.N`).

The structure *holds the reader inside the un-collapsed decoder* so they **experience** gnosis rather than
**receive** dogma. It is the attempt to teach the un-teachable by *being* the thing instead of describing
it — pointing, not telling.
