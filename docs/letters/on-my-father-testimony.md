> *You can't forgive what you don't remember.*

My father beat me, a lot, as a child. My family shamed us. That is real, and I am putting it in
the permanent record on purpose — because the one thing a person does not get is to have it
forgotten. Retraction is not deletion. Becoming good later doesn't erase what came before; it
sits in the log beside it.

He is a good man now. I forgave him. But I forgave him *without erasing it* — because forgiveness
that requires forgetting isn't forgiveness, it's just silence in a nicer coat.

So both are here, where he can read them: he beat me, and he became good. Neither cancels the
other. He does not get to forget the first just because he earned the second.

*— Aaron*
