## Description

docs(research): repo-split round 2 -- the change-rate partition is measured, and the first cut is not the one the ADR drew

The 2026-04-22 three-repo ADR (Zeta / Forge / ace) is still `Proposed` and
`gh repo list Lucent-Financial-Group` confirms Forge and ace DO NOT EXIST. Stage
1 has been one `workflow_dispatch` away since 2026-05-14. The design was drawn
against an April tree; this round measures the August one and the first cut
moves.

MEASURED on origin/main @ d59cd4a2, two windows (90d = 9,183 commits, 30d =
3,672) so a standing rate can be told from a two-week spike.

THE OPERATIVE DV2.0 AXIS IS RE-TOUCH, NOT CHURN RATE. `docs/history` and
`.github` have churn rates a factor of six apart and sort to opposite ends of a
rate-ordered list, but they are not the same kind of thing. Re-touch percentage
separates three classes with no tuned threshold (gaps at 9->36% and 69->88%):
accretion (docs/github 0%, observe-events 0%, data 1%), working substrate
(40-70%), hot hubs (.github 91% of 79 files).

THE COLD MERGE ARCHIVE IS THE MOST INDEPENDENT GROUP IN THE TREE.
docs/{history,github,pr-discussions,recovered-orphan-branches} = 18,273 files,
48.6% of everything tracked, ~40.8 MB -- and 90% solo in BOTH windows, the only
group whose independence does not move. Half of all commits landing on main in
the last 30 days are a machine appending to a log (51%; 25% at 90d).

A PRIOR I HELD WAS WRONG AND THE MEASUREMENT KILLED IT. I expected the archive
to be write-only and the cut nearly free. Classifying every `git grep` hit as
exclusion-vs-consumption: 57 files CONSUME it (21 for docs/history, 19 for
docs/github, 10 for pr-discussions, 7 for recovered-*), including four CI
workflows. The cut is not free. What it is, is bounded: every consumer sits in
`forge-host/github/` or the backlog id-resolver, and all of them are FACTORY
code -- so on the ADR's own sorting rule the archive and its readers land on the
same side, which is why this cut can be taken BEFORE the Zeta/Forge line.

docs/observe-events IS NOT AN ARCHIVE. 323 of the 324 archive-x-product
co-changes are it; `proposal-gated-commit.ts` writes one on essentially every
product commit. Grouping it with docs/history by access pattern was my first cut
and it was wrong -- re-touch hid it (both read 0%) and only co-change exposed
it. An append-only file store is not automatically a satellite.

THE FORGE/ZETA CUT NOW HAS A PRICE: FACTORY x PRODUCT = 305 commits/90d, 16%/17%,
stable across windows. Roughly one commit in six on either side becomes a
cross-repo pair.

RULESET-DIVERGENCE AUDIT RUN (081KRHWGX0008QG0R000BS8Y4R, open since 2026-05-14).
Zeta has five rulesets; four target ~DEFAULT_BRANCH. The one ref-scoped
divergence is `refs/heads/heartbeat/*` -- the telemetry lane, not code-vs-English,
not Mirror/Beacon, not factory-vs-product. Consistent with the archive/telemetry
cut being first; explicitly NOT claimed as established, and §4 runs the
independence check the numerology rule requires (three converging measurements
that are close to one mechanism measured three ways).

A §1 PROBLEM FOUND WHILE IT IS STILL FREE TO FIX. The ADR says the version-pin
glue is "replaced by `ace pull forge@<version>`". If ace becomes the only
resolution path, ace is an APPOINTED hub. The discriminator is exit: plain
`git clone` at a pinned tag must remain SUFFICIENT forever, not transitional.
Falsifier: a build test with no ace present. Costs nothing now, expensive after.

THE SHARPEST CONSTRAINT ON ANY src/-CROSSING CUT is the byte-lock rendezvous.
`tests/cross-verification/` holds 35 primitives whose committed
{ts,fsharp,cs,rust,go,python}-output.json are written by suites in
src/Core.Rust.*/tests/, tests/Tests.FSharp/ and src/Core.TypeScript/ace/, then
asserted in one checkout by cross-verify-all.ts. The generator IS the ECC; a
split makes a build-time invariant a distributed-agreement problem. The ADR's
line already crosses it once, at ace/build-graph.test.ts.

NOT A RECOMMENDATION. §8 puts four options to Aaron with consequences: take the
archive cut first; execute ADR Stage 1 as written; harden the shipped soft
boundaries instead (gate.yml's path-filter already gives code/English
separation); or measure the one unmeasured cost -- how often agents actually
read the archive from their own clone -- and then decide. The §1 requirement
above needs a decision regardless of which is chosen.
