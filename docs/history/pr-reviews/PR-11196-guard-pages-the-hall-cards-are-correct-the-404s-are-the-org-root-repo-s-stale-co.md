## Description

guard(pages): the hall cards are correct — the 404s are the org-root repo's stale copy

Reported 2026-08-14: the hall's parent-relative cards (../data/monitor.html,
../demo/, ../genesis/, ../inventory/) 404. They do — but not on Zeta's site.

Measured, both origins:

  200  /Zeta/hall/            200  /Zeta/{demo,genesis,inventory}/index.html
                              200  /Zeta/data/monitor.html
  200  /hall/                 404  /{demo,genesis,inventory}/index.html
                              404  /data/monitor.html

lucent-financial-group.github.io/hall/ is served by a SEPARATE repo
(Lucent-Financial-Group/lucent-financial-group.github.io) that holds its own
stale copy of this page — 8037 bytes vs our 11201, still missing the Tick
Monitor card added since. That repo has no demo/, genesis/ or inventory/ for
../ to reach, so the same hrefs that resolve under /Zeta/ dangle there.
pages-deploy.yml publishes only to the project site; Zeta never writes to it.

So repointing the hrefs would have broken the working site to paper over
another repo. The fix belongs in that repo (refresh or drop its hall/ copy)
and is not attempted here — no cross-repo change.

What this commit does instead is pin the invariant so it cannot be "repaired"
by mistake:

- audit-dist-internal-links.ts — every internal href/src in the built artifact
  must resolve. Base-path aware: the site is served at /Zeta/, so /Zeta/x maps
  to dist/x and a bare /x escapes to the org root (the exact confusion above)
  and is reported. Skips source-not-served subtrees (design-tool exports, vite
  _src/, book language templates that pages-deploy.yml copies elsewhere).
- Wired into pages-deploy.yml AFTER the book copy, where the language pages sit
  at their served location: 156 links / 30 pages, versus 57 / 19 before it.
- A comment at the cards themselves recording why they are right.

Non-vacuity: nine mutants, all killed (13 tests). Two initially SURVIVED and
the tests were strengthened until they died — the <style> strip turned out to
be unfalsifiable for an href=/src= regex and was removed rather than shipped as
an untestable branch, and a combined "?v=2#frag" fixture had let a mutant that
stopped stripping "#" survive on the "?" split alone.

Limits, stated: this does not detect the org-root breakage (Zeta never builds
that site) and checks existence, not fragments or rendering.
