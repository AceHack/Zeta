## The crux, and it resolves in two parts

A join is many-to-one and reversible circuits cannot lose information, so the row's real question was never gate wiring — it was whether Bennett's uncompute closes.

**It does.** For a **sorted-merge equi-join kernel**, compare / multiply / accumulate each return every ancilla to zero, at ~2× depth, ~2× area, 1× throughput when pipelined. Steady-state ancilla is **O(W² + K) per lane and constant in N** (≈ 1 121 bits at W=32/K=32; ≈ 129 bits row-at-a-time). Uncompute is not an optimization here — it is what makes the design exist: without it ancilla is O(N·W³) and unbounded.

Z-set cancellation earns something real and narrow: a +1/−1 pair leaves the accumulator at its allocation value, so the zero-drop costs **zero uncompute work** where an irreversible design must write-then-clear.

**The scoping is itself a result.** `ZSet.join`'s `sortAndConsolidate` uncomputes only if the sort permutation is retained — O(n log²n) decision bits, ≈13 MB at n=2²⁰ against 3.7 Mbit of ECP5 BRAM. That is the one place the ancilla budget genuinely breaks, and requiring sorted inputs is what removes it. Stated as a precondition, not hidden.

## And the part that changes what happens next

An FPGA's core supply is a **DC rail**. Every LUT output node dissipates ~CV² regardless of whether its function is invertible; charge recovery needs a ramped supply no bitstream can create. Logical reversibility removes the kT·ln2 term (2.871×10⁻²¹ J/bit) and leaves the switching term (~10⁻¹⁴ J/transition) untouched — and the reversible path has strictly **more** transitions, because uncompute is a second pass.

So it will measure ~2× **worse**, and that is CMOS physics behaving correctly, not a negative result about Landauer.

`081KR50HA0008QG0R002Z51PMR` is therefore **not well-posed**, on three specific points, each corrected in the row (original text preserved):

- it treats Toffoli overhead as a *competitor* to a saving that is ~10⁸× smaller and, on a DC rail, absent;
- *"if reversible < irreversible: Landauer saving is measurable"* licenses an inference that is not available — a lower reading is a switching-activity difference. This is the accounting-vs-measurement conflation `key-erasure-meter.ts` exists to make untypeable, appearing in a backlog row;
- XADC measures **voltage**, not power (0.73 mV resolution), and is 7-series-only against the ECP5 target.

**Rescoped** to the question the hardware can answer: measure `R = E_op(REV)/E_op(IRR)` by a three-bitstream differential (REV/IRR/**NUL** common-mode) with a shunt on the isolated V_core rail and the slope method, then report the charge-recovery efficiency a custom substrate would need to pay it back — `η > 1 − 1/R`. That converts a measurement which cannot see Landauer into a hard, checkable requirement on a silicon program, and it never puts a measured number next to kT·ln2 implying proximity. With money attached that stops being a hygiene issue.

Travelling honesty clause: adiabatic recovery reduces CV² and does **not** approach the Landauer floor either. The floor is the asymptote of electronic computing, not a near-term target for any substrate we could build.

## Incidental, CHECKED, load-bearing

The two `Landauer accounting reports zero erased bits` properties **cannot fail**. `erasedWireCount` counts wire ids dropped from the wire map, but the interpreter ends `Map.add step.Target target` and `Map.add` never removes a key — so the key set is invariant under every possible gate sequence.

Mutation-verified: deleting **every gate** leaves both assertions satisfied.

```
REAL circuit   : Ancilla=true erased(i,f)=0 erased(f,r)=0 erased(i,r)=0
GATES DELETED  : Ancilla=true erased(i,f)=0 erased(f,r)=0 erased(i,r)=0
```

Closed row `081KRA5AR0008QG0R000CYY9ZN` discharged that criterion by its **allocator**, not by evidence — and the allocator (a fresh wire per value; measured **34 948 wires / 68 610 gates at W=32**, Θ(W³), linear in N with zero reuse ⇒ ~3.5×10¹⁰ wires at N=10⁶) is exactly what hardware cannot adopt. Filed as `081M05M1R97087G0R0023Z4F9D` rather than fixed here; the design row is pointed at it as a pre-RTL gate.

## Also

- **Target correction**: ECP5/ULX3S via Yosys/nextpnr, not Artix-7 — the open bitstream *is* the reproducibility story, matching `docs/inventory/hardware-to-buy.md`.
- **The single most important implementation note**: Yosys will const-fold the entire uncompute network away, silently turning REV into IRR while appearing to work. Guard is an observable ancilla-zero checksum, which does three jobs at once — prevents removal, proves per-op that uncompute closed, and is third-party verifiable. Acceptance gate: REV LUT count ≈ 2× IRR, else the run is void.
- **Control fairness cuts both ways**: `use_dsp="no"` on *both* designs, or the comparison is macro-vs-fabric. The one asymmetry (`(* keep *)` on ancilla) handicaps the subject, not the control.
- **Prior-art search done** (row-mandated). Finding: the "low-power reversible logic on FPGA" literature reports savings that cannot mean what they appear to. We should not join it, and should say why we disagree.
