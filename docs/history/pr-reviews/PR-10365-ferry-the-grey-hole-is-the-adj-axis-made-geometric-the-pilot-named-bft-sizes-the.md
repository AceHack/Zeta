## The grey hole is the `Adj` axis made geometric

- **Black hole** — information in, nothing out. Irreversible erasure: the **non-`Adj`** operation, the one Landauer charges `kT·ln2` per bit for.
- **White hole** — the time-reverse. The formal adjoint.
- **Grey hole** — both directions, under control. **That is exactly `Adj`**: the operation that *has* an inverse and is never charged.

So "grey hole" isn't a softened black hole — it's the **reversible object**, the geometric name for *never erase; keep the history and emit the antiparticle*. A black hole is what a system becomes when it **commits**; a grey hole is what it stays while it can still **retract**.

*"Not to destroy everyone with an explosion in information space"* names the other-side failure: uncontrolled emission is as bad as uncontrolled absorption, which is why the membrane meters **both** directions.
