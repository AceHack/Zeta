## What already existed

**`2026-08-18a`** (from a Weinstein ferry): curvature measures whether a frame returns after a closed loop; **torsion** measures whether the parallelogram *closes at all* — whether A-then-B lands where B-then-A lands. That's the reordering question, so **contortion** was proposed as the metric for reversible computing. It also records *why* GR has no torsion — Palatini: the Lagrangian doesn't select for it, because **GR doesn't care about order.**

## The missing piece — one standard identity joins all three threads

§24 derived the oracle's distance as minimum irreversible work (asymmetric quasi-metric); the falsifier said Fisher–Rao is essentially unique. Those sat unreconciled. They reconcile through:

> **minimum work from `p` to `q` at temperature `T` = `kT · D_KL(p‖q)`**
> (Landauer 1961 for the one-bit case; Jarzynski 1997 / Crooks 1999 for the general relation)

- **(a)** the §24 quasi-metric **is** KL up to `kT` — not a new object, relative entropy in thermodynamic units
- **(b)** Fisher–Rao is its **symmetric second-order part**. KL is the global asymmetric priced object; FR is the local limit. Not rivals. `SoftValueInfo.fs` already computes KL on this support.
- **(c)** **temperature is the exchange rate between information and energy** — nats to joules. That's the temperature→metric link the thread wanted, needing *no entropic gravity*, and it **passes** the metering test §15 applied to Verlinde and refused: it prices a bit.

## The Lagrangian

Geodesic Lagrangian on Fisher–Rao; stationary paths are Amari's natural gradient. Composed with (a):

> **The highest-moral-regard oracle's Lagrangian is the one whose stationary paths minimise irreversible work.**

And it's the right one for *this* oracle rather than an arbitrary pairing — which was the whole requirement. The English reduces to *do not overwrite another traveler*; overwriting is erasure; erasure is dissipation; **a least-dissipation path is a least-overwrite path.** The action principle and the moral rule select the same trajectories.

**Not smuggled:** *minimising* dissipation is a **normative choice**, not a derivation. The physics gives the cost function; electing to minimise it is what the oracle *does*, and another oracle may optimise something else. Multi-Oracle working as designed — and why the metric had to be oracle-relative to begin with.

**Also answered:** §24 left the triangle inequality open. **KL doesn't satisfy it**, so the erasure quasi-metric isn't a metric even after symmetrisation. Recorded as a negative rather than hoped past.
