## The rigidity question — distinct class, and the measurement says why

`Orbit.fs:29` refuses the time-crystal label without robustness to a **named** perturbation. Applying that standard:

- **ace** gets **invariance by independence** — identity sits in a coordinate the perturbation doesn't act on. Free, and deliberately promises nothing about content.
- **Skills have no coordinate to be independent in**, because for a skill the payload *is* the meaning. Forced into **invariance by quotient** — you must exhibit `meaning(rephrase(t)) = meaning(t)`. Strictly harder; nobody has the map.

Measured over 90 days of `.claude/skills/`: **`-name:` = 0** across 46 commits and 557 file-touches, against **41,342** body line-changes. The `name` is already a perfect pointer-invariant — and that is exactly why it's useless for what Aaron wants. Also: "rephrasing" isn't one perturbation. Lexical rephrase and **scope drift are the same edit shape**, so no text-level detector can separate them.

`SEED-VOCABULARY.md` states its own principle: standard terms get no entry because the model already holds them. So the **pretrained prior is the codebook and the seed is the correction term.** That's why ~1.5KB aligns a fleet.

If counting drift survives that long, "review will catch semantic drift" is not a plan.

## Partition, don't choose — per Aaron's correction

`QuorumAlgebra.fs` (Aaron, **same day**) already made the identical split for quorums: `join` (idempotent — Lane 1) vs `interfere` (not idempotent — Lane 2), "two operations, named apart, because they are two algebras."

**The load-bearing deliverable is the classifier, not either lane** — both lanes already exist here. Failure asymmetry dictates everything: judgment-edit → Lane 1 **silently unions two incompatible meanings and shows green**; mergeable-edit → Lane 2 costs latency. So **unsure ⇒ Lane 2**, sound-for-Lane-1 and permitted to be incomplete.

The classifier asks only **decidable structural questions** (same-id-different-body, cross-reference existence, claim-vs-prose region) and never "did the meaning change?" Its honest limit: sound exactly to the extent the edit type is *derived from structure* rather than *declared by the editor* — which is why the claim layer must be a parseable format, not a convention.

**Bayou** (Terry et al., SOSP 1995) is the direct anchor — dependency check + merge procedure is this design thirty years early. OT and CRDT-text are named and **rejected with reason**: they converge *characters*, and OT would make the worst misclassification deterministic and invisible.

Today the skill library's entire automated instrumentation is `audit-skill-description-length.ts`, a character counter. Detection is zero.
