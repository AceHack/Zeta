**I narrowed the independence claim rather than asserting the brief's.** The routes' **numerators** are genuinely disjoint (point counting vs. divisor sums, no shared input). Their **denominators** are the *same object* computed by disjoint algorithms — algorithmic independence, which alone catches an implementation bug, not a wrong model. That is why the **cube** lane exists: A007245's own OEIS formula line states _"Convolution cube is A000521 (the modular j-function)"_, so cubing the result tests the whole product against a number that entered through neither route.

## Numerology guard (`numerology-vs-number-theory` governs)

**248 is arithmetic here, not a discovery.** For any even lattice `(V_L)_1` is the Cartan part plus one basis vector per root, so the coefficient is *forced* to `rank + |roots| = 8 + 240`. The competitor a bare count could not exclude is `dim E8` (the Lie algebra, also 248) — so the test asserts the **identity** `LatticeVoa.rank + E8Lattice.kissingNumber = dims[1]` from in-tree values, not the literal 248. E8 is the unique even unimodular positive-definite rank-8 lattice (Mordell/Witt), so no competitor object could be confused for it. And four agreeing lanes are not four confirmations if they share a mechanism — the doc says exactly where they share one.
