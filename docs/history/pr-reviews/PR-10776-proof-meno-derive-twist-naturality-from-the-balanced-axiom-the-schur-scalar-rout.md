## What survives — and it is the useful half

The *centrality* intuition is right; only the route through Schur is wrong. **Naturality does
not have to be assumed.** It follows from the balanced axiom alone, in an arbitrary braided
monoidal category, from the hexagons:

```
PreTwist            θ + tensor axiom + one equation at the unit.  NO naturality field.
natural_braiding    θ commutes past c — the categorical form of Δ²σᵢ = σᵢΔ², load-bearing case
natural_tensor · natural_associator · natural_braiding_inv · the unitors
natural_of_mem      hence past every morphism in the braid closure
toTwist             so a PreTwist on a braid-generated category IS a Zeta.MenoBalanced.Twist
```

`Twist.naturality` quantifies over **every morphism of the category**; `PreTwist` replaces it
with **one equation at the unit** (which is *forced* in `⟨V⟩`, where `Hom(V⁰,V⁰) = B₀` is
trivial). `Twist.toPreTwist` goes back, so `PreTwist` is not a weaker object smuggled in to
make the theorem easy.

**Anchor correction that falls out.** The certificate needs only `Δₙ² ∈ Z(Bₙ)` — elementary
from `ΔσᵢΔ⁻¹ = σₙ₋ᵢ` — and **never Chow 1948's strictly stronger `Z(Bₙ) = ⟨Δ²⟩`**, which was
cited in `MenoBraided.fs` for a fact the proof does not use, and whose generation half is
**false at `n = 2`** (`Z(B₂) = B₂ = ⟨Δ₂⟩ ⊋ ⟨Δ₂²⟩`). Corrected in the F# docstring and the
registry. Anchors checked, not cited.

## Non-symmetric witness — the work-item's own recommended next move

081M00EZXN2087G0R003AY3WSJ option (b). `Framed` builds a braided monoidal category that is
**genuinely not symmetric** (`D_{1,1}` has framing 2) carrying a twist with **`θ` at the

believed impossible, now inhabited.

Stated in the file so it is not over-read: it is the **writhe/abelianisation shadow** of `⟨V⟩`
(`β_{m,n} ↦ m·n`, `θ_n ↦ n(n−1)`), and its hom-monoids are commutative, so it does **not**
witness the difficulty of centrality. That is witnessed separately by `generators_not_commute`
(σ₁ and σ₂ do not commute in the action) — without which `natural_of_mem` would be deriving
something that cannot fail.

## Not closed — named, not `sorry`'d

`BraidGenerated ⟨V⟩`: that every morphism of `⟨V⟩` lies in the `⊗`/`≫`-closure of identities,
braidings and coherence isos. Content: Joyal–Street 1993 §2. It is a **hypothesis of
`toTwist`**, and nothing here discharges it. There is **no `sorry` in the file** — a `sorry`
would let a downstream proof believe the obligation discharged, whereas a hypothesis cannot be
used without being supplied.

Note this is *strictly less* than what the work item predicted: option (c) was "braid groupoid
+ Artin faithfulness, weeks"; the faithfulness half is no longer needed for naturality.
