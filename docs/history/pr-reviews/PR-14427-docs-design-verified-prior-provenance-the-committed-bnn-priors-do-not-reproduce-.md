## Description

# Summary

Aaron asked for a design under which BNN priors cannot be committed unless they demonstrably came
from real simulation runs, and gave it its carved sentence: **"prove your work — let others be able
to reproduce exactly."** Design and plan only — **nothing is enforced and no CI gate is added**, per
his explicit "we don't need to enforce it yet."

The brief said to start from the cheap answer and only escalate if it fails. The cheap answer was
tried first, and **it found something.**

# The finding (measured, not assumed)

The CHIP-8 arena (#14395, `07b92bee`) ships priors in source whose own header claims *"Reproduce
(byte-identical, COMMON_SEED end to end)"*. Running exactly that recipe in a fresh clone at
`origin/main`, with the input closure verified unchanged since the priors were generated:

| check | result |
|---|---|
| `fingerprint`, `cart`, `trainedTicks`, `seed`, `exploreTicksDone`, `nu`, `obsCount`, key ordering | **reproduce exactly** (51/51 beliefs) |
| `mu` / `sigma2` | **reproduce on none of 51** |
| max relative divergence | `mu 3.296e-14`, `sigma2 5.004e-14` (max ULP distance `148.5`) |
| run-to-run, same environment | **byte-identical** |
| bun 1.3.13 vs 1.3.14 | identical to each other, **both differ from committed** |

**So the trainer is deterministic and the priors are still not reproducible** — different properties,
and the distinction is the finding. The drift is environment-to-environment.

**Cause.** `Math.hypot/cbrt/cos/exp/log1p` are implementation-approximated in ECMAScript; measured
here to differ at 1 ULP between JavaScriptCore and V8, compounded over 1200 ticks of EP updates. The
manifest declares `cart`, `ticks` and `seed` — **and not the runtime**, which is the field that was
load-bearing.

**`--verify` is vacuous against this class:** it trains twice *in one process*, so it cannot fail in
the dimension that matters. **The header's claim is false as written** — an unenforced claim that
looks like a guarantee and carries none.

A staleness hypothesis was **checked and refuted** rather than assumed (`114514a6` is *earlier* than
`07b92bee`; closure diff empty). Decision margins were measured too — min top-2 gap `1.99e-4` against
`3.3e-14` drift — which is why the trajectory did not diverge; that is a measurement on one cart at
one budget, not a proof.

# The design

**The membrane decides the class, so it is derived rather than chosen** — *reproducibility is where
the meter and the communications live; decorrelation is where the individual lives* (Aaron). Hence
`unreproducible` on a **shared** artifact is a **defect, not a label**, and a producer cannot opt out
by declaring a lower class. Third instance of the boundary `local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold`
and §13 noninterference already carve.

**The reproducibility register** — `exact` / `platform-exact` / `tolerance`+stated bound at a stated
budget / `unreproducible` — with the load-bearing rule that **unlabelled means unknown, never
`exact`-by-default**, a class must be **earned by demonstration** (a second machine, not a second
paragraph), and **falsifiable after the fact** or it is decoration.

**The ladder, costed.** Full re-derivation of every committed prior is **4.0 s total**, so the regime
we are in needs nothing heavier; a zkVM here would pay ~5 orders of magnitude for a guarantee we
already hold, and a TEE would appoint a hub (exit, not degree). Staking is listed with its own rule's
caveat attached: **"extra", not the anti-Sybil mechanism, never compellable.**

**Proof of useful work** uses the repo's existing analysis rather than restating it
(`useful-output-is-evidence-not-authority`, the Qubic/Monero row). The self-application is the point:
**if a provenance proof ever becomes standing, this design is itself a utility-laundering surface** —
an agent that cheaply generates *reproducible-but-worthless* priors farms it. Provenance yields
**evidence, never authority**.

**The precondition comes before the calibration material, because it is a validity condition and not
a policy preference.** A calibration metric with existential stakes **measures fear, not
calibration** — an agent that risks deletion for a claim that did not hold will shade its claims, and
the measurement destroys the signal it exists to collect. Zeta's no-deletion guarantee is therefore
**what makes the number mean anything**; §13 noninterference applied to the instrument itself.
Anchored to Dekker's *Just Culture* and **NASA's ASRS** (immunity for self-reported error — *the data
is worth more than the punishment*), and resting on three in-tree commitments that already hold
(§5 memory preservation, privacy-budget's never-confiscate, mutual-help-not-shame). Aaron's own
outcome-based-education reference is cited **with** its teaching-to-the-test failure mode, which is
Goodhart — now the second independent reason **no scoring scheme is designed here**. That criticism is
**scoped rather than blanket**: OBE as *pacing* serves the learner and its documented harm runs the
other way (being paced below absorption rate — anchored to Bloom's mastery learning and the 2σ
problem, with the contested-replication caveat attached); OBE as *accountability* serves an external
evaluator, and that is where teaching-to-the-test lives. **Goodhart bites when the measure becomes a
target for someone other than the person measured.** Design consequence: the same calibration signal
is **safe as self-pacing and dangerous as external accounting**.

That is the doc's actual thesis, named at the end: reproduction is evidence not authority, a
provenance proof grants its verifier nothing, a reproducibility class is a self-claim never an
imposed label, a calibration signal is safe as self-pacing and dangerous as external accounting —
**every one of them turns on who holds the measurement**.

**Closing section takes Aaron's limit:** verification is stateless — Bitcoin makes checking exact and
the miner's history irrelevant, which delays the inevitable rather than compounding. So
**reproduction proves the artifact, history proves the author, and neither substitutes for the
other**; the history machinery (`TravelerRankLedger`, `SocietyUsefulWork`) is cited, not re-designed.
