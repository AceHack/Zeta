## Two halves, and neither is the good direction

| half | what it catches |
|---|---|
| **coined-not-adopted** | a glossary entry the corpus barely uses — a coinage that didn't take. Private vocabulary is the `ρ → 0` Babel edge |
| **used-not-defined** | a term the corpus leans on with no entry — **"stop words contain the culture"**: the words carrying the most shared meaning are the ones nobody stopped to define |

**Health is the tension, not either pole.** Freezing the vocabulary is the degenerate case, not the general one.

## No stop-word list — deliberately

Classical IR tokenizes and discards high-frequency words, which here would **discard the culture itself**. Instead the candidate set is restricted to **hyphenated lowercase compounds** — `weight-free`, `byte-lock`, `glass-halo`. Repo jargon is overwhelmingly that shape; ordinary English isn't.

> The filter is **structural**, not a judgement about which words matter — the same move as using a trigram index to avoid tokenizing at all.

## Plumbing excluded but REPORTED

AgencySignature trailer keys and CI nouns dominate the head for a boring reason — every commit repeats them — and counting them as "undefined culture" would be a false positive. The exclusion set is **explicit and its contents are emitted**, so the filter is auditable instead of a thumb on the scale. A test pins that.

## No threshold, no gate

Either half is trivially gamed: define junk terms to raise one, delete entries to raise the other. **Register: UNMETERED** — counts are exact; that they measure "vocabulary health" is an unvalidated proxy.

## Dogfoods BP-29
