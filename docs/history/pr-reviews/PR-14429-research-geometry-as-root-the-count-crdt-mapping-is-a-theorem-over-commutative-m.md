# Summary

Aaron mapped §10's extensive/intensive result onto distributed-systems primitives:

> *"for me **a count is CRDT** and **a condition is CASPaxos or CASRaft**-ish."*

**Checked, not agreed with.** Directionally right, **off by one refinement in *each* column** — and
the refinements are the useful part.

**Is it equivalence or co-extension? Co-extension.** The two conditions agree on the examples and
are **separated by idempotence**, which belongs to the **delivery channel**, not to the statistic.
The mapping is a **theorem** over commutative monoids and an **analogy that parts** over counts.

## Left column — a count is a `CmRDT`, not a `CvRDT`

`+` on counts is commutative and associative and **not idempotent**. Those are the **commutative
monoid** axioms; a join-semilattice needs exactly the one that fails. So extensive ⇒ `CmRDT`
(unconditionally true) and extensive ⇒ `CvRDT` (**false**). The `G-Counter` repair is **per-source
keying + elementwise `max`** — a *change of the carried object* (declining to quotient by replica
identity, i.e. keeping a **section** of the coarsening), not a property of counting.

**`src/Core/Crdt.fs` is the in-tree witness** and is exactly that shape: **merge is `max`, read is
`Σ`** — two different operations on two different objects, which is precisely where the categorical
and CRDT conditions part.

**Idempotence is about delivery, not the statistic:** redeliver one increment and raw `+` reads
`10` where the truth is `5`; keyed-max reads `5`. Commutativity/associativity is the **meter** half;
idempotence is the **communications** half — Aaron's own reproducibility split, arriving from
another direction.

> **In-tree defect found by the check.** `src/Core/Crdt.fs:9` calls the Z-set a *"signed-multiset
> **join-semilattice** — commutative, associative, with an identity."* Those are the commutative
> **monoid** axioms; a semilattice additionally needs **idempotence**, which `ZSet.add` does not
> have and cannot have (it is a *group* — it has inverses, the opposite of idempotent). **The code
> is correct and the comment names the wrong structure**, and the tell is that `GCounter.Merge`
> reaches for `max` precisely *because* `+` is not idempotent. Reported, not filed.

## Right column — "intensive ⇒ consensus" is **false**, and this is the bigger correction

An intensive quantity that is a **ratio of two extensives** needs **no coordination at all**: carry
both measures, merge both, divide at read.

| | value |
|---|---|
| `(sum, count)` merged across uneven partitions, then divided | **6.2857142857** |
| direct global mean | **6.2857142857** — equal |
| average of the two partition averages | **7.7000000000** — wrong by **1.414** |

And this is the **thermodynamic definition** of intensive (density = mass/volume, `T = ∂U/∂S`), so
**Tolman 1917 supplies the repair, not just the vocabulary**; the measure-theoretic twin is
**Radon–Nikodym**. What actually forces consensus is **non-commutativity of the update**
(`f(g(s)) = 26 ≠ 23 = g(f(s))`), and **CAS is the canonical non-commutative operation** — so
**CASPaxos is the right primitive, reached by the right reason.** Aaron's right-hand box was
correct; its label was not.

## The degradation tier — what the result is *for*

| statistic | mode | cost | trust gradient decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **count** | CRDT | **wait-free** | nothing |
| **ratio of counts** (density / rate / mean / variance) | CRDT | **wait-free** | nothing — §11.3 moved this row *down* |
| **order-dependent update** (a decision, a CAS, a name election) | **CASPaxos / CASRaft** | coordination | **whether the round is affordable** |
| duplicating channel | per-source keying | free | orthogonal |

**The §11.3 correction earns its keep exactly here.** *"Rate ⇒ consensus"* would have escalated
every density, rate and mean onto the consensus tier. **A criterion that over-escalates is worse
than no criterion** for an architecture whose stated goal is to degrade *as rarely as possible*.

Applied to the naming registry: **measurement is wait-free, adjudication is not**, and the line
between them is now a test rather than a judgement call. It also composes with
`local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold` — a *rate* carries a **duration** in its denominator, so
**carry the count and let the reader divide by an agreed phase**: one design rule, two constraints,
one argument.

**The one genuinely independent contribution is the cost**, and it comes from the distributed side:
category theory says *when* coordination is required and cannot say it is **impossible** under
asynchrony (**FLP 1985**) or unavailable under partition (**Gilbert & Lynch 2002**). That is what
makes *"prefer the extensive formulation"* a manifesto §2 requirement rather than a style preference.
