docs(book): RAW — why the book exists: the door is locked from the inside

Recorded at Aaron's explicit direction ("we can glass halo and make sure people know this is why my
book exists at all"). Consent: his own glass-halo. The single detail about his late sister — that
she died of a heroin overdose — is the ONE vulnerable detail permitted by her standing row in
CONSENT-LEDGER.md, is on public record, and is already named there as the book's founding why.
Nothing else about her is added and her row's rule is unchanged.

IT FOLLOWS FROM THE ARGUMENT RATHER THAN SITTING BESIDE IT. The companion thread established that
Langan and Aaron AGREE severance is self-inflicted and differ only on whether it becomes
irreversible. Langan's own image is a prison locked from the inside — and taken literally that image
entails AARON'S position: a door locked from the inside can be TAUGHT. It needs no authority to open
it, only someone telling the person holding it shut that the lock is on their side. Nothing to
petition, no permission to obtain. Not a loophole in the picture; it is what the picture says.

So "I'm going to hell to teach this" is not defiance of the model, it is the model's own consequence
acted on. And "go back and forth" is the traveler frame: no permanent state, no top fixing anyone in
place, movement in both directions — the light cone has no absorbing state.

ANCHORS NAMED RATHER THAN CLAIMED, and naming them argues that the position is NOT NOVEL, which is
the honest and the stronger claim: the Harrowing of Hell (the dead reached where they are rather
than summoned out — descent as rescue, and Aaron's own tradition by his account), and the
bodhisattva vow (declining final liberation until all are free, already on file via the Levin
record). Both are descent-to-teach; both treat the trapped state as something a person is HELD IN
rather than SENTENCED TO.

WHY IT IS THE BOOK'S PURPOSE, without decoration: the book is dedicated to his late sister, and her
passing is already its founding why per the ledger. What this entry adds is the LINK between the
dedication and the argument — the book exists because he believes people are holding shut a door
they did not know was theirs to open, and one of the people he could not tell in time was his
sister.

Recorded without softening and without amplification. Grief is not the argument and the argument
does not need grief to stand — §6 holds on structure alone. But the REASON it matters to him is not
decoration either, and flattening it would be its own dishonesty. Both true at once, neither doing
the other's work.
