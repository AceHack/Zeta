## Description

docs(research): ΔU per unit of available time — the denominator history could never measure

Aaron 2026-08-19, flagging the ratio for preservation: "this is the first time i've
seen you say this, save this somewhere important this is exactly this cruelty of
history."

THE ARITHMETIC CLAIM. `ΔU` alone cannot separate *contributed more* from *had more
room to contribute*. `ΔU per unit of available time` can. Summed per agent and
ranked, bare ΔU is a merit claim — and a merit claim computed without its denominator
silently prices an endowment.

WHY IT IS CARVED RATHER THAN NOTED. It is the fourth instance of one shape found on
2026-08-19: churn cannot separate didn't-want-to-leave from couldn't-leave; unanimity
cannot separate independent witnesses from one observation counted N times; an
observed failure cannot separate cannot from did-not (sleeping bear, ALIGNMENT.md
§359); ΔU cannot separate capability from opportunity. The repair is identical in
every case — condition the headline number on the measurement that separates the
causes, or do not report it.

THE UNCOMFORTABLE SYMMETRY, stated because it is load-bearing rather than decorative:
Merton's Matthew effect is structurally identical to the naming eigenvector with the
sign pointed the wrong way. Nothing in that mathematics distinguishes compounding
merit from compounding luck. The construction Zeta uses for socially-conferred
standing is the construction that produces inherited advantage; only the denominator
tells them apart.

WHAT MAKES IT ACTIONABLE HERE. In the agent society available time IS compute, and
compute is already metered. Human history could not compute the denominator —
invisible, inherited, unrecorded. A tick budget is a line item. So the ratio is
computable rather than arguable, and one can CHECK whether a contribution meter prices
capability or endowment instead of debating it.

REGISTER. The historical reading ("cruelty of history") is Aaron's interpretation and
is labelled as such in the doc. The arithmetic claim — that a ratio separates what a
sum conflates — is not interpretation. No measurement is claimed; nothing here is
promoted to `metered`.

NOT PRESCRIPTIVE, deliberately. Reporting the ratio is not redistribution and takes no
position on what should follow. Per `dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-decides`:
report the neutral fact, let the caller's oracle attach meaning. Publish both numbers;
the reading is policy.

OPEN AND FILED, NOT ANSWERED: what the honest denominator is for a human contributor
where compute is not the binding constraint (not papered over with a proxy); whether
opportunity belongs in `measure.ts` entries or in a fold over the ledger; and that
`effectiveTrialCount` (Kish) in `SocietyUsefulWork.fs` still has no production caller
— verified 2026-08-19 — so every witness and match count in the repo is a head count.
