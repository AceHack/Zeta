## Description

## What this is

Aaron asked to push k8s verification as far pre-hardware as it will go and leave only a
minimal, named remainder to hardware. The census was the deliverable; the framing it started
from was wrong, and correcting it is the main finding.

**The check this was commissioned to build already exists and already runs.** `helm-validate`
resolves every pinned chart, renders it with our actual `valuesObject`, and kubeconforms the
output — across **both** cluster trees. Measured in full for this PR:

| tree | charts rendered | documents | resources schema-validated | skipped (no schema) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `infra/k8s` | **6 / 6** | 185 | **132** | 52 |
| `full-ai-cluster/k8s` | **36 / 36** | 865 | **695** | 152 |
| **total** | **42 / 42** | **1050** | **827** | **204** |

Zero `helm template` failures. So no new render check was built. What was missing was a
guarantee that the existing ones are looking at anything at all.

## Two silent-coverage defects, reproduced at `89c8a23c40`

Neither is a logic bug. The tool works perfectly and is pointed at less than it claims.

**1. A zero-chart render reported success.** `charts` is built from Applications carrying
`spec.source.chart`; Tests 7 and 8 iterate it, and nothing guarded it being non-empty — in a
file that *does* guard `apps.length === 0`. On a synthetic apps-dir holding one valid
directory-source Application, `--render` printed both test headings, invoked neither helm nor
kubeconform, and reported `Results: 7 passed, 0 failed` / `All checks passed.` at **exit 0**.

`full-ai-cluster` is *accidentally* covered — its ratchet compares an exact count, so a drop
to zero renders moves the number. **`infra/k8s` has no ratchet**, so a silent drop to zero
renders there is a clean green.

**2. An Application in a differently-named file was invisible to every check.** Discovery
matches the exact filename `Application.yaml`, a convention enforced nowhere. Renaming
`orleans/Application.yaml` → `orleans/orleans-app.yaml` took the pre-fix validator from 38
passes to `Results: 32 passed, 0 failed` / `All checks passed.` at **exit 0**, with `orleans`
appearing **zero times** in the output.

## The fixes

- **Test 0** — every anchored `kind: Application` under apps-dir must be reachable by the
  discovery rule. Root app exempt **by path, not by name** (the two trees legitimately differ).
- **Test 9** — asserts three separate questions: charts found; helm succeeded at least once;
  kubeconform **schema-validated** (not merely *skipped*) a non-zero count. The third is what
  `-ignore-missing-schemas` can quietly zero out.

Both land green on both real trees, so they are ratchets, not debt. Ratchet stays at **exactly
13** — this adds two passes and zero failures.

## Two defects in the fix itself, recorded because they are the instructive half

- The summary parser first matched `Valid 14`; kubeconform v0.7.0 emits `Valid: 14`. It fell
  through to zero and Test 9 reported *"schema-validated ZERO resources"* against a render
  where **132** had been validated. A check built to stop false reports produced one on first
  contact. Now accepts both forms **and refuses an unparseable summary by name** rather than
  counting it as zero.
- The zero-charts falsifier first mutated the seven real manifests, broke YAML in four of
  them, and passed *whether or not Test 9 existed*. Caught by stubbing Test 9's refusal and
  watching the case stay green; rewritten against a synthetic tree asserting the exact failure
  count.

## The census

`docs/research/2026-08-26-k8s-pre-hardware-verification-census-four-depths-and-the-irreducible-remainder.md`

Separates four depths that get conflated — **parsed** / **schema-validated as written** /
**rendered + schema-validated** / **applied to a live cluster** — and reports coverage at each.
Headline: **nothing in the 116 files is unverified; what varies is the depth, and the deepest
two lanes are not in the blocking floor.**

Two structural findings worth a decision:

- The **only required** k8s check invokes kubeconform with `-skip "Application,..."` —
  `Application` is the first entry. The 48 ArgoCD CRs driving the whole GitOps tree are not
  schema-validated by anything blocking.
- `k8s-lane-partition` already proves each lane **fits** a constrained runner and renders its
  charts — confirmed it **never creates a cluster or applies**. The lane split Aaron
  remembered exists; the deploy step on top of it does not.

The doc also separates **genuinely hardware-bound** (GPU serving, Longhorn, RWX, Cilium L2,
USB reformat retention, secure boot) from items currently conflated with them that are really
**capacity or credential** (`gitlab`, `hindsight`, `arc-runner-set`) — with a "what Aaron must
decide" section of five open questions.
