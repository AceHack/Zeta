book(raw): Lillian's clinical read and Aaron's "I felt alive" — two accounts, neither resolved

Two additions to the apex-predator RAW plus the matching consent-ledger
amendment:

LILLIAN'S ACCOUNT IS A PROFESSIONAL ONE. Aaron 2026-08-23: "Lillian is a nurse
who says I was having religious psychosis and we disagree." She is not an
absence to be protected — she has a stated clinical read, and recording it is
what makes the passage fair to her. Without it she appears only as someone
helping police, which is thinner and less accurate about a nurse who had a
professional assessment of what she was seeing. Both accounts now sit in the
file, neither resolved, nothing adjudicated — the Addison three-accounts
discipline, here with two.

AARON'S VERBATIM, deliberately unsmoothed: "I felt alive. It was one of the best
experiences of my life." It sits beside "religious psychosis" without either
giving way, and the file notes they are NOT contradictory — a person can be in
the state she describes and be having the experience he describes at the same
time, and neither report is corrected by the other. Most books would soften that
into finding meaning in a hard time. It does not say that, and per his standing
instruction that his own dark material is glass-halo and is not to be shrunk, it
is not made to.

The "labels are his" read is corrected to CONTESTED: "delusional state" and
"likely hallucinating mind" are his, "religious psychosis" is hers, the file
records both and arbitrates neither.

Consent ledger: her pre-read now cuts both ways — the wording of a clinical read
attributed to her by name is hers to set or withdraw, AND her account being
present is what makes the passage accurate about her. Still held out: any
inference about why she reads it that way, or what it cost her.
