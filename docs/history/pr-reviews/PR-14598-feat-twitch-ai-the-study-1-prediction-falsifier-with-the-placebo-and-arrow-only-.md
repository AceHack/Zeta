## Description

The last deliverable of the attention-density spec — the one that decides
whether D1–D5 worked. If watching the overlay does not raise a viewer's
ability to predict the agent's next move, the overlay is decoration and
the honest move is to cut it. Comprehensibility asserted is not
comprehensibility measured.

**Protocol, per trial:** WATCH (~5 s under the trial's condition) → PROBE
(the sim freezes — a real worker-side pause, not a rendering trick — and
the panel asks "where does the AI go next?", four arrows + an honest
"can't tell" skip) → MEASURE (resume; ~1.5 s later the agent's net
displacement grades the guess by dominant axis with a stated tie-break; a
still agent discards the trial, and the discard is REPORTED in the tally
as "held still", never silently dropped).

**Conditions, Latin-square counterbalanced** — the spec named with vs
without; the two controls are the substance of the refutation its author
asked for:

- **full** — the shipped page: boxes, mode, OCR, frost, fixation, WHY.
- **none** — bare pixels.
- **placebo** — the frost/fixation visuals driven by an integer hash of
  the cycle counter: looks like a live field (a few frosted tiles, an
  attended set, a fixation that dwells and saccades), carries zero
  information about the game. Without this condition, "overlay helps"
  cannot be told apart from "any overlay engages the viewer more".
  Δ(full − placebo) is the field's informational value;
  Δ(placebo − none) is the engagement effect.
- **arrow-only** — mode + intent arrow in the header, nothing else. The
  upstream-dominance test: if this condition predicts as well as full,
  the density claim is dominated by the ladder upstream of the field
  (lastDesired + mode), not by the field itself.

**Boundaries kept honest:** scoring reads the frame payload, which always
carries the full arena readout — conditions gate only what is RENDERED.
The schedule, answer key, tally, and placebo generator are a pure module
(`chip8/study-protocol.ts`) with 9 falsifiers: every block holds every
condition once and every condition visits every position; the key is
deterministic with its tie-break stated; the tally's per-condition n's
plus discards always equal total (no silent loss); the placebo replays
byte-identically from the cycle alone, drifts across epochs, and has
live-field shape parity (32 tiles in [0,1], K attended, fixation
attended). A non-full condition hides the WHY chip and closes an open
chain, so the metered overlay family cannot leak across conditions. The
worker's new PAUSE/RESUME messages guard the resume against forking a
second tick chain.

Also fixed en route: a null arena left stale perception boxes rendered —
unreachable before the "none" condition made that path real.

**Headless smoke of the built app (`?study=1`):** the probe froze the
canvas bytes flat for 700 ms; the answer resumed them; grading advanced
to trial 2 under "none" with every overlay surface verified hidden
(0 frost tiles, 0 boxes, "[OBSERVING]", WHY chip gone) and the tally
rendering "full 0/0 · none 0/0 · placebo 0/0 · arrow 0/0 · 1 held still
· chance 25%". That first live trial grading "held still" is real data:
the FLEE-latched agent parked against the wall — the discard path doing
its job in public, and the corner-stall limit from #14471 showing up in
the study exactly where it should.

**Verification:** 126 tests green across chip8 + bayesian (9 new
study-protocol falsifiers); root and app tsc rc=0; eslint 0 problems on
the app dir and both new Core files; vite build green; priors untouched
(no predictor changes).

Scope line: this PR ships the instrument. Running the study on real
viewers and acting on its numbers — including cutting the overlay if
full beats nothing but not placebo — is the follow-up the spec defines.
