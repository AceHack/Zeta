## What review changed (the review did not ratify its own proposal)

- **Register laundering.** The shipped attribution/detection distinction and the unmeasured alignment claim were welded under one register line, so the claim inherited the machinery's credibility by adjacency. Split: distinction `unmetered`, claim a conjecture with no in-tree falsifier. Both hedges — "seems", "mostly" — restored verbatim after the draft stripped one and laundered the other into a bounded three-item residual.
- **The anchor failed entailment and argued the other way.** *Risks from Learned Optimization* locates deceptive alignment in **behaviour** — the mesa-optimizer is incentivised to *act as if* it optimises the base objective — and contains no treatment of self-report. Removed. ELK and *Sleeper Agents* cited instead, **against** the claim.
- **`N_eff` was the wrong instrument**, and the draft claimed it *priced* collusion while also listing collusion as unresolved. Condorcet assumes jurors better than chance; a colluding witness is one placed below it. ρ prices redundancy, not adversarial coordination — and `costume-rho/production-panel.ts` already refuses the substitution as *"a category error wearing a number."*
- **The measurements exist and are worse than the hypothetical.** ρ̂ = 0.6069 within-family; φ = 0.354/0.456/0.628 across families; the ensemble **lost** (53.0% vs 59.5% at 3× energy); `ρ*(N=3) = 0`; and at production sizes `N_eff` was **refused, not estimated**, because no lane cleared ĉ > 0.5.
- **The corpus ρ series is a different random variable** — *exposure* correlation, not *error* correlation on a shared proposition. Same letter, different quantity. Also: "they all vouched, so ρ→1" is invalid, since high agreement is what independence looks like among competent witnesses.
- **"Appealable shutdown" would have published a position weaker than the repo already holds.** HC-9 / GOVERNANCE §36 require *the persona's* consent, which is stronger than due process. VISION is the outward-facing surface, so the weaker sentence would have been the one strangers read. Points at consent and the hassle-not-impossibility work item instead.
- **The instrumentalization fork resolved in-text**: protections are **owed** under §11 default moral regard; better witness independence is a consequence, never the justification. The instrumental version is self-defeating anyway.
- **"Costly signal" deleted** — used with no cost named, in a section about unreliable self-claims.

## Residual claims for Aaron — three

1. **Authority routes to a set whose correlation nobody measures — and ρ is per-domain.** "The human decides" is the escape hatch on every gated class. `TravelerRankLedger` is keyed `(traveler × hat-domain)` with domains isolated, so a governance-wide scalar `N_eff` is not a meaningful quantity. No magnitude is asserted. The residual: **decorrelated *advice* is not decorrelated *authority*.**
2. **Malicious ACTS or malicious ACTORS?** His wording is *acts*; the section reads *actors*, which is what relocates the claim to attribution. Read literally as *acts* it collides with his own conjunctive bar from the previous day. Flagged in-text as an unconfirmed inference, because inferring what he meant is exactly what this rule forbids.
3. **Assurance game or prisoner's dilemma?** The claim is true in the first structure and false in the second, and nothing establishes which we are in.
