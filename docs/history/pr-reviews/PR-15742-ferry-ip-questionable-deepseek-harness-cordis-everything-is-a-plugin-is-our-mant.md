## Description

Ferries Mehul Gupta's Medium article on **DeepSeek Harness (`dsh`) / the Cordis kernel** into `docs/ip-questionable/`, at Aaron's observation that its headline architecture is **a convergence with what Zeta already holds** — *hexagonal ports plus MEF, modernised* — rather than something to adopt.

## Why quarantined, and why not verbatim

The `deepseek-harness` **repo is MIT** (licensed to us); **Gupta's article is not**, so the article is the thing segregated. Prior items in this folder are verbatim auto-transcripts; this is a structured summary with quotation, and that departure is **recorded in the file** so the choice is auditable rather than silent — the README asks for *minimal, attributed, removable*, and a written article is less defensible to reproduce whole than an auto-caption of a talk.

## The substantive addition: the anchors the article omits

Per `anchor-to-human-prior-art`, naming the humans is the work. Cockburn 2005 (ports/adapters), MEF (.NET 4.0), Buschmann *POSA* microkernel, Plan 9's *everything is a file* as the slogan's ancestor, Helland/Young for the append-only log — and above all **OSGi (1999)**, whose dynamic install/update/uninstall **is** the "hot-unload with reversible effects" claim presented as new. The failure modes OSGi documented over two decades are worth more to us than the feature list.

## Registers held rather than rounded up

- Vendor benchmarks are **first-party**, at a configuration the vendor chose; the **95k-stars figure is the article's**, not measured here.
- **Reversible unmount is `unmetered`** under `toy-is-free-metered-must-be-earned` — the cleanup claim carries no falsifier, and unmount-leak is precisely what OSGi found hard.
- Replay over one event stream is the right *shape*, but is only **deterministic** if every entropy crossing is metered (#13). An event log recording what the model saw, while the scheduler still draws ambient time, replays a story rather than a run.

## The design question recorded before any adoption

A kernel that mounts **everything** — loops, storage, sessions, the UI — is a single point all capability routes through. Per `itron-hub-patent-boundary-p2p-is-the-upgrade` the discriminator is **exit, not degree**, so the test is not *how many things are plugins* but **whether a plugin can be consumed without the kernel present**. "It's all plugins" would be the most persuasive possible costume for an appointed hub.
