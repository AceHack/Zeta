## Description

The canonical E8 image shows 240 roots as **8 concentric rings of 30**. That 30 is the **order of the Coxeter element** `w = s₁⋯s₈`, h(E8) = 30 — and the Coxeter plane is w's rotation plane. Building the plane needs a complex 8×8 eigendecomposition (deferred, a rendering concern); the **order is exact in integers**, so that's gated:

- `w` has order **exactly 30** on all 240 roots — searched to 60, so 30 is a *found minimum*, not assumed.
- `w` **fixes no root** — which is why its plane is a clean rotation and the picture closes into rings rather than collapsing onto fixed directions.

Turns the 30-fold symmetry of the visual-cortex-facing image from a picture we trust into a gated fact. 13/13. 🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)
