## The gap this closes

PR #10697 established that Eve protocol's **structure-first half already ships** (`src/Core/DynamicValue.fs`, four-language byte-locked) and that *"how the agreed structure **determines** the translation has no spec anywhere."* This is that spec — and the spec turns out to license a **measurement**, not just a stance.

Aaron 2026-05-12: *"you agree on the structure. You agree on the symbols. And then you assign labels later. And you have to agree on the labels being unweighted, unbiased."*

## The headline

The `DynamicValue` shape lattice carries an imposition budget of **1.0000 bits (strict) / 0.0000 bits (role reading)**. Brute-forced over `Sym(8)`:

| level | rung | `|Aut| ≤` | bits |

The residual bit is exactly the **Int/Bytes transposition**, and the one shipped claim it pays for is the **CBOR major-type assignment**. So the party spending it is **RFC 8949 — neither negotiating party.** That is Lewis salience working, not a defect: a choice no party made cannot carry a party's asymmetry.

## Method — why an upper bound suffices

`Aut(S) ⊆ Stab(invariants)`, so only an **upper bound** is computed. **An upper bound of 1 is exact** (the identity is always an automorphism), so rigidity is certifiable by upper bound alone. The expensive direction is never needed.

Every rung must name an operation the **receiver** can perform unaided, and must carry evidence verified present in the shipped tree. **Deliberately not a rung:** payload-sort cardinality — finiteness is not witnessable by finite exhibition. It would have bought a smaller budget with a check that cannot fail. *(That is a correction to my own first derivation, which used it.)*
