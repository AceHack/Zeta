## Description

Iris flagged the retired `working/idle/attention` state table (F-1). The **table** went, but two **prose prescriptions** survived it — and those are the ones that propagate, because they tell an implementer what to build.

| line | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| 93 | *"Rooms with no 24h activity render as `"idle"` with high epsilon"* | `"cold"` |
| 144 | *"dwellers degrade to "attention" then "cold" automatically"* | `"stale"` |

Neither `idle` nor `attention` is in the shipped `live|cold|stale|heat` DU. An implementer reading line 93 builds a fifth vocabulary — the exact drift the contract change existed to stop.

**The remaining hits at 119/121 are deliberate and stay:** they're the change-log *documenting* the removal. Naming what was removed isn't prescribing it — and a grep that can't tell those apart is why this survived one review already.

Replacements preserve meaning rather than satisfy the grep: a room with no 24h activity is **cold** (watched, nothing there — not `heat`, which means active failures), and an adapter that stops produces frames aging **stale** before **cold**, the actual progression the browser clock yields.
