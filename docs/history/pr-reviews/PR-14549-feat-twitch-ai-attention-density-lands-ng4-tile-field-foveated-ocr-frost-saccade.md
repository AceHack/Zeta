## Description

The four middle deliverables of the attention-density spec, with the
acceptance numbers measured rather than asserted — and the headline is
the spec's own thesis landing as a benchmark: WITH the attention field
computed and the ladder foveated, predict() got FASTER than main
(5694 vs 6152 µs/tick over 3000 mutual-sim ticks, −7.4%). The change
that makes the page watchable made it faster.

D1 — the field crosses the boundary:

- NEW attention-field.ts: one Normal-Gamma belief per 8×8 tile over the
  tile's per-tick change fraction, exactly the λ/α source the spec
  names. The conjugate update is CLOSED FORM (the additive-blend fact
  the GPU-mapping doc rests on) — measured ~ns against ~177 µs per
  Student-t EP update, which is why the first cut (EP per tile per
  tick) pushed predict() +42% and was rebuilt on the NG4 carrier. An
  exponential forgetting factor (half-life ≈34 ticks) keeps a
  converged tile able to re-frost when its content changes character;
  floors keep the marginal proper. Deterministic; snapshot v1 in the
  society snapshot (v3); the Student-t marginal stays recoverable via
  the codec, so no second representation exists to drift.
- The field rides the SAME postMessage as the frame it labels, tile
  arrays as Float32Array transferables (moved, not copied).

D2 — foveation with a loud meter:

- Full OCR runs on the top-K(=8, displayed) variance tiles + one
  deterministic peripheral-sweep tile (tick % 32 — no ambient entropy)
  + every tile that has EVER held a recognised glyph: a discovered
  scoreboard is an INSTRUMENT, and an instrument sampled 1 tick in 32
  cannot produce the consecutive readings the reward channel's
  two-tick agreement needs (found live: the latch falsifier's NORMAL
  run lost its only reward event until instrument-stickiness landed).
- The connected-component pass stays GLOBAL deliberately: field
  coherence is a global property and a local check cannot see it
  (spec session-context §1) — stated in code, not just here.
- The meter: OCR reading-changes over match attempts; when the
  variance field is flat the meter says `ambiguous` instead of
  quietly printing a number over uniform sampling (both halves of the
  meter doctrine).

D3 — frost is the uncertainty channel: 32 persistent overlay tiles,
opacity ∝ normalised predictive variance (clear=known, frosted=not
known yet, attended tiles render as actively clearing). Zeta's frost
vocabulary, deliberately.

D4 — the saccade and the fixation, drawn: the fixation tile latches
through a dwell (FIXATION_MIN_DELAY=6 ticks) — the
suppress-inside-MinDelay split of src/Core/DebouncedOracle.fs one
granularity down, named as such — and the ring's fast left/top
transition IS the saccade sweep; the settle pulse is the fixation
landing.

Aaron's standing constraint — measure ρ, never assume decorrelation —
is wired end to end (society-rho.ts → header), and its FIRST LIVE
READING IS THE CATCH: ρ̄ = 1.00. The three society members' per-key
belief vectors are perfectly correlated on the live cart — the swarm
is presently one agent in three masks, which is precisely the state
that "looks exactly like a working swarm from outside". Now it is on
the screen instead of invisible. Decorrelating the society is real
follow-up work; displaying the collapse is this PR's job and it does.
