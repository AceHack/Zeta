## Summary

Moves the declared GitHub Actions heartbeat cadence from the top-of-hour quarter-hour schedule to `7,22,37,52 * * * *`. This follows GitHub’s documented guidance that scheduled workflows can be delayed or dropped under high load, including at the hour boundary.

The PR adds an executable workflow-source contract that rejects an absent schedule and a reintroduced top-of-hour cadence. It does **not** claim a GitHub-only schedule yields a delivery guarantee. Two newly emitted Alexa receipts remain retained on `heartbeat/alexa` until a future checked flush promotes them.
