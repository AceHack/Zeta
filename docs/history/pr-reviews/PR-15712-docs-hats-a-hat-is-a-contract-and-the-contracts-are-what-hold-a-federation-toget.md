## Description

Connects the **hat literature** to the **contract language** that already existed. Naming only — no hat's authorization changes, no code touched, markdown only.

> Aaron 2026-08-26: *"we talk about hats coming with bounded authorization and restrictions/bindings; this is contract language in disguise … Also Addison in our glossary talks about cluster vs federation, and clusters don't really have hat contracts — they are just loosely connected — and federations have hat contracts they agree on. **Contracts hold federations together.**"*

## The correction I found mid-task, kept in the record

My first draft asserted the hat literature *"never says the word contract."* **That is false of the corpus**, and a fan-out search caught it before it shipped:

- **`vocab/words/hat.md`** has read *"A time-bound, exit-paired, auth-bearing **contract** — the right to speak or act in a room; renewable only by consent"* since June.
- **`Contract` is itself one of Addison Cooper's registered Genesis concepts** — *"enforceable obligation — and **every one contains an exit**"* — sitting in `docs/CONCEPT-REGISTRY.md` beside `Hat`, `Cluster`, and `Federation`, all four by the same author, all `On page: yes`.

So the real gap is **adoption, not invention**. At `eeb29eaf96` the five surfaces an agent actually reads — `hats/README.md`, `src/Core/Hat.fs`, `src/Core/Persona.fs`, the hat-system README, and `GLOSSARY.md` §Hat — carried **thirteen contract clauses between them and zero uses of the word**. A coinage that landed in `vocab/` and never propagated is exactly what `anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability` watches for.

What is *genuinely* absent, and is what this PR adds: **nothing in the tree linked the hat to the cluster/federation split**, though all four concepts are in one registry by one author.

Also verified: **`rg -i "bounded authorization"` has zero pre-existing hits.** That phrasing is Aaron's, not established repo vocabulary — worth knowing so nobody cites it as prior usage.

## What landed

- **`docs/GLOSSARY.md`** — new §`Hat contract`, plus cross-references on §Hat, §Cluster, §Federation, and §Universal Exit Principle so a reader arriving from either side finds the other. **The existing Cluster/Federation entries are not restated** — they were good.
- **`hats/README.md`** and **`full-ai-cluster/k8s/applications/hat-system/README.md`** — the contract framing, and the four CRDs read as contract instruments (`Hat.spec.authority` = scope of authority; `HatBinding`'s `Pending → Warmup → Active → (Probation) → Revoked` = formation → probation → breach → termination; `quorumGated` = counter-signature; `conflictsWith` = conflict-of-interest; cooldown/warmup = notice periods).
- **`docs/research/2026-08-26-a-hat-is-a-contract-*.md`** — the satellite: the clause table, the four claims, the payment-terms half, two **marked proposals**, and the convergence check.

**Exit is the discriminator, and it needed no importing.** Addison's `Contract` puts exit *inside* the definition; `vocab/words/hat.md` calls a hat *exit-paired*. Two surfaces, months apart, different authors, already agreeing — which is stronger evidence for the identification than anything this PR argues.

## Two additions, marked as proposals rather than canon

Neither implemented, neither with a work-item minted:

1. **A typed incentive-alignment field** (`issuer-pays | consumer-pays | staked | none`) rather than prose. Deriving *"author pays verifier"* → *"approval-biased"* is a non-trivial inference, and the evidence is that credit ratings got it wrong for decades **with full disclosure**. Honest limits stated in the doc: the four members are a guess at the partition, a declared alignment is a self-claim nothing checks, and `none` means *no economic relationship*, not *no bias*.
2. **Attestations carry which hat contract produced them.** Without it a staked verification and a paid one look identical downstream and nobody can discount either.

## The two-scale convergence — checked, not asserted

The glossary explicitly warns that *Cluster* is not a k8s cluster, so the burden was on the shapes.

- **Matches:** agreed terms are the *precondition for federating* at both layers. `cluster-network.nix` — *"two clusters that federate exchange identity anyway"*, requiring disjoint CIDRs and distinct ids. That is name/membership/obligations in Nix.
- **Does NOT match:** a k3s cluster is **tightly coupled internally**. The mapping is between the *relations*, never between the objects — stated plainly in the doc so nobody reads it the wrong way.
- **What excludes "it's just a reused word":** the infrastructure layer arrived at that precondition structure independently, on Cilium's constraints, without citing the glossary.
- **It earns its keep by predicting a gap:** there is a **join** path (`injected-server-join.nix`, `k3s-join-observer.nix`) and **no leave path**. The Universal Exit Principle says that is not optional. No work-item minted — naming it is the whole claim.
