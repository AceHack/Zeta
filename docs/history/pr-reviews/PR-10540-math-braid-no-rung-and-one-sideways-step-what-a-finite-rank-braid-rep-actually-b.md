## Verdict

**The stop stands.** Nothing is built. "Build nothing" was a first-class candidate throughout and it is most of the answer — but the *question* was worth the hour, because asking **"what capability do we lack"** instead of **"what is the next rung"** turned up exactly one real gap, and it is not on the ladder, not in the candidate list, and served by the **unfaithful** representation.

## Does the amplitude-layer news change the MTC verdict? No — and the reason is stronger than Q4's

Q4's premise (*"no unitary ℂ-linear substrate, and none planned"*) did go stale on 2026-08-13. It was never load-bearing.

| | |
|---|---|
| **(a) rank vs scalar** | MTC's blocker is that `V = ℤ[F_n]` is free of **infinite rank**. The rank of a free module is **invariant under base change**, so `ℤ → ℂ` cannot touch it. `ℂ[F_n]` is still infinite-dimensional. The ambient swap still makes every object transparent, so S stays maximally degenerate. **An amplitude layer is a scalar problem; the obstruction is a rank problem.** |
| **(b) no functor, only a shared adjective** | "⟨V⟩ could be ℂ-linear" + "the quorum is ℂ-valued" is a **count-level coincidence**, not an identification. Legitimate generator, illegitimate conclusion (`numerology-vs-number-theory`). |
| **(c) the new substrate is float** | **CHECKED** in `src/Core/AmplitudeEmu.fs`: `Complex` is `Real: float; Imag: float`, and `merge` drops at `magSq ≤ 1e-12`. Inadmissible under the exact-arithmetic discipline even if (a) and (b) were solved. |

It changes the timeline of nothing — not even the timeline, because the obstruction it would have to remove is not the one it addresses.

## All three nominated candidates rejected

- **Matrix structure for the quorum** — the consumer is float; the layer's real gap is a **phase source with meaning**, and a braid records *interaction order* while quorum cancellation is supposed to encode *disagreement* (wrong semantics, unmeterable); and B3 is a **provenance** failure whose named fix is the EP cavity, which phase cannot aim.
- **CJKLS quandle cohomology** — needs a **finite** quandle (ours is the conjugation quandle of the free group); and it produces a **link invariant**, a quotient of the braid, strictly weaker than `Braid.equal`. Dies twice. *(Recorded as a pointer: quandle cohomology is the right home if anyone reopens Q2's framing datum.)*
- **Turaev's enhanced YB operator** — **does not sidestep the rigidity blocker, it restates it.** The enhancement conditions are stated as a partial trace `tr₂`, and `tr₂` is exactly what rigidity provides; Turaev assumes `V` finitely generated projective so it exists. Blocker inherited verbatim. And it yields a Markov-trace link invariant anyway, so it dies twice and the second death does not depend on my reading of the definition.

## The reframe that found the one survivor

Q4's rule — *anything weaker than `Braid.equal` is DOA* — is a rule about **decision procedures**. Faithfulness is what lets a representation *decide* equality; it is **irrelevant to measuring**.

> **The faithful representation is the one we do not need.**

## Evidence — CHECKED (exact integer arithmetic, two independent re-implementations)

**`Braid.equal` is worst-case exponential, and the exponent is the braid's own geometry.** On `(σ₁σ₂⁻¹)^k` in `B₃`, the Artin image reaches **1,028,457 letters at |β| = 28**, successive ratio **2.6180381** — against the pseudo-Anosov dilatation `(3+√5)/2 = 2.6180340`. Intrinsic, not an implementation defect. `MenoBraided.rep` inherits it. Dual-use: a **cost** in `Braid.equal`, the **instrument** in `BraidEntropy.growthRate`.

**Reduced Burau at `t = −1` has integer entries** — no Laurent ring, `t` is gone.

Its exact char poly over ℤ reproduces the exponential float estimator on every pseudo-Anosov case (`x²−3x+1` → 2.618034 vs 2.618038; `x³−5x²+5x−1` → 3.732051 vs 3.733476) — and is **more correct at the discriminating boundary**: `growthRate` returns a spurious ≈0.0202-nat residual on zero-entropy braids, which is why its own docstring says *"threshold it."* The integer char poly replaces a hand-tuned threshold with an exactly decidable question.

## Recommendation: file, do not build

The one item worth a work-item is that characteristic polynomial as a **one-way certified lower bound** on dilatation, complementing (never replacing) `BraidEntropy`. Cost: `(n−1)×(n−1)` integer matrices, Faddeev–LeVerrier over ℤ, Sturm. No new number type.

**Falsifier, stated up front — it convicts and never acquits.** Burau is unfaithful for `n ≥ 5` (Bigelow 1999), so `ρ = 1` is `Unmeasured`, never `zero`; any implementation reporting "entropy 0" from `ρ = 1` has smuggled an acquittal. **That is the mutation test the item must carry.** Burau's unfaithfulness surfaces here as measurement *weakness*, never measurement *error*. **CONJECTURE tier** — the bracket `ρ(Burau(−1)) ≤ λ ≤ exp(raw Artin growth)` has named anchors and no proof of mine. Hands to Soraya.

**NOT recommended: Lawrence–Krammer.** Faithful, so it buys decision strength we already have; and it is the wrong tool for the one gap it *would* address (polynomial-size certificate) — that gap wants a **canonical form**, which is Garside, already required by work-item `081KZZVC3DD087G0R0035SZN58` for `Δ²`. Observation, not a proposal.

## Two side-findings, handed off

1. **`AmplitudeEmu.merge` sums** (not idempotent) with an **order-dependent** `EPS` drop, so the quorum layer is **not** an instance of `universal/evidence`'s join contract — and nothing says so (manifesto §12). Deliberate departure, undeclared.
2. If amplitudes are ever byte-locked they **need no floats** — unitary modular data is cyclotomic (Vafa 1988; Coste–Gannon 1994; ENO 2005; Ng–Schauenburg 2010), so `ℤ[ζ_N]` is an exact home. PROPOSED, out of my scope to decide.
