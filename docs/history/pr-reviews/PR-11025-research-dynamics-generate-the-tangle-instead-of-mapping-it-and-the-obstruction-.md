## Description

Aaron, watching ζ's analytic continuation: *"instead of recursing downwards and doing cartography and using the demon and external observers to map it, this looks like a generator for the singularity outwards."* He labelled it metaphor. Most of it is not.

## His reading of our code is accurate (verified from source)

- `PhasePortrait.fs` rasterizes an orbit because *"visualizing an orbit is not a shortcut for this class of object; it is the native method that found it"*
- `Orbit.fs` reaches the chaotic regime via `largestLyapunov` — `nudge`, iterate, measure divergence, average

Iterate downward, probe from outside, record. Cartography by design.

## The generator is standard and literal

The **poles** of the meromorphically continued Ruelle zeta function are the **Pollicott–Ruelle resonances** — the decay rates, obtained by generating from an Euler product over periodic orbits rather than by perturb-and-measure.

This **cashes the Ruelle bridge** from earlier today, which Aaron endorsed but had not derived and which read as taxonomy. It isn't taxonomy — Ruelle's zeta is exactly what converts orbit cartography into a generating function.

## Two properties that are already ours

**Uniqueness is error correction.** Continuation is *forced* by the identity theorem, so two oracles continuing the same local data cannot disagree without one being wrong — the N-oracle byte-lock restated in complex analysis.

**Measurement erases; derivation doesn't.** Cartography pays the Landauer floor that continuation does not, and `LandauerFloor.lean` already proves both the floor and Bennett's zero-heat result. An energetic difference we hold a machine-checked proof about, not a stylistic preference.

## The obstruction — why this ships as a question, not a plan

Many dynamical zeta functions admit only a **natural boundary** past which no continuation exists. Ruelle proved meromorphic continuation for **Axiom A** systems; outside that class the generator may not be there, and cartography would be the only method rather than the lazy one. Whether **our** systems continue is unknown and nothing in the repo answers it.

§5b adds Aaron's follow-up, which is half exactly right: **escape rate is the mechanism** (the Euler product converges only for `Re(s) > h_top`, and the leading resonance *is* the escape rate for open systems), while the **horizon analogy inverts** — a horizon is a coordinate singularity that analytic continuation carries you *through* (Kruskal–Szekeres), whereas a natural boundary is genuine non-existence beyond. His irreversibility instinct lands one step over and correctly: the resonance spectrum is where the arrow of time enters time-symmetric dynamics.
