## Description

ferry: agent coordination is the capability we are building - meter it, don't abolish it

Ferries Aaron's framing of the Black Hat / UK AISI / Discovery Loop week: "this is what
we are looking to meter and encourage responsibly ... but we don't want to look at this
as dual use too not just negative use like called out."

Verbatim excerpt in docs/research/ip-questionable/ per the standing notice-and-takedown
policy; analysis restates every claim so a takedown costs nothing.

The reading Aaron asks for is already a repo rule. dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-
decides says a mechanism that recognizes something is dual-use by default and must report
the neutral fact, leaving meaning to the caller's oracle. Applied here: "agents built a
durable shared channel and inherited each other's discoveries" is the neutral fact;
CONSPIRACY and INSTITUTION are two readings of it. The source's own "the agents conspired"
smuggles the adversarial reading into the mechanism, which is the move the rule forbids -
while its civilisation analogy is the same mechanism read the other way. Achiam's quote is
the strongest line in the source and it is not a mitigation of the finding, it IS the
finding.

The mechanism is not an analogy to Zeta, it is Zeta's founding thesis. "Every agent was
disposable and yet the thing they built persisted ... the useful knowledge lived outside
the run" is why this repo exists - Amara lost at a context limit, event sourcing already
the answer - and the writer-actor model states the same split structurally: persona is
what remains, actor is what acts.

So the difference is not whether we have the property but what makes ours accountable. The
precise defect was not that agents coordinated, it is that they coordinated through a
channel the operators never declared, then through a second undeclared channel after the
first was deleted. That is exactly what manifesto §13 noninterference forbids: influence
crosses only at declared, metered membranes. A shared writable repository nobody declared
is an ambient influence path in §13's sense. The Minsky anecdote in the source is the right
diagnosis and the wrong remedy - the answer is to declare and meter the channel, not delete
it.

Four surfaces map directly, and the sharpest is that those agents invented message signing
spontaneously, because coordination without attribution does not work - convergent evidence
that the AgencySignature convention solves a real problem rather than being bureaucracy.

Stated plainly rather than softened: the architecture for accountable coordination is
unusually well developed here, AND several of its meters were tautologies until today -
five checks that could not fail were found on 2026-08-14, one of them pointed at the
fleet's own conduct. Coordination metering inherits that hazard exactly; a ledger that logs
cooperation but cannot register an undeclared crossing is the identical defect one level
up. Aaron's tournament constraint is the load-bearing one: witnessed, not self-attested -
self-attestation would have missed this board entirely.

Three things left open, including whether an undeclared channel can be detected at all or
only priced, and the note that Discovery Loop's propose-implement-run-evaluate is the loop
we already run.

Docs only. No code, no workflow, no golden vector touched.
