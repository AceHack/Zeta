## Description

research: attention density and legibility are one constraint — foveation, frost, and the child-prediction falsifier

Aaron 2026-08-23: "how do we improve this for more local browser-based
intelligence/attention density, under demonstration that most humans — even
low-IQ ones who are young — will understand?"

The two halves look opposed (density buys compression; compression is what makes
a system opaque) and are not: density means spending compute where it matters,
spending compute where it matters IS attention, and attention is the one
mechanism a child already owns because it is how their own eyes work. So the
optimisation and the explanation are the same object rendered twice.

Six design moves, each labelled by register:
- The belief field IS the display. Since belief update = additive blend on
  rgba32float (Diaconis-Ylvisaker, established in the GPU-mapping doc), the
  posterior already lives in a texture and showing it is a blit, not a second
  render pass. You stop maintaining two representations, and there is no
  translation layer to drift.
- Foveation: full resolution only where variance is high. The compute win and the
  moving spotlight are the same mechanism. Anchored to Yarbus 1967 (scanpaths
  vary with the question asked) WITH the honest limit that his stronger
  decodability reading has replication trouble (Greene/Liu/Wolfe 2012) — cite him
  for what this design uses, not for what it does not need.
- Uncertainty renders as frost, reusing GlassHalo/RoomBoundary vocabulary, so a
  child learning the arena learns the real system's words rather than a parallel
  simplified set.
- The attention mechanism is already built and merely undrawn: DebouncedOracle
  already splits saccade (suppressed, prediction step) from fixation (accepted,
  update step), already in the eye's own terms.
- Stump Dad mode: a WHY button that bottoms out at "I don't know", which is the
  FEATURE — true, the four-register discipline in a UI, and what makes it honest
  to a child who will absolutely keep clicking. Constraint: answers must be
  generated from the state that drove the decision, never a string table, or the
  explanation is the vacuity class wearing a teaching voice.

And Aaron's correction mid-draft — "we have an attention router for the society
level vs the individual" — which changes the proposal: the society router is
already in-tree (InformationValue.fs, Lindley/Friston), so foveation is that same
allocator one granularity down. Recorded as a CHECKABLE claim about manifesto §9/
§10: if they are genuinely the same allocator, one implementation serves both with
a granularity parameter, and if two are needed the self-similarity claim is
decorative and should be dropped. Honest failure mode named (society allocates a
scarce indivisible turn; the field allocates a divisible parallel resource).
Payoff for the demo: show both zoom levels at once and self-similarity is
demonstrated rather than explained.

The section that matters most is the falsifier, because comprehensibility
asserted is not comprehensibility measured and a demo never put in front of a
person is a check that did not run: pause at a decision, ask the viewer "where
will it go next?", compare accuracy with and without the overlay. If the overlay
does not raise prediction accuracy it is DECORATION. Cheap, can fail, needs no
instrumentation, and is outcome-based in Aaron's sense — the claim is about what
the viewer can now do.

Density likewise metered not asserted, per his spec that meters measure precisely
and fail loudly on ambiguity: useful-work fraction = invocations that moved a
belief by more than epsilon. When the variance field is flat the allocator has no
signal and the meter must say AMBIGUOUS rather than silently sampling uniformly
and reporting a number.

Labelled toy throughout; §6 and §7 are what would earn the promotions. Honest
limits kept, including that foveation MISSES things and the demo should show that
happening rather than hide it.
