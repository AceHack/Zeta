## Summary

Closes `081KZZYETRX087G0R000Q52KAA`. The chaos sweep's burst-length grid was `[1, 2, 4, 8]`. Nothing in it can produce a gap of `MAX_NACK_GAP` (64) at a realistic sample size, so the receiver's desync branch was never walked by the harness — only by hand-built unit cases. That is the instrument defect that hid `ULT-34`'s bound.

The top of the grid is now derived from the window, so the two cannot drift apart:

- `deriveSweepBurstLengths(maxNackGap)` — powers of two through the NACK window, then `maxNackGap + floor(maxNackGap / 2)`
- `SWEEP_BURST_LENGTHS` — that function applied to `MAX_NACK_GAP` (currently `[1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 96]`)
- **UCH-28**: exported grid equals the derivation, max exceeds `MAX_NACK_GAP`, and a GE trace at the past-desync L produces runs wider than the window
- **UCH-27**: walks the derived in-window points and the derived past-desync point (no remembered `100`)
- **UCH-12**: still samples `[1, 4]` — cliff characterisation at 2000 blocks cannot afford the full grid; the comment says so
