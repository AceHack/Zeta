## Description

Recovers 210 lines stranded when #10447 auto-merged on its opening commit.

Auto-merge was armed; the gate went green on the first SHA and GitHub merged it. Two later pushes to the same branch — the **society-claim addendum** (the Press & Dyson answer, adversarial teaching as a required game class) and the **TAS-capability-class addendum** (derive the label from observed channel access, never accept a self-declaration) — never reached main.

Content is unchanged from what was pushed. This is recovery, not revision.

## The mechanism, worth recording

Third stranding this session, and the first caused by my own arming. **Auto-merge plus append-to-an-open-PR is an unsafe combination**: arming makes the merge fire at whatever moment the gate goes green, which races every subsequent push to that branch.

Two ways to avoid it, both cheap:
- arm auto-merge only when the PR is final, or
- append by opening a **new** PR rather than pushing to an armed branch.

The standing habit of verifying content on `origin/main` after every merge is what caught this, which is why the loss was one tick rather than permanent.
