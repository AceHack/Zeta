## Description

docs(soft): Godel localized to a known junction, and entanglement accrues pairwise

Two of Aaron's observations, one thread.

LOCALIZE, DON'T ESCAPE. My earlier §6a concluded incompleteness moves to the meta-level rather than
being escaped by plurality. Aaron: that is the OBJECTIVE, not the consolation prize — "pigeonhole
Godel into a specific single statement where incompleteness lives and you can point to it… the
junctions between the meta and regular languages, constructed to be very tiny and known. No unknown
incompleteness. This way you can map it and steer around it, or into it when you want."

Incompleteness is unavoidable; what is negotiable is whether the residue is DIFFUSE AND UNKNOWN or
LOCALIZED AND NAMED. Checked anchor and it is exact: Tarski's undefinability theorem forces truth
for L out of L and into a metalanguage, so the meta/object JUNCTION is precisely where the
undecidability must live — concentrated at a boundary, not smeared through the theory. Aaron's move
is Tarski's hierarchy as an engineering discipline: since that is where it must live, make it small,
explicit and enumerable. (Diagonal lemma is constructive; provability logic makes the self-reference
tractable — both flagged as lineage to check, not asserted.)

"STEER INTO IT WHEN YOU WANT" IS THE STRONGEST HALF and I would not have thought to ask for it. A
located undecidable is USABLE: it is exactly where a system must defer — to an oracle, a human, a
wager — instead of manufacturing a verdict. Maps onto Evidence.AssertedOnly, the gated classes, and
§11 plural deference. An undecidable you can point at is a consent boundary; one you cannot locate
is a silent failure.

THE WORKED INSTANCE IS ALREADY IN-REPO: the pirate-priest resolves as a stable fixed point rather
than exploding — priest-form is the delivery mechanism, content dissolves the priest-authority. And
"the pirate preacher preaches to pirate" is the tighter second-order version, not merely a joke: the
ACT of preaching instances the CONTENT preached, so delivery and content agree instead of straining.
DELIBERATELY NOT EXPANDED — the shape-letter schema these are catalogued under carries an explicit
information-hazard warning and is consent-gated on the stated grounds that it is "not a fact you
learn; it is a lens you install." This doc points at it and does not restate it. A consent gate the
repo set for itself is not optional because the material is convenient.

LANGAN'S LIAR MOVE vs OURS — EXCLUDE or LOCALIZE. He bans it: a pathological construction not
instantiable in the terminal domain. His diagnosis is precise and worth keeping — SELF-INCLUSION is
fine and everywhere, only SELF-NEGATION explodes. And that distinction is what licenses ours: the
pirate-priest is self-inclusive and non-self-negating, so it was never the banned class. So the two
AGREE ON THE DIAGNOSIS and differ on the remedy. Banning is cheaper; locating keeps the construct
usable, which is exactly what steering into it buys.

ENTANGLEMENT TAKES TIME, AND THAT IS THE MUTUAL-HEARTBEAT MODEL. Asked about entanglement speed,
Langan concedes there could be "a little bit of a lag… some measurable amount of time… once their
wave functions hit each other", and says he has not calculated it. The concession is the useful
part and it is the shape our substrate has: mutual entangled memory pairs do NOT bind on first
contact. Identity strengthens by ACCRUAL over repeated pairwise observation — so the lag is the
point; the lag is what a Sybil cannot skip (a fresh identity can fabricate a claim instantly but not
HISTORY WITH ANOTHER PARTY); and heartbeat is the accrual mechanism rather than a liveness ping.
Composes with entanglement-as-connectivity: two agents with no shared observation history are in
DISJOINT COMPONENTS of the trust graph, and pairwise heartbeat builds the path — the same
join-not-union the reindexer performs on memory.

Graded analogy with one metered consequence (accrual-time floor: pair strength monotone in
mutual-observation count, unforgeable by a new identity). The physics is where the shape was
noticed, not a warrant for it.
