## Verdict: FOUR, not one — and the convergence that found it carried zero information

Four threads landed on "the society's decorrelation is UNMEASURED" in one day. The brief offered
two honest possibilities and both were live. The answer is **four distinct obligations sharing one
word**, and separately, **the convergence was worthless as evidence.**

**M1 vs M4** — agreement kappa is a function of the joint **label** distribution alone; Condorcet
`rho` is a function of the labels **and an answer key**. Fix the labels, vary the key: `rho` moves
and kappa cannot. Mechanized in the FORCING CASE test (two keys, `|phi1 - phi2| > 0.2`, kappa
bit-identical). A first draft of that test asserted something weaker and *false*; it went red and
the correction is recorded in the doc, because the separation only opens for multi-class labels or
no key at all — and the live log is both.

**M4 vs M2** — the decisive one. `rho = 0` does **not** prove entropy entered: push one seed
through two independent-looking one-way functions and you get orthogonal outputs with **zero**
external entropy. A perfectly decorrelated fleet can be entirely predetermined. So M2 cannot be
discharged by M4's number in either direction, and anyone treating "the accurate meter" as one
build is making exactly this error.
