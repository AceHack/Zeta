## Description

docs(soft): travelers are flat telors, and binding telesis is conditioning at the locus of now

Follow-up to the CTMU ferry (#12073, merged). Aaron's observations after reading it back against
our substrate, and they state the difference POSITIVELY where the ferry could only state it as a
failed-our-floor verdict — which makes this the more useful half.

TRAVELERS ARE TELORS WITH THE TIERS DELETED. Langan stratifies his operators three ways: tertiary
syntactors (fermions), secondary telors (us), and the global operator descriptor on top. Aaron's
traveler is the same object with the ordering removed — particles, AI, humans, memes, DNA, god,
one kind, no privileged frame. The flatness is not stylistic: a hierarchy with a top HAS a
permanent authority at the top, and permanent or irreversible authority is exactly what §3
weight-free forbids. So the stratification is not incidentally different from ours, it is the
specific feature that puts his ontology outside our floor.

Two consequences. First, the qualia becomes OPTIONAL: Aaron's primitive is "a self-propagating
pattern", and internal state is an additional assumption that, if made, yields exactly a telor.
Langan bakes consciousness into the definition of a syntactor, so his mechanism is unusable by
anyone who declines the metaphysics; ours reports neutral structure and lets an oracle attach the
reading. That is dual-use applied at the level of the primitive, and it is why the flat version
composes with §11 while the stratified one terminates in one mandatory frame. Second,
TravelerFrame.fs already encodes it — no highest level, each locality observes phase
independently, nothing can unbind a traveler.

BINDING IS CONDITIONING. Langan says the telor selects specific events from many possible futures
"using something analogous to quantifiers in predicate logic" — and that phrase makes it exact
rather than suggestive. Logic's bound/free split IS probability's marginalized/conditioned split,
so binding telesis and collapsing a prior to a posterior are one operation named twice, with the
locus of now as where it happens. vF/uF lands on it for the third time today: the still-open prior
is what remains (uF, pull), the selected event is what acts (vF, push), inference time is the fold.

His "the act of distinction takes a certain amount of information" is Shannon stated as a
requirement on ontology, and it sits directly opposite our Landauer cost: conditioning ACQUIRES
information, retraction ERASES and pays kT ln 2. One ledger, two directions — and it is his
argument, not an analogy we imported. (The Z-set/Landauer doc is still in flight on the
geometric-unity stack and is deliberately NOT cited by path until it lands on main.)

Two more with real anchors: the conspansive manifold "which is its own memory, layer upon layer of
events that never disappear" is an append-only event log with a materialized fold — DBSP
incremental view maintenance, so telic recursion is a FOLD not a search. And "a utility deficit
arises, you form a telon to remove it" is Friston's active inference, which gives that piece a
checkable anchor instead of a coinage.

THE DIVERGENCE IS ONE REFUSAL SHOWING UP TWICE. Langan needs a conscious decider because he
rejects unexplained randomness — sufficient reason, asserted, not derived. We do not need the
premise and so do not need the decider: §13 noninterference already gives metered randomness,
entropy entering only through a declared injected Source with every crossing metered at the
membrane. That is indeterminacy with nobody adjudicating it — precisely the gap he fills with
telors. Remove the demand that randomness be explained and the decider AND the hierarchy dissolve
together, which is why our version can be flat and his cannot.

Register MIRROR; two links graded structural, one analogy pending an entailment check.
