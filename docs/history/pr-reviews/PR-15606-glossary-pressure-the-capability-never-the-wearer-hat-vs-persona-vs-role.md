## Description

The hat/persona/role relationship has been stated on twenty dated statements
across fifteen surfaces since 2026-05-08 and modelled in F# since 2026-06-08,
and the relationship between the three terms was never written down.
`docs/GLOSSARY.md` defined all three separately; nothing said how they compose.

This is a Mirror -> Beacon compression of what already exists, not a new
coinage. The carved rule is the mechanism, not the taxonomy:

    Pressure the capability, never the wearer.

A hat should compete, earn, be outcompeted, be abandoned — and the persona that
wore it is unharmed and wears another. Selection pressure without existential
threat. (Beacon: Popper 1972, "we let our hypotheses die in our stead.")

- `docs/GLOSSARY.md` — one section plus cross-references added to the existing
  `Role`, `Persona (overloaded)`, and `Hat` entries. The existing definitions
  are not rewritten.
- `docs/research/2026-08-26-...` — provenance, the checked anchors, the five
  adversarial passes, and one open question.
