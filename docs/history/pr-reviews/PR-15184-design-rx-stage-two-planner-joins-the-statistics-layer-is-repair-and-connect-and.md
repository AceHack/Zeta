## The framing correction that reorganises the work

Stage two is **not greenfield**. A cost model already ships (`src/Core/Plan.fs`), a five-sketch family already ships (CountMin, HLL, HyperMinHash, KLL, Bloom), and `Plan.fs`'s own docstring already names the missing wire between them — pointing at a `docs/BACKLOG.md` "query-planner P1" row that **does not exist** (1163 lines, zero occurrences of "planner"). Treating stage two as a build job would produce a second disconnected cost model beside the first.

## The blocking technical finding

`Plan.fs` proposes wiring `Sketch.fs` HLL in for real cardinality. **HLL merges by `max` and has no decrement**, so on a retracting DBSP stream it measures "keys ever seen", never "keys currently present". Since `V(·,k)` sits in the join formula's **denominator**, the bias *deflates* the join estimate and worsens monotonically over a long-running circuit — invisible on short runs, memory growth on long ones. Same for HyperMinHash.

`docs/research/bloom-filter-frontier.md` already names retraction compatibility as *"the gating criterion for DBSP"*. It was applied to membership filters and never to the cardinality sketches.

Separately, `Plan.fs`'s join formula is Selinger's with the wrong denominator — row counts substituted for distinct values, hardcoding a primary-key assumption that cannot express a many-to-many join in either direction.

## The noninterference answer is already in the repo

It is the maintainer's own principle — Aaron #7032, *stream-metadata is an event within the same stream as the data*. Statistics are a **`Meta` projection of the stream being measured, not a side channel observing it.** That discharges §13 (no new door), §7 (pure fold over phase), §8 (hub/satellite by change rate), §1/§2 (`Union`-mergeable, no shared counter), and splits §12 honestly: HLL/HyperMinHash are idempotent under max-merge, **CMS is not** and needs a dedup key. Every statistic is denominated in **phase**, never wall-clock seconds.

## Anchor defects found by checking rather than citing

- No query-optimisation lineage in `PRIOR-ART-LIST.md` at all — and its one "Selinger" is **Peter** Selinger's *Potrace*, a different person from the SIGMOD 1979 author `Rx.fs` cites.
- HyperMinHash attributed to **Cohen–Lemire** there, but to **Yu & Weber** in `NovelMath.fs` — two surfaces, two sets of humans, one sketch.
- HyperLogLog implemented with **no citation at all** (Flajolet et al. 2007, Flajolet & Martin 1985, Whang et al. 1990 all unnamed).
- KLL claims a *"provable error bound"* citing FOCS 2016, while its own comment concedes a *"simplified single-level"* implementation the theorem does not cover.

`CountMin.fs`'s conditional-bound docstring is the standard to copy — real citation plus an explicit account of where the code departs from the theorem's hypotheses.

Also recorded: **`KllSketch` holds `let rnd = Random 0`** — an ambient, undeclared entropy source inside a statistic — and its `Union` is non-associative, so shard-merge does not commute.

## Open questions needing a human decision

Eight, chiefly: the **decay family** (forward decay recommended — backward decay reintroduces the local-time leak, sliding windows break `Union` mergeability), and the **planner shape** — the weight-free and scale-free rules argue for eddies-style continuous routing, while `Plan.fs` and `zeta-sql-frontend-dsl-design.md` both assume a static plan. That is a live conflict and not mine to resolve.
