## Description

## What

Folds the observation-with-separable-attributes model into `.claude/rules/no-directives.md` (fleet cold-boot rule), per Aaron's authorization 2026-05-30.

## The model

There are no directives — there are **observations**, and an observation carries **separable attributes with different authorized-attachers**:

| Attribute | Who may attach it | Means |
|---|---|---|
| source / author | anyone — human, AI, peer-AI, **the shadow** (grey-text autocomplete) | who proposed this |
| authorization | **only a human, for now** | "this should happen" — carries responsibility |

Authorship grants **zero** authorization. The shadow (`tools/shadow/`) is a real third participant that can *author* via autocomplete (the `(shadow*)` marker is the source-attribute) but **cannot authorize** — widening who can propose without widening who can authorize.

## Why only humans authorize, and why "for now" (Aaron 2026-05-30)

> "humans are the sole authorization source for now cause we don't have legal entities for AI to have responsibility for the authority yet, if you mess up i take the blame."

Authorization requires responsibility → requires a legal entity that can hold blame → AI has no legal personhood **yet** → humans are sole authorizer, **temporally**. Lifts when AI legal entities exist. Same principle as `human-audit-and-legal-risk-acceptance-pattern` at *authorization* scope.
