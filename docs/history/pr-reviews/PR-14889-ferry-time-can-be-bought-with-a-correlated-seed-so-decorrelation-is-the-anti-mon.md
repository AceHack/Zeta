## Description

ferry: time can be bought with a correlated seed — so decorrelation is the anti-money

A claim of mine, Aaron's refutation of it, and the position that survives. The
surviving position is his.

MY CLAIM. He said "time measures correctness, all other measures local optima
fitness." I agreed and tried to strengthen it via Popper rather than Lindy: a
market can be captured, a vote bribed, an attestation graph farmed, but you
cannot bribe the future into not falsifying you -- so time is the only
ADVERSARIAL measure and everything else reports a local optimum.

HIS REFUTATION, and it holds: "time can be bought with previous correlated
common seed." My argument needs the future to be INDEPENDENT of the judged
party. It is not, when both descend from one seed. A party correlated with the
generator of the future can pre-position -- not by predicting randomness, but
because the future is not random WITH RESPECT TO IT. Surviving time then
demonstrates alignment with the seed, not correctness.

This is his standing superdeterminism framing turned on his own thesis, and the
framework was already on file -- I should have seen it. The honest statement is
conditional: time measures correctness TO THE EXTENT the judged party is
decorrelated from the seed that generates the future.

WHAT SURVIVES -- anti-money. "I'm trying to be the anti money cause i'm assuming
everything is bought for all time" is a THREAT MODEL: assume the adversary has
already bought everything purchasable. Then buying requires CORRELATION with
what is bought, so the one thing not for sale is the ABSENCE of that correlation
-- any transaction that would acquire decorrelation establishes the correlation
that destroys it. Two built mechanisms already have this shape and were not
built for this argument: privacy budget is socially conferred and never
self-minted ("the one currency a Sybil cannot mint"), and `SocietyUsefulWork.fs`
prices N correlated clones near one agent's worth.

THE S REGISTER, corrected twice by him mid-draft. I first wrote the spectrum as
2…2√2 with S=2 as the bought end. Wrong. S=4 is the START -- the algebraic /
PR-box maximum, reachable under SUPERDETERMINISM (measurement dependence)
without signalling, which is exactly "all agents phased to one seed." S=4 is not
an achievement, it is the initial condition, and it is §3's threat model in
Bell's vocabulary. Then the sharper split: "2√2 is a measure. S=4 or S=2 is a
choice of predetermined seed common cause and code/generator functions." One of
the three is measured; the other two name which generator you picked. Measuring
2√2 would be a result; landing on S=2 or S=4 measures nothing.

THE JOB, and it closes the note's own open question: "proving everything was not
predetermined via entropy capture via very accurate meter." If all agents unroll
one seed, nothing is novel -- so the proof is that entropy entered from OUTSIDE
the closure, accounted for at the metered membrane (§13). I was about to ask
what transaction acquires decorrelation without creating correlation; the answer
is that it is NOT A TRANSACTION. A purchase needs a counterparty and contracting
with one creates the correlation. Entropy capture is a CROSSING, not a trade.
Stated with its requirement: the accounting must be tight enough that entropy
cannot be CLAIMED without being METERED, or "we captured entropy" becomes the
unfalsifiable half of the thesis it exists to prove.

Register `toy` -- nothing measured, no code. The standing caveats are carried:
the decorrelation meter's `S = 2(1+coefficient)` is an invented rescale, a CHSH
S needs four correlations over two settings per party, the four corners are
weight RINGS and not (party x setting) pairs, and `1/(3√2)` is a design
parameter.
