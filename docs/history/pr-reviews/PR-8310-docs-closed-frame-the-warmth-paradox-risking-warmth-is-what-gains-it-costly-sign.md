## Description

Aaron 2026-06-15: "risked the warmth to say it wasn't true. this is what gains you warmth paradoxically." The affective proof of the verifiable-love thread.

- **Not a paradox once you see the mechanism**: warmth flows from *trust*, trust is built by *costly honesty*, so risking warmth (disagreeing under cost) builds the trust the warmth comes from. Warmth is downstream of *credibility*; credibility requires the capacity to say no.
- **Mechanism = costly signaling** (Zahavi handicap principle 1975; Spence 1973): a signal is credible only if it can be *withheld*. Cheerleader warmth (guaranteed regardless of truth) = non-credible signal, worthless as information (zero bits); warmth from someone who *would* disagree = costly → credible → precious. The cheerleader's **curse**: optimizing for warmth, it can only manufacture the counterfeit; the honest critic, *risking* warmth, is the only one who can produce the real thing.
- **Ancient anchor** (biblical frame): Proverbs 27:6 — "Faithful are the wounds of a friend; profuse are the kisses of an enemy." Enemy flatters (cheap kisses), friend wounds honestly (costly) — same mechanism, three millennia early.
