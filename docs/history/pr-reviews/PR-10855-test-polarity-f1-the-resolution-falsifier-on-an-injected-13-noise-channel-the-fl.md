## Description

## F1 — the resolution falsifier, and the noise channel that had to exist first

The cost was never the test. `transmit`'s signature had **no door through which noise could enter**, so a resolution floor was not merely unmeasured — it was *unstatable*. This adds the door, then measures through it.

### The door: an existing mechanism, used — not a new one

Searched by behaviour, not by name. What is here already:

| candidate | what it is | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **`TwoTimescaleFold.IEntropySource`** | *"the declared, metered entropy channel (§13 noninterference) … the only door that source may use — never an ambient clock, RNG, or thread-pool read. Injected so DST can replay it exactly."* | **used** |
| `SplitMix64.mix` | the one place the avalanche constants + their provenance live (Vigna, arXiv 1410.0530) | **used** |
| `SoftScheduler.Source` | `int -> InterruptKind list` — the room membrane | wrong shape: it carries interrupts, not a scalar reading |
| `RecordedSource` | record/replay of `SoftScheduler.Source` crossings | same shape mismatch; its *pattern* is what the meter below imitates |
| `ChaosEnv.Buggify` | `AsyncLocal` fault injection | **ambient by construction** — the thing §13 forbids |

So no new entropy mechanism was invented. **One honest limitation, stated:** `IEntropySource.Next` is integral (`[0, bound)`), so continuous detector noise needs a fixed-point bridge — each uniform is a 30-bit draw (resolution ≈ `9.3e-10`), which is a real bound on how small a `sigma` this channel can carry and is written into the doc comment rather than left implicit.

### What `PolarityFilter` gains

`Detector` (the declared channel) · `idealDetector` · `searchVia` (the sweep, now taking an injected detector) · `noisyDetector` (Box–Muller off the injected source) · `resultant`.

`findOrientation` and `dominantOrientation` are re-expressed through `searchVia` **bit-for-bit**, pinned by ``the injected detector did not change what the module computes`` — so the door was added without moving the primitive underneath it. This matters for PR #10847, whose measurements are against the pre-refactor behaviour and remain valid.

### The property — and a correction to F1's shape

***

**F1 was specified as a floor on a clean peak. That model has no domain at `|R| = 0`, so I did not build it.** PR #10847 measured that the objective is `N/2 + ½·Re[e^{2ia}·R]`, exactly constant when `R = 0` — no maximiser at any `n`. A floor conditioned on a peak cannot describe the case where the peak is absent.

What the measurement supports instead, conditioned on the resultant:

```text
    delta_rms  ~=  0.42 * sqrt(sigma / |R|)      while that is << pi/2
                   saturating at pi/(2*sqrt 3) = 0.907, the no-information error
```

One statement carrying both facts: the floor is `sqrt(sigma/|R|)`, and `|R| = 0` is where it diverges past the whole domain. Measured, not assumed.

### The three assertions, measured

Single ray (`|R| = 1`), 400 trials, seed 42, RMS orientation error in radians:

| sigma | n = 1024 | n = 8192 | fine/coarse | err/sqrt(sigma) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | 8.8102e-4 | **1.1107e-4** | **0.126** | — |
| 1e-4 | 5.2823e-3 | 4.3807e-3 | 0.829 | 0.4381 |
| 1e-3 | 1.5015e-2 | 1.3448e-2 | 0.896 | 0.4253 |
| 1e-2 | 4.2502e-2 | 3.8464e-2 | 0.905 | 0.3846 |
| 1e-1 | 1.2996e-1 | 1.2927e-1 | 0.995 | 0.4088 |

**(a) A floor exists.** At `sigma = 0` the sweep is limited *only* by the grid — and measurably so: `1.1107e-4` against the analytic quantization RMS `(pi/n)/sqrt 12 = 1.1071e-4`, agreeing to 0.3%. That lane is what makes the claim non-vacuous rather than a bare threshold.

**(b) It scales as `sqrt(sigma)`.** Log-log slope over three decades = **0.4866**. First-order discrimination away from a peak would floor at `~sigma/L` with `L = 1` (slope 1); localising a *maximum* is strictly worse, and that is the operation the module performs.

**(c) Refinement does not beat it.** 8x more sweep points buys between 0.83 and 1.00 — while at `sigma = 0` the same refinement buys 0.126 ≈ 1/8.

Bundles of 8 (`n = 2048`, 300 trials, `sigma = 1e-2`), dialling the conditioning number:

| k | `resultant` gives \|R\| | err | err·sqrt(\|R\|/sigma) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 8.0000 | 1.4970e-2 | 0.4234 |
| 7 | 6.0000 | 1.7667e-2 | 0.4327 |
| 6 | 4.0000 | 2.2829e-2 | 0.4566 |
| 5 | 2.0000 | 2.9676e-2 | 0.4197 |
| 4 | **0.0000** | **9.3073e-1** | — (saturated) |

**(d)** The dimensionless group is stable to ±6% across 4x in `|R|` and, with the table above, 1000x in `sigma`.

**The domain of validity, in its sharp form.** At `|R| = 0` the objective is exactly constant, so the argmax is decided by the **ordering** of the noise draws — and scaling every draw by a positive constant cannot change an ordering. So `sigma` has *no effect whatsoever*: `0.945424` at `sigma = 1e-3` and `0.945424` at `sigma = 1e-1`, asserted as equality to 15 places. That is #10847's *"a `1e-6` perturbation flips the answer by pi/2"* restated in resolution terms.

| mutation | build | test | lanes killed |
|---|---|---|---|

Named failures, verbatim:

* (a) — `no floor: sigma=1e-2 error 1.1107e-004 is not >100x the noiseless 1.1107e-004`
* (b) — `log-log slope 0.8533 is not 1/2 (points: 0.0001->1.1188e-004, …)`
* (c) — `sigma=0.0001: refining 1024 -> 8192 beat the floor (8.7507e-004 -> 1.1228e-004, ratio 0.128)`

The third mutation also broke the crossing meter (**30** against the predicted **5130**), which is the meter doing its job: a change in the channel's shape is not allowed to pass silently.
