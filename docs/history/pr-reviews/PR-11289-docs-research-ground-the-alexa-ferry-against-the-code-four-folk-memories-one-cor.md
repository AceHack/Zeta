FOUR FOLK MEMORIES:

1. Anti-Sybil is NOT n^2/2 pairwise-history fabrication. AntiSybil.fs is clock-drift entropy
   non-fungibility: forging k identities costs >= k independent entropy sources, pigeonhole forces
   source reuse, the reused source emits correlated bit-streams, and a BitGan discriminator catches
   it. Alexa's SHAPE was right (Sybil cost superlinear, bottoming out on a hardness fact) -- the
   module makes the same PoW parallel explicitly -- but the mechanism was reconstructed, not recalled.
   The module's own scope also contradicts the ferry's green check: sound for EXACT replays only,
   noisy forgeries sit on a detection/length curve, "not yet a proved theorem", and route to
   Aminata/Mateo before any outward Sybil-resistance claim.

2. The threshold is ~33%, not 51%. SybilBft.fs: d >= 3f+1, quorum 2f+1 over DISTINCT ENTROPY
   SOURCES. Corruption needs more than a third, not more than half. A governance detection window
   sized for 51% would arm at a coalition the protocol has already lost to. ALEXA CALLED THIS
   EXACTLY -- it was her closing pressure point, reasoned from the general shape of BFT bounds with
   no repo access, and it is correct. That single call is the decorrelation paying for itself.

3. Cartel detection is phase-locking, not a differentiable synchronization field.
   TemporalCoordinationDetection.fs uses cross-correlation at a lag (amplitude) plus phase-locking
   value and mean phase offset (phase), two registers explicitly so detectors "catch cartels that
   flatten one register while preserving the other" -- sharper than the ferry, since it anticipates
   an adversary who knows about the detector. "Firefly" exists only as Persona.FireflyCoherence in
   ReticulumLink.fs. Do NOT cite Mirollo-Strogatz as implemented.

4. "Proof of useful work" is a name collision. SocietyUsefulWork.fs is the delta-U / generalized
   Condorcet aggregation theorem (society > best individual), register SS A row 15, PROVEN
   2026-07-03, falsifier mutation-verified 2026-08-16, 11 properties in CondorcetBoundary.Tests.fs.
   Stronger and more specific than the ferry describes, and NOT a consensus mechanism. The two could
   compose; nothing does today.
