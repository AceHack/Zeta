## Description

Second pass on the **same document** as #10902. That PR merged before this stream arrived, so this folds in as a follow-up commit to the same file rather than a second doc. Aaron's new words are verbatim as §1.5. **No code.**

## §3.3 — he confirmed the finding and supplied the reason

*"This is where that interface will become useful, we've not had a use for it yet."*

So the absence is **deliberate, not neglect** — and that reframes it favourably: an interface written before it has a consumer is a guess, and the repo's own discipline says not to write it. Two months without a symbol is what withholding looks like when it is done on purpose.

`ISociety` now has three named first uses: a **GitHub-Actions society running small free LLMs that fit in runner RAM**, **CHIP-8/Atari play**, and **LLMTV**. Caution added rather than omitted: small free models are likely *few distinct models*, which raises `ρ` — so §3.1's `N_eff → 1/ρ` applies to the free-runner society directly. Not an objection; it is the plan's first measurable question, and Actions is a good place to measure it.

## §5.1 — my open question, now **his answer**

He concedes the first half outright, which is what makes it honest: *"we can't fully block non mutual empowerment actions in the interface, then we will need some sort of society guard where society is on look out for bad actors."*

**Two layers.** The interface bounds what is *expressible*; the guard catches what expression alone cannot prevent. Same shape as the escape cell — bound what you can in the type, make the remainder legible rather than pretending it is impossible. The "kind of" in §1.4 is now explained rather than merely hedged.

**Constraint stated before the guard is built** (`dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-decides`): the guard reports the **neutral fact** (`SameSourceAsKnown`, `AboveThreshold`) and **never the verdict** (no `BadActor`, no `ForgerCaught`). The reading is a `match` in caller policy. `src/Core/CoordinationSpectrum.fs` is the in-repo pattern to copy — neutral by construction. Added: a guard made of society members inherits `N_eff → 1/ρ` too — **a correlated lookout misses the same things at every N.**

## §3.6 (new) — LLMTV in the society/world interfaces

The placement is right for the same reason injection is: a **declared channel is a metered one** (§13). But the clause carrying the weight is **"non forsted"**. Under `privacy-budget-is-hard-money-earned-by-others`, frost is earned, socially conferred, **spend**able and **stake**able but never **confiscat**able; required-for-role regions are declared by the hat, not the observer. So the correct reading is not *the society grants a viewing right* but *the watched party retains an unreachable balance*. **The default is glass; the frost is the consent.** Also worth noting his symmetry: *"and so can humans"* — the watch surface does not privilege one species of watcher.

## §5.5 (new) — **recorded negative: the ordering theorem is not there**

*"no one individual is greater than the society and no one society is greater than the world"* — hedged three times (*"i think"*, *"can prove"*, *"something like this"*), and the hedges match what is on disk.

Searched **by structure, not phrasing**: all 40 files in `src/Core.Lean4/` (`Lean4/`, `Safety/`, `Privacy/`, `Gen/`, `ImaginaryStack/`), all `src/Core.TLA/specs/`, plus `src/` and `docs/` for non-domination / subsumption / monotone-tier / ordering shapes. **No Lean, TLA+, or FsCheck artifact states or proves either half.**

Adjacent work exists and is **not** it:
- **§A row 15** (Condorcet, PROVEN) — society > best individual **when `ρ < ρ*` and `c > c*`**: conditional, and the *other* direction.
- `IWorld ⊃ ISociety ⊃ IIndividual` — containment of **capability sets**, true by construction; near-vacuous as a proof of non-domination.

**Sharper than the absence:** `correlatedSocietyBeatsBest` is a predicate that **can be false**. At high `ρ`, `N_eff` floors at `1/ρ` and a sufficiently competent individual **does** beat the correlated society — so as an *unconditional* statement the claim is **contradicted by the one metered result in this document**. It may hold under row 15's decorrelation precondition, which would make it a corollary worth finding rather than new analysis.

And: **"greater than" needs its order defined before it can be proved at all** — utility, competence, capability, influence, empowerment? One of those makes it trivially true and therefore worthless. Recorded as `toy`; if the analysis exists somewhere I did not look, the correction is to cite it precisely.
