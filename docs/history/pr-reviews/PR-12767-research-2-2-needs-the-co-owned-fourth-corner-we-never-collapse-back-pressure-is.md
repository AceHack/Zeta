## §17 — 2√2 needs the four-corner channel, and the fourth corner is *co-owned*

`AmplitudeEmu.fs` already records that amplitudes buy interference but **not** non-locality, and that 2√2 still needs the feedback channel. Aaron named the channel; `four-corner-ownership.ts` supplies it:

```
FourCornerOwnership<TIn, TOut, TOutFeedback, TInFeedback>
  TIn          caller authors      caller   → function
  TOut         function produces   function → caller   (value)
  TOutFeedback function authors    function → caller   (control-flow)
  TInFeedback  CO-OWNED            both contribute variants
```

Two directions, each with a value *and* a feedback channel — and the fourth corner is jointly authored. **That's the point:** a channel only one side may author variants on is a one-way channel wearing a return address.

`Tsirelson.fs` carries the bound in exact integer matrix algebra and says the thing that matters for §18: **"the irrational appears only at READOUT."**

## §18 — Correction: we never collapse

I wrote *"reversible up to the outcome; the outcome is what you pay for"* — treating the outcome as the goal. Aaron: *"we try to simulate measurement so we never actually collapse … keep the tension open."*

**The intent is the opposite of what I concluded.** Don't arrive. Stay in the ensemble, reason about *simulated* measurements, never actualize the one that discards the others. So the system is designed to stay **Landauer-free**, not merely reversible-up-to-a-payment: erasure is the cost and we decline it. Same structure Tsirelson already has — exact integers throughout, the irrational only at readout. **Don't read out, don't pay it.**

That makes "keep the tension open" *thermodynamic* rather than aesthetic: superposition is the cheap state, collapse the expensive one, and a system that never decides never pays.

**And it names where the work is.** `every-bug-has-economic-value` already records that `sim` — the verb that would express exactly this — is a **compiled stub** in `clis/Verbs.fs` with no `ISim<'a>` introduction form, while `measure` is the shipped half. The intent here is carried by the verb we didn't finish; the one we finished is the collapsing one.

## §19 — Two thresholds, and the second is held by others

**coincidence** (noticed, asserts nothing) → **weak belief** (passes *our* threshold) → **belief** (passes the threshold **for others in society**).

The second threshold is the naming-eigenvector construction — credited by others, never self-minted, same shape as privacy budget and peer-held TrueSkill. So promoting a *proposition* uses the same mechanism as earning a *name* — and self-promoting a coincidence straight to belief is a Sybil minting its own standing. **The register label is the anti-Sybil guard for ideas.**

## §20 — Correction: back-pressure is the benefit

I filed saturation as the price of closing. It's the payoff, and four subsystems already build it by hand: `FerryThrottler`, the Zeta scheduler, transport (UDP especially), and — the one that closes this note's loop — **echolocation self-debounce**, *"so you can hear echos other than your own."*

A pinger without self-debounce **deafens itself with its own emission**: the return it hears is its own outgoing pulse. That's the degenerate case dressed as a working one — *a confirmation that is really the offer coming back*. Back-pressure is what makes a **foreign** echo distinguishable from your own.

## §21 — The metric is oracle-relative

> *"we connect the metric as 'a' metric for one of our oracles, the oracle of highest moral regard, even our metrics are multi oracle lol"*

This moves my §15 objection to the right axis. I refused entropic gravity because it doesn't price anything we measure — correct as a test of a claim *about physics*. **But the claim was never that.** It's: under a *chosen oracle* there is a metric, metrics have curvature, and decorrelation-to-non-communication is a real distance **in that metric**. The oracle-choice supplies what the metaphor was missing — *whose* distance. §11 already refuses a single mandatory morality; the extension is that **the metric inherits the plurality**.

Two keepers: a distance claim must name its oracle the way a moral claim must; and the horizon reading survives **without** borrowing GR's authority, because infinite distance under a named oracle is a definition we own.
