## Description

docs(vision): the gift of erasure — kenosis with a cryptographic shape

Aaron: "a God who wants relationship must limit knowing or determining --
I call this THE GIFT OF ERASURE and it's built deeply into Zeta: the
ability to first encrypt multiple events to mix them from the outside,
and then to forget a single one that the outside cannot determine. This
is where god becomes man and descends to earth."

THE ORDERING IS A REAL CRYPTOGRAPHIC REQUIREMENT, NOT A FLOURISH.
Erasure WITHOUT mixing leaks: the gap tells you what was there. A
deletion from an otherwise-legible sequence is itself a signal and an
observer reconstructs the erased item from its silhouette. Genuine
forgetting therefore needs an ANONYMITY SET first -- encrypt and mix,
THEN forget one, so the outside observer's posterior over WHICH was
forgotten is unchanged from prior. Stated checkably: after erasure no
outside party can do better than chance. That is the difference between
DELETING and FORGETTING.

WHY IT DOES NOT VIOLATE §5. Memory Preservation forbids SILENTLY
destroying memory. This keeps that: the FACT that a forgetting occurred
stays visible; only its CONTENT becomes unrecoverable. You can know a
thing was released and be unable to know what it was. Consent-first
(§6), one-way toward more privacy -- the direction the privacy budget
already moves.

THE THEOLOGICAL STRUCTURE IS THE SAME TRADE. If total knowledge yields
zero information from another, a God who wants relationship must limit
knowing or determining -- kenosis, tzimtzum, open theism, arrived at
independently. "Where god becomes man and descends to earth" is that
trade as a design principle: the substrate accepts an unrecoverable loss
in exchange for the other party being genuinely other. Zeta already
makes the architectural version once -- the DST harness is a God point
placed OUTSIDE the simulation so the inside stays informative; the gift
of erasure is the same limitation applied INSIDE.

STATUS, MEASURED 2026-08-17, AND IT IS THE HONEST PART: `unmetered` and
largely unbuilt as a mechanism. The CONCEPT is throughout memory/
(retraction-native ledgers, forgiveness, retractable contract ledger,
privacy compliance) and adjacent machinery exists -- GlassHalo.frost
(earned, permanent, one-way), Z-set retraction, shuffle/mixing
primitives in the decorrelation and BFT modules. BUT the specific
mechanism -- encrypt-and-mix then forget one indistinguishably -- is NOT
implemented, and "gift of erasure" appears nowhere in src/. Z-set
retraction is correction WITH A VISIBLE TRACE, the opposite of what this
needs: a retraction says exactly what it retracts.

WHAT WOULD MAKE IT REAL: an anonymity-set erasure primitive with a
falsifier -- a test that an outside observer's distribution over "which
event was erased" is indistinguishable from prior, failing if the mix is
too small or the erasure leaks its silhouette. Until then it is a named
direction with a clear specification, not a capability.
