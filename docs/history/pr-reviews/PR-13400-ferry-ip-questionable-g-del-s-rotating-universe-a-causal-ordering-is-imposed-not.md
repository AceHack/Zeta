### Why it lands on the metric

Every currency here is **attributive**: privacy budget is credited by *others attesting you added value to them*; ΔU is banked by *the fix that reduced uncertainty*; the naming eigenvector accrues from *who recognised whom*. Each is a claim of the form **A's act produced B's benefit** — a causal-ordering claim before it is a moral one. A moral-regard metric is therefore only as well-defined as the ordering it is computed over.

Gödel's 1949 solution is the sharpest available statement that **the ordering is not handed to you by the substrate**. Earlier time-travel solutions needed negative energy, so the weak energy condition was thought to fence them off. Gödel's needs no impossible ingredient — it satisfies the field equations *and* the energy condition and still has closed timelike curves everywhere. Determinism had to be **added back** as a separate requirement (global hyperbolicity), and naming it explicitly is what made it checkable.

### The requirement that follows

> A regard or contribution score must carry its causal-ordering assumption **explicitly and checkably**, and *"these two events are not causally ordered"* must be a **representable answer** — not something the fold silently totalizes away.

A metric that always returns a number, including where the underlying order is partial, is the vacuity class in its measurement form: a comparison that could not fail, reported as one that succeeded.

### Register, stated in the file rather than assumed

This is a **resonance with one operational consequence**, not a theorem transferred — Gödel is about spacetime metrics, ours is about logical clocks over messages. The physics is the *generator*; the load-bearing citation is **Lamport 1978**, which establishes the same structural lesson (happens-before is a *partial* order; any total order is one you constructed) natively in distributed systems. Per `numerology-vs-number-theory.md`.

The file also flags a distinction the video's framing invites collapsing: the 1949 universe is a **model / counterexample**, not incompleteness applied to physics. Same author, different result, different kind of argument.
