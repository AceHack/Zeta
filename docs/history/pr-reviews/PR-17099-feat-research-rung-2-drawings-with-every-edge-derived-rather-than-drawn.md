feat(research): rung 2 — drawings, with every edge derived rather than drawn

Aaron's progression for the rendering surface: "we can go fome lines to drawings
over time then 3d renderoring based on multi lary tellesaling over time." Rung 1
(lines) landed in #17095. This is rung 2.

The 240 roots are the vertices of the GOSSET POLYTOPE 4_21, and its edges join
roots at 60 degrees. In the doubled integer coordinates already used, |r|^2 = 8,
so 60 degrees is exactly <r,s> = 8 * cos(60) = 4 -- an INTEGER test on integer
vectors. No tolerance to tune, no threshold to pick, nothing eyeballed. The edges
are read off the algebra the same way the vertices were.

THE FALSIFIER. 4_21 has 6720 edges and is 56-regular. Measured:

  edges: 6720   distinct vertex degrees: {56}   240 * 56 / 2 = 6720

Regularity is asserted over EVERY vertex rather than as an average, and the two
quantities are cross-checked against each other, so an edge test that happened to
preserve the count would still be caught by a non-uniform degree distribution.

Mutation-checked: changing the inner product from 4 to 2 fails four of the five
rung-2 assertions (8 pass / 4 fail); restored, 12 pass / 0 fail across both rungs.

The SVG emits exactly 6720 lines and 240 circles -- asserted, so the drawing
cannot quietly diverge from the structure it claims to be. That keeps the
invariant rung 1 set and rung 3 must also keep: NO COORDINATE AUTHORED BY HAND.
A rung needing a designer to place a vertex has left the substrate, which is the
line Aaron drew -- "if we cant generate it from clifford that's not our research."
