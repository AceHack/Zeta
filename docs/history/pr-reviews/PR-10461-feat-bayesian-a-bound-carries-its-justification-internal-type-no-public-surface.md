## 1. Does this earn a public type? No.

`internal` to `Zeta.Bayesian`, plus one `InternalsVisibleTo("Bayesian.Tests")`. **Zero public members added.**

Checked: every consumer of `OrbitalAsymmetryBudget.deltaMaxMs` lives inside `Zeta.Bayesian` — `ReticulumBusMeter.fs` (2 sites) and `GossipTelemetry.fs` (2 sites) — each narrowing immediately to the `int` of milliseconds `BusRegime.regimeOf` takes. Nobody outside the assembly constructs a justification and nobody reads one. The only caller that needed access was the test assembly, which is §19's `InternalsVisibleTo` redirect verbatim.

The asymmetry decides it: `internal → public` is a widening available any day; `public → internal` is a break. Under Hyrum's Law, shipping the DU publicly today buys a forever-contract for a consumer we have not met.

## 2. The narrowest form that catches the `1.2` — and the finding I did not expect

**The proposed DU alone would not have caught it.** `1.2` was a bare literal inside the expression that produced the float:

```fsharp
abs vProj * rttS / C_KM_S * 1000.0 * 1.2
```

Attaching a reason to the *result* does not help — one justification covers the whole expression and the multiplier stays invisible. A type pairing a float with a reason is decoration.

What catches it is deleting the operation:

> **A bound is the sum of named, justified terms. There is no multiply.**

`Bound`'s union case is `private`, so a total is derived by summation and never authored. To widen a bound you must state *what*, *how much*, and *why*. This encodes PR #10418's refutation rather than recording it: the residuals a margin was meant to cover are **additive**, so the refuted shape is structurally unrepresentable.

**Honest limit, stated in the module:** leaf magnitudes are still `float`. The guarantee is *no unnamed, unjustified contribution to a total* — **not** *no wrong arithmetic*. Rung 1 for the silence, rung 4 for the arithmetic. Overclaiming the guard would repeat the defect one level up.

## 3. Scope

**In:** constants whose being-too-small produces a *false verdict* (`BusRegime` convicts `OutOfCone` when `best > deadline + δ_max`). **Out:** policy knobs — a 30-second timeout has no truth value.

The maximal version costs a public `Zeta.Core` type, a migration touching hundreds of sites, and a `Justification` distribution ~95% `Assumption` — a discriminator that returns one case for most inputs discriminates nothing.

## 4. Byte-lock

**It never reaches the byte-lock, and that is the correct boundary.** Justification is provenance; the locked artifact is the number. It enters no hash, no wire, no fold. TypeScript has no DUs, so the guarantee would not survive the crossing anyway — and PR #10418 established `deltaMaxMs` is **not byte-lockable today at all** (`sin`/`cos`/`atan2` are not bit-identical across the oracles). F#-side only.

## 5. Migration — no sweep

An `Assumption "pre-dates this type"` pass makes thoughtless annotations indistinguishable from considered ones, destroying the audit signal on the day it is created. Instead: the type applies where a bound is authored or touched; an unwrapped `float` is honest because it claims nothing. Named limit — **this is not a census mechanism**; finding unwrapped constants is rung 3, a different job.
