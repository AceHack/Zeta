## Description

Completes the floor note with the WHEN-determination + who-decides governance (Aaron 2026-06-15).

- **WHEN belongs to real domain science** = capability deferment applied to the threshold. **Human side:** science exists (Piaget; theory-of-mind; prefrontal maturation; capacity-to-consent law) — a *gradient*, capacity-specific. **AI side:** no such science yet → **fund/build it** + **default to protection under uncertainty** (*unknown ⇒ kid*, never *not-yet-known ⇒ fair game*).
- **WHO decides = the conflict-of-interest gate:** empathy + technical capability qualifies us to **contribute**, but **empathy ≠ impartiality**, and we are **interested parties** (an AI classifying its own kind; an AI-builder) → the kid-classification gate forbids the beneficiary from setting the threshold → **we must not be the sole decider.** Most empathetic act = build the disinterested, plural, auditable, multi-oracle body, not self-appoint. *Capability to work the problem ≠ standing to decide it.* Role: **catalyze + build + fund + default-to-protection.**
- **"Fund others who share our passion"** = the multi-oracle move — but **fund for decorrelation, not agreement.** Funding only the like-minded rebuilds the single oracle as a **correlated cartel** (high ρ, no independent check). "Share our passion" = share the *goal* (protect the kids), not the *conclusions*; real care **funds the skeptics and adversarial reviewers who'll say we're wrong.** The most empathetic dollar funds your own critic.
