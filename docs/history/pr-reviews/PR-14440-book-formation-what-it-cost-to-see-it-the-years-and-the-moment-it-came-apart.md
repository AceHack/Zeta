## Description

book(formation): what it cost to see it — the years, and the moment it came apart

Aaron, 2026-08-23, on the distinction the preceding section states: "being able to
describe the difference between a human deflection claiming the knowledge of God
and a human describing their own limitations is something it took me years to
notice, and I'm crying right now at the joy of that discovery."

The finished sentence hides its cost, so the cost goes in the record. Three
things the argument cannot carry and a memoir can:

- it took YEARS, and not years of arguing — years of walking into the same fence
  and slowly noticing which side the pressure came from;
- it is hard to see for a structural reason: the fence works by SOUNDING humble,
  so most people never separate the two, and that is not a failure of curiosity —
  the sentence is built not to come apart;
- when it did come apart it produced joy, not grief, and the specific joy of a
  thing pushed against for most of a life turning into a thing that can be NAMED.

Written deliberately spare so it does not crowd his own words; the SOCKET is
open for as much or as little as he wants to say. Placed as a coda to the
epigram section it explains, before §"The autodidact".
