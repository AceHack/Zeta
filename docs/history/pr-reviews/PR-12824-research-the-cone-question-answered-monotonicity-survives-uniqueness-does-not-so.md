## Description

§34 handed over one question: `BeliefConvergence.observe` folds **unnormalized** `int64[]` weights while Čencov's hypothesis is **normalized** measures. Does the claim survive, or does it need the quotient?

**Answered. Verified locally before commit: 12 checks, 0 failed.**

## The claim isn't where you'd look for it

`BeliefConvergence.fs` makes **no metric claim at all** — commutativity, associativity, non-idempotence, dedup, zero-absorbing, nothing else. Neither do its tests. It lives in **three prose surfaces, all downstream of one sentence**, and the hypothesis violation sits *inside that sentence, one clause apart*:

> folds **unnormalized** non-negative weights … the **only** metric invariant under sufficient statistics, up to scale

And the module computing from it **silently takes the quotient the code does not**: `probabilities()` is a softmax on a reduced chart with pivot `θₙ = 0` — which kills exactly the direction Campbell's term measures.

## Verdict: the quotient buys UNIQUENESS, not MONOTONICITY

| check | result |
|---|---|
| **D1** Fisher–Rao, 20000 Markov morphisms, genuinely unnormalized `p` | **0 violations**, worst ratio `0.9494151528` |
| **D1n** Euclidean, *same morphisms* | **2673 violations**, worst ratio `2.5430683899` |
| **D3** `g_Fisher + c·mass`, `c ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 5, 100}` | monotone for **every** `c` |

So the three surfaces want the words **"up to scale, on the simplex."** On the cone, Campbell (1986) gives a one-parameter family and *"the only metric"* is false.

## Currently vacuous — said plainly

`BeliefConvergence` has **zero non-test callers in `src/`**; nothing computes a belief distance; KL runs on `SoftValue`, whose `build` divides by `total` — simplex by construction. **A distinction that changes no computed value is not yet a bug**, and the finding is not inflated into one.

## Where it *does* become load-bearing

> **A choice of `c` decides which of two beliefs is closer — and on the simplex that choice is invisible.**

- **D4** — **8833/20000 (44.2%)** of tangent pairs are order-flippable by some `c ≥ 0`; hand instance crosses at exactly **`19/81`**. **D4n** — after the quotient: **0/20000**.
- **D2n** — max mass term: cone `61.4896`, simplex `2.4074e-31`. *That* is why it's invisible.
- **D3n** — positive definite for `c > −1`, and **`c = −1` degenerates exactly on the radial direction**. So **the `c = −1` endpoint IS normalization** — computed, not asserted.

`c = 0` is a **choice, not a default**: it asserts total evidence mass carries no information.
