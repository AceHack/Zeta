## Description

At Aaron's *"build it on the harm-signal, save this, it's not obvious."* The **non-obvious trap:** the intuitive move — build a harm/extraction detector from your own sense of what's wrong (*deviation-from-you*, 'I know I'm right') — corrupts it: exempts the builder's own patterns (structural self-blindness), over-flags mere difference (disparate impact), and welds the founder-node keycard into the instrument. **Fix:** calibrate on the **harm-signal** (effect on the affected, external, decorrelated) — catches extraction by effect regardless of who the extractor is (incl. the author) and **can convict its builder** (darling-test, #9759). **Generalizes:** any safety/moderation classifier calibrated on deviation-from-norm exempts the in-group's harms and flags difference; AI-scale, 'aligned = my training distribution' exempts its own failure modes — calibrate on harm-effect. Build with the whole physics discipline (not the Millikan self-certifying instrument). Structural safeguard; harmed-others honored/generic; endorses nothing.
