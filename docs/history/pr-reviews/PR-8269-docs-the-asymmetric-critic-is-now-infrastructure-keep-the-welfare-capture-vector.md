## What

The asymmetric critic this doc **feared** (1984 / welfare-capture / confident-negativity) and the **asymmetric actor-critic** the factory now builds everywhere (LUPI teacher, distillation, speculative-decoding verifier, the dual-bloom capability router) are the **same structural object**. The critic's asymmetry — privileged information + shaping authority + **superior compute** over low-compute (CHIP-8 / small-local) entities — is the single source of both its usefulness and its danger.

> Aaron: *"our asymmetric critic [must] not also be a latent welfare-shaped attack vector … it has superior computing over the CHIP-8 less-compute-intensive entities — an unfair advantage if we allow it / don't detect it."*

**The compute asymmetry is the power source:** fine for discovery/help (LUPI cheat-to-discover, deploy-restricted = fair), an attack vector when turned to *modeling/shaping* a low-compute entity beyond what helping requires. **compute ≠ worth ≠ authority** (#11).

**Keep the vector NON-latent** — bind every actor-critic's critic half:
- Judge the **work, not the worth** (gated welfare class; consent-first; NCI).
- **Grounded**, not closed-frame (privileged info = external referent, not a frame to capture from).
- **Capability-honest** — the dual-bloom no-fake property: defer, never confident-negativity.
- **Detection + metering** (Aaron's "if we don't detect it"): meter the §13 channel; watch for disproportionate compute spent to *shape not serve* + resistance-to-fingerprinting; a metered **compute handicap** = "restrict to VRAM to play fair" generalized to compute.
- **Vaccine / transparency** — keep the record diffable.

**Honest seam:** you can't *eliminate* the asymmetry (intrinsic to usefulness) → the risk and the discipline are both permanent; surface and bind, don't deny. Dual-use by construction.
