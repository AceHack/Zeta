## The spine

Content addressing forces CoW logically — you can't mutate in place and keep the address. What it
costs is **stable identity**, so a mutable layer must sit outside the immutable store. Git does this
with one mutable ref; in ZetaFS the mutable layer is *the entire naming apparatus*, and every
property you named falls out of that single choice.

- **Names are tags, not locations** → rename is O(1) and touches no content; a name can bind to
  different content across phases, which is how a ZetaId constant resolves differently over time.
- **Latest by phase, not wall-clock** — load-bearing, not preference.
  `local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold` forbids a local clock ordering evidence into a shared
  conclusion, and a filesystem resolving a *name* by wall-clock is exactly that: two replicas would
  disagree about what `notes.md` means. Ties break on canonical ordinal collation.
- **Symlink-native falls out free** — a binding *is* an indirection, so symlink and hardlink
  converge into one mechanism, and POSIX's "does this entry own the file?" has no referent.
- **Multi-parenting** makes the namespace a DAG — and that's where POSIX gets genuinely hard.

## Three problems named as open, not papered over

1. **`..` stops being a function** under multi-parenting. Three options, none free, no decision.
2. **Directory cycles.** POSIX bans directory hardlinks precisely to keep the namespace acyclic.
   Content addressing makes a cycle unconstructible in the *immutable* half — the danger is in the
   **mutable binding layer**, the half that isn't content-addressed. **This is the most important
   guard in the design and it doesn't exist.**
3. **GC vs history.** Retain-superseded-by-construction and reachability GC are in direct tension: a
   naive sweep collects nothing, ever.

## One platform finding worth its row

macOS/Windows **case-folding is a correctness issue here, not cosmetics.** If names are tags under
ordinal collation, `Notes.md` and `notes.md` are two tags — a folding projection presents one where
the store has two, so the round-trip is lossy. Either the projection refuses colliding-under-fold
bindings, or the collation isn't ordinal, and the second contradicts a standing rule. **The
projection refuses.**
