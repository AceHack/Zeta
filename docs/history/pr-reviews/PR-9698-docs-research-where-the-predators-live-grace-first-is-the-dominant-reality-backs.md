## Description

At Aaron's *"I've been to jail and mental hospitals and not met these predators — I don't know where they live."* Adds §5.5, the empirical grounding: the labeled-predator population (jail, psych wards) is a **scarcity population**, not a predator one — strong evidence grace-first is the *dominant reality.* The rare true predator lives where that test can't reach: **camouflaged among the flippable** (§5.1) or **insulated by power** (never caught → never jailed).
