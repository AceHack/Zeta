## Description

The ratchet-benchmark doc said the PR archive (`docs/history`, **+36.5 MB**) was *"the ongoing half"* of the observability growth. True over a 30-day window, **misleading as a rate** — and wrong in the same way as believing a dated measurement without re-running it, which is the error that document argues against two sections earlier. I committed it about an hour after writing that.

| window | Δ per day |
|---|---|
| −21d … −12d | 0.75 – 1.7 MB |
| **−12d → −11d** | **+15.95 MB** in one window — 12 commits, 1,803 files, a day of very high PR volume (ordinary per-PR archiving, not a backfill) |
| **last 9 days** | **0.00 – 0.47 MB, averaging ≈0.27 MB** |

So the archive's **current** rate is roughly **8–14 MB/month, not 36.5** — about a five-fold drop, beginning right where the cadence lanes were disabled. The lane is still active (it archived today), so this is a *slowed* rate, not a stopped one.

**Which means the size-reduction measures are working, and a 30-day total hid that.** A window long enough to contain a spike reports the spike as a trend.

The correction costs nothing except the admission. Leaving it would have argued for splitting out a repo on a number four times too large.
