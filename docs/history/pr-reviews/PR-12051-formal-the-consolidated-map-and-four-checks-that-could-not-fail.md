## Description

Routing + coverage audit of the whole formal-verification surface, plus the
vacuity sweep Aaron asked for. Governing rule:
.claude/rules/toy-is-free-metered-must-be-earned.md -- toy / unmetered /

WHAT THE INVENTORY FOUND

The portfolio is healthier than the folklore. registry/tlc-models.json pins 53
TLC model runs (52 gate + 1 extended), 14 of which EXPECT A VIOLATION -- a 26%
negative-control ratio, and the registry marks 22 of the 53 deadlock checks
"on-vacuous" in its own text because they cannot fail. That artefact is the
honesty standard for the rest of the portfolio and needed nothing from me.

The soft spots are in the ENFORCEMENT layer, not the specs.

FOUR VACUOUS CHECKS -- THREE REPAIRED, EACH MUTATION-PROVEN

1. lint-discharge-certificate-consistency.ts scanned ZERO rows and printed a
   tick. It matches the literal phrase "section-A DISCHARGED"; that phrase
   survives on six lines of the register and ALL SIX sit inside DEMOTED banners
   the matcher correctly excludes. It runs in gate.yml:1335 and reported an
   empty set in the words of a passing check -- the same failure the Lean axiom
   audit was fixed for on 2026-08-15.

   Repaired two ways. The empty scan now says it measured nothing and names its
   follow-up (081M0B2R2BQ087G0R000EC2E9Y). And a section-A ANCHOR check was
   added that DOES have jurisdiction: every backticked artifact token in
   section A must resolve to a tracked file. 49 anchors now checked per PR.
   Negative control run: an injected fake anchor exits 1.

   Deliberately NOT done: widening the matcher to the live "PROVEN" phrasing.
   Section A is a 43-row table with nested sub-tables; naive widening flags 23
   rows, mostly false, and a check that goes red with false positives is a
   check that gets switched off. Filed instead.

2. The new anchor check went RED ON MAIN. Section-A row 25 ("Reticulum

3. ComputeReceipt.Tests.fs CR-6 and CR-7 could not fail. Both matched on

NOT VACUOUS, AND DEFENDED

The scan flagged 41 F# and 33 TS assertions of the form f(x) = f(x). Most are
the falsifier for manifesto section 13 noninterference and must not be deleted.
The discriminating rule, which is the deliverable rather than any deletion:

  a determinism assertion is metered iff the callee's transitive call graph can
  reach an ambient source; otherwise it is unmetered decoration.

Likewise the 101 Assert.True(false, "msg") hits are the standard F# match
fail-with-message idiom, not tautologies.
