## Description

## What

Adds **MurmurHash3 fmix32** as the **second `generated-from-ir` cross-verification primitive** (after splitmix64), proving the DynamicValue IR vocabulary generalises across a **new primitive** AND a **new integer width (u32)**.

The only thing that differs between the two IR-driven generators is the **row**: same `mul`/`xorshr` op vocabulary, plus a single `width` field. That is the whole point — the IR is not splitmix64-specific.

## How (honest tiering)

- `_gen/fmix32.ir.json` — the finaliser IR as a **canonical-JSON DynamicValue** (`width: 32`). fmix32's u32 constants are < 2^31, so they fit `DynamicValue.Int` (int64) directly with **no signed reinterpretation** (unlike splitmix64's u64 constants).
- `_gen/gen.ts` — reads the row, decodes through the project's **real** `fromCanonicalJson`, folds with a **width-aware** mask. The mixer algorithm appears **nowhere as code**; it lives in the row. Emits `ts-output.json` tagged `generated-from-ir`.
- 5 **independent** hand-ports (F#/C#/Rust/Python/Go) recompute fmix32 from scratch — genuine peers, deliberately NOT sharing the interpreter (a shared module would defeat N-way independence).
- `_gen/gen-ir.test.ts` — generator-fidelity self-test: the row is a canonical DynamicValue (decoder accepts + fixed point), decoding reproduces the golden, and corruption **bites** — incl. a **width-is-load-bearing** case (folding the same ops at width 64 diverges from the u32 golden).
- `DynamicValue.Canonical.Tests.fs` — **cross-language byte-lock**: the real shipping F# `toCanonicalJson` reproduces `fmix32.ir.json` byte-for-byte and round-trips.
