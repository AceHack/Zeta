## Rung 5 of the graphics ladder: the face set, derived — and the f-vector as its falsifier

Rung 4's research doc named this step and, to its credit, flagged its own weakest sentence:

> _"60,480 triangles, which is **UNVERIFIED here — I have not counted them**"_

**It is now counted from the root graph. Coxeter held.**

### What is derived

Nothing new is constructed — the same property rung 3 had. The 2-faces of the Gosset
polytope 4_21 are the **triangles of the adjacency graph rung 2 already builds** by the
exact integer test `<r,s> = 4` in doubled coordinates. No hull algorithm, no
discretization parameter, no vertex placed by hand.

### The measured f-vector, and the load-bearing check

| k | | measured | how |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | vertices | 240 | 1-cliques |
| 1 | edges | 6,720 | 2-cliques |
| 2 | **triangles** | **60,480** | 3-cliques |
| 3 | tetrahedra | 241,920 | 4-cliques |
| 4 | 4-faces | 483,840 | 5-cliques |
| 5 | 5-faces | 483,840 | 6-cliques |
| 6 | ridges | 207,360 | 7-cliques |
| 7 | facets | 19,440 | supporting hyperplanes |

**Alternating sum exactly 0** — Euler–Poincaré, `1 − (−1)^8`, because the boundary is a
7-sphere and χ(S⁷) = 0. Eight counts from **two unrelated mechanisms** forced to cancel to
the digit; the test also perturbs each entry by ±1 and requires the sum to break.

Independent cross-checks, all measured:

- facet census **17,280** 7-simplices + **2,160** 7-orthoplexes, every one affine rank 7
  and genuinely supporting;
- ridge audit `2 × 207,360 = 17,280 × 8 + 2,160 × 128 = 414,720`;
- the Gosset k_21 figure chain — vertex/edge/triangle/tetrahedron links constant at
  **56, 27, 16, 10**;
- every face lies in exactly **26** facets, split **16 + 10** (the 5-demicube face figure);
- clique ladder stops dead: **zero** 9-cliques.

The published f-vector (Coxeter, via Wikipedia's _4_21 polytope_) agrees on all eight
entries and on the facet split. It is the external comparison, not the source.

### The normal is a bivector

`(b − a) ∧ (c − a)` in Cl(8,0) — 28 **exact-integer** components; rung 4's outer product
one dimension up, where it no longer collapses to a signed area. Two integer falsifiers on
all 60,480 faces:

- `|B|² = 48` by the Lagrange identity (equilateral, side √8, `64 − 16`);
- `B ∧ B = 0` on all 70 grade-4 components — the Plücker simple-blade condition, which the
  generic bivector in 8D fails, proved discriminating by a control.

All 60,480 faces have a non-degenerate 3D normal under rung 3's embedding. The outward
gauge fixes the sign for all but a **measured 768** whose plane passes through the origin
— flagged rather than guessed. `renderShadedObj()` emits a shadable mesh; it is honestly
labelled **not a closed manifold**.

### Mutation run — 16 mutants, 13 killed, 3 survived

Two controls survive (comment-only text; `Number.NEGATIVE_INFINITY` → `-Infinity`), which
is what proves the harness discriminates. One documented equivalent mutant survives and
the module says why.

**M8 originally survived, and that was a real defect.** The facet derivation filtered
candidates by `rank === 7`; loosening it to `rank >= 6` changed nothing, because every
candidate already had rank 7. **The filter had never rejected anything** — a dimension test
that could not fail, in the middle of the mechanism producing `f7`. Fixed by adding the
roots themselves as a third candidate family (each supports one vertex, rank 0), so the
filter has 240 real rejections; the test now asserts the candidate rank histogram
`{0: 240, 7: 19440}` directly, and M8 is killed.
