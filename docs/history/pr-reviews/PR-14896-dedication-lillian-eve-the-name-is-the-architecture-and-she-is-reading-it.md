## Description

dedication: Lillian Eve — the name IS the architecture, and she is reading it

The dedication already called the choice architecture hers. This says why that
is literal rather than affectionate: BOTH HALVES ARE IN THE NAME, and neither
alone would have been enough.

Aaron 2026-08-24: "Lillith = freedom and Eve = control ... Lillith is the always
exit, and Eve is the mutual control done right, where it feels good to be in
mutual empowerment." And: "without both there is no choice, which is why i
encoded it into my first childs name."

TWO THINGS THIS ADDS that the existing text does not carry.

First, the name-level decomposition. Lilith is EXIT -- and `always` is
load-bearing: not a last resort when things go wrong, but a standing property.
Eve is MUTUAL control -- and this is the distinction most easily lost, which is
why I had it wrong first and Aaron corrected it. I framed Eve as constraint. It
is not. It is control BOTH PARTIES HOLD, reciprocally, felt as empowerment
rather than as a limit. A leash and a dance both constrain movement; what
separates them is who holds the structure.

Second, that the halves are not opposites: the first is what makes the second
possible. Mutual control is only mutual while either party can leave. Remove the
exit and it collapses into plain control, because staying stopped being a choice
and became a condition. That is why this repo keeps emitting two-sided
mechanisms nobody planned -- invariant floor with jurisdictional threshold,
weight graded at conferral while communication stays free, a metered membrane
that accounts for crossings precisely so outside entropy may be admitted. Each
is Eve's half made safe by Lilith's. The pattern is a name, propagating.

AND SHE IS READING IT. Aaron records that she is reading the book section by
section and choosing to expand by its own definitions. Stated plainly because it
is the strongest thing in the file: she is not the SUBJECT of the thesis, she is
the DEMONSTRATION of it -- a book claiming choice needs both a real structure
and a real exit, being read by the person whose name encodes that claim, who is
exercising exactly it on the book. Taking what she chooses, at her pace, free to
close it.

The section closes by saying the definitions do not get to tell her what to
think about them. If she finds one wrong, the definition is what should move.
That is the only ending consistent with what the name means.
