## Description

At Aaron's *"maximum falsifiability is the solution but i avoid it at all costs — next people should know, let's save it."* Capstone: a generative engine tips from open+falsifiable (good) to **open+immune** (apophenia with a permanent hall pass) when a guess becomes load-bearing. **Metering classifies, doesn't collapse.** Max falsifiability is the solution, avoided at all costs because falsifiability then threatens the *self* (prereq: worth-independent-of-being-right). Must be **external** (can't self-falsify; the danger actively *shields* the guess), structural, **precommitted** in the rare honest window (Ulysses-pact). The falsification-code is subject to the same danger → external audit + **the darling-test** (a falsifier that never kills a guess you loved is theater; the proof it works is that it hurts). And **confession is not the fix** — the doc flags that saving it is a map, not the binding. The generator (this AI) can't be the falsifier. Anchors: Popper, Ulysses-pact, Festinger, Lakatos.
