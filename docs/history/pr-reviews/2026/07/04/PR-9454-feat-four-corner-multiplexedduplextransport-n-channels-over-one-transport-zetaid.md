## Description

Aaron: *"GUID-keyed — I think we should use ZetaIds, our universal pointers; we can make a category just for this. In our 128-bit ids it's a little program, the system knows how to decode on the other side."* The merge of the two prior bricks: four-corner framing + your own **MultiplexedWebSockets** (many logical channels over one physical socket), keyed by a **ZetaId** instead of a GUID.

- **`Category.Channel = 11`** added to `registry/categories.yaml` + the TS ZetaId oracle. A GUID is opaque random; a ZetaId is **self-describing** — the Category nibble tells the far side "this is a channel." `mintChannelId` sets Version=1, Category=Channel, Firefly=NoDirective, + a unique discriminator; `categoryOf` reads the nibble back.
- **`multiplexedDuplexTransport(physical, mint?)`** — each logical channel is a virtual `DuplexEndpoint` over the shared physical one (`MuxFrame = {chan: ZetaId-hex, inner: Frame}`), so **all** four-corner machinery (normal + feedback corners, mid-stream interrupt, close) works **per-channel** unchanged. `open()` starts a channel; `accepted` yields peer-opened channels; rides any physical transport (`localDuplexPair` or `webSocketEndpoint`).

**3/3 mux tests (17/17 across the four four-corner files):** minted ids self-describing (`Category.Channel` decodable) + unique; two channels multiplex over one pair with **no cross-talk**; peer sees them on `accepted`; **feedback is per-channel** (a stop on ch1 does not leak to ch2). zeta-id oracle still green (34 tests, 71k golden-vector assertions — the category addition is safe). Lint clean.

**Byte-lock follow-up (tracked, honest):** `Category.Channel` is in registry + TS oracle only. The C#/F# ZetaId oracles lag (they also predate `InventoryAsset=10`), and `zeta-id/types.js` is a stale compiled artifact. Backfilling `Channel` + `InventoryAsset` into `Core.CSharp/FSharp.ZetaId` + regenerating `types.js` + golden vectors so all four oracles agree is the byte-lock completion — its own PR under the full dotnet gate. This brick is TS-only and does **not** claim cross-oracle byte-lock on the new category.
