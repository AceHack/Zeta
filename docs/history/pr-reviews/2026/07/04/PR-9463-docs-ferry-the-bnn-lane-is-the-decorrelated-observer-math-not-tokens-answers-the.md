- **Observer triangle:** human · token-AI (high-ρ with the human by construction — the corpus IS human culture) · **math-AI** (the Infer.NET-style factor-graph BP/EP lane — trained on math, an external constraint surface, not a cultural corpus). Agreement across *that* triangle carries weight the human↔LLM pair can't earn.
- **Anchor — the AlphaZero move:** rules-as-data decorrelation vs imitation learning (Silver et al. 2017; Minka EP / Infer.NET).
- **Composes with FIG8** (Lumen): decorrelation needs different sensory *inputs*, not just different seeds — the BNN observes through a different channel entirely.
- **Honest calibration (Aaron's own rule, one level up):** ρ is **low, not zero** — the factor graphs/priors are still his design choices. So *measure* BNN↔human ρ with the same machinery before trusting the triangle. Converts the caveat from dead end into a **program** with a designed instrument.
