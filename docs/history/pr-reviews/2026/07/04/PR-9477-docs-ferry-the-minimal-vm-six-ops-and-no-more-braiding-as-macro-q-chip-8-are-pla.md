Either the word exists (braiding earned by composition, primitives unchanged) or the minimal addition gets named exactly. `only-the-irreducible-is-primitive`, applied at the ISA level.

Playgrounds scoped honestly: Q#/CHIP-8 are scaffolding to prove the concepts execute; the product is the six-op ISA + db-as-IR + ZetaId pointers + soft-regime runtime + the Futamura self-hosting ladder. Minimality is engineering, not aesthetics: smaller treaty to byte-lock, smaller drift surface, cheaper Futamura ladder.
