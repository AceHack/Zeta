## Description

## What changed

- Added a source-owned `zeta.heat.receipt.v1` TypeScript shape and row-to-receipt projection for Dark Hall heat rows.
- Rendered optional heat receipts in the zero-JS LLMTV temperature lane with outcome, policy, source, ppm rails, and reasons.
- Preserved receipt-bearing temperature bundles through the LLMTV broadcast decoder so Reticulum frames can carry the provenance contract.
- Regenerated `hall/tv/index.html` from the LLMTV transcript.
- Fixed two markdownlint issues found by `preflight:quick` on latest main.

## Why

The temperature/black-body lane showed the scalar heat picture but not its cause. This makes the compact LLMTV tile explain where heat came from without pulling runtime state or GitHub/host APIs into the browser frame loop.
