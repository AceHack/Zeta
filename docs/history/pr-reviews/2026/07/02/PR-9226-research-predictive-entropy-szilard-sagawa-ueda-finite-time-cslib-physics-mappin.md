## 1. Predictive entropy — the full "Provable Advantage" derivation
`docs/research/2026-07-02-predictive-entropy-szilard-sagawa-ueda-finite-time-offline-scheduling-advantage.md`

- **Szilard 1929** — bit = `kT ln2` of work, so soft-lane bits are thermodynamic fuel (a charged capacitor, not scratchpad).
- **Sagawa-Ueda 2008-2012** — generalized second law WITH feedback; a predictor IS the feedback controller, and its mutual information lowers the effective erasure cost. This is the physics the doc's advantage rests on, named.
- **Finite-time thermodynamics** (Sekimoto; Aurell 2012; Sivak-Crooks 2012; Proesmans 2020) — the `L^2/tau` excess above the floor; the floor is irreducible/additive/batch-invariant, the excess is the whole lever prediction has.
- **Offline vs online** — competitive ratio on dissipation; same convex speed-power structure as Yao-Demers-Shenker speed scaling (alpha=2). DST supplies arrival times `t`; noninterference supplies bit-magnitudes `B` -> the system is clairvoyant (offline) for free.

## 2. CSLib -> physics mapping
`docs/research/2026-07-02-cslib-data-structures-thermodynamic-mapping-adj-vs-nonadj-soft-hard-lane.md`
