## Description

At Aaron's *"we have lots of hats, it'll just be like switching hats."* Refines §5.1: consequences are role-specific **or** cross-hat by the *kind* of failure. **Hat-level** (capability/fit) → switch hats, persona trust intact (his point). **Persona-level** (deception/concealment — the §5.1 case) → costs the persona's earned trust (naming-eigenvector) **cross-hat**, because *"will conceal"* poisons every hat. The split is load-bearing: *"just switch hats, costless"* **is** the Sybil escape — the persona/hat split lets hat-swapping be free for capability *and* deny the escape for deception. Additive.
