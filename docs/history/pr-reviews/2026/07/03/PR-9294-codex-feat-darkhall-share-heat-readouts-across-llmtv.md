## Description

## What

- Adds a shared `darkhall-ui/heat` vocabulary so room heat and LLMTV/root-site heat use the same typed signals.
- Renders `data-heat-signals` on LLMTV readout panels so no-JS/CSS surfaces can distinguish cold, invalid, denied, expired, stale, and other heat.
- Maps root-site replay invalid/rejected/expired/stale evidence into those heat signals and covers the cases in focused Bun tests.

## Why

Aaron asked for heat to expand across all surfaces. This keeps information loss and refusal evidence visible at the membrane instead of collapsing every failure into a generic hot readout.
