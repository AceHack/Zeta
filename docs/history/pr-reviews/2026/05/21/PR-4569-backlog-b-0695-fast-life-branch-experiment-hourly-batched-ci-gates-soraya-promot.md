## Substrate-honest cost math (corrected)

Today's empirical: 12 PRs merged in ~12-hour active period. Per-PR cadence vs hourly-batched:

- Per-PR: 12 × 30 checks = 360 runs
- Hourly worst-case (1 PR/hour): 360 runs (cost-neutral)
- Hourly cascade pattern (today's actual): ~240 runs (~33% reduction)

Real win is cost-during-cascade-bursts, not absolute. The PR-latency tradeoff (faster per-PR merge vs hourly-batched delay) is per-pattern: fast-iteration work prefers per-PR; substrate-engineering work tolerates hourly.
