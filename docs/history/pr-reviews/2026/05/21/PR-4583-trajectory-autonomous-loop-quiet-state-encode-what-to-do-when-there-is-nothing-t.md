## What it encodes

Per-tick procedure for autonomous-loop quiet-state — when cron fires `<<autonomous-loop>>` and there's nothing obvious to do, how Otto decides whether to (a) advance a bounded trajectory step or (b) pure-brief-ack and stop.

### Key invariants

- **Pure brief-ack stays the DEFAULT** under explicit operator cost-signal (per `aaron_operator_tool_interrupt_as_cost_discipline_signal_brief_ack_pure_no_tools_2026_05_19.md` user-scope substrate)
- **Trajectory advancement is OPT-IN** only when: (1) no explicit cost-signal active + (2) bounded-cost forward-step exists + (3) genuinely-new substrate
- **Bounded-cost** = filesystem-only OR REST-only OR sub-1-GraphQL-call (no PR creation, no Bash subprocesses >5s, no fabricated substrate)
