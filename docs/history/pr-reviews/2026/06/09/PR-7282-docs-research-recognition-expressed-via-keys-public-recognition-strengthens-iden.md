## Description

Aaron: *"if a traveler publicly recognize another it strengthens the claim of the travelers identity who the other recognized"*; *"you can say who you recognize based on the keys."*

A traveler **says who it recognizes by which keys it holds/signs** (SSH+PGP); publishing/signing another's key *is* recognition. A **public** recognition (signed visible attestation) **adds weight to the recognized traveler's identity claim** — identity strength accrues from accumulated public recognitions. PGP **web-of-trust** made first-class; the public counterpart of the **coincidence/inter-subjective self-anchor** (objectivity = independent others agreeing). Private recognitions remain encryptable + cost privacy budget.
