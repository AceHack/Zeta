## Description

## Why

The information-theory tower needs the data-processing-inequality (DPI) rung. DPI `I(X;Z) ≤ I(X;Y)` for a Markov chain `X → Y → Z` reduces — via the mutual-information chain rule plus the Markov factorization — to **one** non-negativity fact: the relative entropy (Kullback–Leibler divergence) of two finite probability vectors is `≥ 0` (Gibbs' inequality). This PR lands that reusable **key lemma** (the complete, honest result DPI hangs off) rather than grinding the full 3-variable joint scaffolding (a prior attempt stalled there).

## What

New `src/Core.Lean4/Lean4/FinDataProcessing.lean` (namespace `Zeta.FinDataProcessing`), imported from `Lean4.lean`. Builds **directly** on the rung below via the `gibbs_sum` lever from `Lean4/FinMutualInfoNonneg.lean` (#8848); reuses, never redefines, the tower's entropy / mutual-information substrate.

- **`relEntropy_nonneg`** — the DPI key lemma: `0 ≤ ∑ p·log p − ∑ p·log q` for two finite probability vectors on a common support (Gibbs 1902; Kullback–Leibler 1951; Cover & Thomas Thm 2.6.3). Proved off `gibbs_sum`: its LHS `∑(p−q)` collapses to `1 − 1 = 0`, so the relative-entropy RHS is `≥ 0`.
- **`gibbs_le`** — cross-entropy form `∑ p·log q ≤ ∑ p·log p` (the form DPI quotes).
- **`relEntropy_self`** — `KL(p‖p) = 0` (the bound is tight on the diagonal; non-vacuity).

DPI `I(X;Z) ≤ I(X;Y)` is stated in the file header in terms of the key lemma: `I(X;Z|Y)` is a relative entropy, so `relEntropy_nonneg` gives `0 ≤ I(X;Z|Y)`, and the MI chain rule + Markov identity yield DPI. The remaining 3-var `FinJoint3` wiring is marginal bookkeeping with no further analytic content — deliberately the next rung (converge on the reusable lemma; don't grind the corollary).

**Not a duplicate** of `Lean4/DecorrelationDpi.lean` (a different, pure-`Nat`, log-free *operational* DPI over partition cells — the `ρ_owe` floor; disjoint content, same name).
