## The brick

The **bottom brick of the information-theory tower** (target #1, "finite Shannon entropy root"): a real-ℝ, Mathlib-backed Shannon entropy of a finite distribution + its foundational lemmas. The rest of the tower (conditional entropy → MI → DPI → capacity) hangs off this and is **deliberately out of scope here** (later rungs).

Sibling of `Lean4/EntropyMeasureTheoretic.lean` (#8808 min-entropy `H_∞ = − logb 2 pmax`): same finite-distribution setting, different functional — Shannon `H = ∑ negMulLog (p x)`.
