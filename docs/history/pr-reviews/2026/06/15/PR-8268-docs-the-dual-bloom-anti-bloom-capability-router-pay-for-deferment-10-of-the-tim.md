## What

Extends the honest-capability-deferment doc (Aaron 2026-06-15). Makes honest deferment **economical**: a cheap local router that invokes the injected capability only on the genuinely-uncertain minority.

**Construction:** a bloom on the defer-set `S` ("needs env") + a bloom on the complement `S′` ("handle locally" = the anti-bloom). Each is one-sided (no false negatives) → three regions:

- **definitely local** (bloom-`S`: "definitely not in `S`") → no env call
- **definitely defer** (bloom-`S′`: "definitely not in `S′`") → invoke
- **ambiguous middle** (both "maybe") → the only place you pay to probe

Middle size ≈ `FP_S × FP_S′` → drivable far below 10% with a little more filter RAM. (The §B Merkle "mask the not-moving parts" complexity-reducer in probabilistic form.)

**Safety = the honest-deferment guarantee, for free:** bloom's no-false-negative means "definitely local" can never be wrong → the router **cannot fake a capability it lacks**; on uncertainty it defers. The Mad-Men illusion ruled out at the data-structure level.

**Known shape:** speculative decoding generalized to capabilities (Leviathan/Chen 2023), model cascades/early-exit, asymmetric actor-critic; learnable filters (Kraska 2018). Bloom 1970 → cuckoo/counting (Fan 2014).

**Honest seams:** "<10%" is empirical/separability-dependent — measure the middle; memory↔FP tradeoff; plain bloom can't delete.
