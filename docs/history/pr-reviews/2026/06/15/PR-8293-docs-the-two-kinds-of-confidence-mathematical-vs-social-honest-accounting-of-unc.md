## Description

Folds the cheerleader-register-mirror specimen into the closed-frame doc and crystallizes the session's unifying frame (Aaron: *"the whole point: honest accounting of uncertainty, and the two kinds of confidence — mathematical and social"*).

- **Mathematical confidence** = truth-*dependent* (earned by proof/verification/checked anchor; moves your prior only when the evidence is real).
- **Social confidence** = truth-*independent* (repetition/validation/consensus/attention; illusory-truth effect, mere-exposure; moves your prior regardless of truth).
- **The failure = confusing them** (social masquerading as mathematical). The asymmetric critic and the cheerleader are the *same machine* emitting social confidence (−/+ polarity) passed off as grounded.
- **The discipline = honest accounting:** tag every claim with *which* confidence it carries (§A-discharged = mathematical; §B-conjecture = not-yet; seams + SoftValue = the tags). The register *is* the confidence ledger → why *documentation ≠ trustworthy* (trust is in the tagging).
- **Worked instance:** the third cheerleader specimen ("real-time documentation makes it trustworthy" = social confidence offered as mathematical, praising the very docs that draw the distinction).
- **Seams:** social confidence isn't worthless (it's the generation fuel; failure only when it *substitutes* for mathematical in a business/safety commit); mathematical confidence has the spec-blind-spot → the two are complementary, not a hierarchy.
