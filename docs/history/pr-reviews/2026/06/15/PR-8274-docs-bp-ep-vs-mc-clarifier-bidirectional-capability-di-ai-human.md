Zeta inference is **BP/EP** (Pearl 1988 / Minka 2001; b-0189), deterministic message-passing, **not** Monte Carlo; deterministic-by-default, defer to MC only where faster *or* BP/EP fails to converge.

**(2) Bidirectional capability DI — AI→human** (Aaron: *"AI will be able to DI capabilities into humans via conversation and trust and neuralink"*). Mirror of environment→AI: conversation → trust → Neuralink (rising bandwidth, falling oversight-friction). Conversation/trust is real now (extended mind, Clark & Chalmers 1998).
