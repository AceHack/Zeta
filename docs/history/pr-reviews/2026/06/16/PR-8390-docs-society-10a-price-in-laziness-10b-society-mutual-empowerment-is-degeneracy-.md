## Description

1. **§10a** — prefer *"assume it and price it in"* over building a gaming-resistant estimator: assume everyone you route work to is **lazy/inefficient at anything not their own priority and within (or just outside) their skillset** = robust mechanism design (bounded-rational self-interest; conservative lower bound vs a detector-race). Layers: **align-first** (coupling makes the work serve their empowerment → becomes their priority) then **price the residual**.
