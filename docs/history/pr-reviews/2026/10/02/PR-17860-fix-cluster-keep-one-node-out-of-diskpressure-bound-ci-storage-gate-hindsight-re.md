## Description

﻿## What

The owner's single node (node-5b2dfa, root fs ~119 GiB) went DiskPressure on 2026-10-02 and evicted ~150 pods: several parallel image-heavy GitLab CI jobs pushed imagefs under its 15% eviction line, and the crash-looping `hindsight` pods (Secret `hindsight-llm-api-key` absent) were replaced after every eviction -- 168 `Failed` pod objects. Measurements and the OS-level options NOT taken: `docs/ops/NODE-DISK-HEADROOM.md`.

GitOps only. No OS, NixOS or kubelet change.

- **gitlab**: runner `concurrent` 4 -> 2; per-job `ephemeral_storage` request/limit (1Gi/6Gi build, 64Mi/2Gi helper; keys verified against gitlab-runner v17.6.0 source); `LimitRange gitlab-ephemeral-storage` backstop (6Gi default limit, 256Mi default request).
- **cluster-hygiene** (new Application, dev-excluded, restricted PSA):
  - `pod-janitor`: reaps `Failed`/`Evicted` pods that stayed Failed across one full interval; RBAC pods-only plus a `ValidatingAdmissionPolicy` that denies that ServiceAccount any non-`Failed` pod.
  - `hindsight-secret-gate`: holds hindsight's two Deployments at 0 while the Secret is absent, restores the declared count when it exists; failed probe = unknown, never scales.
- **hindsight**: pins `replicaCount`, ignores `/spec/replicas` on its two Deployments (`RespectIgnoreDifferences`); render and ledgers unchanged, still `converges-only-after-an-operator-action`.
- Ledgers: sync-wave graph, lane footprints, two resourceClaims, snapshots, dev exclusion + census, budget prose.

## Proof

- Offline, fail without the change: `cluster-hygiene.test.ts` (runs both scripts against a stub kubectl; RBAC, admission policy, gate-desired == hindsight-declared replicas), `gitlab-runner-bringup.test.ts` (b)(i).
- Live on a THROWAWAY k3s v1.35.6 container (not the owner's node): janitor mark/reap, VAP denies non-Failed pods, gate 1->0->1 as its ServiceAccount.
- Cluster suite in a Linux container: no failure that the base tree does not also have.

## Not proven

ArgoCD honouring `RespectIgnoreDifferences` here; the CronJob pods themselves (image pull on the node); a kaniko job being evicted cleanly at 6Gi; anything on the real node.
