## Summary
Measured over 96 reviews on the Waypoint run (2026-09-20/21): a reviewer looked at **19 things on average** — the dashboard (88 of 96), the item twice, each evidence attachment through its own `observe` call — before reading a line of the change. 213 reviews were $204 of $478; ~21 turns each, and every turn re-reads the whole context. The evidence for the step under judgment is a few kilobytes the runtime already holds.

- `adapters.ts` — `commandReview` hands `ORG_REVIEW_TITLE` / `ORG_REVIEW_BRIEF` / `ORG_REVIEW_EVIDENCE` (`inlineEvidenceFor`: text evidence verbatim, head-and-tail up to 15k chars; files stay refs).
- `tools/claude-agent.cjs` review — prints the item and what the step produced, and says the dashboard is not needed for a step review. The worldview is still `observe`: the item's history, parent chain and other steps' attachments live there, and the reviewer is still told to read the change and run its tests.

Expected effect: ~5 fewer turns per review (~25% of review cost), no change to what is judged. To be measured on the next run against the same ledger.
