the constrained first-boot replica on main (2026-09-30, run 36685251210, 4 vCPU,
173 "Liveness probe failed" lines, zero OOMKilled) shows dapr-operator, dapr-sentry
and a dapr-scheduler-server replica killed. All five dapr components render the
chart's `initialDelaySeconds: 180, periodSeconds: 10, failureThreshold: 5` and the
chart templates expose NO timeoutSeconds, so a RUNNING pod dies after
(5-1)*10+1 = 41s of slow answers.

The one lever the chart gives is the threshold: 12 -> (12-1)*10+1 = 111s, set on
dapr_operator, dapr_sentry, dapr_sidecar_injector, dapr_placement and dapr_scheduler.
