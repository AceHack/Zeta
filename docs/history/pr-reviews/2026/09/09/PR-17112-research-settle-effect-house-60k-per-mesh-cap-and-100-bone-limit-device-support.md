## Description

Settles the two blocking questions on the TikTok Effect House mesh budget decision.

## Q1 — the 60k / bone conflict: SETTLED, and the answer splits across both pages

Both official pages are live, both conflict, and **neither carries a last-updated
marker** (verified by grep for six modified-date signals). The tiebreaker is the dated,
versioned release-note changelog:

- **v4.2.0, May 23, 2024** — *"The following improvements have been made to the triangle
  count limit for models imported to Effect House … The triangle count limit for meshes
  with blendshape animation has been set to 60K"*. Scope is per-imported-mesh and
  itemised per mesh type, so **60,000 is a PER-MESH hard cap** (reading (a)). The
  Technical Optimization page is right; the 3D Asset Preparation page's "less than 60k in
  the entire effect" is a separate, weaker recommendation reusing the same number.
- **v3.7.0, January 22, 2024** — *"The maximum supported bone count of imported models has
  increased from 50 to 100"*. So **100 bones is current**, and the Technical Optimization
  page's "Each FBX file can have up to 50 joints" is **stale by two and a half years**.

**Neither page is uniformly authoritative** — that is the finding, and it inverts the
prior you would form from the pages alone (the QA page the tool links to is the one that
is wrong about bones). A 2023 Internet Archive capture of a now-404 official URL confirms
the 50-joint sentence predates the increase and survived a doc reorganisation unrevised.

## Q2 — device support

- **No published minimum iOS or Android version** anywhere in the docs (negative result,
  with the fourteen pages searched named in the doc).
- What is published is a floor-by-example plus a capability requirement, repeated on three
  official surfaces: effects *"do not currently work on devices equal to or lower than …
  Samsung Galaxy J3, LG Leon H324, Huawei Honor 4X, and Cubot J3"*, with WebGL named as
  the mechanism.
- Unsupported devices do **not** silently hide the effect — they show the toast **"This
  effect doesn't work with this device."** Visible refusal, per-effect, not per-app.
- Region variation is content-moderation-driven, not capability-driven. No account gate
  beyond an ordinary TikTok account for desktop publishing (mobile from-scratch creation
  *is* region-limited).

## What it changes for us

The **8 MB package is the binding constraint, and it is not close.** A 60,000-triangle
mesh with 52 ARKit targets carries roughly 18.7 MB of morph deltas alone — over twice the
whole package budget — so the triangle cap is unreachable for a face-driven character.
One 1.5–4.3 MB character fits; two 4.3 MB characters cannot. The head/body split is the
biggest lever, and it only works *because* the 60k cap turned out to be per-mesh. A
4-second maximum load time is a second, independent constraint. Every byte estimate is
marked INFERRED and the doc names the measurement that would replace it.

Every claim is marked VERIFIED / INFERRED / UNVERIFIED, with a sources table separating
pages read in full from pages that would not load (given zero weight). No community forum
is load-bearing.
