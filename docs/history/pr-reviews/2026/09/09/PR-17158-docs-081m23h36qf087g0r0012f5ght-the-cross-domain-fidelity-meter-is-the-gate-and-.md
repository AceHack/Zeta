## The four answers

**1. The measurement problem (§2) — the most valuable half.** *"Mind, body, English and emotional propagation projected onto the same space with high fidelity"* cannot fail as stated: any four finite sets embed somewhere, "high fidelity" names no quantity, and "the same irreducible object" is an **identification**, which under `numerology-vs-number-theory.md` needs invariants rather than a score.

Proposed meter, in two tiers because one is not enough:

- **Tier 1 — alignment.** Mutual k-NN (Huh et al. 2024), CKA/RSA (Kornblith 2019; Kriegeskorte 2008), and the generalised **shape metric** (Williams et al. 2021). Three statistics, because ReSi (arXiv 2408.00531) shows they disagree. Three nulls, all required: permutation, matched-covariance, and a **random-carrier control at equal dimension** — the one that decides whether Clifford is doing any work at all.
- **Tier 2 — identification.** Grade, norm class, rank of span, stabiliser, orthogonal decomposition. Score-match with invariant-mismatch is reported **"aligned, not identified"**, which is a result.

**Verdict: constructible, not runnable. The blocker is correspondence data, not mathematics.** Cheapest first experiment (E1) rides on the existing, cross-verified `LexicalGeometricReceipt.fs` and the rung-3 embedding, paired through Wierzbicka's spatially-read NSM primes, with the disjunctive negative control the 2026-09-03 seed spec already requires.

**2. The algebra verdict (§3).** Gunn (PGA wins on rigid Euclidean motion; CGA's co-dimension exceeds its own solution space) and de Haan/Cohen/Brehmer AISTATS 2024 (plain PGA *"not sufficiently expressive"*; conformal and improved projective win) appear to disagree and **both are right** — different criteria. The sharper correction:

> A Clifford algebra is an **object**. A braided monoidal category is a **composition law**. "The same space" is the wrong ask; **"the same composition law over different carriers, related by functors"** is right, strictly weaker, and already published (DisCoCat over `FdVect`; `ConvexRel` over convex spaces is compact closed).

Stated plainly, as the brief asked: **no algebra demonstrably carries affect and geometry with a measured fidelity.** Pustejovsky (arXiv 2604.25902) proposes exactly that and reports no empirical evaluation.

**3. Rx / Fran (§4).** Fran (Elliott & Hudak, ICFP 1997) is the direct precedent and Meijer/Rx is already an anchor here. *"Completely in Rx"* is wrong for the inner loop — Elliott's own 2009 push-pull paper diagnoses both pure-push and pure-pull. The right property is **`O(Δ)` change propagation**, and it has been **shipped in F#**: Wörister et al. HPG 2013 and Haaser et al. HPG 2015, both in the Aardvark platform. Zeta already owns its strongest algebraic form in **DBSP**. Rx belongs at the authoring edge; incremental computation belongs under the scene graph.

**4. Hidden control structure (§5) — the practical artefact.** A ten-row inventory, each row with its published alternative: implicit warp sync (unsafe since Volta), subgroup-size assumptions, BVH divergence (Aila & Laine 2009), material dispatch (Laine/Karras/Aila 2013), inter-workgroup barriers (Sorensen et al. OOPSLA 2016), scan (Merrill & Garland; Smith et al. SPAA 2025), float-atomic reductions, sort tie-breaks, persistent threads, depth ordering. **Rows 7–8 are DST conflicts, not performance notes** — a renderer using float atomics in any reduction is not replayable and no seed fixes it.
