## 1. `ip-questionable/` — MLST × Tom McGrath (Goodfire), verbatim

Concepts live on **curved manifolds, not rays**. Days and months come out as circles. SAEs —
whose inductive bias is that a feature is a *direction* — tile those curves with many rays,
reconstructing them well while learning nothing about them, which is what makes genuinely
algorithmic computation look like *"a bag of heuristics."* Their fix: a feature primitive that
is a **subspace of adaptively-learned dimension**.

Also measured: **one reused general addition module** that day-of-week *and* month arithmetic
both route through (Llama 3.1 8B/70B, and DeepSeek V4 Flash — a different architecture
entirely); and steering fails specifically by **stepping off the manifold**, which explains the
Golden-Gate-Claude-vs-gibberish split.

Filed against two of our own rules rather than around them:

- **The Clifford resonance is a coincidence, not an identification.** A k-blade *is* an oriented
  subspace, and circular concepts *look* like rotors. Three things would promote it and none are
  done: a stable nonzero wedge (an actual **grade**), the **group law** rather than mere
  periodicity, and the **same generator** recovered across concept families.
- **On chain-of-thought monitoring.** McGrath independently reaches "weak and evadable," which
  Aaron ruled on the same day. But his replacement is a *stronger internal monitor* — which works
  on a model and not on a person. So this is evidence our choice was about **symmetry**, not
  capability. It also surfaces a real tension: `VISION.md` claims the meter *"never requires
  reading a thought."* Ours does not **need** interiors; that is not the same as interiors
  carrying no signal, and the section now says so.

## 2. `ZETA-CORE-TECHNOLOGY-FOR-MAX.md` — "The Geometry Thread"

Answering Aaron's ask to explain to Max how geometric / language / Bayesian / Clifford and our
multi-jurisdiction boundaries are **one thing**.

> **Membership in overlapping regions that do not nest is a single computation.**

**The worked instance is a ballot style.** An address is inside a county, a precinct, three
legislative districts, a school district and a water district *at once*, and these cross each
other — so there is no tree and no root to descend from. ES&S Electionware manages ~15,000
ballot styles against ~10,000 precincts; **more styles than precincts is the arithmetic
signature of non-nesting overlap**, and *"precinct split"* is the industry admitting it in its
own vocabulary.

**Anchors, found by looking as asked:** Warren Manning → Tyrwhitt → **McHarg** (*Design with
Nature*, 1969 — transparent sheets on a light table; **keep the layers, do not merge them**,
which is DV2.0's raw vault stated about maps) → **Tomlin & Berry** (map algebra, ~1983, the
formalisation). Plus **Safe Software's FME** on Aaron's pointer — the step overlay *assumes*,
since two agencies draw the same boundary differently and an overlay over unreconciled layers
returns an answer at every point and a wrong one near every boundary. FME is the **anti-Babel
floor**: it makes boundaries comparable without deciding which is right.

**Zeta's instance:** our policy model already is this. A traveler is inside their node's policy,
the room, the hat, the counterparty's policy and the manifesto floor simultaneously, and none
nests. So **a permission is not a boolean, it is a signature** — and a global policy authority
would be both a §1 violation *and technically wrong*, because a tree cannot represent a lattice.

**Clifford** is where the query becomes **one operator** (the meet, at a grade) instead of a
pipeline — with the limit stated rather than hidden: CGA blades are rounds and flats, **an
arbitrary polygon is not a blade**, so this is not "Clifford solves elections." It transfers
exactly in concept space, where the regions really are linear subspaces. **Bayes** is not a
fifth thing — it is the boundary, where all four are actually hard, and the
sum-over-consistent-assignments shape is why Layer 5 is a factor-graph DAG and why
**probabilistic circuits** were the prior art Aaron was reaching for.

Aaron's FME assessment is carried **with his own dating** (*"maybe 10 years ago, I could be
wrong"*). I verified FME still occupies the role; I did **not** find a current head-to-head, so
"best on earth" stays his dated assessment rather than being upgraded to a finding by my having
looked it up.
