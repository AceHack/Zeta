## Description

## What

Closes a naming thread that has been open since 2026-08-30. The term Aaron was
reaching for is **probabilistic circuits**.

> "i'm pretty sure the word i was looking for for our factor graph DAGs instead of
> traditional linear neural networks is probabilistic circuits" — Aaron, 2026-09-02,
> after Lumen referenced it

## Why the earlier search missed it — this is the useful part

`docs/research/2026-09-01-a-dag-of-bayesian-networks-candidate-anchors-none-confirmed.md`
records its own miss: **Sum-Product Networks were offered and not recognised.**

SPNs are one *model class*. **Probabilistic circuits** is the *category* that
unifies SPNs, arithmetic circuits, PSDDs and cutset networks. Offering the
instance to someone reaching for the genus reads as "no, that's not it" — and did.

## The anchors

| anchor | who | when | what it contributes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Probabilistic Circuits | Choi, Vergari & Van den Broeck | 2020 | the umbrella; tractability from **structural** properties (smoothness, decomposability, determinism), not model family |
| Arithmetic Circuits | Darwiche, JACM | **2003** | **compiles a Bayes net into a circuit** — both the "built on Bayesian networks" and "older than I expected" halves |
| Sum-Product Networks | Poon & Domingos | 2011 | the instance offered and correctly rejected as too narrow |
| PSDD | Kisa, Van den Broeck, Choi & Darwiche | 2014 | structured-decomposability branch |

A probabilistic circuit is a rooted **DAG of sum/product nodes** — not a chain, a
sub-circuit may feed several parents, composition is the defining operation, and
the DAG's structure is what buys tractable exact inference. That matches every
phrase in Aaron's description.

## What this does NOT claim

Naming the tradition is not a claim that our construct **is** one. Whether the
RFFH / equivariant factor layer satisfies smoothness, decomposability or
determinism — and so inherits any PC tractability result — is checkable and
currently unmeasured. It is with the math review. Until then the honest register
is "the tradition our construct sits nearest."
