## Description

**In plain words:** one microphone, many instruments — work out who is playing. Same problem as
one power meter, many appliances — work out which are on.

**Register: `toy`.** A design plus falsifiers. Nothing trained, nothing measured, and F1 below
predicts its own loss.

## They are literally the same model

Not an analogy we chose to draw. **Hart 1992** (Proc. IEEE — the Itron-domain root Aaron worked
inside) → **Ghahramani & Jordan 1997** factorial HMM → **Kolter & Jaakkola 2012**, *additive*
factorial HMM for energy disaggregation. The audio side reached the same decomposition as NMF
(Lee & Seung 1999; Smaragdis & Brown 2003), PLCA, and Bayesian NMF (Cemgil 2009). Additive
superposition of overlapping sources over one observation — which is a factor graph, i.e. Layer 5.

## The sharp claim, from the jurisdiction thread rather than the music literature

**The fixed 4-stem set (vocals/drums/bass/other) is a partition imposed on a lattice** — the same
tree-on-a-lattice error as hierarchical election districts. A piano is percussive *and* harmonic.
A synth pad straddles bass and `other` continuously. And **`other` is the residual bucket**: the
exact analogue of a **precinct split**, the representation admitting in its own vocabulary that
the regions overlap.

So: don't assign a bin to a stem — give it a **signature**, a distribution over which sources
participate, at adaptive cardinality. Which is Goodfire's ray→subspace move, arrived at
independently in interpretability for the same reason.

## Why the substrate is the differentiated part

- **DBSP incremental view maintenance** makes it genuinely online — and a revised belief about an
  earlier frame is a **Z-set retraction**, not a recompute, so late evidence corrects the past
  without reprocessing it.
- **Probabilistic circuits** answer the intractability Kolter & Jaakkola had to *approximate* —
  the word is in their title, and PCs are tractable by construction.
- Sources sharing a bus, room or performer are **dependent**, and a factor graph has somewhere to
  put that.

## The tool lineage — and why F2 is the product, not a metric

Aaron is a first-hand **Cool Edit → Audition → Pro Tools** expert. The descent is literal:
Audition's spectral editing *is* Cool Edit Pro v2, whose **Spectral Frequency Display** turned the
spectrogram from a readout into a **canvas**. Then spectral repair (iZotope RX), then AI rebalance
(RX 12 Music Rebalance, ARA 2).

**The defect that survived every step: none of them tells you where it is guessing.** A repair
looks identical whether the interpolation was well-constrained or invented. So:

> **A calibrated per-bin source-presence probability *is a spectrogram layer.***

That closes the McHarg loop from the audio side — stems + residual + confidence are **layers you
look through, not a merged result**. Cool Edit's canvas and McHarg's 1969 light table are the same
idea. (Noted from the same check: Adobe Audition has **no native AI stem separation in 2026** —
the vendor that owns the canvas hasn't put the AI in the box.)

## Falsifiers, including the one that predicts we lose

- **F1 — SDR.** MUSDB18-HQ / `museval` vs **BS-RoFormer** and **HT-Demucs**. **Predicted: we lose
  on raw SDR**, stated in advance, because a from-scratch Bayesian model will not top years of
  tuned discriminative transformers — and a design doc implying otherwise is what this repo exists
  to prevent.
- **F2 — calibration.** ECE on per-bin source presence: the metric the leaderboard **cannot
  enter**, since nothing on it emits a probability.
- **F3 — the overlap case.** Piano, pads, shared reverb bus. If signatures don't beat the
  partition *here*, the sharp claim is a story.
- **F4 — streaming.** Incremental posterior must equal batch posterior under DST replay, or the
  DBSP claim is unfounded.

## It makes the meter thread falsifiable

The charlatan/magician/teacher taxonomy is `toy` because our domains lack ground truth about what
was really read. **MUSDB18-HQ ships the true stems.** Charlatan = confident stems it can't
support. Magician = separates well, reports nothing. Teacher = stems *with* calibrated
uncertainty. Which makes **every current SOTA separator a magician by our own definition** — a
disposition, never a verdict, exactly as Aaron corrected it.

**Honest limit:** RX is mature and well-funded; "we will build a better RX" is *not* the claim.
And a calibrated-but-mediocre separator may be less useful than an uncalibrated excellent one —
named in §6 rather than discovered later.
