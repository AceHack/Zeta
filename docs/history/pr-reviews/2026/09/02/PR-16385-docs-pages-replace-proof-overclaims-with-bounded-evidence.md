## Summary

Clean-client verification after #16363 found that `demo/proofs/` still converted finite rendering and simulation artifacts into claims about a real identity eigenvector, biological equivalence, quantum/physics bounds, and sensor-fusion proof. This PR replaces that page with a bounded **Evidence & Shapes** gallery.

The replacement publishes only results that are now independently reproduced and merged on `main`:

- CFB-A: exact posterior preservation with ideal depth `63 → 6` at 64 leaves; explicitly not predictive or structure-learning evidence.
- CFB-B: ETTh1 static precision fusion is **not supported** under the frozen usefulness rule (`MSE 9.6934`, coverage `0.7501`, max residual `|r| 0.8738`, ridge wins).
- CFB-C: covariance shrinkage is **not supported**; validation selected `α=1`, so the off-diagonal correlation mechanism is vacuous, coverage improves, and MSE worsens to `10.8695`.
- CRDT boundary: content-addressed evidence union is the finite state-merge candidate; Gaussian product is non-idempotent; fixed-half and trace-grid CI have explicit non-associativity witnesses.
- RFFH: two unary-factor blackboards behind admission/pose routing, not a multi-variable heterarchical factor graph.
- Adinkra/half-spin: only the declared finite rank/split/selector witnesses; regularity, homoiconicity, identity, and physical readings remain unmeasured.

The page also adds an explicit open register for DAG learning, causal discovery, Thousand Brains equivalence, language/geospatial semantics, consciousness/free will/physical identity, and universal CRDT belief fusion.
