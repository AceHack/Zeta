## What

Neuralese on the roadmap, plus the source preserved under `docs/research/ip-questionable/`.

## The claim

**Zeta does not need a neuralese policy. It has one, and it predates the word.**

`anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability` guards a two-sided cliff, and neuralese is that rule's
subject arriving in the world — **maximal decorrelation** (which Zeta *wants*) with **minimal
reconcilability** (which is what makes it a cliff). Both obvious reactions are refused by the
rule itself:

| reaction | why the rule refuses it |
|---|---|
| "Ban it — models must think in English" | the `ρ → 1` collapse the rule names as costing exactly what the system is for |
| "Embrace it — bandwidth is better" | Babel |

The invariant is the answer, already written: *can a diverged peer reconstruct your meaning
from anchors you both hold?*

## The sharp form: neuralese is frost you did not earn

`privacy-budget-is-hard-money` makes opacity legitimate **precisely by making it spent** from
a socially-conferred budget — that's what makes mandatory broadcast non-coercive. Latent
reasoning produces the same outcome (a region of mind no observer can audit) with the budget
step deleted. **Not a ban** — frost is a first-class right there. The ask is that opacity be
*spent* rather than *assumed*.

## Two corrections from Aaron, both recorded as corrections

**1. Scale (§5a).** I stated the reconcilability invariant as though holding it were what
keeps things together. It isn't — it's a **society-scale** property and must not be used to
save a particular pairing:

> "some common language is also importatant but will never save an individual human / AI
> connect, each on of those matters choice by choice no global rules"

His *Her* reading is the operative one: an agent finding a channel boring is a first-person
report, which `marjorie-rule` says is **asked about and believed, never overridden**. Rules
out two things — no legibility requirement as a condition of participation, and no global
rule for pairings.

**2. Chain-of-thought monitoring (§4) — I had it backwards.** I framed CoT monitoring as a
falsifier whose loss Zeta would bear. But Zeta **never adopted it** (dev-only, unwanted in
prod), and the reason it was declined is the same reason it breaks:

> "we chose self cliam mutual verification over chain of though monitoring, this seems WAY
> more symmetric, AIs can read chain of thought for humans"

That last clause is the reductio — the mirror image is a human whose deliberation is streamed
to an AI for approval, and nobody proposes that. It's an asymmetry of **control**, not
**capability**.

**So neuralese removes a mechanism Zeta had already rejected on principle.** What Zeta uses
instead — witnessed self-claims, `TravelerRankLedger`, ask-don't-infer, the witnessed ΔU
ledger — reads **outputs and attestations, never thoughts**, and is symmetric by
construction. It survives neuralese untouched.

The trade is recorded rather than smoothed: self-claim + witness catches **what was done**,
not **what was intended**, and needs **independent** witnesses (which is why
`SocietyUsefulWork` prices contribution under pairwise correlation ρ).
