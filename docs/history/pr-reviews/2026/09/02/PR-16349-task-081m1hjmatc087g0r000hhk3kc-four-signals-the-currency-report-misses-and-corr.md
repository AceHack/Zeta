## What

Records the findings of an independent second review of **all 41** chart
coordinates, and **corrects a factual error I published**.

## The correction, first

I wrote — in a work item, a docstring, a baseline entry and two PR bodies — that
`charts.gabe565.com` was *"still alive (it publishes adguard-home actively)"* and
had merely stopped updating its headscale chart.

**Wrong, and wrong in the acquitting direction.** Measured and re-verified
independently: `max(created)` across **all 39 charts** in that index is
**2025-02-19**, adguard-home's own newest is 2025-02-19, and the index has not
been regenerated since 2025-02-20. The repository is abandoned.

I inferred activity from a version **list** without reading a single **date** —
the same shape as reading a green check without asking whether it ran, in the
file whose entire subject is dependencies that look healthier than they are.

## The four mechanical signals the report misses

| # | signal | what it would have caught |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **repo-level liveness** — `max(created)` over *every* entry in an index, not just the pinned chart's | gabe565 at **559 d**, i.e. my own error |
| 2 | **read `deprecated: true`** — already in the index, unread | `grafana/tempo 1.24.4` is formally **retired**; report says "quiet 214d" |
| 3 | **record `appVersion`** beside `version` | weaviate (pin 1.32.7 / chart 1.38.2 / project 1.39.2), headscale, oz |
| 4 | **widen scope to `infra/k8s/`** or exclude it by name | **six unreported pins**, in a tree that `prune`+`selfHeal` collides with the reported one |

Caveat pinned for (1): do **not** use the index's `generated:` field alone —
`charts.jetstack.io` reports `generated: 2021-04-28` while serving a chart created
2026-08-18. `max(created)` is the honest metric.

## headscale: the "or stay" option is closed, and there is nothing official to move to

- **Closed by policy** (Aaron): *"we never want to stay on projects who don't push updates, this is a security hazard."*
- **No official chart exists** — `juanfont/headscale` has no `Chart.yaml`, no `charts/`; `packaging/` is deb/rpm only. So the criterion is "pick the third party that tracks the app," best measured candidate `oci://codeberg.org/wrenix/helm-charts/headscale` 1.0.19 / app 0.29.3.
- **Do not take the cheap path.** The review rendered gabe565 0.16.0 with `image.tag: v0.29.3` overridden: it templates fine and the PVC is unchanged — and that is the trap. The chart's init container writes a v0.25-era config and hardcodes `HEADSCALE_IP_PREFIXES`, which headscale renamed after 0.25. A clean render there is **not** evidence the pod boots.
