## The defect, and its fix

`SoftValue.foldRetained` was documented as commutative. It holds for the normalized product and **fails for the contradiction predicate**: `build` refuses once total mass falls to or below `EPS`, and `observe` feeds it the *posterior-weighted* likelihood mean, which depends on the prefix. Two nodes with the same evidence set, differing only in arrival order, could diverge into **belief vs contradiction**.

Diagnosed in exact rational arithmetic — **103 of 400 random evidence sets disagreed under permutation at `eps=1e-12`, 0 of 400 at `eps=0`** — so it was never rounding; the threshold was the defect. Fixed by accumulating unnormalized and normalizing once, in log-space with a **canonical (sorted) summation order** so the result is bit-identical under reordering. Declared treaty bounds (`MAX_EVIDENCE_COUNT`, `MAX_FOLD_WORK`) close a measured DoS surface: 4× the evidence cost 27× the time, and the order-invariant fix is also the 27×-faster one.

**Non-vacuity proven by mutation:** restoring the original implementation fails exactly the three new near-floor falsifiers while all 16 original tests still pass — the old suite used likelihoods five orders clear of the floor and could never reach the corner.

Also fixes `chiralityViolations`, which was **a check that could not fail** (parity preserved identically for every input and every option, including the existing mutant), and gives it a witness that can move it.
