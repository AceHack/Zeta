**A recovery PR, and the cause is mine.**

#16662 was armed for auto-merge and I kept pushing to it. It merged at head `a4d2a74ba` — the *ferry* commit — the moment checks went green on that SHA. Two later commits never landed:

- `4a56fe8cc` — upstream's CI diagnosed OpenSearch, the fix, the **lift**, and `upstream-chart-ci.ts`
