## What looks like the same shape — recorded as correspondence, not result

- **Born-as-consequence.** The talk derives the Born rule as a *coherence constraint on gambles* — normative, not descriptive. Aaron's account of our own history is that the Born rule *fell out* of modelling mutual agent memories and heartbeat verification. Two different routes to the same disposition, filed as a coincidence with its register attached.
- **Never-collapse.** The tenet that probability 1 has no grip on the world is the refusal `SoftValue.fs` already implements, and which names itself *"the never-collapse discipline"*.
- **A normative coherence layer over per-agent beliefs.** `LocalConsensus.fs` defines consensus only on entangled subgraphs and is *"not a vote; it is posterior convergence"* — a coherence constraint among agents rather than an aggregation rule.
- `QuorumAlgebra.fs` already carries *"where Aaron placed the Born boundary"*, and its interference sum is explicitly non-idempotent — a join could not cancel.

## What it would actually cost

An informationally-complete fiducial measurement and the deformed law of total probability that goes with it. With SIC-POVM at **zero** occurrences, the honest first question is whether a Gaussian/EP analogue of a fiducial measurement exists at all — not to assume one does because the story is attractive.

**Register:** the correspondence is *argued*, not measured. The file-count table is the only measurement in the note, and it measures our corpus, not the physics. Every anchor the talk names is unverified here.
