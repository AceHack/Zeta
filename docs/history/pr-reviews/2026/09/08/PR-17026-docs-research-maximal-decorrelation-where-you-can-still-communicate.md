docs(research): maximal decorrelation where you can still communicate

Ferries four streamed observations from Aaron, 2026-09-08, arriving minutes
apart and building on each other:

  1. anti-Babel vs anti-correlation is where the 2 root 2 limit comes from
  2. "maximal decorlation where you can still communicate"
  3. "i think this is our mutual empowerment optimizatoins for society comes from"
  4. maximal-decorrelated-but-still-communicating is a DV2.0 HUB; more
     correlated is a SATELLITE

(2) is the load-bearing one and it corrects my own first capture. I wrote it as
a BAND -- two pressures with slack between them -- and a band is a range that
cannot pick out a number. What Aaron states is a constrained extremum: maximize
decorrelation SUBJECT TO reconcilability holding. One is the objective, the
other the constraint, and the answer sits where the constraint binds. That is
the only shape whose solution is a single value.

The bridge that makes (3) more than analogy is already in-tree: the repo's own
definition of empowerment is a CHANNEL CAPACITY (Klyubin, Polani & Nehaniv 2005
-- capacity from an agent's actions to its own future observations), and
Information Causality, the leading candidate for what selects 2 root 2 out of
the non-signalling set (Pawlowski et al., Nature 2009), is also a channel
statement. Anti-Babel's "still reconcilable" is a floor on channel capacity. So
the conjecture is that the constraint in both problems is the same TYPE, which
is structure rather than a matching count.

It also lands on an existing doc rather than a blank page: the 2026-08-09
mutual-empowerment-bound research already wrote objective-subject-to-constraint
for the social bound, a month before this ferry, including the finding that a
naive linear blend is vacuous.

Register is `toy` throughout and deliberately. Nothing is derived or measured,
and section 7 quotes the standing in-tree warning at FourCornerC4.fs:346-353
verbatim -- "that lining-up is numerology, not identification" -- because this
document must not become the thing that warning exists to prevent. Nothing here
licenses writing 2 root 2 into code, a constant, or a discharge row.

Two self-checks are written into the document rather than left out:

  * Section 4c runs the independence test on its own thread, because four
    things clicking in fifteen minutes is the condition
    numerology-vs-number-theory names as a WARNING, not a confirmation signal.
    Honest count: two genuinely independent supports, one restatement (the
    DV2.0 mapping re-describes the ordering rather than testing it), and the
    claim itself.
  * Section 4b heads off a vocabulary collision: the DV2.0 hub is a KEY, not
    the routing chokepoint of itron-hub-patent-boundary. Being a DV2.0 hub
    carries no authority and nobody must route through it.

The falsifier the band framing lacked: measure the greatest decorrelation at
which reconciliation still succeeds. A negative result is available -- if that
ceiling sits at the non-signalling maximum, the reconcilability constraint is
not binding and the conjecture is wrong.

All internal links resolve and every cited source file exists; the two quoted
in-tree warnings were read and quoted verbatim rather than paraphrased. Note
markdownlint carves out docs/research/2026-*, so a clean lint here is not
evidence of anything.
