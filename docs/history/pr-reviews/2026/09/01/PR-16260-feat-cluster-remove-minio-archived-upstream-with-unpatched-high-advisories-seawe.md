## Why, and why not deferred again

`minio/minio` is **archived and read-only**; the chart source lived *inside that same repo*, so the stale chart and the dead server are one fact. Development moved to proprietary AIStor. And the AGPL community build carries **four unpatched HIGH advisories, two of them unauthenticated object write** — every fix ships only in AIStor.

That's an in-cluster S3 endpoint nobody will ever patch.

This was already surveyed on 2026-08-21 in [the research doc](../blob/main/docs/research/2026-08-21-minio-is-archived-upstream-what-replaces-our-s3-and-seaweedfs-is-already-running.md), which recommended retiring MinIO but **sequenced behind the metal boot**. You overrode that sequencing today. The advisories are why removing beats waiting.
