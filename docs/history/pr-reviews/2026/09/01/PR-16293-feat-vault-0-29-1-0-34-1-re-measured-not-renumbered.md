## What this evidence does NOT cover

Stated plainly so *"the audit passes"* is not read as more than it is: the **Vault binary goes 1.18.1 → 2.0.4, a major**. These rules encode what the *chart* renders from our values, not what the Vault binary does with the resulting config, and **none of them would notice a behavioural change inside Vault 2.0**. Recorded at the pin and beside the constant rather than left implied.

The direction is strongly favourable regardless: `hashicorp/vault:1.18.1` was last published **2024-10-30** — nearly two years — against `2.0.4` on 2026-08-04.
