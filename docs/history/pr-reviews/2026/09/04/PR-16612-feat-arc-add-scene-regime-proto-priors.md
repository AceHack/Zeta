## Why

The coordinate policy currently treats visible components uniformly. Exact frame hashes would memorize levels, while a single motion flag cannot distinguish translation from recoloring, shape change, split/merge, or appearance. This adds reusable grid-only evidence without reading engine state or promoting an uncalibrated acting policy.

- generate a byte-locked next-mover benchmark with a required counterexample
