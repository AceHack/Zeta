## Summary

Block IO is a device-shaped write, not unique crash recovery and not a measured speed win.

- `ArmTornSector`: overlay first N of NEW, rest of the LBA stays OLD, write acks (distinct from crash which throws, and corrupt which XOR-flips a full write).
- `GroupCommitDiskDeltaLog` defaults to `IBlockIo` / `ZGL2`. POSIX append (`useBlockIo = false`) stays for whole-file Dispose tests.
- `BlockCas.Put` skips an existing key (D9, does not promote recovery out of `toy`).

POSIX append already recovers torn tails. No bench compares the two doors. Native NVMe is not claimed.

## Honesty peel

Crash recovery stays `toy`. Unmetered: 4K RMW of a small record may allocate more than a record-sized POSIX write.
