## Summary

Two grow-only blocked Blooms -- Bloom(I) over ever-inserted keys, Bloom(D) over ever-deleted keys -- return `{Absent, Present, Unknown}`. Bits never decrement. This is the ZD10 construction on the ZetaDB/Core query-membership path, not a filesystem feature.

Does not reopen WONT-DO deletable Bloom (Rothenberg 2010 silent false negatives). Counting Bloom stays the join-probe path. Toy until ZD10 measures `fp(I) x fp(D)` vs counting space and the unknown region under retraction churn (including resurrection).

- `x not in I` -> Absent (Bloom has no false negatives)
- `x in I and x not in D` -> Present (error is only `fp(I)`)
- both -> Unknown (product `fp(I) x fp(D)`)
- retract of unseen stays Absent; insert-delete-insert is Unknown
- `MergeFrom` unions I and D (G-set)
