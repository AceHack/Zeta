## What and why

Follow #16770's exact algebra comparison with a genuinely trained, bounded F# recurrent learner on the published Mess3 process. The observation-only learner uses no neural library and does not enter the database runtime. ## Measured result and limits

- Freeze the protocol before training, then retain all nine width/seed combinations and all final parameters. Each sees 2,097,152 prediction positions; probe fitting and evaluation use independent draws.
- All nine trained networks beat their empirical unigram/bigram and untrained-network controls on next-token and joint-three-token KL, at both context lengths.
- Main-panel trained next KL is 0.000654 to 0.002535 bits. The known-model filter remains exact, with zero excess KL by construction.
- The output-only belief probe beats the hidden-state probe in every main-panel run. This process already identifies beliefs from next-token probabilities; no extra information or causal Bayes circuit is established.
- Numeric parameter-plus-state payloads are 288, 1048 and 3096 bytes, not total heap usage. Cumulative training allocation is about 145 MB. No runtime, peak-memory, ARC-transfer, fractal-dimension or quantum advantage is claimed.
