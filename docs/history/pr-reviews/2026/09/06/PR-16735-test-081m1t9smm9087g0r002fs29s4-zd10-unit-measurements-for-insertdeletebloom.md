## Summary

ZD10 unit measurements for the two grow-only Blooms (I, D):

1. Space at n=10000, p=0.01: pair is smaller than 4-bit counting.
2. Never-inserted Unknown stays at or below Present (product region).
3. After retracting every inserted key the pair is Unknown for all of them; counting MayContain is false. Resurrection stays Unknown on the pair and becomes present on counting.

Not a MemoryDiagnoser bench. Does not replace counting Bloom on the join-probe path. Does not reopen WONT-DO deletable Bloom. Not a filesystem feature.
