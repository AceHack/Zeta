# Summary

Aaron: continue after the load-site classifier (#16791). Measurement of a candidate `bao` belongs in installer/first-boot, not in the overlay. This slice parses ELF64 little-endian `PT_INTERP` from injected bytes. It does not spawn `readelf`. It does not land `seal "pkcs11"` in Application.yaml.

- `ptInterpFromElfBytes`: truncated / 32-bit / non-ELF is unmeasured, not a glibc proof.
- `captureBaoElfFromRead`: installer may open a bao path. Overlay still does not. `.so`, the restore pointer, and `/dev/tpmrm0` are not opened. Site stays named.
- `nodeBaoElfRead`: one `readFileSync`. ENOENT is unmeasured. Other errno rethrows (no `existsSync` then `readFileSync`).
