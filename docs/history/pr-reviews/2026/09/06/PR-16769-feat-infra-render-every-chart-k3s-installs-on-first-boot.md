## Description

The **render half** of *"k8s/helm testing that closely matches metal in CI."* The pin half
landed in #16766.

## The gap

`validate-applications.ts --render` renders all 37 charts under
`full-ai-cluster/k8s/applications/` and kubeconforms the output. The `helm.cattle.io/v1`
HelmChart CRs under the two `k8s/bootstrap/` trees got **no such treatment** —
`validate-bootstrap.ts` checks each pins an exact version and that the roster matches
`services.k3s.manifests`, and stops.

**Nothing had ever asked whether they render** with the `valuesContent` sitting beside them.

They are the manifests K3S auto-applies on **NixOS first boot, before ArgoCD exists**, and
**no CI lane applies them** — kind and k3d bring ArgoCD up from `dev-cluster/use-cases.ts`.
So the metal boot path was simultaneously the most metal-specific surface in the tree and
the least checked one. A values key that stopped being valid at its pinned version would
have been found by someone's hardware, at boot, with no ArgoCD running to report it.

## What it proves, and what it does not — stated in the file

**Proves:** the chart+version resolves upstream, the templates evaluate against these exact
values, and the objects satisfy their schemas.

**Does not prove:** that the release comes up. `helm template` is not `helm install`. That is
what the NixOS/QEMU tests already in `full-ai-cluster/nixos/tests/` are for
(`k3s-first-boot-roster.nix`, `k3s-first-boot-apply-order-eval-test.nix`), and **this does
not replace them** — it is the cheap check on every PR, ahead of the expensive ones that
need a VM.

## Refusals and falsifiers

It **refuses below a floor of 6 CRs** rather than reporting "0 failed" over a collapsed scan.

The render needs helm and the network, so the proof that eight charts template clean is the
CI step; the six offline cases cover everything **upstream of the spawn** — multi-document
parsing (`spire-install.yaml` really carries two, and a first-doc-only reader would render
half a file and still print a confident `Results:` line), skipping a CR missing
chart/repo/version rather than handing helm `undefined`, the K3S defaults for absent
`valuesContent`/namespace, and one case reading the **real** tree that asserts **both** trees
contribute.
