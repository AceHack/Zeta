## Description

Two more of the four first-boot pin divergences (#16766). They moved **together and in this
order** on purpose: `trust-manager` consumes `cert-manager`'s CRDs, so paying trust-manager
first would have measured the wrong variable.

## `cert-manager` v1.16.2 → v1.21.1

Rendered with the bootstrap's **own** `valuesContent` at both, compared as **kind/name pairs**:

- **52 objects → 50, nothing added**
- the two losses are `Role/cert-manager-tokenrequest` and its RoleBinding
- every value still lands: 6 CRDs at both, all three Deployments at `replicas: 1`, no
  ServiceMonitor at either

This independently reproduces the Application's own 2026-09-01 note — which had already
measured the same two losses — **including the trap it warns about**: the newer chart
**quotes `metadata.name`** and the older does not, so a raw-text diff reads all six CRDs as
removed-and-re-added. Comparing kind/name pairs is what makes *"nothing added"* trustworthy,
and it's why these numbers agree with a measurement taken five days ago by someone else.

## `trust-manager` v0.15.0 → v0.24.0

- **14 objects → 15, nothing removed**
- the one addition is `ClusterRole/trust-manager-cluster-view`
- **the load-bearing value survives**: `Role` and `RoleBinding` `trust-manager` still land in
  namespace **`openziti`** at v0.24.0 — the Secrets-reading Role ziti-controller's Bundle
  depends on, and the entire subject of that file's `MUST MATCH` note

**And that note now guards the version too.** It said a *value* must match the Application
while the *version* sat three minors apart — the file was guarding the smaller half of its
own hazard, in a paragraph that correctly described the hazard.

## Both baseline entries are deleted, not edited

The key carries both versions, so editing one would be a fresh acknowledgement of a
divergence that no longer exists.

**One left: `external-secrets` 0.10.7 vs 2.10.0.** Its own entry says to treat that as a
migration rather than a bump, and it is **not** being done here on the strength of two easy
ones landing.
