## Summary

WP15's catalog audit (PR #17516) flagged 46 containers with a `livenessProbe`, no
`startupProbe`, and a kill budget (`initialDelay + (failureThreshold-1)*period`)
under 120s. 2 were fixed on the spot (hindsight); the other 44 were grandfathered
into `KILL_BUDGET_ALLOWLIST`, tracked by workitem
`081M348H97G087G0R0020EA0NY`.

This WP ranks and fixes 17 of the 44 against real evidence, rather than fixing
them all by guess:

1. **CI restart evidence** — extracted per-container restart counts / CRASHLOOP
   events from 5 recent `k8s-argocd-health-test.yml` "live kind included" runs
   (35713533700, 35709838861, 35707656488, 35703810167, 35703051689) and one
   `first-boot-replica.yml` run (35700790207, real k3s manifest roster,
   artifact `first-boot-replica-report`).
2. **Known-slow-starter classification** (JVM/DB/migration-on-start) for the
   rest, per the WP scope.
