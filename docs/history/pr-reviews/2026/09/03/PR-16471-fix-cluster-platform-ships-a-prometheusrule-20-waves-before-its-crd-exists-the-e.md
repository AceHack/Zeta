## Description

Aaron: *"we should try to fill in the gaps if not in ace at least in our app of apps"* — and his correction that **the app-of-apps IS our developer API**, so inter-Application ordering is ours to declare, not Helm's to provide.

## Derived, not guessed

Service-DNS derivation found 7 cross-app edges and **all 7 were already declared**. This is the next evidence class: scan every in-repo manifest for non-core `apiVersion`s, map each custom kind to the Application installing its CRD. Four Applications use CRs in-repo (cdi, cilium-lb-ipam, kubevirt, platform); two are self-referential, cilium-lb-ipam→cilium was declared, and **one edge was missing.**

## The finding

`platform/monitoring.yaml` ships a `monitoring.coreos.com/v1` **ServiceMonitor** (line 29) and **PrometheusRule** (line 50). `monitoring` is one of the 15 names in that Application's own `directory.include` glob, so **both are applied**. kube-prometheus-stack installs those CRDs — at wave **0**, against platform's **−20**.

**A check I ran on myself:** my first glob test reported "matched by glob: 0", which would have made the file inert and the finding vacuous. That was Python's `fnmatch` lacking **brace expansion**, not a fact about the tree — ArgoCD's glob does support braces. Recorded, because the wrong reading would have retired a real defect.

## Why it's registered separately from platform's two sibling inversions

They degrade differently. A Gateway with no LB pool is **admitted** and merely address-less; a PVC with no StorageClass **pends** — both quiet waits. **A custom resource whose CRD does not exist is rejected by the API server**, so platform reports SyncFailed on those two objects on every cold boot until wave 0 lands. ArgoCD's retry converges it — bootstrap noise and a degraded-status window, not permanent failure.

## Not repaired, on purpose — four repairs, not equivalent

1. Move platform after kube-prometheus-stack — **expensive**; platform installs eight CRDs others create resources against.
2. **Split `monitoring.yaml` into its own Application at a later wave** — cheapest, and it's the only object in platform needing a wave later than −20.
3. `SkipDryRunOnMissingResource=true` — silences the symptom, keeps the inversion.
4. Drop the two objects.

(2) looks right and is **still a maintainer call**, because it moves ownership of the platform's own telemetry.

The machinery is why this is visible at all: declaring the true edge produced a **NEW unregistered** disagreement and rc=1; registering it with a reason returns **rc=0 at 9 known / 0 new**. The registry can't rot into an allowlist — a registered entry whose disagreement disappears also fails.
