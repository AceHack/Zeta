## What

Spec for the society connection as Aaron stated it — *"operates over CRDTs by default and then upgrades to more and more consensus based on disagreement and distrust"* — written on top of what already ships, not around it.

`docs/research/2026-09-03-society-crdt-default-consensus-escalation-spec-per-key-ladder-over-disagreement-and-distrust.md`

- **Inventory (§1)** with file:line of every shipped piece: `Crdt`, `TwoTimescaleFold`, `BeliefConvergence`, `QuorumAlgebra` (`Conflicted`, `interferenceExcess`), `ClaimLane` (Lane 1/2), `CasStore`, `SybilBft*`, `Consensus.decide`, `TravelerRankLedger`, `AntiSybil`, the decorrelation meters, the TLA+ model — and the four things that do not exist: a rung that moves on a *measured* quantity, per-key scoping of BFT, hysteresis, and a rank-ledger reading that is a function of the evidence set.
- **Two metrics (§3)** computed from the evidence set alone: disagreement `D(k) = 1 − max_v n_v / N` over anti-Sybil-collapsed, non-equivocating sources; distrust `U(k) = 1 − mean trustBand` (min over a source's names, in the key's hat-domain). No order, no multiplicity, no clock.
- **Ladder (§4)** as a typed state machine: `R0Union → R1Witnessed → R2Quorum → R3Bft`, plus `Surfaced` for what the ladder refuses to auto-resolve (roster below `3f+1`, non-compensable non-monotone ops). Non-monotone ops go straight to rung 3 (CALM). Downward moves on evidence: certificate-settling at rung 3 now; `Settle`-count hysteresis once agreed-phase stamps exist. The rung changes what a node *reports*, never what enters the set.
- **Why per key (§5)**, **anchors checked for entailment (§6)** with the unmetered residue marked `toy`, **eleven falsifiers with their mutations (§7)**, and what is buildable now vs. what waits on the in-flight order-independence work.

## One measurement

`TravelerRankLedger.update` (ADF probit) is order-dependent: the same multiset {hit, hit, miss} folds to trustBand 0.5994 / 0.6032 / 0.6088 depending on order; 1.3 pp apart at eight observations (§3.3, script in Appendix A). A distrust threshold read from arrival order would put two nodes on different rungs from identical evidence. The spec fixes a canonical fold order (content-address order), the same move as `Consensus.decide`'s ordinal tie-break.
