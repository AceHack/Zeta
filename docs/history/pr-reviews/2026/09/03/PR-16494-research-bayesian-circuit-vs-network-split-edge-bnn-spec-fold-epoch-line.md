## Bayesian circuit vs. network: the split, the edge-BNN spec, and the fold/epoch line

Research doc only — no file moved, no behaviour changed. Lands `docs/research/2026-09-03-bayesian-circuit-vs-network-split-and-edge-bnn-spec-fold-vs-epoch-learn-parameters-not-messages.md`.

**Aaron's ask (2026-09-03, math team):** an in-depth spec for Bayesian NNs at each connection of the Bayesian DAG, the most optimal way to connect them, and a split of code and tests so they honestly say "bayesian circuit" vs "neural network" — "we clump a lot into BNN that should likely be bayesian circuits cause they are a dag."

### What the audit measured (every verdict carries `file:line`)

- 49 modules in `src/Bayesian/`; exactly **one** (`ToyBosonFermionBnn`) holds a posterior over weights. `MinimalBnn` and `MultilayerBnn` have no weights, no learned map, and exact inference on trees — linear-Gaussian probabilistic circuits (Darwiche 2003 arithmetic circuits) misnamed as networks. The clumping is real and one-directional.
- "network" has three referents in `src/Bayesian/` (Pearl / neural / social); "circuit" already means DBSP in `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. Both senses are held, dated, per VISION §definition-drift and `anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability.md`.
- **Defect found (latent):** `FactorGraphExactness` is computed from topology ∧ convergence only (`MultilayerBnn.fs:625-629`). An EP site or a learned edge in an acyclic graph would still read `ExactAcyclic`. The spec adds a factor-class axis with a monotone meet — the invariant-(2) guard.

### The spec (register: `toy`, unrun)

- The only legal learnable site is `Factor.ComputeMessages` (a learned marginalisation node); variable nodes are the cavity and must stay exact or EP is ill-defined.
- **Fold vs. epoch** is the crux: inference is a commutative, idempotent fold over the content-addressed evidence set `E`; learning is an order-dependent epoch that mints a frozen, content-addressed weight treaty `Θ_k`, consumed by the fold by hash. Agents agree iff they hold the same `(E, S, Θ_k)`. Only conjugate sufficient-statistic accumulation (L0) may be folded, and it must be proven L0 by a permutation falsifier.
- **Recommended topology:** learn the *parameters* of closed-form factors (conditional circuit, Shao et al. 2020) by default; learned *messages* only on edges with no closed form (NEBP, Satorras & Welling 2021); never on every edge; never one amortised network. Costs, exactness impact, falsifiers, and what would refute the recommendation are in §6.
- Falsifiers F-1..F-7 and CIRC-1 named per claim; five anchors flagged "needs page-check", which keeps §5–§6 at `toy` regardless.

### Split plan (not performed)

`Zeta.Bayesian.Circuit` / `Zeta.Bayesian.Network`; `MinimalBnn → GaussianCell`, `MultilayerBnn → GaussianDagCircuit`, `AdinkraEquivariantFactorLayer → AdinkraSectorFactor`; `Compat.fs` shims with `[<Obsolete>]`; test ids (`MLBNN-*`, `RFFH-*`) kept as keys; byte-diff of serialised outputs as the no-behaviour-change acceptance; a proposed drift-tier lint so the clumping cannot re-accrete. Phased; phase 3 (shim removal) is the only breaking step and goes through public-API review.
