## Summary

This PR adds a finite, versioned candidate English seed and deterministic lexical coverage audit. It advances the existing syntax-and-uncertainty substrate without overstating it as an English-speaking or grounded-language system.

The v0 audit has 65 declared candidate entries and measures the first sentence of 122 headings from `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and `docs/SEED-VOCABULARY.md`. It finds 670 known tokens among 2,137 considered tokens: `0.31352363125877397`. No heading is fully covered. Those unknowns are retained in the receipt.
