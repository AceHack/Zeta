## Description

Item 2 of the agentic-organization port list: the **observe-act promotion gate**.

From `agentic-organization/docs/OBSERVE_ACT_PROMOTION_GATE.md`, the sentence that makes it worth porting:

> *"The gate is deterministic. Agents may select from legal menu slots, but they do not decide whether the organization is safe to promote a lane."*

That is the shape this whole harness is built out of — a guardrail the agent cannot argue with — applied to the one decision that matters most: **whether the loop's actions reach the world.**

| mode | what happens |
|---|---|
| `shadow` | the loop observes, builds the menu, chooses and RECORDS; dispatch is a shadow implementation |
| `primary` | dispatch uses the real injected runtime |

Thresholds unchanged from the org doc: promote on ≥100 ticks **or** ≥24h soak, zero illegal slot selections, divergence ≤5%; demote on ≥2 selector rejections **or** ≥1 control-bypass rejection in the last 30 minutes.

## Four properties, each a fail-open if dropped

| property | mutant that kills its removal |
|---|---|
| **Demotion outranks promotion**, first and unconditionally | M3 demotion checked after promotion |
| **An insufficient window resolves to shadow** — absence of evidence is not evidence of safety | M9 an absent window promotes |
| **A non-finite metric resolves to shadow** — every comparison against `NaN` is `false`, so a naive `divergence > MAX` reads a corrupt counter as "not too divergent" | M1 validation after thresholds / M2 NaN accepted |
| **A refusal emits evidence too** — a gate that only records its yeses cannot be audited for its noes | (asserted directly) |

Two more on the executor side:

- The shadow executor reports `ok: true`, not a refusal. Otherwise every shadow tick reads as a broken tick and **poisons the very divergence rate the gate reads** — no lane could ever soak its way out of shadow.
- It reports tier `fake`, because nothing ran. Same rule the room sandbox follows: never misdescribe where work ran.

## Wiring

`run-loop-real.ts` evaluates the gate each tick and passes `executorForMode(gate.mode, executor)` to `execute()`. Shadow **discards** the real executor rather than wrapping it — one that held a reference would be one refactor away from calling it. Window path overridable via `ZETA_PROMOTION_WINDOW`; default `db/promotion/window.json`.

## Honest scope

This is the DECISION, not the plumbing. The org side folds its window from durable `observe_act_tick` events in Cockroach; here the window arrives as a value, so the gate stays pure and replays under DST. What it cannot do is verify that the numbers it was handed are true — a lane reporting its own clean window is trusting the reporter. That is a property of the window SOURCE, stated in the module rather than implied away.
