## Description

Second pass on the hats/Zadeh ferry. Four changes and one confirmation, all from Aaron.

THE GROUNDING, and it answers a question both prior documents left open. Lumen said the
conflict discount "must come from data" and pointed at TravelerRankLedger. Aaron is more
specific and more radical: "statistics based on visible disjoint histories each traveler
chooses to trust is what makes this algebra. it's relative, based on pairwise memories."

So THERE IS NO VIEW-INDEPENDENT (t, f). The pair is a statistic over the histories this
traveler can see and has chosen to trust, and two travelers can hold different pairs for
the same proposition with neither being wrong. Three consequences:

  * the residual becomes a property of the OBSERVER as well as the evidence -- r > 0 can
    mean "the histories I trust do not settle this", a fact about vantage;
  * it gives the knowledge order a MECHANISM, which Lumen identified but left abstract:
    you move up it by admitting more trusted history;
  * "pairwise" becomes load-bearing rather than descriptive -- the belief is an EDGE
    property, same shape as the naming eigenvector and the privacy budget, and that is
    why society-scale can stay smooth while pair-scale tangles. A global belief would
    need a global observer and Section 1 forbids one.

Unbuilt, with a cheap falsifier: two travelers with deliberately different trust sets must
derive DIFFERENT pairs from the same evidence, or the implementation has smuggled in a
global view.

ASSUME ACTORS ARE NOT INDEPENDENT -- this inverts the previous default. The first pass said
"independence must be earned" and treated correlation as the failure mode. Aaron: "we
assume they are not, and have a dual-use oracle around cartel detection and forgery of
identity that tries to make this first class, and let the society decide the dual-use
outcome over time."

Better, and for the reason never-assume-malice gives: correlation between two actors has an
enormous space of innocent explanations -- shared context, shared training, simply being
right together -- so a mechanism that treats correlation as evidence of collusion has
attached a verdict its measurement cannot support. The detector may say Correlated(a,b,deg);
it may not say CartelDetected. And the dual use is real in BOTH directions, which is what
makes it a Section 11 case rather than a security feature: high correlation is a cartel
under one reading and a genuine consensus under another, and the second is what you want
when the two are independently right. Partly built already -- AntiSybil/CoordinationSpectrum
report SameSourceAsKnown as a neutral fact; the cartel half does not exist.

HATS ARE SWITCHABLE MID-CONVERSATION. Aaron on the name/hat distinction: "my most competent
colleagues and I can wear multiple hats at once and switch them mid-conversation if asked --
it's like asking the other person to strongman your argument while they ask you to weakman
it." Two testable consequences: a hat must be cheap enough to put on and take off inside one
exchange (a real implementation constraint), and simultaneity is a mark of COMPETENCE rather
than a pathology. And the strongman/weakman example is the mechanism in miniature: an
explicit, consensual, temporary exchange of hats -- which is also MANUFACTURED
DECORRELATION, two parties deliberately taking opposed stances to keep the pair off rho -> 1.
The correlation detector above would see them becoming LESS correlated on request, which is
a signal about method, not collusion.

WHETHER THE TOWERS GENUINELY JOIN MAY ITSELF NEED MANY TOWERS. "in a perfect world yes, but
this will likely take many disagreements to settle on if it's true, or this also needs multi
towers." Self-referential and not a joke: the question may have several inequivalent
formalisations settled by persistent disagreement rather than one proof -- anti-babel's
monodromy clause applied to the meta-question. Sets the honest expectation: do not wait for
the join question to be settled before building.

Two confirmations kept with their caveats. Mint-based addressing holds "unless we come up
with some universal theory of everything, which i'm not actively trying to do" -- recorded
as the stated exception rather than pretending none exists, since a single root is exactly
what a universal theory would be. And on Mac Lane: "if this theorem holds here it might
strengthen it" -- which inverts the usual direction of borrowing, so the claim to NOVELTY
needs the same check anchor-to-human-prior-art demands of a claim to lineage. Marked
SPECULATIVE; nobody has checked whether Zeta's setting is outside the theorem's known
instances.
