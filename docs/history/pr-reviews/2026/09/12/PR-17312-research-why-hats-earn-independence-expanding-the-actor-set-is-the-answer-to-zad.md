## Description

Ferries five observations Aaron made reading Lumen's belief-pair analysis. One is
flagged by its author as a FIRST UTTERANCE and supplies a reason for a mechanism this
repo has been operating on instinct.

WHY HATS EARN INDEPENDENCE. Zadeh's counterexample -- two near-certain, near-disjoint
experts fused by Dempster's rule return CERTAINTY for a hypothesis both put at 1% --
is a property of a TWO-PARTY fusion. Aaron: "we can overcome this easily with a witness
or quorum ... we can always expand actors in our equations. this is closely related to
our hats and why they earn independence. this is the first time i've said why our hats
earn independence."

The fix is not a better rule, it is MORE ACTORS. And that is what a hat is: a role
ranked separately per domain (`TravelerRankLedger.fs`, held by others, never
self-asserted, domain-isolated). Domain isolation is exactly what makes a second hat an
INDEPENDENT actor rather than a correlated copy.

So the same theorem arrives from a third direction. Knight-Leveson: correlated versions
fail in correlated ways, so N correlated observations are one observation counted N
times. Zadeh is that with the sign flipped: two ANTI-correlated observations, fused by
a rule that divides their conflict away, manufacture a certainty neither held. Both are
cured by actors whose independence is EARNED, and the hat is the unit in which this repo
earns it.

Caution recorded rather than suppressed: expanding the actor set does not answer
"whose fault is the conflict", it dilutes it -- and a quorum of correlated actors is one
actor wearing several hats, which is the Sybil shape. The existing defence is named
(contribution does not scale with copies; `SocietyUsefulWork.fs` prices clones near one
agent's worth).

UNCERTAINTY IS TURTLES. Lumen's scoping line -- "the analysis is void without the
declared semantics" -- treats the declaration as a certainty supplied from outside.
Aaron: "we need to allow for uncertainty even in the algebra and dual confidence levels
all the way down." If the belief pair carries "how sure are we of this proposition",
then "which algebra governs this lane" is a proposition and takes one too. That is
manifesto Section 9 recursive and Section 10 self-similar applied to the uncertainty
representation itself; a system that demands certainty about its own modelling choices
has a privileged frame, which scale-free forbids. Cost stated honestly: the honest
version is BOUNDED regress with the truncation NAMED, not infinite regress -- the same
move `SoftValue.resolve threshold` already makes.

SOCIETY SMOOTH, PAIRS CHAOTIC. "pairs can be chaotic and entangled like our homoclinic
tangles; the overall society interaction should try to be clean and smooth." Complexity
licensed at pair scale, refused at society scale -- and the useful half of Poincare's
tangle is its BOUNDEDNESS: arbitrarily intricate, inside its region. This lands on
Lumen's result directly: the pair keeps the tangle (knowledge order, conflict retained),
the society reads the smooth projection (truth order, decided), the boundary operator IS
the scale separation, and the residual is the only society-scale evidence that a
pair-scale tangle existed. Tension with anti-babel named and resolved: the principle
governs the interaction SURFACE, not the contents.

THE BOUNDARY TAKES A SET OF ORACLES, NOT ONE. "it could be a set parameter that accepts
an oracle class of N." Stronger than Lumen's formulation and follows from the same rule:
if the objection to a hardcoded boundary is mandatory judgement, a parameter admitting
exactly one oracle has only relocated the mandate to the call site. N makes the rule's
own test expressible IN THE TYPE -- two parties disagreeing about what a measurement
implies becomes a value the boundary returns. Open cost priced: Lumen's Section 3.3
already refutes "combine the N" as a primitive, so the likely honest answer is that they
do NOT compose and are held, which makes the result type a set.

TWO PIECES OF VOCABULARY LANDING -- bilattice, and reverse indexes as materialized views
over DBSP. Both are Mirror->Beacon compressions. Caution attached: a name that FITS is
not an identification; the bilattice claim earns the word by invariants and explicitly
excludes C4, and every further candidate has to pass that test on its own.
