## Description

## The finding, measured first

`SoftValue`'s invariant is *"non-empty, all weights > 0, weights sum to 1"*. Every normalising constructor — `certain` / `ofWeighted` / `map` / `bind` / `observe` / `combine` / `widen` / `foldRetainedBounded` — routes through `build`, which divides by the total. So the residual `r = 1 - SUM(w)` is pinned at **0** for every reachable value: the knowledge order has no extent, and two of Belnap's four corners — `Neither` (nobody has looked) and `Both` (two sources landed and disagree) — are unreachable.

**The limit is the INVARIANT, not the carrier.** `SoftValue.unnormalized` builds the same `WeightedSet` with normalisation skipped and holds both corners fine. Both halves are now falsifiers in `DualScore.Tests.fs` (section THE FINDING), quantified over the constructors — not a summary of somebody's reading.

## What landed

**`src/Core/DualScore.fs`** — two independent chances in `[0,1]`, neither derived from the other, with the **signed** residual first-class: `r > 0` ignorance, `r < 0` contradiction, `r = 0` classical. All three regimes representable and all four corners named (`vacuous` / `onlyTrue` / `onlyFalse` / `both`, plus a `corners` roster). Constructors **refuse** out-of-range and non-finite legs rather than clamping; the **sum is deliberately unconstrained**, so `{1,1}` and `{0,0}` both construct.

**`src/Core/SoftValueBelief.fs`** — the bridge. Gives `SoftValue` itself a first-class signed residual (the N-candidate generalisation of the same Boole slack), and projects a `SoftValue` onto a `DualScore` through a **caller-declared predicate** — the research note's section 9 scope note made mechanical, in the same boundary-passed-as-a-value shape `SnapPolicy` already ships.

## Sibling, not a mutation — and no caller moved

`SoftValue` is a distribution on the **value** axis; the pair is on the **truth** axis. `docs/research/2026-09-11-the-belief-pair-...-lumen.md` section 9 reaches the same conclusion independently: the belief pair *"composes with SoftValue rather than replacing it."*

So **nothing in `SoftValue.fs` changed**, both new modules are additive (the `SoftValueInfo.fs` pattern), and **all 52 existing F#/C# `SoftValue` call sites are untouched — kept, none migrated**. The sum-to-1 behaviour remains exactly as it was, which is correct for the cases that genuinely are distributions.

## The Eve protocol reference, honoured rather than guessed

Eve is this repo's consent-first fusion-negotiation cycle — `GSet -> (banana-split) -> ZSet (+/-1, retraction-native proposal space) -> (fuse) -> GSet` (`docs/FROZEN-CORE-AND-CONJECTURE-REGISTER.md`; work item `081KRW63S0008QG0R0030F8ZXA`, *"neutral polymorphic diplomatic language"*), whose defining property is **non-coercion**: *"positions translate AS-IS, not laundered through diplomatic euphemism."*

A single normalised belief weight cannot carry that, because **normalisation IS the laundering**: two witnesses at `{1,0}` and `{0,1}` average to `0.5`, which reads identically to *"nobody has looked"* — the one state they are furthest from. The pair keeps them at `{1,1}`, contradiction 1, and hands the disagreement onward intact. That is the raw-vault sentence arriving as an order-theoretic fact: the knowledge order is the raw vault, the truth order is the mart.

`toyJoin` is the **knowledge-order** join and is therefore the non-coercive one. The truth-order join `(max t, min f)` is the **deciding** one and is deliberately **not shipped**, because deciding is the boundary's job and the boundary is a caller-supplied value.

## Rung stated honestly — no algebra is claimed

No `and` / `or` / `combine` / `normalise`, and **no `ISemiring` or `IRing` instance**. Three sufficient reasons, each in the header:

1. **The obvious algebra is refuted** — under the independence-motivated operations distributivity fails (`a = 1/2, b = c = 1`). No additive inverse, so no retraction.
2. **One slot, two orders** — `ISemiring<'W>` has a single `(Add, Mul)` pair; a bilattice has four operations. Instantiating it would silently discard the axis carrying the signal, and the choice would not even be recorded in the type.
3. **Picking a fusion rule today would be guessing** — Dempster's rule is pathological under conflict (Zadeh 1979/1986) by exactly the mechanism of dividing the residual away.

The one combining operation is `toyJoin`; the one judging function is `toyClassify`; **neither ships a default margin or tolerance**. This follows `IntervalRing`'s demotion precedent (`081KWGA0C7`) one rung further down, and deliberately avoids the shape of open item `081M29N1AX9087G0R001GSJD6G`.

## A measurement that changed the API

Building the falsifier turned up a third fact. **In `float` the normalisation does not land on the coherent slice**: the 7/2/1 distribution totals `1.000000000000000222` (`r = -2.22e-16`) and a `combine` posterior totals `0.99999999999999988898` (`r = +1.11e-16`) — one ULP either side, and the **sign in that band is rounding, not evidence**.

So `SoftValueBelief` ships **no exact shape reader at all**; `massShapeWithin` takes its tolerance as a value and the library supplies no default. `DualScore.massShape` keeps the exact form for legs a caller stated directly (TypeScript parity) with the trap named on it. A further measured detail is recorded rather than assumed: partitioning into two legs sums differently from the N-way total and can round back onto the slice — which is luck, not a guarantee, and is exactly why the tolerance cannot be derived from the type.

## Vocabulary parity with the TypeScript oracle

Same names as `src/Core.TypeScript/belief/dual-score.ts` (PR #17308, now on `main`). Four divergences, each stated in the header with its reason:

1. `dualScore(t, f)` -> `DualScore.create t f` — the module already carries the name under `RequireQualifiedAccess`.
2. `residual` **added and signed** — the sign is what carries the Boole-slack / Dutch-book meaning; `ignorance` and `contradiction` remain, defined in terms of it, so nothing diverges numerically.
3. **The four corners are named** — a corner that cannot be named cannot be tested for.
4. `massShapeWithin` **added** — forced by the float measurement above.

`equals` is not ported (F# structural equality is that function); `parseDualScore` is ported as `ofDynamicValue` over the repo's own dynamic carrier.
