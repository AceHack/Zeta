## Description

Phase 0 of the graphics/physics roadmap (#17170). Aaron's direction: **least-computational wins, 8D is dropped unless it earns its place, graphics and physics may stay separate engines.**

The roadmap named one thing as gating Phase 0 — *"the H3 result is measured for the **ring**, not the **light** … Exact shading over an H3 orbit polytope is `toy` and unrun. If Phase 0 proceeds, that is the first thing to falsify — before the byte-lock, not after."* **It was run first.**

Rung 6's construction **transfers**: the **sum of a facet's vertices is exactly its normal**, on every facet of every solid, checked two ways (orthogonal to every facet edge; parallel to the cross-product plane normal, which rank 8 does not have). It is a theorem about orbit polytopes — the facet's stabiliser fixes the vertex sum and has no nonzero fixed vector in the facet plane — not a coincidence.

## What object, and does it self-intersect

**All four H3 orbit polytopes, derived** (12 / 20 / 30 / 120 vertices). Nothing authored: the simple system is *found by its Gram matrix* (the Coxeter diagram `o—5—o——o` is the input, the coordinates are the output), the Wythoff seeds are fundamental weights obtained as cross products, every vertex is an orbit image. **Zero reflections leave `Z[φ]`.**

**Measured anyway, exhaustively: zero improper self-intersections** — every one of the 190 / 630 / 1,540 / 27,730 triangle pairs, no BVH, no sampling. The detector's control (two deliberately crossed triangles) reports 1. Steinitz guarantees it; a theorem never checked against the shipped triangles is a check that did not run.

**Consequence: Phase 1 as written is not needed for this content.** Phase 1 existed because 4_21 cannot be an occlusion-correct 3D scene. An H3 orbit polytope already is one.

## The level count saturates

Rung 7's non-root integral light buys 4_21 **49** levels. Here `L = (2,3,6)`, `|L|² = 49` a perfect square so `|L| = 7` is rational and the light adds no irrational at all:

| solid | facets | root light (signed/two-sided/lit) | **integral light** |
|---|---|---|---|
| icosahedron | 20 | 7 / 4 / 3 | **20 — every facet** |
| dodecahedron | 12 | 5 / 3 / 2 | **12 — every facet** |
| icosidodecahedron | 32 | 11 / 6 / 5 | **32 — every facet** |
| trunc. icosidodeca. | 62 | 19 / 10 / 9 | **62 — every facet** |

**The answer is not a number like 49 — it is saturation.** One distinct exact level per facet, zero facets on the terminator, on every solid. That is the ceiling: **the posterisation disappears at rank 3**, and the level count stops being a property of the algebra and becomes a property of how much geometry you built.

`|L|² = 49` matching rung 7's 49 levels is a **coincidence and is recorded as one** (`numerology-vs-number-theory`). The light gauge is *discharged*, not asserted: all 30 root lights give an identical intensity multiset.

## The byte-lock — held first try

126 lines, 7,064 bytes, and **no floating point anywhere**: every locked quantity is an integer or a `Z[φ]` pair, so no `Number`/`System.Double` formatting treaty exists to diverge on. Four deliberately different algorithms across the oracles — exhaustive supporting-plane facets (F#) vs weight-orbit argmax (TS); `p`-first vs `q`-first `zSqrt`; angular comparator vs gift-wrap; worklist stack vs BFS rounds. Independence enumerated rather than asserted.

## Mutation testing — 20 mutants, 2 survivors, both reported

**M1 (`zSign`: `>` → `>=`) SURVIVED and found a real defect:** the equality branch was **unreachable code wearing the shape of a case**, since `p² = 5q²` forces `p = q = 0`. Deleted in both oracles, with a test checking no such pair exists in a 401×401 window.
