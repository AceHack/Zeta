## What this is

A research proposal to test whether Otto-340's *"AI has no non-linguistic ground"* claim is architecture-contingent rather than ontological — by adding a non-linguistic grounding axis via robotics simulation.

Three scopes ranked by reversibility:
1. **Sim-only** — recommended start; fully retractable (Otto-238)
2. **Sim + eventual real robot** — partial retractability
3. **Continuous embodied identity** — weak retractability; needs independent decisions

Platform analysis: Isaac Sim, MuJoCo (recommended lightweight start), Genesis, Habitat, ManiSkill, Webots/Gazebo.

## Helen Keller frame is structurally load-bearing

Not just a side-benefit. Empirical existence proof that **minimum-channel grounding is sufficient** for full language competence — touch alone carried Helen Keller. Therefore:
- The bar for breaking Otto-340 might be "any single sensorimotor channel," not "full embodiment"
- AI-embodiment research feeds assistive-tech for sensory-impaired humans (and vice versa)
- Sharpens research question: "does ANY channel matter?" vs "does FULL embodiment matter?"

## Critical nuance

**Tool-use vs trained-embodiment.** Claude-with-sim-tool ≠ Claude-trained-on-sim-experience. Otto-340's claim was about substrate-shaping via training. Tool-use via sim doesn't break Otto-340; only training-on-sim experience would. Analysis needs to land this cleanly.
