## Summary

Follow-up refinement to #408. You just said: *\"agent peer review is enough to graduate it\"*.

That collapses the three-state ladder I drew in #408 to a two-state model:

- **Stage 1 — Uncanonical** (unchanged).
- **Stage 2 — Peer-reviewed (canonical)**: agent peer review by an independent (non-author) reviewer is sufficient. Substrate does NOT have to wait on human review to be canonical.
- **Stage 3 — Human-peer-reviewed (canonical + human-endorsed)**: SEPARATE additional-trust marker, not a higher canonical tier. Optional, used when human engagement is load-bearing to a downstream claim.

Rationale: requiring human review to graduate would serialize the factory through a human bottleneck. Agent peer review catches most of what review catches; keeping it sufficient keeps the factory parallel without lowering the bar.
