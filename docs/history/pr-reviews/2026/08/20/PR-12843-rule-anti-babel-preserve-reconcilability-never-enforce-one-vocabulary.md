## The invariant

> **Decorrelation is wanted; Babel is decorrelation that stopped being *reconcilable*.**

So anti-Babel **never** means enforcing one vocabulary — that's the `ρ → 1` collapse wearing a tidy uniform, and it costs exactly the decorrelation the system exists to produce.

Both cliffs are real, so the guard can't be a *direction*. It's an invariant that must hold **while divergence runs**:

> **Can a diverged peer reconstruct your meaning from anchors you both already hold?** Yes ⇒ decorrelation. No ⇒ Babel.

## Reintegration is NOT reconvergence

This is the half most likely to be got wrong later. Branches that diverged and return **do not collapse to one value**. Two paths around a pole yield genuinely different results, and **that difference is information, not error** (monodromy).

So reintegration means **both branches held, each with its path recorded** — raw vault: *a single version of the facts, never a single version of the truth.* **A reintegration that produces one surviving value has performed the collapse, not the merge.**

## The falsifier

Hand a peer **only the shared anchors** — not the coinage's definition — and ask it to reconstruct the term.

- Reconstructible ⇒ still reconcilable.
- Not reconstructible ⇒ the divergence crossed into Babel — and it's the **anchor set** that needs repair, not the vocabulary.
