## Description

## The gap

`ErasureClass` (PR #12259) landed the **diagnosis** — a thermodynamic class declared beside each
operation, measured by exhaustive sweep, enforced in both directions, with `Unmeasured` returning
`None` rather than `0L`. **Nothing charged it.** `algebra/entropy-tracker.ts` charges five sites and
none is a compaction, a snapshot-supersedes-log, an eviction, or a witness mix. The research doc
§12g deferred the charge deliberately, for a good reason: two of §12's rows moved in the *opposite*
direction from the intuition that motivated the thread, and a meter wired to an intuition would have
been charging for months.

This is that deferral taken up, narrowly.

## The rule: the classification IS the charge, and there is no second list

`src/Core/ErasureCharge.fs` and `src/Core.TypeScript/algebra/erasure-charge.ts` fold a declared
profile into a disposition, reading **`Classification` and `Evidence` only**. Never
`Representation`, `Operation`, or `Observation`; no table keyed by name.

That restriction is the whole lesson of §11b/§11i made mechanical. A name-keyed list of erasing
operations was written twice in that document and was wrong twice, and cannot be completed in
principle, because `TruncateAsync` is `Erasing` under `InMemoryDeltaLog` and `Reversible` under
`GitDeltaLog`. Both law packs pin it: rename all three string fields of a real profile to garbage
and the disposition must be identical.

| disposition | reached from | ledger effect |
|---|---|---|
| `Free` | `Reversible` backed by a **measured** fibre of 1 | nothing — Bennett |
| `Charged ppm` | `Erasing` backed by a sweep, `ppm > 0` | the declared, measured bits |
| `Unmeasured reason` | `NoAdmissibleMeasurement` + a written reason | a **hole**, never a zero |
| `Malformed complaint` | a declaration at war with its own evidence | a hole — **fails closed** |

`Malformed` did not previously exist. A profile claiming `Reversible` over a fibre of 4 is not free
and is not measured; it is broken, and charging it `0` is the closed-ledger free lunch arriving
through a data-entry error rather than through a missing meter.

## `Unmeasured` charges nothing and is not free — the type enforces it

Three options, and the middle one is the trap:

| option | verdict |
|---|---|
| charge `0` | the demon exactly — free because the ledger is closed |
| charge a stated upper bound | **there is none without inventing a coefficient**, which is the toy-presented-as-metered failure this thread exists to prevent |
| refuse the fold, carry the hole **in the total's type** | taken |

A `Reading` is `Complete bits` or `LowerBound (bits, holes)`, and **there is no function in either
implementation that returns the bit total as a bare number** — `readingParts` returns the pair,
`readHeat` returns the flag beside the figure. A caller cannot obtain a total without also learning
whether it is the whole cost. That is `ErasureClass.bitsErasedPpm`'s `int64 option` moved to the
place where a total is actually formed.

A lower bound is also the physically correct direction: Landauer's `kT ln 2` is a **floor**.

**Measured-zero and unknown are now different values.** `GroupCommitDiskDeltaLog`'s no-op truncation
reads `Complete 0`; an undeclared backend reads `LowerBound (0, [hole])`. Before this they were both
"nothing happened."

**Bits are never summed across observations.** An `Account` is keyed by observation and offers no
operation that collapses it — §12b's lesson, structurally. A sum across observations describes no
observer.

## The wired site

`RecoverableSpine.CommitAsync` — the one genuine snapshot-supersedes-log site, named in §11e as
charging nothing. Same code path, three bills, decided by the injected backend:

- `InMemoryDeltaLog` → `Complete 3_700_440` ppm per truncation (fibre 13)
- `GroupCommitDiskDeltaLog` → `Complete 0` — a **measured** zero
- a backend implementing no `IErasureDeclaring` → `LowerBound (0, [hole])` naming it. This is the
  drift guard at runtime, where reflection over our own assembly cannot reach a caller-supplied type.

The fold is charged separately and on **every commit**, not once per snapshot cadence — §11a's
headline as an accumulating figure rather than a sentence.

## Witness-correlation formation: a hook and a declared hole, not a number

`WitnessCorrelationErasureDeclaration` declares `QuorumAlgebra.join` **twice**, because there are two
questions:

1. **Marginal — MEASURED.** `join` is idempotent and commutative, so arrival order and contribution
   multiplicity are gone. Swept over a bounded 2-source/2-value model: fibre 3, `1_584_963` ppm.
   *Idempotence is erasure* — the same finding `IBackingStore.Save` produced from an unrelated
   direction in §12d.
2. **Conditional `H(A|B)` — UNMEASURED, with the reason written down.** This is the quantity del Rio
   et al. (Nature 474:61–63, 2011) actually runs on, and the repo states its own gap at
   `algebra/erasure-derivation.ts:49`: the tracker carries no caller-retained side information.
   There is no upper bound to charge that would not be invented, so the fold refuses it.

Declaring the conditional `Unmeasured` is **not** a dodge, and the structure is what shows it:
**row 1 is swept.** `Unmeasured` is used for the one observation this substrate has no instrument
for, never to avoid measuring something measurable.

`entropy-tracker.ts` gains the missing third door for the same reason: it had `measure(k)` and
`permutation()` and **no way at all to say "unknown"**, so a caller facing an unswept operation had
to choose between an invented number and a silent zero.

## Cross-check (BP-16)

The F# module and the TypeScript module implement the same four-case rule over the same declaration
shape, and assert the same dispositions on the same fixture profiles. A divergence in either is
caught by the other. Single-tool evidence would not have been enough here.

## What is NOT done — the edges, stated

- **`SybilBft.decide`, `Consensus`, `TravelerRankLedger`** remain unclassified and uncharged. Their
  marginals are sweepable, and this work does **not** paper over that by declaring them
  `Unmeasured` — that would be the vacuity class wearing the honest register's clothes.
- **`GiftOfErasure.mix` declares but nothing posts it at runtime.** `AnonymitySet` is an immutable
  value; threading a ledger through it changes a heavily-tested shape, so it is a design question.
- **§11f's demon is untouched** — `Heat.fs:285` `dispositionOfKind` still reads
  `denied-list.compacted` as free. Different ledger (shed disposition, not bits).
- Rust / C# / Q# oracles unclassified (§11h, §12h — unchanged).
