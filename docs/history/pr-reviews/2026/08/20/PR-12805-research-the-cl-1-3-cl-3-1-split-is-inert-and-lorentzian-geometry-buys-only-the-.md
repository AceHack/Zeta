## Task 1 — Cl(1,3) vs Cl(3,1): NO CROSSING. No bug filed.

Audited every occurrence. Search terms are listed in §1.1 of the doc, because "not found" is a
claim about search terms.

`Cl(1,3) ≅ M₂(ℍ)` and `Cl(3,1) ≅ M₄(ℝ)` are genuinely different — `CliffordPeriodicity.classify`
separates them and `CliffordPeriodicity.Tests.fs:59` already pins it. **Nothing crosses**, and the
reason is structural rather than lucky:

- **`o(p,q) = o(q,p)` literally.** Replacing `η` by `−η` leaves `Aᵀη + ηA = 0` unchanged, and
  `−η` has signature `(q,p)`. Every "6 Lorentz generators" claim in the tree is convention-free
  by construction.
- **`Cl⁰(p,q) ≅ Cl⁰(q,p)` for all `p,q`.** The two clock positions sum to `2 mod 8`, and every
  such residue pair sits in the same row of the Lawson–Michelsohn table. Computed against the
  repo's own module: **0 counterexamples over 196 signature pairs**, while the *full* algebras
  differ in **168 of 210** ordered pairs.
- **The workhorse signature is the common even part.** `Cl(3,0)` (×171) *is* `Cl⁰(3,1)`. The repo
  has been living in the convention-neutral subalgebra all along.

**Disposition: document the split, do not unify.** The P3 dual-signature row
(`081KSNY2Z0008QG0R002FX66H0`) stays the right call; §3 gives a one-line reason per scope. A
crossing could only ever bite in the **odd** part — real spinor modules, chirality,
Majorana/reality conditions — and nothing in the tree touches those. The guard is filed as a
**task**, not a bug: `081M0FTPJ4X087G0R000E91P3Y`, routed to FsCheck (P1, one tool; BP-16's
two-tool floor does not fire and saying so is part of the job).

## Task 2 — clean negative, by theorem rather than by shrug

The Lorentzian-vs-Euclidean framing is mis-posed: Euclidean has no causal structure and was never
a competitor. The competitor is **no geometry**. Against that bar the answer is exact:

- **Malament 1977** (after Hawking–King–McCarthy 1976): for distinguishing spacetimes a causal
  bijection is a conformal isometry. The causal order determines the metric **up to a conformal
  factor**, and determines nothing more.
- **Sorkin**: *Order + Number = Geometry.* In a discrete causal set the conformal factor is
  supplied by **counting** — longest chain for proper time, element count for volume.

The metric buys the factor; counting supplies the factor; **the metric buys nothing.** And
importing the geometry imports the causal-set **Hauptvermutung, which is open** — an unproven
premise the poset-plus-counting statement does not need.

### Two computed falsifiers, gated in CI

1. **Our causal order does not embed in `1+1` Minkowski.** `M^{1,1}` causal order is *exactly* the
   order-dimension-≤2 posets (light-cone coordinates `u=t+x`, `v=t−x` **are** a two-element
   realizer — proved in two lines in §7.3). A reachable six-process vector-clock configuration
   realizes the standard example `S₃`, order dimension 3. Exhaustive over all **48** linear
   extensions and every pair. `S₃` *does* embed in `2+1`, so the negative is dimension-specific —
   both halves are kept so it is not over-read.
2. **`origin/main` is a chain.** Concurrency width **1.000** — longest chain 5999 over 6000
   elements, independently confirmed by `git log --format='%P' -n 3000` showing every one of the
   last 3000 commits with exactly one parent. Adding 400 `heartbeat/*` refs gives 1.090.
   **The geometry has nothing to act on**: a light cone's whole job is to separate timelike from
   spacelike, and our persisted record is essentially all timelike. The squash-merge is where the
   spacelike structure died — `git-DAG = causal set` is true on `main` but *trivially* so.

## Also: the numerology rule applied to our own docstring

`HexCore.fs`'s *"6 walls = 6 Lorentz bivectors"* is a **count** — `dim Λ²(ℝ⁴) = 6` for *every*
4-D signature, so a `6` selects *four-dimensional*, not *Lorentzian*. The discriminating invariant
is named: `so(4)` and `so(2,2)` split into two 3-dim ideals, `so(1,3)` does not. Exhibit a bracket
on the six walls and check whether it splits — that is the promotion test. `HexCore` already
hedges; this supplies the specific missing invariant that would discharge the hedge.
