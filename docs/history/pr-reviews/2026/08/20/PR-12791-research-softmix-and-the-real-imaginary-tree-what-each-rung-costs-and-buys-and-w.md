## The one line

> **`SoftMix<TWeight>` has not decided what a weight is. The ring you hand it is a position on the Cayley–Dickson tree, and every rung you climb costs an algebraic law and buys a physical capability. The interpreter never changes. The physics does.**

## Why each rung is what it is

**`ℝ` is "no interference"** because probability weights are non-negative — two paths to the same state can only add. **Cancellation needs a phase, and a phase needs an imaginary direction.** `AmplitudeEmu.fs` says it from the other side: drop the merge + real weights ⇒ no interference; restore merge + complex ⇒ the two-slit falls out.

**`𝕆` is where our code comes from**, derived end-to-end in the register: the octonion multiplication table is *proven* (convention-independent) to form a **Fano plane** — Steiner `S(2,3,7)` — whose triples span `[7,4]` Hamming, whose parity extension is doubly-even, which **is** the `[8,4]` adinkra generator. **Our ECC is a shadow of the octonion multiplication table.**

**`𝕊` is the cliff.** Hurwitz (1898) forces zero divisors to first appear at the sedenions — and per the idempotent knot, multiplication by a zero divisor is what collapse, erasure and overwrite *all* are. So the tree has a moral reading:

> **Below `𝕊`, destruction is not merely forbidden — it is inexpressible.** Our adinkra generator sits at `𝕆`, the last rung before that.

## Where the ImaginaryStack sits — and the trap next to it

`ToyModel.lean` proves bulk-from-boundary: **16 coordinates split `12 ⊕ 4`**, the code subspace is the **graph of a linear generator `G : boundary → bulk`**, and `reconstruct G = id.prod G` recovers every codeword from its 12 boundary coordinates. `ErasureDistance.lean` strengthens to arbitrary erasure; `PhaseClockErasure.lean` applies it to missed heartbeats.

The tempting story: 16 coordinates, and 16 is the sedenion dimension — *the level at which you can destroy is the level at which you need the code.*

**Filed as coincidence-as-generator, not identification.** The ToyModel's 16 is a coordinate count over a field with a `12+4` split; the sedenions are a 16-dimensional real algebra. Same number, different objects. And **the ToyModel names the gap itself**: *"which specific `G` the imaginary-stack multiplication table induces … is NOT proven here"* — which is exactly the link the tidy story would need.

## The pairing worth keeping

**`SoftMix` is the dial; `ImaginaryStack` is one setting studied in depth.** Turning the dial changes what physics the same fold performs; the stack asks what survives erasure once you stop turning it.

And the reason to hold them together:

> **The capability you gain at a rung is the capability you then need protection from.** You climb to `𝕆` for the Fano plane and get the code; you climb one more for expressible destruction and immediately need the code you just got. **The error-correction and the thing it corrects arrive one rung apart.**
