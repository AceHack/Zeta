## 1. "Give up being a division algebra" conflated two claims

> *"we are a division algebra, our division is unorthodox, look up harmonic division"*

**Hurwitz stands** — `ℝ,ℂ,ℍ,𝕆` are the only normed division algebras; `𝕊` has zero divisors. What was wrong is treating *"has zero divisors"* and *"cannot divide"* as one statement:

| | |
|---|---|
| total division **inside** the algebra | dies at `𝕊`. Theorem |
| division as a **partial operation completed by continuation** | does **not** die |

**And the repo specified the second on 2026-05-13** — Geometric Inversion Check in `ConstraintSolver.fs`, Riemann sheets, branch cuts, **L'Hôpital pole erasure "for zero-divisors"**, and holomorphic `Φ(τ)` from discrete composition to geometric product. Exactly Aaron's *"anti-algebra under division … analyticish continuation."* **The pole is not a wall; it is a point you go around.**

**Fourth false absence today, same cause** — I searched the conclusion (`division algebra`) not the work (`inversion|riemann|projective`). The latter found it first try.

**Anchors:** von Staudt 1847 (*algebra of throws* — builds `+`,`×` from the harmonic conjugate alone, so **harmonic division is more primitive than division**; the free-object rule at the foundation of arithmetic) · cross-ratio `−1` · `ℝP¹` where `x↦1/x` is a **total bijection** `0↔∞` (the "anti-algebra" is the other chart) · inversive geometry · wheel theory (Carlström 2004).

**Honest limit:** totality ≠ normed division algebra. Corrected sentence is **"`𝕊`'s division dies; Zeta's division is not `𝕊`'s division."**

**And PGA already bought it deliberately** — `Cl(3,0,1)`'s `e₀²=0` is a zero divisor by construction, and it is what makes points at infinity representable. Listed as an owed decision in that same memory.
