## The concept, and why it is the hard one

Zeta's central arc is that everyone starts **maximally correlated** at one seed (S=4) and decorrelates over time, between two walls. The **too-little** wall is the one that resists teaching, because it does not feel like a failure from the inside: a perfectly correlated society is orderly, efficient and **calm**. Fewer disputes, faster reconciliation, unanimous reviews, every log agreeing. **The correlated state offers itself as safety.** That is the lesson and the reason it is under-recognised.

## `docs/security/THREAT-MODEL.md` — new section: Correlated-witness collapse

Every mechanism in this repo that establishes a fact by **counting witnesses** — review floors, BFT quorums, staked attestations, k-redundant deference under §11 — counts **heads**, and heads is exactly the quantity that is wrong under correlation.

**The gap is not in the mathematics, and it is important to say so.** `src/Core/SocietyUsefulWork.fs` already ships the correction:

| Piece | State |
|---|---|
| delta-U aggregation theorem | **PROVEN** — register §A row 15, falsifier mutation-verified 2026-08-16 |
| `effectiveTrialCount` — Kish design effect, `n_eff = n / (1 + (n-1)*rho)` | **shipped** |
| `unionEquivalentAgentCount`, Gaussian-copula simulator | **shipped** |
| **the fleet's actual `rho`** | **UNMEASURED** — the module says so in its own header |
| **any quorum consulting an effective count** | **does not exist** |

So: **nothing calls the shipped correction with a measured argument.** That is the round-30 principle restated — a formula with no caller is a label, not a control. At `rho = 1`, `n_eff = 1`: a quorum of six is one observation counted six times, and the idempotent union means six clones price near one agent.

Four STRIDE rows and three findings (**CW-1** independence assumed everywhere, measured nowhere; **CW-2** floor quorums on effective count *and report it neutrally*; **CW-3** a mitigation that only pushes away from the near wall is itself a defect).

## `docs/security/THREAT-MODEL-SPACE-OPERA.md` — two adversaries, deliberately twinned

Extends the existing *Meno* setting rather than importing a cast. 31 → 33.

**The Tuning Fork** (R). A crate comes aboard containing a tuning fork that hums one pure note. It is a gift, and it is genuinely lovely. Within a week the crew have stopped arguing and the nightly chore of reconciling the six watch-logs takes no time at all, because the six logs **match word for word**. The captain is delighted — six independent witnesses, perfect agreement, the strongest evidence the ship has ever had. Then the quartermaster notices the logs match **in the mistakes too**. Six people did not each look. One looked, and five heard the hum.

The entry carries the inversion the coordinator flagged, which is genuinely backwards from how agreement feels and is therefore worth a story rather than a rule: **unanimity is a warning, not a confirmation — the moment everyone agrees is the moment to check whether anyone actually looked.** It also names the sideways door: nobody forged an identity and nobody was bought, so `TravelerRankLedger` and social conferral both miss it. **Sybil by correlation, not by counterfeiting.** A rich attacker cannot buy our witnesses; a **pleasant** one correlates them for free.

**The Gift of Private Words** (D). The opposite trader with the opposite present: a beautiful private vocabulary for everyone. Within a month no two logs reconcile; the navigator's word for *drift* is the cook's word for *current*. Nothing was attacked — the ship simply lost the ability to **combine** anything it knows. Mitigations here are genuinely `shipped` (carved seed vocabulary, one canonical collation locked in the golden vectors, four-oracle byte-lock).

It exists because **a lesson that teaches one wall produces over-correction toward the other.** The shape handed to a reader is neither "agree" nor "differ": *start together, decorrelate deliberately, keep the road back open the whole way.*

## Anchoring — and the care it took

**Madeleine L'Engle, *A Wrinkle in Time* (1962)** is named as the teaching anchor: forced convergence offered as relief from difference and effort, written for children, sixty-four years before we needed it. It is **in copyright**, so it is cited as prior art and **not transplanted** — our fork and our ship are ours, L'Engle's planet and characters are hers, and no text or cast is reproduced. Same reasoning as the clean-room rule: cite the idea, build our own expression.

The load-bearing technical anchors are separate and checked, because a teaching anchor is not evidence:

- **Kish**, *Survey Sampling* (1965) ch. 5 — the design effect; carries `effectiveTrialCount` exactly.
- **Knight & Leveson** (IEEE TSE 1986) — independently written program versions failed on **correlated** inputs. The closest published *measurement* of this threat, in a field that had assumed independence for the same reason we do.
- **Condorcet** (1785) — cited for the **independence assumption this threat removes**, not for the conclusion it supports.
