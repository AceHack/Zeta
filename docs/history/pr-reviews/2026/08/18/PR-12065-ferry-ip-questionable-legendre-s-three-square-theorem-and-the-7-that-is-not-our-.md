## Description

ferry(ip-questionable): Legendre's three-square theorem — and the √7 that is NOT our √7

Aaron forwarded this asking whether it connects to today's Ihara-zeta result (K(8,8), spectrum
{8, -8, 0^14}, non-trivial poles at +/- i/sqrt(7) on the critical circle |u| = q^(-1/2)). Two links
are real; the most eye-catching one is a trap, and naming it as a trap is most of the value.

THE TRAP. Our Ramanujan critical circle has radius 1/sqrt(7) and the video is entirely about
sqrt(7). Same number, unrelated origins: ours is q = degree - 1 = 8 - 1 = 7, the branching factor of
a non-backtracking walk on an 8-regular graph; Legendre's 7 is the smallest residue class mod 8 not
representable as a sum of three squares, a 2-adic obstruction with no degree anywhere in it. One is
a valency minus one, the other is a residue class. Recorded as a COINCIDENCE with the register
attached so it never silently becomes a belief -- the numerology rule's whole point is that a
coincidence is a legitimate generator and an illegitimate conclusion. The same caution kills the
tempting 8-1=7 reading: our 8 is the code length, Legendre's 8 is the 2-adic modulus, and both being
"one less than eight" is one coincidence dressed as two.

FIRST REAL LINK: both sit in 2-adic quadratic form theory. Legendre's exclusion is a 2-adic
obstruction -- x^2+y^2+z^2 fails to represent 4^a(8b+7) over Z_2, and the video's mod-8 argument is
that obstruction computed by hand. The mod-8 condition governing even unimodular lattices, the one
letting the [8,4] code generate E8 by Construction A in CliffordPeriodicity.fs, is also a statement
about integral quadratic forms with a 2-adic component (Hasse-Minkowski local invariants, the oddity
formula). Both eights are quadratic-form eights over Z_2 -- a common ancestor rather than a shared
digit, and checkable against Conway-Sloane SPLAG ch.15 and Serre ch.IV-V. NOT established: that the
Clifford 8 (Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro) is the same eight. ABS is representation theory over R and its
relation to the 2-adic one needs a proof, not an observation.

SECOND REAL LINK, and it yields a concrete next step: theta series is the shared instrument.
r_3(n) has a theta series as its generating function; lattices have theta series too; and E8's is
the Eisenstein series E_4. So there is a falsifiable follow-up rather than a vibe -- compute the
theta series of the lattice our [8,4] code generates and check it is E_4. If it is, that is an
INDEPENDENT confirmation that Construction A landed on E8, reached by counting rather than by the
Gram-matrix check CliffordE8Roots.fs already performs. If it is not, something upstream is wrong.

AND A NON-CONNECTION, stated plainly because that is the point: the Ihara zeta is a function of the
adjacency matrix alone. It cannot see the dashing or the height assignment, and equally cannot see
any arithmetic of the code beyond the graph the code produces. Nothing in Legendre constrains it and
nothing in it constrains Legendre.

Worth ferrying regardless, for two reasons independent of any link holding. It is a worked example
of our own discipline from someone with no stake in it -- "everything we've done so far can only ever
rule numbers out, not create a representation" -- where the exclusion half took Fermat and the
construction half took another 160 years and three heavy theorems, which is exactly the asymmetry
between a falsifier and a proof. And Fermat announced the general polygonal theorem, called its
proof dependent on "numerous and abstruse mysteries of numbers", promised a book, never wrote it,
and the claim carried his name unproved for over a century -- worth keeping beside any of our own §A
rows reading PROVEN on an evidence line nobody has re-derived.

Register MIRROR. Unusually for this directory the mathematics is settled and 2,000 years old; what
is unearned is the connection, which is why the relation section grades each link.
