## Description

docs(clifford): scope a Lorentz for one oracle, and correct the "one proof, many domains" claim

Two documents, one of which is a refutation of something already being built on.

HOMOICONICITY HOLDS ONLY FOR THE UNCODED CUBE. Alexa's review proposes that any Z2-graded system can
be faithfully represented as an adinkra, and draws the consequence that "a proof about the adinkra
is a proof about all of them -- you don't need separate verification for the continuation semantics,
the Eve protocol, and the Clifford algebra." The refutation was already in the tree four days
earlier, in Lumen's 2026-08-14 obstruction doc, and it is decisive.

Homoiconicity is a real theorem for the UNCODED N-cube: 2^N vertices, edges coloured 1..N, Q_I
sending S -> S xor {I}, which IS left multiplication by gamma_I on the blade basis of Cl(0,N). The
module is free of rank 1; the operators are among the data. Aaron's instinct is right and right for
a sharper reason than the usual "Gates drew the equations as pictures" line.

But quotient by a doubly-even code and it FAILS. By the Doran-Faux-Gates-Hubsch-Iga-Landweber
classification, adinkraic chromotopologies are (Z/2)^N / C with 2^(N-k) vertices, while Cl(0,N)
does not shrink -- so for any k > 0 the module is no longer free of rank 1. Minimal and homoiconic
are in direct tension, and the boundary is exact: minimal AND homoiconic iff N <= 3. Our [8,4]
adinkra has N=8, k=4 -- 16 vertices against a 256-dimensional algebra. The very feature that makes
it interesting, the doubly-even self-dual code that generates E8 by Construction A, is the feature
that destroys homoiconicity. We chose minimal.

The second error is independent and wider: adinkras are not general Z2-graded objects. They carry an
N-edge-colouring satisfying the garden-algebra Clifford relations; a general Z2-graded system has a
grading and nothing else. DFGHIL is a classification, i.e. a restriction, which is the opposite of a
universality claim. Alexa states the correct version herself -- "Z2-graded systems are everywhere,
and the adinkra is the right language for all of them" -- and then escalates past it. The hedge was
right; the escalation is not.

TWO READINGS OF MINIMAL, AND AARON MEANT THE OTHER ONE. On reading the above he clarified that his
"smallest" was not the literature's (k maximal, fewest vertices) but Rodney's razor -- least
complexity for the same essential job -- and that his own wording was fuzzy. The tension result does
not touch that question, so the doc now answers it separately rather than leaving the correction
over-broad.

Deflationary first step: homoiconicity BY ITSELF is free. Every unital algebra acts on itself, so
(A, A, id) is a homoiconic pair for any A, and the smallest homoiconic structure is trivially R. All
the content sits in what travels with it. Once the bundle is named -- homoiconic AND Z2-graded AND
self-similar under a doubling that preserves both -- the answer is affirmative and cheap: the
UNCODED tower starting at N=1. Cl(0,1) = R+R is the first rung with non-trivial grading, and the
doubling preserves homoiconicity at every rung forever because no quotient is ever taken. Nothing
about N=8 is forced by homoiconicity.

That reframes the [8,4] choice as a trade rather than a defect: the uncoded tower keeps
homoiconicity and gets no E8; the coded adinkra gets E8 and the ECC reading and loses homoiconicity.
Both are minimal under different orderings and neither dominates. What stays genuinely open is the
one thing Aaron flagged himself -- the razor cannot rank them until "the same essential job" is
named, which is the essential-vs-accidental cut applied to the requirement rather than the artefact.

This is worth correcting loudly because the proposed consequence LICENSES SKIPPING VERIFICATION.
That is the vacuity class at architectural scale, and worse than an ordinary wrong claim because its
effect is to stop checking. The Eve mapping is explicitly incomplete on exactly the case that
matters -- declining -- and a "proof about the adinkra" would say nothing about it.

A LORENTZ FOR ONE ORACLE: WHAT IS MISSING IS THE METRIC. We do not have a Lorentz transform, and
FrameDelta.fs says so itself, naming the missing piece: "the full non-abelian Lorentz group would
require a boost-velocity / metric the discrete causal model does not carry." What we DO have is the
causal cone -- and that is a checked anchor rather than a borrowed metaphor, since Lamport 1978
draws the special-relativity analogy himself (happens-before = timelike, concurrent = spacelike).
A partial order gives the cone but no interval: it says WHETHER two events are causally separated,
never HOW FAR. That gap, not the group, is the whole problem. O(1,1) is two-dimensional and fully
classified; borrowing it costs nothing. Earning an invariant is the work.

AND ONE INVERSION WORTH STATING PLAINLY. "Commutative messaging should allow for exotic curvatures"
runs backwards: path-independence is ZERO holonomy, and curvature is precisely the failure of
transport to commute. A perfectly commutative fold is the trivial connection, and out-of-order
tolerance RESTORES commutativity, so it removes curvature rather than producing it. The instinct is
one step from something real, though, and the object is already in the tree: what carries curvature
is the DEFECT -- the associator/commutator measuring what reordering costs when it is not free --
which is the right type (two directions in, failure-to-commute out) and which Lumen's doc already
identifies as a nontrivial 3-cocycle. So: do not look for curvature in the commuting part, look for
it in the residue. Two oracles that price a reordering differently see different defects, and the
transformation between their views is where a boost would live, if one exists.

A candidate invariant is offered as an explicit toy -- s^2 = (dtau)^2 - (du)^2 over tick separation
and accumulated uncertainty, with O(1,1) boosts -- and named toyOracleBoost with the two things that
would have to be shown and can each fail: that anything actually preserves it, and that the
transformations close into a group. The Aurora immune math can supply structure (self/non-self is a
second Z2) but explicitly not magnitudes: its four non-claims travel unchanged, and thresholds are
un-calibrated, so anything resting on them inherits that.
