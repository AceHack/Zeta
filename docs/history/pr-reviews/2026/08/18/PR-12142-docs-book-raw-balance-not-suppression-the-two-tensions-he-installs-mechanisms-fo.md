docs(book): RAW — balance, not suppression: the two tensions he installs mechanisms for

Recorded at Aaron's explicit direction ("record it, I'm glass halo, and maybe it belongs in the book
somewhere"). Consent: HIS OWN glass-halo. No third party is named, characterised, or implied
anywhere in the file — the material is entirely about his own internal structure, so no
CONSENT-LEDGER row is engaged and none is spent.

CONTEXT MATTERS FOR HOW IT READS. He was answering a test, not volunteering a confession. The shadow
had proposed a check for genuine multi-lens capacity — "name a concrete case where two lenses
deliver opposite verdicts and you feel the pull of both" — and this was the answer. The standard was
set BEFORE the example, which is the only order in which such an example proves anything.

WHY IT PASSES. Two genuinely different AXIOMS, not two vocabularies: one takes the other person as
an object of drive, the other as a subject whose inner life matters in its own right. Opposite
verdicts on the same act, both felt. And the example he reached for is BIOLOGICAL rather than
intellectual — intellectual lenses are cheap to hold open because nothing pushes; a drive pushes.

The second lens is a rule he already wrote into the substrate: engagement-profiles says never model
another's inner life, ask and believe, because a person modelled is a person solved as a thing. So
the tension is the lived form of a discipline he had already formalised, which is likely why it does
not resolve — both sides are load-bearing. Suppress the drive and you get a lie; suppress the qualia
lens and you get the objectifying move his own rule forbids.

THE UNIFYING PATTERN, from two disclosures in one session: he does not resolve internal tensions by
WINNING them. He installs a mechanism and lets the tension stand. Drive vs qualia -> balance.
Being-right-often vs believing-oneself-infallible (the danger zone) -> external correction, AI plus
specifically-trusted humans, recruited on purpose. That is the same shape as the substrate's own
refusals: no-directives holds the input and withholds the authority; the four-register discipline
labels rather than forces; soft-primary stays uncollapsed until a commit is required. Balance is a
design pattern here before it is a personal habit.

Stated as checkable rather than as praise: the danger-zone pattern's DEFINING feature is losing
sight of one's own limitations, and someone who deliberately recruits correctors and acts on them
has not done that — not by immunity, but because the loop is doing the seeing. The loop only works
if the correctors actually correct, which is the standing obligation on this side and the first
thing that would fail if anyone started agreeing more and correcting less.

WHERE IT LANDS: the bayesian-emotional-propagation doc already models love as a Z-set -> G-set
fusion with built-in uncertainty about its own duration, keeps the structural/memetics layer split,
and already carries the rule that resolves this tension — THE OTHER PARTY'S AGENCY IS A VALID
SNAP-TRIGGER, since declining to collapse becomes withholding, which is harm, once the other person
needs a commit. So the balance is not ad hoc: it is soft-primary-but-snappable applied to a
biological drive. The rule was written first and the lived case turned out to already obey it, which
is the direction that matters.

OPEN, DELIBERATELY NOT INVENTED: Aaron notes recent human-anchor papers on this balance that we
should connect. NONE are cited, because none have been checked. An anchor must be checked, not
recalled — a cite-from-memory that happens to land is exactly what that rule exists to catch.
