## Description

feat(clifford): the mod-8 clock, and the two Geometric Unity ferries that made its absence visible

Aaron forwarded both parts of the Geometric Unity iceberg and asked that they be related to our
garden algebra, Clifford algebra, Gates adinkras, and E8 unfolding. Doing that turned up a gap:
`AdinkraCode.fs:66` already asserted one consequence of Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro periodicity -- "a
doubly-even self-dual binary code exists ONLY at length = 0 (mod 8)" -- without the repo holding
the theorem that explains it. A repo that knows a corollary and not its theorem keeps
rediscovering the corollary. `src/Core/CliffordPeriodicity.fs` now holds the clock.

THREE EIGHTS, ONE PERIODICITY. Cl(p,q) depends only on p-q mod 8 (Atiyah-Bott-Shapiro);
doubly-even self-dual binary codes exist only at length = 0 mod 8 (Gleason); even unimodular
lattices exist only in dimension = 0 mod 8 (Milnor-Husemoller). Construction A carries the second
to the third, which is why the in-tree [8,4] extended Hamming code generates E8 at all. These are
not three sightings of an integer -- they are one periodicity in three categories joined by known
constructions, which is the numerology-vs-number-theory line.

THE TWO HALVES SEPARATE ONE TICK OVER. Asked whether the clock splits an adinkra into "what acts"
and "what remains", the answer is no at s and yes at s+1: Cl^0(p,q) = Cl(p,q-1), so the
grading-preserving half sits one position forward, and the halves separate exactly when
s+1 is in {1,5}, i.e. s in {0,4}. Cl(7,7) is NOT split, yet Cl^0(7,7) = M64(R) + M64(R) -- so GU's
two 64-dimensional Weyl pieces ARE the even subalgebra splitting, and reading IsSplit off the full
algebra gives the wrong answer. The N=8 adinkra sits at Cl(0,8), also s=0, giving M8(R) + M8(R):
two 8x8 blocks for the 8 bosons and 8 fermions of the 16-node adinkra. The block sizes fall out of
the clock rather than being chosen. The odd part swaps halves (a supercharge acts); the even part
preserves them ({Q,Q} = d_tau remains) -- which is the same layering `AdinkraClock.fs` landed on.

CORRECTED ON AARON'S READ. The first draft said GU "goes somewhere else entirely" from E8. Both
halves were wrong: Weinstein argues against E8 *in physics*, and E8 is one of our towers rather
than the tower. Pati-Salam does embed -- SU(4)xSU(2)xSU(2) = Spin(6)xSpin(4) < Spin(10) < Spin(16)
= D8 < E8 -- and the sharper relation is that GU's spin(6,4) and E8's Spin(10) are two real forms
of the same so(10,C). One complexified algebra, two real forms, two directions. The choice of real
form IS a signature choice, which puts it back under the same mod-8 clock.

FALSIFIER. 20 tests. The pinning invariant is dimension reconstruction: rebuilding dim_R from the
classified Morita type must return 2^(p+q) at every signature, which constrains all eight rows at
once. Anchored against four independently-known cases (Cl(0,1)=C, Cl(0,2)=H, Cl(1,3)=M2(H),
Cl(3,1)=M4(R)), and the algebra-split vs halves-separate predicates are shown to genuinely
disagree so that neither check is vacuous. Mutation: swapping the complex row to real kills 2 of
14. My first attempted mutant, (n-2)/2 -> (n-1)/2, was EQUIVALENT -- s=4 and s=6 force n even, so
integer division makes both expressions identical. It passed, and that was a bad mutant rather
than a weak suite; recorded because the same mistake is easy to repeat.

The ferries are MIRROR register, in ip-questionable because their IP status is uncertain. Part 2
carries the result that bears on our own work: GU is not chiral fundamentally but must produce a
chiral world effectively, via a coupling that weakens as curvature drops. That is the shape our
decorrelation lane already runs -- a symmetric substrate presenting as asymmetric once a control
parameter moves -- and it is the honest answer to where handedness comes from without putting the
asymmetry into the axioms. On CPT: we have the Z2 x Z2 involution group (grade involution,
reversion, and their composite), which is real and checkable; we do not have the CPT theorem,
whose hypotheses -- Lorentz invariance, locality, unitarity -- our substrate does not satisfy.
Stated separately so the structure is not read as the guarantee.
