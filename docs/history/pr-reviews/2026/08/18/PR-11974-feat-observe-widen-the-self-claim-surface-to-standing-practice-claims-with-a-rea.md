## Description

Aaron: *"we likely need to route to background agent(s) to extend our self claim surface — this is the
core of our wellness app for humans and AI."* The surface had two claim classes and a measured vacuity:
a reliability score with zero production consumers.

WIDENED — `practice-claims.ts` is the third class: a **standing** claim about one's own practice ("every
commit I author carries the signature block"), contradicted by the subject's own RECORD rather than by a
deadline (`self-claims.ts`) or by a second claim (`identity-claims.ts`). Admissible only where drift is
decidable without judgement, so a claim binds to a total, pure, three-valued `PracticeCheck`, and
`undetermined` is never a counterexample.

Five sovereignty properties, each with a refusal that falsifies it: nothing is checked unbound (the
registry is a menu, inert until a subject binds it); only the subject writes; only the subject's own
evidence is examined; evidence predating the binding is never drift; released and superseded bindings
stop applying and are kept.

CONSUMER — `self-claim-standing.ts` reads `db/self-claims/practice-bindings.json` and this repo's real
commit history (subject = the `Agent:` trailer, self-supplied; phase = first-parent depth, never a
clock). It is advisory by construction: always exits 0, and deliberately does not publish into
`hygiene/drift-ledger.ts`, which carries an MTTH SLO. What the observation changes is the subject's own
repair menu — recurrence adds `restate` and removes nothing, which is Aaron's correction that people do
not feel repair, they feel expansion of choice.

CHARITY GRADIENT — `CharitableReading` / `PatternReading` are imported from `identity-claims.ts`, not
redefined, so there is exactly one union in which a moral reading could appear and two exhaustive
switches guarding it. A single counterexample can only be read as `accidental` or `growth`; the widest
set this module can ever return is `accidental | growth | ironic | unresolved`. A type-level assertion
fails `tsc` if a moral constructor is added.

The delivery score still has no production consumer, and the header now says so precisely: consumer (1)
is *refused* by `composition-read.ts` §2 (no parameter through which B supplies a number about B), and
(2) would be vacuum-to-vacuum since `optimal-cadence.ts` itself has no non-test callers.

Falsifiers: 98 tests, most of them about what the fold declines to conclude. Mutation-checked with the
repo catalogue occurrence-wise over all three modules: 51/75 killed on the first sweep, 24 survivors
triaged (supersession windows and multi-subject isolation were genuinely untested), then 75/75 killed.
