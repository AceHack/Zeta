## Why it was ferried — recorded as questions, not conclusions

- **Zig is already a shipped byte-lock oracle here** (`src/wasm-dla/bytelock/dla-canonical-zig.wasm`), so the language's design priorities are not academic to us.
- **"Avoid a dependency for your core product"** — his stated reason for removing LLVM — is the same shape as the appointed-hub argument in `.claude/rules/itron-hub-patent-boundary-p2p-is-the-upgrade.md`, reached independently and for engineering rather than topological reasons.
- **BDFL vs committee, with money as the corrupting input.** He states the hierarchy exists to resist *"oxidation from being in contact with money"* — and then says plainly that it is **not sustainable**. That is a live tension against §1 scale-free and §3 weight-free which he does not resolve, and the not-resolving is the honest part.
- **TigerBeetle, annotated with Aaron's framing on receipt:** a named competitor, valued for one-time memory allocation and predictable latency because trading algos cannot absorb GC latency tails. Noted in the doc that this is a **latency-distribution** claim rather than a throughput one — the win is the *absent tail* — and that the DoP-knobbed ferry throttle is the same instinct applied to concurrency.
- **The no-LLM policy and "contributor poker."** His argument is not output quality alone; it is **mentorship as the point of code review**, with reviewer time as the scarce resource. Stated by someone running the tradeoff, not theorising about it.
- **The MIT-licence irony he names himself** — banning AI contributions while permitting AI training on the corpus. He calls it ironic and declines to resolve it. Filed as-is: declared irony is not detected irony.
