## Description

## The ruling

Aaron, 2026-08-02, on the collision between the two commitments Zeta calls inviolable — earned frost that can never be revoked, and the protect-the-children hard line:

> if this is in code then we become the court system, that's not what i'm building

and, sharpening it:

> zeta is trying to make this impossible, there are no thresholds for breaking frost other than the key holder giving you the key

**Frost wins — and not by clearing a tall gate.** There is no threshold because there is no mechanism. Frost is undone exactly one way: the owner hands over the key.

## The correction is the substance

My first draft said *"frost wins absent massive proof against the frost holder, and courts adjudicate."* Aaron corrected it, and the correction is the whole point: that phrasing describes a **break path with a high bar**. Writing a bar into code does not document an existing escape hatch — it **builds** one, and then argues about its height.

## Three consequences, captured in the entry

1. **A threshold IS a mechanism**, and it makes the substrate the judge — precisely the role manifesto §11 (Multi-Oracle Principle) forbids it. Whoever last edits the constant chooses the morality.
2. **There is nothing to build, and that is the point.** No operator, no escrow, no recovery key, no lawful-access intake. The substrate cannot reveal a frosted region as **capability**, not as policy. Any such path would be the centralised design — the standing architectural boundary for this project.
3. **Courts act on people, not on the substrate.** A jurisdiction may compel a human who holds a key; that is true of anyone holding a secret, is outside Zeta's control, and is not a break path in the system. The technical guarantee (*we hold no key*) and the legal one (*whether a person can be compelled*) are separate promises and must stay separate in docs.

## Why WONT-DO and not just memory

The omission **looks like an unfinished feature**. A contributor reading the frost rule will notice there is no defined bar and try, helpfully, to supply one. CLAUDE.md routes every agent through `docs/WONT-DO.md` before proposing work, so this is the surface that actually intercepts them — a persona-scoped memory would not.

## Scope

- Left **explicitly undecided**: a minor is also a dweller, and a child holding inviolable frost *against a guardian* is where the two hard lines genuinely touch. Out of scope here; a maintainer call.
- `.claude/rules/privacy-budget-is-hard-money-earned-by-others.md` is **not** edited — amending a HARD LIMITS surface is a gated class.

Docs-only. New `## Governance / adjudication` section, one entry, standard ADR-vocabulary shape (Status / Decision / Proposal / Why not / Revisit when).
