## What is structural

- **The uncoded N-cube adinkra IS the left regular representation of `Cl(0,N)`.** Vertices are blades, `Q_I` is left multiplication by `γ_I`, and the operators are therefore *elements of the same space as the data*. That is homoiconicity in the strict sense, and it is stronger than the "equations drawn as pictures" framing this repo has been using — that one only says the picture *encodes* the operator.
- **The A/B fork is a factorization, not a choice: `A = B / (∂_τ = 1)`.** Over the full SUSY algebra `S = ℝ⟨Q, ∂_τ⟩`, no adinkra is homoiconic — `S` is infinite-dimensional and the vertex module is finite. Homoiconicity becomes possible only after fixing a unit of time, and the **valise is exactly that quotient**. `LayeringBToA` was the right name for the wrong reason.
- **A discriminator with a live failure mode.** Structure route (is the vertex module free of rank 1 over the algebra its own edges generate?) and clock route (does `{Q,Q}` act as a scalar?) share no intermediate, and are **predicted to disagree at N=4**. At N=1 they genuinely coincide — which is the algebraic reason the probe was silent, arrived at independently of the code-path reason already on file.
- **Minimal ∧ homoiconic ⟺ N ≤ 3**, because a nonzero doubly-even codeword does not fit in fewer than four coordinates. So the thesis's two readings of "minimal" pull in opposite directions above N=3, and the boundary is structure rather than coincidence.
- **Claim 2, as Aaron narrowed it, holds.** Provably infinite (the doubling functor is total, the embedding is a split unital mono forever) and self-mapping on a named list. The invariant preserved at every rung is the **additive** GF(2) structure — and `CayleyDicksonDoublyEven.lean` already proves it sorry-free, its own honest-scope note ("MULTIPLICATION and CONJUGATION do not enter") being precisely why it survives. That proof landed two months ago for a different purpose and nobody had connected it.

## Honest negatives, stated rather than smoothed

- **homoiconic / homoclinic share only the morpheme `homo-`** (*eikon* vs *klinein*). Zero evidence. And there is a *positive* refutation, not a shrug: the doubling tower has no contracting direction, hence no saddle, hence no homoclinic orbit by construction. The burden is now specific — exhibit a map with a hyperbolic fixed point.
- **The degradation schedule does not force external witnesses.** It forces *conventions* (order, bracketing) and local guards. A convention is free; a witness is metered. Claims 2 and 3 do not merge, contrary to the hoped-for collapse.
- **`|Aut|`, the Lane-2 trigger, and `h_top` are not one quantity.** They share a schema (rigidity vs moduli) and a genuinely useful common unit (witness bits), and they differ in cost class: a *cardinality* closes with a finite witness, a *rate* never does. That distinction is decision-relevant for the substrate.
- **Zero divisors do not endanger the self-mapping claim** (a correction to the brief): they break invertibility of `L_x`, not the tower's self-embedding.

## What survives strongest

Aaron's last sentence. The obstruction is the **associator, a nontrivial class in H³** — and a cohomology class is precisely a thing no change of representative removes. Mac Lane coherence says it is *relocatable* into the ambient category and *not deletable*. "Force of nature" is the vernacular for that. The same anchor (Albuquerque–Majid) separates the two towers by one invariant — is the twisting 2-cochain a cocycle? — which settles that Clifford and Cayley–Dickson are **not one tower** past rung 3.

It also yields a short diagnostic the repo can use today: *is your non-associativity a representation defect or a cohomology class?* `QuorumAlgebra`'s float non-associativity is the first kind, so its named cyclotomic exit will work.
