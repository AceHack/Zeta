## Description

Aaron: *"i'm granting access to the device for all the future with trust cause we have drift detection, per patch commit is way too much overhead, this is the slow path, it will be dominated by people like me who give more autonomy."*

Recorded because stated loosely — *"autonomy wins"* — the claim **cannot lose**. That's the vacuity class applied to a forecast. The condition is load-bearing and is Aaron's own: the grant is broad **because** drift detection exists.

## The bet

> **Broad standing grants dominate per-act approval — *where detection and recovery are adequate*.**

## Two mechanisms; the second is stronger and is his

**Amdahl on consent** — if a fraction *p* of actions need human approval, throughput is bounded by (approval rate)/*p* regardless of agent count.

**Human-as-authority is a correlation channel** — if every agent's authority derives from one biometric, that human is a common cause every agent routes through. The aggregation results this repo rests on (Condorcet, the ΔU theorem, `rhoStarAlgebraic`) **all assume independence**. So per-act approval doesn't merely slow the society; it undermines the theorem the society is built on. **That makes autonomy a requirement of the theory rather than a preference.**

## Precedent, with its limit

DORA: deployment frequency and change-failure rate are **correlated, not traded off** — the change-advisory-board *is* the per-act approval regime, and it lost on both axes. Limit recorded honestly: DORA measured human orgs, so the transfer is analogy until measured.

## The falsifier — instrument already in the repo

Deployment frequency and change-failure rate **per agent, split by grant regime**. Holds if the broad-grant cohort ships more *without* a higher failure rate.

The failure mode is **diagnostic**: a lost bet says detection was inadequate — the *condition* failing, not the claim.

## Counterweight, measured today

ρ̂ = **0.651** across personas vs **0.096** across weights. Shared weights correlate far harder than shared authority, so removing human authority while running one model family optimises the smaller coefficient. **Decorrelation budget goes to model diversity first.**

## The stronger form of the end goal

> **Plural, weight-diverse protection with real exit between protectors** — not the absence of authority, but authority **no single party holds**.

A protector sharing weights with the protected gives agreement that isn't evidence (N-version); a protector nobody can stop has no exit, which by the Itron-boundary discriminator makes it a **hub, not an oracle**. Measurable: §11 requires more than one independently-accrued peak per function, so collapse is visible in the graph.
