## Description

Follow-up to #10950, addressing the relay from the sibling's #10945 (merged).

## The relayed result

The **Dominance Lift Theorem**: an aggregation rule beats its best part **iff it can imitate its best part** — every projection `pi_i` lies in the class the rule is optimal over. No `n`, no `c`, no correlation parameter, no identical-agents assumption, so it inducts to arbitrary depth. The constraint it puts on the law surface: **`deferential` belongs to the aggregation RULE, not to the level.**

## Checked against #10950 — no redirect was needed, one thing was added

- `LevelLaws.holdsAtEveryLevel` was already "one law, quantified over levels"; it is the module's stated purpose.
- `WorldLaws` is **not** a parallel law module — all three definitions are written in terms of predicates #10925 shipped (`isWorld` *is* `isClosed`). The naming is nonetheless an attractor for future duplication, so the prohibition on a per-level dominance law is now written at the declaration site.
- **Added** `Levels.Aggregation` in both oracles: `canImitateEveryProjection` (the theorem's hypothesis as a decidable predicate over caller-supplied witnesses) and `concentrateMassOn` (the CTM's witness).

## The CTM tournament discharges the hypothesis, and the witness is derived

Because `f` is additive under a match and a chunk wins with probability proportional to `f`, an input in which one processor carries **all** the rank mass makes the tournament return that chunk with probability 1, for *every* draw. So mass concentration is the imitation witness, and it falls out of the paper's own competition rule rather than being constructed to pass.

Both vacuity modes are tested: unconcentrated inputs **fail**, and an empty witness list is **not** a discharge.

## Register discipline on that discharge

It is the **hypothesis**, not the conclusion. Concluding that the CTM's global broadcast *dominates* its best processor additionally needs the theorem's optimality-class premise, which is the sibling's and is **not checked here**. A pass on `canImitateEveryProjection` must never be cited as a dominance result — stated at the declaration site in both languages.

## No correlation threshold, by construction

The same sibling PR showed `rho` is **not a sufficient statistic for the verdict** — a counterexample at `m = 9`, `rho = 0.2495` sits inside the published safe `rho*(9) = 0.25` and still loses over 40M trials. A law predicated on `rho < rho*` would be unsound. No law in `Levels.fs` or `Ctm.fs` takes a correlation parameter, and the reason is now written down so none is added later.

## One point held rather than conceded

The relay says the `ISociety <: CTM` subtyping claim *"remains §B open"*. #10950 does not leave it open — it **refutes** it, by an in-repo counterexample: the gossip salon is a working society with no competition and no global broadcast, so `ISociety <: ICtm` cannot hold **unconditionally**. What stays genuinely open is whether *particular* societies are CTMs, and the adjunction row; those are different statements from the one the 07-04 doc made.

The relay's other half is right and adopted: the subtyping claim does not have to carry the statistics, and the non-composition result does not bear on it either way. Nothing in the sibling's proof rests on the subtyping claim, so this disagreement costs their result nothing — it is recorded so the discharge table is corrected once rather than argued twice.
