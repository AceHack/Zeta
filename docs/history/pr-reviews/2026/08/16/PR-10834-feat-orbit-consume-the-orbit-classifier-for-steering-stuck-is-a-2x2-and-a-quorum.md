## What this is

Aaron 2026-08-15: *"the chaotic regime might be noise to most but it can be navigated and have cartography
done to it … it's noise that requires active avoidance system for getting stuck in homoclinic tangles"* and
*"it's not all grouped together."*

His read was right on both counts. Most of this existed and was ungrouped. This PR is **the grouping doc**,
plus the one genuinely missing piece (a consuming layer), plus **three measurements** that came out of
testing a synthesis rather than adopting it — two of which refuted parts of it.

## The grouping (`docs/research/2026-08-15-navigating-the-chaotic-regime-*.md`)

Five named pieces located and their real state recorded. Highlights:

* **Scheduler branch-pruning and the ferry throttler are ONE piece, not two.** `Vision.boatGrowth` charges
  each `FutureBranch` (`BranchCost {SpaceBytes; TimeTicks; BytesPerTick; …}`) against a byte tank and splits
  into **`Boarded`** / **`Deferred`**. That is a ferry applied to futures. A `prune` grep finds nothing
  because the verb here is **defer/board**. Aaron's memory of the capability is accurate; the search term
  was wrong.
* **Honest limit on it:** `BranchCost` is *caller-supplied*. The scheduler enforces a budget it is told; it
  does not estimate one. So "prunes on predicted big-O" is `metered` as budget enforcement and **absent** as
  cost prediction. *(That seam is a sibling agent's — untouched here.)*
* **CHIP-8 time dilation:** mechanism exists (`VirtualTimeScheduler.AdvanceBy`, `AdinkraClock.stepPure`) and
  so does a **safety proof** I did not expect to find — `AdinkraClock.isMetricFree` shows the causal trace is
  invariant under rescaling tick duration, i.e. variable-rate playback is *licensed*. The **policy** is
  absent: nothing derives the skip factor, and nothing consumes `Orbit.classify` at all. The hole is one
  function, `Orbit.Kind -> int64`. *(`AdinkraClock.fs` is a sibling's area — read-only here.)*

## The missing piece: nothing consumed the classifier, and "stuck" had no definition

`rg` for `Orbit.classify|classifyDynamics|Orbit.Kind` across `src/` returns nothing but one docstring
mention. `src/Core/TangleNavigator.fs` is that consuming layer. Its content is that **"stuck" is a 2×2**,
not a class:

| | confined | escaped |
|---|---|---|
| **λ ≤ tol** | `Frozen` — static. Nothing to be stuck *in*. | `Drifting` |
| **λ > tol** | **`Trapped`** — full price of chaos, going nowhere | `Navigable` |

Both signals load-bearing: λ>0 alone is just `Orbit.Chaotic`; confinement alone is a fixed point. **Two
false-positive modes are demonstrated on purpose** (budget-induced, tolerance-induced) — an avoidance system
that cannot report a false positive is not a control system.

## Three measurements

1. **`FigureEightEnsemble`'s "the groupthink spiral IS the homoclinic tangle" is wrong.** On file since
   2026-07-04, elevated to *"the unifying result of the whole day"*, never measured. **λ = −0.029276** —
   strictly contracting, classifies `Frozen`. A tangle is a saddle with λ>0. The *phenomenon* (ρ→1, monotone)
   is exactly as reported and the *conclusion* it supported is untouched; only the mechanism identification
   fails.
2. **Steering is cheap in chaos and unavailable in order.** The same bounded search cuts a stall 53 → 7 steps
   on a chaotic saddle and finds **nothing at any perturbation size** on a rotation. This is the sign
   reversal in Aaron's correction, measured.
3. **A quorum's escape bound is set by decorrelation, not headcount.** A *correlated* quorum buys nothing
   (exactly, for any N — `DebouncedOracle`'s ρ=1 at L=0, given a dynamical consequence). A *decorrelated* one
   bounds the escape then **saturates**, at a floor tracking `ln(1/ε)/λ`. Tested, not asserted.

## Added on Aaron's authorization: the cost side (§4a)

Everything else in this PR meters escape from the **escapee's side only**. §4a adds the second
observable. The structural argument is the coordinator's, and is labelled in the doc as an argument
rather than a measurement: **you cannot decorrelate unilaterally** — decorrelating *is* perturbing the
joint system, the same act seen from outside. Checkable form: **does the escape path meter its effect on
the correlated set, or only on the escapee?** Every mechanism in §4 currently fails it.

**I ran the falsifier.** The §4 harness could not test it — its probes are independent trajectories, so
there is no joint system to perturb. So: a 6-node ring of the same r=4.02 saddle under diffusive coupling
(coupled map lattice, Kaneko 1984), one node kicked, **the other five nodes' dwell times measured**
against an unkicked baseline (4 000 trials, 20 000 neighbour samples).

| coupling | neighbours whose dwell changed | mean change | significance |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00 (negative control) | **0.0 %** | **exactly 0** | — |
| 0.05 | 63.4 % | +1.17 (SE 8.73) | 0.13 σ |
| 0.20 | 64.8 % | +8.88 (SE 8.06) | 1.10 σ |

* **The mechanism claim survives** — about two in three neighbours have their dwell changed by a
  perturbation applied to someone else, and at zero coupling *exactly none* do, so the harness is not
  manufacturing the effect. Escape is not locally free.
* **The "on average" claim is exactly where this model has no power, and that is the honest finding.**
  Mean within ~1 σ of zero at both couplings, **median exactly 0**, per-instance sd ≈ 1 200 against a
  2 000-step budget. Large per instance, unmeasurable on average — the model neither confirms nor refutes
  the "for the better" half. The qualifier was doing real work: its variance dominates its mean.

Register: scratch harness, **not a shipped test** (said so in the doc); the parameters in the doc are the
whole specification. Kept structural — no personal material, which is book work with its own consent
handling.

## Corrections flagged

* To the earlier `2026-08-02` lensography synthesis: avoid *getting stuck*, not *the chaotic regime* — chaos
  is the only regime where steering is cheap.
* To the synthesis I was handed: self-escape without a witness **is** possible (measured before the
  correction reached me, so independently); and the escape time is exponential, so the *mean* is finite —
  it is the **tail** that is unbounded.
* `LyapunovContraction.fs` is a Lyapunov **function** (KL contraction), not a Lyapunov **exponent**. Name
  collision, different subject.
* Pruning and dilation are related but **not identical**: pruning drops branches (lossy); dilation skips
  steps (lossless on periodic segments, which is what `isMetricFree` proves).
