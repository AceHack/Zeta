## The tension is stated, not resolved

Emitting IL re-enters the wall #10819 found: **.NET types are not GC-granular**, the smallest collectable unit is an `AssemblyLoadContext`, and `ShivaGc` stops applying at that boundary. Bonsai trees are **values**, so they stay GC-granular and content-addressable — which is the real argument for Aaron's Bonsai-first ordering, beyond "easier to reason about than IL".

Anonymously-hosted `DynamicMethod` is named as **a seam, not a solution**: it buys methods, not types. The section says in as many words that reading it as "IL is unblocked" is reading it wrong.

## Anchors added (checked for entailment, not adjacency)

- **Amin & Rompf, *Collapsing Towers of Interpreters* (POPL 2018)** — entails the close-over-itself step: a *tower* of interpreters collapses under a single-level specializer. Futamura gives one rung; this gives the tower.
- **Bolz, Cuni, Fijałkowski & Rigo, *Tracing the Meta-Level* (ICOOOLPS 2009)** — entails that a JIT is obtainable by tracing the interpreter. Cited as the **alternative** route and as the honest reference class for "JIT-like".
- **Reynolds (1972), *Definitional Interpreters*** — defunctionalization is literally what `defaultEvalDef` is, and is *why* a specializer can read the interpreter's rules.
