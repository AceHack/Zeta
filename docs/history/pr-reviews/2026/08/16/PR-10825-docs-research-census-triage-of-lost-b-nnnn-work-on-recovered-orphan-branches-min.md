## Correction to the brief's premise

The brief said B0732 / B0733 exist *"only"* in the recovered tree and *"never landed on main"*. **They are on `main` today, open** — `081KSE6WT0008QG0R002YBWBB1` and `081KSE6WT0008QG0R00102H071`, with identical line counts to the recovered copies. Consequence worth flagging: **a recovered-tree hit is not evidence of loss.** All 63 items appear there; 58 are fine. I left B0732/B0733 untouched — that is the sibling agent's lane.

## The decisive signal

Filename and alias-map matching were not enough. What separated *stale idea* from *load-bearing loss* was counting citations from **live rows on `main`** to each never-landed id: **13 of 14 are cited by live rows.** The quarantine README's bulk label of *"~86 stale B-xxxx backlog ideas"* was applied to a heterogeneous pile and is wrong in a checkable way — these were dependencies, not stale.
