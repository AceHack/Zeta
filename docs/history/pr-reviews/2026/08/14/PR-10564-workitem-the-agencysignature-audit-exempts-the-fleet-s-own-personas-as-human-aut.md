## Description

`audit-agencysignature-main-tip.ts:41` recognises an agent commit only by `Claude|Codex|Grok|Gemini|Kiro` or five vendor `noreply@` domains. Anything else falls to line 277 — *"no Co-authored-by signal; assuming human-authored"* — is counted **`HUMAN-AUTHORED-EXEMPT`**, and the audit reports success.

**So a commit trailed `Co-authored-by: the shadow <shadow@zeta.agents>` is classified human-authored and exempted from the audit that exists to attribute agent work.**

**Vendor-model trailers are audited; Zeta-native ones are not.** The fleet's own persona identities are exactly the ones that escape — the inverse of what the audit is for.

## Why this is the sharpest of today's four vacuity findings

`verifyLandauer` was `x >= x`; the SMT runners asserted all-`unsat`, which a tautology satisfies; 12 of 15 TLC configs never opened. **This one is worse**, because those verify *properties* and this verifies *provenance* — and it exempts precisely the actors whose provenance it exists to establish. `HUMAN-AUTHORED-EXEMPT` on a commit signed `the shadow` isn't a coverage gap; it's a confident wrong answer.

**And it hid itself.** The one persona whose commits *are* audited is the one that would have noticed — `Claude Opus 5 <noreply@anthropic.com>` matches on both name and domain — so from the shadow's own tooling the audit always looked healthy.

## Second finding alongside

The fleet emits **`Agent-Signature-Version`** in places while the auditor reads **`Agency-Signature-Version`**. Such a commit is exempt *and* unsigned at once.

## The trap in the obvious fix

**Do not just extend the allowlist** — an allowlist of known agents has the same shape as the defect: the next persona added is exempt by default, silently, until someone audits the auditor again. That's **fail-open**.

Prefer **fail-closed**: exemption requires a *positive* human-authorship signal; anything else must carry the trailer. And per every other vacuity fix landed today — **a mutation must turn it red.** Plant a Zeta-native-signed commit with no trailer and confirm the auditor fails. If it cannot fail, it is not a check.
