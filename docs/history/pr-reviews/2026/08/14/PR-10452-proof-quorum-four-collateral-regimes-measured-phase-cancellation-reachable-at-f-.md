## Description

Routing verdict first, and it **splits** — which is the discipline working, not an inconsistency.

## Routing

**CONFIRMED** for the collateral layer. Discrete, finite, temporal; nothing needs a real number, so there is no discretisation to accidentally verify instead of the thing. Wrong-tool cost named: Z3 would have proved the quorum counting (true and easy) and been unable to express *reachability*, where the entire result lives.

**PARTIALLY REFUTED** for the amplitude layer, and that is a finding. Phase is real-valued and TLA+ has no reals. So the phase set is a **restriction of the adversary** to the 4th roots of unity — exact Gaussian integers, no rounding — not a discretisation. Results travel one way only: **reachability transfers up** to arbitrary real phases, **non-reachability does not**. Every claim is stated in that direction, and the general-phase magnitude bound is routed to Z3/Lean rather than inferred from a green TLC run.

## The correction that matters most

The brief expected the resolutions to differ in Byzantine tolerance. **They do not.** TLC explores worst-case behaviour; slashing is an incentive mechanism; incentives are invisible to a worst-case adversary. Safety holds at f LT n/3 identically in all four. The right question is whether the tolerance is **renewable**.

**R4 is not a fourth proposal.** `privacy-budget-is-hard-money` was amended 2026-08-09 with the spend/stake/confiscate table, so the rule already permits the wager and R4 needs **no exception carved**. `NoSeizure` is written as a *conservation* condition — budget may leave an identity only into that identity's own at-risk pot — because naive monotonicity would flag a dweller's own wager as a violation and get the rule backwards.

**The unexpected finding:** `NoCompulsion` fails under **R1** at depth 6, *post-slash*. An identity whose burnable asset gated participation can now participate nowhere. The free arena is not R4 scaffolding — every slashing regime needs it, and the rule's own guard (*"a stake must never be required to hold a role or to participate"*) is exactly what the check mechanises.

## Phase cancellation

**Reachable at f = 1** when magnitudes are uncapped — trace: `m4 = <<-3,0>>` annihilates three honest `<<1,0>>`. No 1/3 threshold anywhere; the threshold is **amplitude normalisation**. Adopting amplitudes without normalising drops tolerance from f LT n/3 to f = 1.

**Not distinguishable** from honest disagreement when honest members may carry opposite phases and the honest count is even — and the counterexample survives *full per-member visibility*, because the values are identical. The odd-count green is a **parity artefact**, reported as one rather than banked.

## WagerSolvency

Without a structural floor a dweller wagers into subsistence and the loss becomes the society's liability — the stake was socially insured and never deterred anything (Kelly 1956: log utility undefined at zero wealth). The privacy/solvency pair is a **genuine contradiction** needing a construction (proof-of-reserves + ZK range proof), not a compromise. And the self-reference bites: an attestation goes stale through the owner's own later actions. Honest note — TLC's shortest counterexample is loss-induced, not the frost path I predicted; the trace on record is the one it found.
