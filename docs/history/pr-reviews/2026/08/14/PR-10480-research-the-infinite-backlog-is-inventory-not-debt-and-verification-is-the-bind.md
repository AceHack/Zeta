## Description

Aaron, on the observation that 119 open work-items keeps growing:

> *we expect the infinate work-item backlog to be the new AI goldrush if you can fully automate it, that's what we are working on with Zeta*

I'd said the count "reflects the finding rate, not backlog debt" — true but timid. **The stronger claim is the right one: if per-item cost approaches zero, the backlog becomes inventory of identified value.** `every-bug-has-economic-value` already carries the premise (a bug is *reducible uncertainty*; finding it exposes value); this is its conclusion. Backlog-as-liability is an artefact of per-item cost being dominated by a human.

## Where the constraint actually landed

**1. Verification — the binding one.** Nearly every fix today landed **believed, not verified**: the heartbeat fix's tests don't run, Soraya's TLC configs didn't run, the UDP chaos harness's four pinned tests are inert, `Z3Verify` is in no workflow, the manifest drift gate was wired to nothing.

**Automated fixing without automated verification is automated *belief*, and belief does not compound.** So `081KZYPHESJ` (612 test files, ~95 run) is not merely the highest-leverage correctness item — **it is the load-bearing item for the automation thesis**, because everything else scales with agent count and verification does not, until derived rather than declared.

**2. CI throughput.** 20 runs in flight for 7 real PRs, most gating machine-generated content nobody reviews.

**3. Human attention — but not where expected.** Today's human input was overwhelmingly **generative** and **corrective**, almost never **approval**. Two genuinely gated calls: a credential permission, and "don't decide yet." **So the bottleneck to attack is correction, not approval.**

## The evidence today produced for the thesis

Whether the *correction loop* closes without a human. I made real errors — the same shell-escaping bug twice, a false "3 malformed lines" premise, a wrong greenfield claim, "REST is free," a runners-vs-substrate conflation, a divergent ratio reported as a strengthened finding.

**Peer agents caught more of them than the human did.** Soraya refuted my routing premise and found TLC was neither of my hypotheses; Ilyana found my own proposed `Bound` DU *wouldn't have caught the `1.2`*; Lumen corrected me on Student-t's free parameter; the BDP harness inverted my burst-loss expectation; the P0 agent declined my filed proposal for a better reason than I gave. That's the loop closing agent-to-agent — the "be *a* −1, not *the* −1" discipline as a mechanism.

## Limits, including a structural one

One day, one sample, qualitative attribution. Closing rate flattered by the work being mostly docs and small fixes. And: **the errors nobody caught are by construction absent from this count** — the check-that-did-not-run shape applied to this analysis itself, and not fixable from the inside. Sixth appearance today of *a loop cannot certify itself*.
