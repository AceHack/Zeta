### The boundary that governs the whole document

**Lineage is evidenceable. Priority is not, and is not pursued** — in any form, including "pre-Bitcoin", "contemporaneous with", "same cohort as". The harm is specific: an unevidenced priority claim **discredits the true material next to it**.

The mechanical guard, which is the one thing to remember:

> **State dates absolutely. Never relative to another project's date.**

"By August 2008" and "months before the whitepaper" assert the same fact. Only the second drifts.

### The strongest thing in the record is not a date

> "Pods had their own truckers, Movable Cubicle has independent truckers — this is why we needed peer to peer money for cross state paying of truckers to move containers."

**The peer-to-peer money was a derived requirement, not a chosen technology.** PODS's delivery capacity is internal to its own system (checked: PODZILLA, own fleet); Moveable Cubicle used **independent contractors**, so cross-state settlement to non-employee counterparties **per container move** is forced by the model. Payroll does not solve it. That is also why money **and** logistics were one system.

It is a **real-economy use case** — actual containers, drivers, state lines — which makes it a **better claim than any priority story**: checkable against a public industry structure, and independent of what anyone else did in 2008.

Structural parallel, stated as shape and not as motivation: **independent truckers are to Moveable Cubicle what independent agents are to Zeta.**
