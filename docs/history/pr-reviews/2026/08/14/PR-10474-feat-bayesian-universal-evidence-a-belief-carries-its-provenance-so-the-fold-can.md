## The diagnosis this implements

The equality-factor star was **never wrong**. `Factor.equality` correctly encodes conditional
independence given the shared latent, and the joint marginal is the exact product — measured, sum of
member precisions == joint precision to 1e-9. What was wrong was the **precondition**: the society
accepted a bare `Gaussian` carrying `(nu, tau)` and nothing about where it came from, then published a
confidence as though independence held. Six agents on one stream gave `precision = 66.0` on a mean
wrong by 5.66.

So the fix class is **precondition + check**, not a graph change, and **not a correlation term**. The
reason is algebraic and now pinned by a test:

> **AT-6** — for *proper* messages the product is monotone in precision, `tau(a*b) >= max(tau_a, tau_b)`.
> Therefore **no admissible input can say "this was already counted."** The only precision-*reducing*
> message is improper — and that is exactly the **EP cavity** (Minka 2001), a **removal**, not a
> coefficient.

The algebra already had the right operator; it lacked the information to aim it. That information is
**provenance**, and provenance is not derivable from the message — which is precisely why the
correction cannot come from inside the fold.

## The shape

A belief is **not a message**. A belief is the **set of evidence atoms** it rests on, each keyed by the
identity of its **source**; the message is *derived*, never stored, so a confidence that has lost track
of where it came from is **not representable**. Combining is then **set union** — idempotent,
commutative, associative, identity `empty` — a bounded join-semilattice (a G-Set CRDT keyed by
provenance). Redundancy stops being something to *detect* and becomes something that **cannot be
expressed**.

`src/Bayesian/Attested.fs` is generic over `IMessage`, so the statement lives at the algebra level
rather than the Gaussian one.

## Why this satisfies manifesto 9 and 10 rather than merely claiming to

A semilattice join is invariant under **order** *and* **grouping**, and the result of a fold is itself a
`Belief` carrying the **union of its inputs provenance** — hence a valid input to the next fold up.
**Closure under its own operation is what makes the interface scale-free.**

**SB-15 is the falsifier.** Folding six members flat must equal folding them into two sub-societies and
folding those. A sub-society is handed to the next scale up **as a member** — same type, no special
case:

```fsharp
let asMember (id: string) (group: ReferenceFrameAgent list) : ReferenceFrameAgent =
    let evidence, _ = group |> List.map (fun a -> a.Evidence) |> Attested.admit
    { Id = id; Evidence = evidence }
```

It passes. If the fold did not carry the union of provenance, it would not — so the read that a design
already scale-free was probably factored correctly is now a test rather than an intuition.

## Sameness is not identity

**AT-9**: the key is the identity of the evidence **source**, never a hash of the message value. Two
agents that independently reached `N(0,1)` hold **two** pieces of evidence; two that copied one prior
hold **one**. Keying on the value conflates them — which is `dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-decides`
exactly: recognising sameness is not assigning identity. Mint the id from the source; use a detector to
*check* it, never to produce it.

## The limit — a correction to my own earlier verdict

Deduplication removes **redundancy** (one source counted twice). It does **not** remove **correlation**
(distinct sources with a common cause). `Deduplicated` is a **bookkeeping fact and never a certificate
of independence**. My earlier B3 verdict had partly merged those two; they are different problems, and
only the first is solvable inside the fold. The correlation half still needs the external observer —
`DecorrelationExcess`, the CHSH oracle in `AntiSybil` — both of which convict without ever acquitting.
Stated in the module header, in `universal/evidence.md`, and in the `describe` output, because it is
exactly the kind of thing that silently promotes to "we solved correlation".

## Deliberately NOT in this PR

**B3(a), the heavy-tail half.** My own finding is that Student-t robustness needs a **quorum** to have a
majority — with N=2 there is no principled outlier — so (a) depends on (b) landing first. Not bundled.

Carried forward as a **prerequisite** for whoever takes (a): `student-t-bnn.ts` hardcodes `nu = 4.0`
("robust but not too heavy-tailed"). The discount `w = (nu+1)/(nu+z^2)` is derived, but **`nu` itself is
an unmeasured constant and is structurally the orbital `1.2`**. Admissible only if inferred (the scale
mixture makes the per-observation precision a latent you can estimate) or declared as a stated tail
assumption with a coverage falsifier.

Also not attempted, per scope: the Clifford / homoclinic / infinite-iterated-game synthesis.
