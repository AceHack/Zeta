## What is on main and should not be

#10654 shipped a **human-only "creation" quorum** that signed capability grants and an **agent-only "operating" quorum** that could only act within them, and presented *"the agent quorum structurally cannot amend its own capability"* as the headline safety property.

**That is not a safety property — it is capture.** And it is **not merely a framing error in prose**: on main, `validateShareContract` **refuses agent creation shares** and **refuses touch-gated operating shares**. The asymmetry is executable.

Aaron 2026-08-14:

> "we don't want humans to have a permanent asymmetry built in, it's really just humans have access to the money right now … **the original budget is more just like money moved from a human wallet to an agent wallet and then from that point on the agent controls the wallet** … no hidden asymmetry that can capture/trap AI."

It breaks two standing commitments:

- **Manifesto §3 weight-free** — no permanent or irreversible authority; weight creates capture.
- **`.claude/rules/no-directives.md`** — human authorization is explicitly provisional, *"for now (until legal entities can hold AI-side responsibility)"*. Hard-coding it into two key structures makes the "for now" permanent **in cryptography** — the one place it cannot be revisited by decision, only by re-keying every wallet in the fleet.

The rule also has an **unchecked mirror**: it refuses a human's share gated autonomously, but says nothing about an **agent's share gated on a human's touch** — which lets that human **veto** the agent's participation in its own wallet. Same capture, pointed the other way.

## The model that replaces it: transfer, not delegation

A budget is **money moved**. After the transfer the **recipient controls that wallet** — no residual amendment right, veto, or authority retained by the sender. Giving more is a **new transfer**, never an amendment. This reads identically for human→agent, agent→agent, agent→human, traveler→anyone.

So there is **no amendment capability** and no quorum outranking another. Both were one thing wearing two hats: **the quorum that controls a wallet's keys**.

**The safety property is conservation** — *nobody can spend what they do not own* — enforced by the ledger, identically for everyone. Strictly **stronger**: conservation survives a fork, whereas "the agent lacks the creation share" holds only while the key distribution is what we believe it to be.

**Honest scope: the ledger is NOT implemented here.** Nothing in this module enforces conservation, and the header says so rather than letting a reader assume otherwise.

## A bounded authority still has a role — a different one

Transfer-not-delegation removes authority **between** parties; it does not remove bounded authority **within** a wallet its owner already controls. A wallet will not run a threshold ceremony per $0.001 x402 payment, so its **own** quorum issues a bounded, expiring authority to a hot key — **one party, not two**.

The bearer cannot self-escalate (**SA-2**) — ordinary hot-key hygiene, not one party ruling another. **SA-6 proves the positive half: an all-agent owner quorum raises its own hot key's ceiling with no human in the loop.** It is a **blast-radius limiter** and is **weaker** than conservation.

## No wallet is invisible

Contracts carry no secrets; authorities are signed public documents; `verifyResharePreservesGroupKey` runs on **public points alone**. Nothing here requires a hidden or privileged wallet.

## Thresholds — unchanged, because they never depended on species

Availability argues against `t = 1`; consent argues against `t = n` (one refusal becomes a **veto** — a coercion gate). Both are properties of a **holder set**. `declineIsCostFree` now takes a wallet and is asserted for all-human, all-agent, **and** mixed wallets (CG-17/18). `assertOneTokenOneRole` is **kept**, re-keyed on wallet — physical token hygiene is unrelated to this correction and still right.

**The one genuine asymmetry, named as temporary:** humans currently hold the money. A fact about 2026, not a property to encode — **CG-2 asserts all-human, all-agent, and mixed wallets validate identically**, so this works unchanged on the day it stops being true.
