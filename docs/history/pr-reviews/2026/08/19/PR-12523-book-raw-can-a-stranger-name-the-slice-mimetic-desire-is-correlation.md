book(raw): can a stranger name the slice — mimetic desire is correlation

Aaron 2026-08-19: "save this to things hidden since the foundation of the world and
peter thiel and memetic desire this is such a reduction is impressive, i think maybe

THE SENTENCE. "Can a stranger name the slice?" — the soundness test for
subset-verification. A claim requiring ALL our history to evaluate is unfalsifiable to
outsiders by context asymmetry, not by concealment. Subset-verification fixes that
(inclusion proofs; a light client verifies a chain without the chain) but only if the
CHALLENGER picks the subset. If we pick, "verify against a subset" degrades into
"verify against the subset that makes me look right" — which is why Fiat-Shamir derives
the challenge from a hash of the commitment rather than letting the prover choose. The
test is binary: they name the slice and it is a proof; we hand them a window and it is
a demo.

THE REDUCTION AARON IS POINTING AT. Girard's mimetic desire — desire is triangular, we
want what the model wants — restated in this substrate's vocabulary is rho -> 1 in
preference space. A crowd of imitators is not N independent wanters; it is one wanting
counted N times, which is precisely the failure that breaks Condorcet, ECC, boosting,
and diversification alike. And Thiel was Girard's student, which makes "competition is
for losers" a corollary rather than a brag: competition is mimetic, and his monopoly is
decorrelation — occupy the space nobody is imitating you into.

So the stranger test is the ANTI-MIMETIC test. If only insiders can name the slice, the
verification is itself mimetic: the group checking itself against its own criteria,
where every check confirms what the group already wanted true. A stranger's choice is
drawn from outside the correlated preference field, and that is the one operation that
breaks the loop.

Fifth instance today of one junction: you cannot verify yourself from inside. Godel for
consistency, Tarski for truth, Kish for witness counts, Lincoln-Petersen for universe
size, Girard for desire.

REGISTER, held explicitly in §4 because it is the part that would rot. The arithmetic
claim (self-chosen subset proves nothing) is not interpretation — Fiat-Shamir is the
checked anchor. "Mimetic desire is rho -> 1" is a STRUCTURAL ANALOGY and labelled
`unmetered`: Girard did not derive it and no correlation has been measured over a
preference field, so it is stated as "consistent with" per numerology-vs-number-theory.
The Thiel reading is recorded as Aaron's reading, not as Thiel's stated position.
