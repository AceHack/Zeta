correct(research): §4's falsifier fired -- the Landauer placement claim was wrong

§4 of the Maxwell's-demon doc offered a falsifier and it was taken up within
hours of landing. It fired. Recorded as an in-document correction rather than a
quiet edit, because a placement claim that was corrected is worth more as a
record than one that appears to have been right all along.

CLAUSE 1 -- five preimage-destroying operations not on §4's list, all declared in
src/Core/WSetHeat.fs:50-114 and MEASURED by exhaustive sweep in
tests/Tests.FSharp/Formal/WSet.ErasureClassification.Laws.Tests.fs, which fails
if declaration and measurement disagree in EITHER direction: plus (fibre 3,
1.585 bits -- the fold's own addition), consolidate (fibre 11, 3.459 -- the
Z-set constructor path), bornProb, discard, tensor. The erasure lives in the
ordinary arithmetic of the fold, not at a GC boundary. ZSet.ofSeq runs
consolidate on construction and ZSet.fs:211,216 drops a zero-weight key, so +1
then -1 leaves nothing. §4's list was not merely incomplete -- it pointed AWAY
from the highest-frequency erasing operation in the substrate.

CLAUSE 2 -- FerryQueue.dequeue/flush are eviction-shaped and measured REVERSIBLE
(0 bits) because the payload is returned.

WHAT WAS WRONG: §4 typed the list by LIFECYCLE STAGE (compaction, squash,
snapshot, eviction) -- a lifecycle taxonomy wearing a thermodynamic one's
clothes. The real invariant is INJECTIVITY. An operation erases iff it is
non-injective, not iff it is called garbage collection.

WHAT SURVIVES: the dichotomy (retraction free, erasure elsewhere) is CONFIRMED --
negate is fibre 1, 0.000 bits -- and the F# test states it directly two to five
days BEFORE this doc was written. So the repo already held a machine-checked
CI-gated answer and I asserted a placement without looking for it. That is
anchor-to-human-prior-art's failure mode committed against our own prior art.

§7e's FALSIFIER WAS ILL-FORMED, and the flaw is this document's own §3:
SybilBft.decide satisfies it trivially, but the witness is produced by absence of
instrumentation rather than by a fact about the world -- any unmetered quorum
satisfies it. A falsifier that fires whenever the meter is missing measures our
books, not the second law. Restated as a ledger claim.

§7e's CONDITIONAL is CONFIRMED and is the real finding: our ledger meters neither
compaction nor witness-correlation formation. Three distinct ledgers exist;
entropy-tracker.ts charges five sites and none is on §4's list;
RecoverableSpine.fs:74 truncates the absorbed tail and charges nothing; and
erasure-derivation.ts:49 states the §7c gap itself -- the tracker does not carry
caller-retained side information, which is exactly H(A|B).

§11f, verified in source: Heat.fs dispositionOfKind matches the whole dotted kind
including source prefix, and the file's own comment names denied-list.compacted
and rejection-sampler.evicted among the cases -- a compaction and an eviction the
meter can read as free, under-charged in the unsound direction.

§11g replaces §4: meter by injectivity, extend the existing WSetHeat machinery to
the spine/log/eviction sites, do not build a second list. §11h marks four things
unknown rather than fine.
