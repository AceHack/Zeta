## Description

docs(vision): echolocation over time — the Z-set fold measures correlation, and the interference formula IS the variance algebra

Aaron 2026-08-19: "please save this into our vision.md over our zsets, this is our
echolocation over time, the debounced together without root-2 interference —
noninterference."

TWO ENDS OF ONE AXIS. Decorrelation fails from opposite ends of TIME, and treating them
as one problem is how a fleet certifies independence it does not have. PAST correlation
is the S=4 common seed — one origin, shared cause, everything downstream inherits it.
FUTURE correlation is mimetic desire (Girard) — imitators converge on the same prize
regardless of where they came from. Decorrelating one does not buy the other.

Consequence, and it lands on a measurement made today: the rho computed this evening
from `db/mutation-findings/*.jsonl` is entirely BACKWARD-looking — which files agents
did sample. It says nothing about whether they converge tomorrow. The forward half has
no instrument.

THE Z-SET FOLD IS THE INSTRUMENT. Emit +1, retract -1; the fold across time is a ping
and a return. That is echolocation — you do not observe position directly, you emit and
read what comes back. The commutative fold means a late return still locates correctly,
so the instrument tolerates reorder, loss and skew without losing the fix.

THE INTERFERENCE SIGNATURE IS NOT AN ANALOGY. For N sources at pairwise correlation rho,
Var(sum) = N s^2 + N(N-1) rho s^2. At rho=0 that is N s^2 — incoherent addition,
amplitude as sqrt(N), which is the same statement as "standard error shrinks as
1/sqrt(N)". At rho=1 it is N^2 s^2 — coherent addition, amplitude N, intensity N^2. The
optical interference formula and the statistical variance formula are the same equation
written once. So the interference signature IS the correlation measurement: check
whether the sum scales as sqrt(N) or as N.

LIVE INSTANCE ALREADY IN-REPO: QuorumAlgebra's bug B3 — six agents on one stream,
precision = 66.0 on a mean wrong by 5.66, and the test comment names it exactly: "six
times the amplitude, 36x intensity." N and N^2 where sqrt(N) and N were wanted.

WHY §13 IS THE GUARD RATHER THAN A SLOGAN: you can only distinguish coherent from
incoherent addition if every contribution arrived through a declared, metered channel.
An ambient path — unmetered channel, shared clock, leaked prior — is an undeclared
coupling that makes two "independent" sources coherent without either knowing. §13 keeps
the channel list complete enough for the sqrt(N)-vs-N test to mean anything.

GAP STATED RATHER THAN IMPLIED: `effectiveTrialCount` (Kish) is the shipped correction
for exactly this and has NO PRODUCTION CALLER as of 2026-08-19 — verified twice today.
Every witness and match count in the repo is a head count.

REGISTER: the variance/interference identity is algebra, not metaphor, and is shown in
the section rather than asserted. The past/future framing is Aaron's, recorded as his.
No claim is promoted; the forward-looking measure is named as absent, not as planned.
