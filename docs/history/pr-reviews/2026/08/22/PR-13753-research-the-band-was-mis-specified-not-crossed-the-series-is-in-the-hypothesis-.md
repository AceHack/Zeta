## Description

`docs/research/2026-08-22-the-decorrelation-meter-left-its-band-and-i-may-be-the-reason.md`
named a falsifier and did not run it: compute ρ per commit across the history of
`db/mutation-findings/` and plot it against wall-clock time. It is run. The tool is
`src/Core.TypeScript/society/rho-series.ts`, the 751-row series is in
`db/effective-agent-count/`, and the doc is corrected IN PLACE rather than softened.

REFUTED, and not narrowly. The live (windowed) correlation meter peaked at 0.8545 on
2026-08-21T00:11:11Z — about twenty hours BEFORE the overnight window opened — and was
flat across it: mean 0.6987 inside versus 0.6819 in the preceding twelve hours, against a
bootstrap sd of 0.0423. That is 0.4σ. The cumulative statistic crossed 0.6 at
2026-08-22T11:23:49Z, three and a half hours AFTER the window closed. The fleet's
correlation really did quadruple — on 08-18 → 08-20, three days earlier — and had already
plateaued and begun to recover before the overnight agents started.

THE CROSSING IS INSIDE THE NOISE. The excursion was 0.0012. Bootstrap sd at this corpus
size is 0.0256 and 61.9% of replicates sit above 0.6; the median single-heartbeat step in
ρ is 0.0031, 2.5× the excursion. The bound fired on a boundary the estimator cannot
resolve.

THE BAND WAS NEVER DERIVED. It entered at #12548 as a ±0.13 sanity window around the
then-observed 0.4729 and has not been touched since. Its sibling ICC bound was already
widened once, in #12733, with an explicit argument that a static window over a drifting
quantity re-expires on a timer.

AND THE STATISTIC CANNOT CARRY A FIXED UPPER BOUND AT ALL — this is the finding that
outlives the hypothesis. `--null-model` simulates three agents whose sampling distribution
NEVER changes, drawing iid from a fixed pool of f·N frame files, and the cumulative ρ
climbs 0.156 → 0.949 as the corpus grows, crossing 0.6 at about 300 draws each, which is
where the real fleet is. Nothing about the agents moved. The honest limit is stated and
tested: at f = 1 the cumulative ρ stays at ~0 forever, so growth alone is not the cause —
the drift is pool restriction × saturation, and the band is mis-specified in BOTH
directions, the upper bound expiring and the lower receding out of reach.

The fix is the DOMAIN, not the number. Windowing restores both properties a gate needs and
both are pinned as tests: invariance (windowed ρ stays inside a 0.1 band while cumulative
climbs 0.16 → 0.95) and power (halve the true pool mid-run and the cumulative fit does not
move at all, because an accumulated union is a ratchet a later narrowing cannot undo,
while the windowed fit recovers the new value within one window). A meter with no power is
not a safer meter.

THE BOUND IS NOT WIDENED AND NOT TOUCHED. `effective-agent-count.test.ts:392` is left
exactly as it was and main stays red there. The replacement is written out in the doc with
its derivation (0.90 ≈ observed windowed max 0.8545 + 1.1σ, a degeneracy bound, not a
belief about where the fleet sits) and deliberately NOT applied: swapping a failing bound
for a passing one in the same change that argues the bound is wrong is indistinguishable
from widening it, whatever maths comes attached. The red is now explained rather than
unowned, which was this document's actual complaint.

A THIRD FINDING the hypothesis had no way to reach. The module header insists the universe
must come from outside the agents' behaviour and enumerates N = 757. That is not the
population sampled. `mutation-runner.ts:419` builds its candidate list from
`git log --since="24 hours ago"` and picks `targets[hash(agent, tick) % targets.length]` —
so all three agents draw from the source+test pairs among files changed in the last
twenty-four hours, measured at 4 to 176 files, typically 30–70. `assertFrameContainsDraws`
cannot catch this because that list is a SUBSET of the declared frame. Three agents hashing
into a shared list of length L collide at a rate set by L, so the meter has been
substantially reading repo churn breadth, which no amount of agent decorrelation can move.
The 24-hour list fell to 10 files on 08-20, the day the windowed ρ peaked; six points,
monotone, stated as consistent-with rather than as a result, but the mechanism is not an
inference — it is what those two lines literally do.

MEASURED, NOT REIMPLEMENTED. The series imports `rhoFromUnionCoverage`, `iccOneWay`,
`parseFindings` and the frame predicate from `effective-agent-count.ts`; two seams were
extracted there (`universeFromFileList`, `parseFindings`) and the refactor is verified
behaviour-preserving by byte-identical `--json` output. `--verify-head` asserts
`pointAt(WORKTREE) === measure()` field for field by `===`. That check earned its keep
immediately: it went red because `measure()` enumerates the INDEX and a historical point
reads a TREE, and adding rho-series.ts beside its test made those 758 and 757. Comparing
against WORKTREE instead of HEAD keeps it a correctness test rather than a
tree-cleanliness test.

FALSIFIERS RUN, NOT CLAIMED. 18 new tests; 7 mutants applied and all 7 killed, with counts
