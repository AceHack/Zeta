## Description

## Two things, and the first is an error of mine

### 1. I moved a bound three consumers price against — putting it back

#13784 raised `runnerEnvelope.freeDiskGib` from 14 to 70 on the strength of two
runner measurements. Right number, wrong shape of change: it broke three
assertions in `lane-partition.test.ts` on main. **Checked, not inferred** —
reverting the field to 14 turns that suite green and nothing else in it moves.

The three that flipped are not test bugs. They are *"all applications together
do NOT fit one runner"*, *"hindsight and vllm are the measured self-hosted
case"*, and the partition `packLanes` produces. Those are **conclusions about
the tree**, and they should move as a deliberate decision alongside the
partition work, not as a side effect of one agent reading `df`.

The measurement now lives where it cannot silently reprice anything:
`measuredFreeDiskGib: 77` beside the declared bound, carrying the run id, both
runners' figures, the proposed correction, and the measured consequence of
taking it. Only the report reads it — and it prints **both** verdicts per cohort
and lets them disagree out loud.

### 2. Are our figures compressed or extracted? Compressed. And the estimate was HIGH.

Settled by pulling them on a hosted `ubuntu-24.04` (run
[32588276375](https://github.com/Lucent-Financial-Group/Zeta/actions/runs/32588276375))
rather than multiplying by a ratio:

| image | compressed | est ×2.67 | **actual extracted** | df delta | real ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `hindsight-api:0.1.1` | 8.81 GB | 23.51 GB | **15.58 GB** (14.51 GiB) | 15.80 GB | **×1.77** |
| `vllm-openai:latest` | 9.11 GB | 24.33 GB | **21.42 GB** (19.95 GiB) | 21.74 GB | **×2.35** |

**The runner survived both.** 85.73 GiB free at job start → 50.05 GiB free after
hindsight, its postgres + control-plane closure, **and** vllm. 35.68 GiB
consumed in total; 176 s and 172 s to pull.

**So: neither image is self-hosted-only on disk.** hindsight costs ~14.5 GiB,
not ~23 GB, and the extracted reality is *smaller* than the estimate rather than
larger — the direction that was worth checking was checked, and it went the
reassuring way.

The 2.67 ratio stays in the catalogue: it is the only figure measured across a
*spread* of images, and swapping it for either of two contradicting single-image
ratios would be choosing the number that flatters the conclusion. Both are
recorded instead.

### The real ceiling, stated

`85.73 GiB free at boot` and `77.06 GiB after checkout + toolchain install` are
different numbers; the second is the one a k8s lane starts from. After both
giants: 50 GiB. The `applied` cohort's whole image set is ~34 GB estimated —
and the estimate now has a known bias toward over-counting.
