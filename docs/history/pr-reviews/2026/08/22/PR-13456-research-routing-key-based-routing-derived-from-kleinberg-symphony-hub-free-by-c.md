## Two findings the brief did not ask for

**1. The Albert–Jeong–Barabási targeted-attack fragility does not apply here.** The brief asked for it as the honest limit. It is not one, and AJB scope themselves out in their own second paragraph, calling Erdős–Rényi and Watts–Strogatz *"fairly homogeneous"* as against scale-free. The fragility is a consequence of the degree **distribution**; Symphony caps in-degree at `2k` with out-degree `2+k`, and harmonic sampling has **no preferential-attachment term** — the draw depends on ring distance, never on the target's current degree. There is no heavy tail to target.

The limits that *do* apply, in its place: the homogeneous class's `f_c ≈ 0.28` fragmentation threshold under random removal, and the identity-side Sybil/Eclipse class (Douceur 2002; Castro et al. 2002) — which is why identifier binding is a **precondition**, and why `docs/BUGS.md`'s live "Reticulum announce wire is unsigned AND `dest` is unbound to `zid`" entry sits on the critical path.

**2. On the EXIT discriminator, the geometry we already ship is measurably weaker.** `itron-hub-patent-boundary-p2p-is-the-upgrade` sets the test — *can you defer elsewhere, or must you route through?* Gummadi et al. (SIGCOMM 2003) turn that into a number, and by their Table 1 and 65,536-node simulation at equal state per node:

| | Ring | XOR (what `dht-discovery.ts` ships) |
|---|---|---|
| Route selection, optimal paths | `c₁(log n)!` | **1** |
| Sequential neighbours | native | **unsupported by the geometry** |
| Paths failed at 30% node failure | **under 7%** | **~20%** |

Kademlia's α-parallel iterative lookup recovers some of this in practice — a mitigation the static-resilience metric deliberately does not model, since it measures the *geometry*. The geometric fact stands. Stated as **an input to a decision, not a mandate to rewrite** working, tested, DST-pure code that would have to re-earn its properties.

## Named open gap, not papered over

Key **responsibility** is route-through by construction in any DHT — one manager per key, no alternative. Replication over a successor list is the standard answer; we have no key-value layer at all. So the §1 compatibility claim in the doc is about **routing only**, and §6.3 says so.
