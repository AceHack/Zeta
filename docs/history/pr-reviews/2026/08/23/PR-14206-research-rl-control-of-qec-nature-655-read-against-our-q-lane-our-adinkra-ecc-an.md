## The comparison that costs us something

Their **entropy-regularisation coefficient is swept** — Fig. 5a reads off a critical drift frequency of ≈ 1/150 epochs, consistent with the independently measured 130-epoch response time. Our `tsirelsonThreshold` = 1/(3√2) says in its own docstring: _"A DESIGN CHOICE, not a first-principles derivation."_ Theirs is `metered`; ours is `unmetered`. Under `toy-is-free-metered-must-be-earned` that is the whole comparison, stated plainly.
