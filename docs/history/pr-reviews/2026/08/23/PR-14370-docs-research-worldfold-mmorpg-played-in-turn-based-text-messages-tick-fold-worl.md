Design write-up requested by Aaron 2026-08-22/23: an MMORPG delivered as turn-based text messages, Android+iOS, invite-anyone-at-any-time, FF6-ensemble feel, 2–10 minutes of world exploration per turn — plus the follow-up correction: turns are **playable, rewatchable balloons** (GamePigeon model: little programs that go back and forth), not just prose.

One new satellite doc, no code:

- `docs/research/2026-08-22-worldfold-text-message-mmorpg-tick-fold-world-ff6-shaped-design.md`

Design highlights, all landed on the existing substrate: world tick = phase-ordered fold (the local-time rule is the convergence theorem for async MMO turns); turns = idempotent `(traveler, tick)`-keyed deltas (SMS is at-least-once, so the medium *demands* idempotency); combat = seeded deterministic replay over the ring algebra (the 7-language byte-lock is both anti-cheat and free rewatch); the balloon is a renderer, never a transport of state (SMS players stay first-class); identity = ZetaId; jobs = hats; endgame renown = attestation-only (naming eigenvector). Status: toy/unmetered; six named falsifiers in §Metering; game-side WONT-DOs stated (no purchasable AP, no loot boxes, no streak shame).
