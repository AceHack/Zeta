## The four results

**1. The formalisation is right, and it needs one more distinction that changes the design answer.**
The universal property of the quotient makes *"pushforward needs constancy on fibres"* an **iff** —
no weaker condition (bounded variation included) gives *exact* descent. But there are **two**
pushforwards with **opposite** freedom: a **function** (intensive — a per-item rate) pulls back free
and pushes forward only if fibre-constant; a **measure** (extensive — a count) pushes forward free.
So *"the cluster's change rate"* needs **no hypothesis at all** if it means total changes per unit
time, and needs the full condition if it means the rate each member has. And that condition is the
DV2.0 criterion restated: **within-fibre change-rate dispersion is not noise in the measurement, it
is the measurement that the cluster has merged a hub with a satellite.**

**2. The variance word is not doing the work.** Physics names how *components* transform relative
to the **basis**; category theory names how a functor acts on **morphisms**; a basis change is
itself contravariant in the components, so the conventions differ by **exactly one `op`** —
derivable, not memorised. For covectors it is not even an analogy: **a covector *is* a
`Func<in T, out TResult>`**, so *"covectors pull back"* and *"`Func` is contravariant in `T`"* are
one sentence in two vocabularies. Scope stated honestly: the notion of **direction** is identical,
and everything the **metric** supplies (raising/lowering indices) exists on **one side only**,
because a subtyping preorder carries no metric.

**3. "More precise in Clifford space" — refuted as stated, and the true weaker claim kept.** GA
does not unify the two variances; the **musical isomorphisms** do, and they exist in any
non-degenerate inner-product space with no Clifford algebra anywhere. What GA buys is **index-free
notation**, because the metric lives inside the product. Two cautions recorded: GA carries **two
distinct dualities** (musical vs pseudoscalar) that are easy to conflate, and the `V ≅ V*`
identification is **pointwise and not natural** — on a statistical manifold the Fisher matrix is not
constant (condition number **508**, measured 2026-08-20), so *"vectors and covectors are the same
thing"* is exactly the error that document caught, in nicer notation.

**4. The route gives Q3 a real error term, and lands on the same boundary by a different road.**
EP's m-projection **literally is** a coarsening — its fibres are the distributions sharing a
sufficient statistic. The exact Bregman decomposition

```
E[ KL(p_i ‖ q) ]  =  E[ KL(p_i ‖ p̄) ]  +  KL( p̄ ‖ q )
                     └─ fibre-VARYING ─┘   └── DESCENDS ──┘
```

splits the part that does **not** descend (and **is** the approximation error) from the part that
does. Amari's Pythagorean theorem and the **law of total variance** are the same identity in
different costumes. On the **conjugate** lane the fibres are **singletons**, the error is
identically zero, and the technique says nothing — which is **§3.5's refutation reached by a
completely different road**. *A new method landing on the same boundary is evidence; one that
dissolved an inconvenient boundary would have been the warning sign.*

## The deepest part — it re-derives the ambiguity rather than dissolving it

*"Measure over the cluster"* does not name one operation. The same four members with the same
weights have **two** KL barycentres, and they are far apart:

| barycentre | closed form | already in the tree as |
|---|---|---|
| `argmin_q E[KL(p_i ‖ q)]` (right-KL) | **moment average** — `(0.500000, 4.050000)` | **EP moment matching** (Minka 2001) |
| `argmin_q E[KL(q ‖ p_i)]` (left-KL) | **natural-parameter average** — `(0.947368, 1.052632)` | **NG4's `mix()`** — log-linear pooling (Genest 1984) |

**Both are already implemented, in different files, under different names.** That is §3.3's
e-versus-m ambiguity arriving by a different door — so the variance route does **not** dissolve
Q3's ill-posedness, it **re-derives** it, and names the missing datum: *fix which affine structure
is canonical for the purpose; it is a modelling decision, not a geometric fact.*

## What this changes upstream

- **§3's verdict is unchanged**, now with a second independent corroboration.
- **§7.1 is strengthened:** *root the metric, not the update.* The Bregman machinery is entirely a
  metric-layer choice and has **no content** at the affine layer, where the fibres are singletons.
- **It does not rescue the Gärdenfors half** — a coarsening yields a quotient, not a convex region.
- **One design consequence, ready before the registry is built:** publish the **within-cluster
  dispersion** alongside the cluster's rate. A cluster reporting a rate without it is a pushforward
  asserting an unchecked hypothesis — `unmetered`, not `metered`.
