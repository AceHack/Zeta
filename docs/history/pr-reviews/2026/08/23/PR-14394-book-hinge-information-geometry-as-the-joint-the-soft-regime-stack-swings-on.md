## Description

book(hinge): information geometry as the joint the soft-regime stack swings on

Adds a fourth worked instance to `HINGE-as-method` — after the argument, the
history, and the code — from 2026-08-23.

Aaron proposed making geometry the root of the soft regime and demoting the
Bayesian machinery to a fast approximation. Stated plainly that reads as a
rewrite. It is one joint: information geometry (Amari 1985) already says
exponential families are dually flat and their natural parameters ARE the flat
coordinates, which is why conjugate update in those coordinates is vector
addition. Grant that and the Bayesian layer was never beside the geometry — it
was a chart on it, written in statistics vocabulary. Nothing discarded, nothing
rebuilt.

The tell that it is the real joint: a day earlier an agent measuring
quantisation had found a quantile grid 2.8x worse than uniform by KL and wrote
that "every surveyed method minimises an L2 on point weights, none targets a
divergence" — an information-geometry observation made without reaching for the
name. The hinge was bearing load before anyone pointed at it.

Written as material for Aaron's voice, not in it, per the chapter's own note.
The chapter exists because he made the same observation last time the word came
back around.
