## Description

fix(ci): drift (loud) reported ITSELF as drift — a self-amplifying latch, caught in its own live output

Observed on gate run 32654127165, in the reporter's own table:

  | `drift (loud)` | flapping -- intermittent | job red, `gate (required)` green | 3 | 2 | 66.7% | 0 |

The fold is not wrong. `drift (loud)` is a non-floor job that fails beside a green
rollup, which is the definition of an absorbed failure — the definition is right and the
reading is useless.

WHY IT HAD TO GO. Its red X *is* the drift display, so a row saying "the drift display
went red" reports one event twice rather than adding information. And it is a LATCH, not
a measurement: every loud run raises its own failure rate, so it converges on SUSTAINED
regardless of whether any real drift exists. An `::error::` that is always present is an
`::error::` nobody reads — the reporter would have drowned out the signals it was built
to carry, while looking like evidence for them.

NOT SILENCING, and the distinction is the whole justification. Nothing is made quieter:
`drift (loud)` and `drift-canary` keep their own conclusions, their own annotations and
their own place in the checks list, and both stay in the CENSUS — `assertDetectorLive`
still goes red when the canary is missing. What is removed is a self-referential row
that measures the measuring and whose only effect on a reader is to raise the noise
floor.

`isCanary` therefore generalises to `isInstrument`, with the rule written down for
anything added to it later: a subject belongs on that list only when its failure IS the
reporting mechanism firing. Anything whose failure is an independent event stays a
finding, however noisy. The falsifier for the exclusion is a test that a genuinely
SUSTAINED subject beside the reporter is still annotated at `::error::` — the exclusion
must remove the self-reference and nothing else.

This is the third defect the live surface has produced in a day, and none of the three
was reachable by reading the code: the frozen ledger, the API's step-conclusion blind
spot, and now this. That is the argument for the whole design — a surface you have to
run to trust.

Verified against run 32654127165: the self-row is gone, the canary stays out of the
alarm and in the census, detector live via the annotation channel, and exit 1 is
preserved for the genuine PUBLICATION NOT LANDING defect
(081M0QPZD9C087G0R002W8QC2A). 44 falsifiers; repo typecheck clean.
