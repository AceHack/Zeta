## The three sharpest results

**Q3 refutes "Bayes is a fast approximation of a geometric root" for the conjugate lane.** By Q2
the conjugate update *is* translation in the e-affine chart, so the approximation error is
**exactly zero** — Bayes is not approximating that geometry, it is an isomorphism onto it.
"Retrofit as optimization" is then a **modelling change wearing an efficiency label**, which is
what the register rules exist to catch. The label is correct only for the *non-conjugate* lane,
where the error is `D_KL(p ‖ Π_M p)` with an additive Pythagorean decomposition (Amari & Nagaoka
Thm 3.8/3.9).

**Convexity is coordinate-relative, with a witness.** Normal-Gamma superlevel sets are **not**
convex in the `(μ, τ)` chart — `A = (−6.00, 0.25)` at `log f = −6.829442` and `B = (−1.00, 6.00)`
at `−6.312361`, midpoint at `−20.556474`, 13.7 nats below both — and **are half-space preimages**
in sufficient-statistic coordinates (checked to `5.7e-14`). Convexity is *affine*, not metric, and
not diffeomorphism-invariant, so a Gärdenfors claim has **no truth value** until the quality
dimensions are fixed. A dually flat manifold offers two affine structures; Fisher–Rao gives a third.

**Q5 is the vacuity class as stated.** `LinguisticSeed.indicator` sends every point to a distinct
orthonormal basis vector, so **every subset of every set is convex** — no categorisation of
English, natural or adversarial, can fail. Infinite-dimensionality is *not* the obstruction (a
correction to the framing): affine convexity is an ambient-space statement and the feature image is
thin. Repair: prototype/Voronoi **model comparison**, falsifiable, and **explicitly not a proof
obligation**.

## What this does *not* do

- **The root does not inherit the measured layer's confidence.** NG4 is measured (max KL 4.0e-8,
  0 invalid decodes, ten falsifiers); the geometric root has zero implementation and three of five
  answers are negative. The defensible version of the inversion is smaller and *is* supported by
  every measurement on file: **root the metric** (Fisher–Rao, canonical by Čencov), **not the
  update** (already affine, already earned).
- **Aaron's *"I see English geometrically in my head"* is not treated as evidence.** Authoritative
  about his experience, zero weight for the formal claim — and an excellent generator, which is
  what produced Q5's real result.
