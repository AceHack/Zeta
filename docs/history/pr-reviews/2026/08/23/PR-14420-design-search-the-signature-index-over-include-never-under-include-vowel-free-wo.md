## Description

## What this is

Aaron specified a **deliberate divergence from Lucene**. This PR is the measured design, plus the one piece cheap and safe to ship now: **the key function and its falsifiers**. The index build, spell-check pre-filter, naming registry and code index are **filed, not built**.

Design doc: `docs/research/2026-08-23-signature-index-over-includes-never-under-includes-vowel-free-word-keys-order-free-phrases-two-tier-cascade.md`

## The contract — the point, and not a performance story

> **Tier 1 MAY over-include. Tier 1 MUST NEVER under-include. Tier 2 may reject freely.**

A filter that admits false positives and refuses false negatives **cannot produce a false zero** — the exact defect this work-item exists to remove, since a `grep` reported **0 files** for a term that is in **447**. An under-including index reproduces that failure at speed; an over-including one makes it structurally impossible.

Tier 1 is allowed to be **imprecise**. It is *not* allowed to be **approximately right**.

## Order-freeness is at the WORD level — and the correction was priced

My first reading was character-level (an anagram key). Aaron corrected it; the measurement says what the correction was worth:

| key | unique-key rate | candidate inflation p90 | max class |
|---|---|---|---|
| **word-level (shipped)** | **93.0%** | **1.00** | 15 |
| character-level `sortChars` | 59.5% | **26.00** | 130 |
| character-level, no threshold | 56.4% | **113.25** | 160 |

A word's letters keep their order; a **phrase** is the *set* of its word signatures. `ZSet` and `WSet` never collide.

## The threshold is measured and has a knee

Predicate on **surviving consonants**, not input length — `audio`, `queue`, `eerie` pass a length test and degenerate anyway.

| `minSurvivingConsonants` | unique-key | inflation p90 | max class | cand % corpus p90 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0 | 83.9% | 4.14 | 151 | 0.22% |
| 3 | 87.7% | 1.40 | 39 | 0.07% |
| **4 (shipped)** | **93.0%** | **1.00** | **15** | **0.05%** |
| 6 | 98.5% | 1.00 | 7 | 0.03% |

**Viability:** the candidate set is **0.003% of the corpus at the median, 0.05% at p90** — 1 to 16 documents of 33,193. The "filter returns 40% of the tree" failure is **four orders of magnitude** away.

**Soundex's keep-the-first-letter rule was checked and DECLINED** — 93.0% → 94.4% does not pay for the special case, and it is a claim about English surnames, not about code and technical prose.

## Two things I got to only by measuring

**The degenerate case is not empty.** Aaron guessed *"maybe there are none"*. There are **151** all-vowel tokens after stop-word removal, and **`ai` alone is in 5,208 files**. Mapping them to the empty key would have merged every one. The threshold rule already covers them — the exception he suspected was unnecessary is load-bearing.

**Collision classes ARE typo classes.** I printed the collision *cost* and got the *mechanism*:

```
rsrch  research reserch reserach resarch researach reseearch reaserch …
lngg   language langague langauge languag languague langugae lanugage …
```

Verified on typos absent from the corpus: `determinstic` → **deterministic(11,981)**, `idempotencu` → **idempotence(185)**, `seperate` → **separate(3,026)**.

**Honest limit, pinned by a test:** the signature absorbs vowel substitution and transposition. It does **not** absorb consonant deletion, insertion or doubling — `occurance`/`occurrence`, `reponse`/`response` are not caught.

## DV2.0 in etymological space is the SCHEMA, not a framing

Concepts are **hubs**, names are **satellites**, and a **hot-spot satellite** is an un-extracted abstraction. Aaron's sharpening: **semantic distance defines the UNIT; change-rate still defines the PARTITION** — you measure DV2.0 churn over a *cluster*, not a token. So the rule needs a **coarser unit, not a new metric**, and hubs can be *discovered* by clustering rather than declared.

Then he inverted the obvious constraint. Measuring churn over a cluster is a pushforward, so the natural move is to require coherence and refuse when violated. Instead:

> **Disagreement is not an obstacle — it is the measurement.**

Which is the move `anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability.md` already makes for monodromy: *"that difference is information, not error."* The payoff is a third diagnosis and it is **the one that rule actually needs**: uniformly-low churn = a settled concept; uniformly-high = one still forming; **disagreeing = a rename in progress**. That rule's concern is drift becoming irreconcilable, and drift is currently only visible after the fact. This is the difference between a post-mortem and an instrument.

**Registered as `proposed`, not measured.** What follows from the schema is the *ability* to compute it. No churn metric is invented. And the refuter is named: if disagreeing churn tracks **file churn or author count** rather than anything etymological, the table is measuring the repo's commit pattern and calling it culture — a cheap confound that must be excluded first.

The **rule** change is **filed, not made here** — `.claude/rules/` is startup-loaded and razored.

## Prior art — checked, not merely cited

**Faloutsos & Christodoulakis (1984)** signature files — the tradition, *and* why its historical defeat by inverted files turned on a cost axis (disk scan) we are not on. **Bloom (1970)**, **Orenstein (1986)** filter-and-refine. **Soundex (1918)** / Metaphone, and the abjad lineage. **Damerau (1964)** / **Levenshtein (1966)** / **Norvig** / **SymSpell**. **Russ Cox (2012)** + **`zoekt`** — code search converged *independently* on the same two-tier shape with a different `sig`, which is why §8's abstraction is real rather than a tidy story. **Deißenböck & Pizka (2005)** — one concept one name / one name one concept, the model behind the registry as an **abstraction detector**. **Lucene stays** as the contrast case.

## Unchanged, and it outranks all of the above

The artifact records its **git rev**, and the query **refuses rather than answering from a stale corpus**. An over-including index that silently answers from the wrong tree is still a false zero — just a better-engineered one.
