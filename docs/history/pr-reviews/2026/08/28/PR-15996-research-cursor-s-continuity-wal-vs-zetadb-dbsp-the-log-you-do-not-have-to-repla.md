## Adopt unchanged

**The DAG must not live behind a network hop.** Their strongest evidence is a *negative* result — Shawn Pearce's JGit/DHT attempt at Google worked but died on clone latency, because a per-object round trip turns a graph walk into a latency chain. A documented failure by a competent team is rarer and more informative than a success story, and it is a direct warning for **ZetaFS**: "content-addressed, so put every node in the object store" is obviously right for durability and obviously wrong for traversal.

**Source of truth in object storage, nodes as cache** — because it makes *routing stop being state*. No repo→node table, no repair jobs, no "pets not cattle".

## Where Z-sets + DBSP is a different answer

A WAL's entries **are not commutative** — which is precisely why Continuity needs an S3 CAS as a linearization point. Z-sets are commutative, associative and invertible by construction (retraction is `-1`, not a tombstone), so:

- **Compaction leaves the restore path.** Continuity must compact *because* full restore replays every entry. A DBSP materialised view is maintained by deltas, so the view *is* the compacted state, continuously. Their periodic expensive job becomes our steady state.
- **The ordering requirement dissolves** for the commutative remainder — Aaron's "avoid consensus everywhere".

## What it deliberately does not claim

A Git **ref update is a compare-and-swap by nature**: "move `main` from A to B, fail if not at A" is conditional, and no CRDT makes it associative. Where Zeta needs those semantics it needs a linearization point too. The win is **shrinking the set of operations requiring coordination**, not eliminating it.

And there is **no benchmark**. We have no number beside their 120 pushes/s (S3 Standard) or 300+/s (S3 Express One Zone). The compaction argument is about shape and asymptotics, and says so.

## Generalised

§4 extracts the tail-at-scale arithmetic: with `r` replicas and `k` round trips you take `r·k` chances at the latency tail, which is why adding read replicas *degrades* writes under 3PC. That is a standing check to run against our own fan-out-and-wait paths.

**Anchors:** Budiu et al. (DBSP, VLDB 2023) · Shapiro et al. (CRDTs, 2011) · Dean & Barroso (*The Tail at Scale*, CACM 2013) · Merkle (CRYPTO '87).
