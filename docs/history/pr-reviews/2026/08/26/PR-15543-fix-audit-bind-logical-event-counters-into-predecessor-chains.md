## Summary

Strengthens event-minted zero-crossing audit identity with:

- a logical per-emitter counter, explicitly not a wall-clock timestamp;
- a deterministic emitter genesis hash and predecessor-hash link;
- a hash-minted event identity binding emitter, sequence, predecessor, and content fingerprint;
- fail-closed detection when two visible events claim one logical counter;
- an out-of-order diagnostic that keeps a valid child unresolved until its predecessor arrives.

The revision cites the repository's existing rules: a sameness detector cannot mint identity, and local time never enters the shared fold.

## Non-claims
