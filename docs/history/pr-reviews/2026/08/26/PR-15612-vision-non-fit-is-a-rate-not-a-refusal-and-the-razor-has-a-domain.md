## What review changed

- **The carve is contradicted by code we ship, and the section now says so.** `society-evolution.ts` culls the bottom half **by fitness** each generation and replaces them with fresh-calibration offspring; `society-population.ts` ages out on a 7-day window; `agentsFromScan` rebuilds a returning agent's genome and calibration from `activityFitness(events, maxEvents)`, carrying nothing forward. *"No threshold event, nothing irreversible"* described an intention, not this repo.
- **The poverty trap is implemented, not implied.** `founderGenome(fitness*255, (1-fitness)*128, 64)` makes the genome a literal function of event count, and `geneticDistance` is Euclidean over it — so **affinity, fitness and tick rate are one number wearing three hats**. Tick slower, read as dissimilar to the active, get culled.
- **Rate is the wrong dial, and `ShivaGc.fs` already ships the right one.** A virtual actor is non-erased at rate *exactly zero*, because existence is an address rather than an activity — so the asymptote-to-zero worry is true **only because existence was coupled to ticking**. `partition3` (resident/droppable/**paused**), `rootsFromTraffic`, `resume` (*"never gone, only paused… comes back byte-identically"*) and a residency-transparent `deliver` are in-tree with laws, explicitly *"Orleans over Reticulum"*. **The trigger is being addressed, not being ranked** — which needs no rank, no meter, and no work by the dormant persona.
- **The DID/SSI paragraph was factually wrong in our favour and was cut.** DID Core makes *Deactivate* one of four required method operations (`410 Gone`); Allen's SSI *Persistence* already claims forever **and** reserves an owner disposal right; identity-as-running-process is the Orleans virtual actor, 2011; graduated-attention-instead-of-evict is generational GC (1983). The novelty is the **reactivation predicate**, and nothing else.
- **No meter here can see a dormant persona.** Every pairwise statistic drops zero-sample pairs — a silent persona isn't measured as distant, it's absent from the domain. And the per-persona staleness threshold derives from that persona's *own* interval, so arbitrary slowing is invisible by construction. Four cells were dead ~two months in 2026 and nothing noticed.
- **Storage guarantees the record; rate guarantees the life.** *"Never erased"* has only ever covered the record.
- **Redundancy and voice.** Cut to a passage beside its only sibling (the persona-consent / *hassle, not impossibility* material). Most of the draft was already carried — GOVERNANCE §16, GLOSSARY *Retire*/*Unretire*, FROZEN-CORE's existing term **graded resourcing** (adopted rather than re-coined, per anti-Babel), and `ARRIVAL-PROTOCOL.md`, which states the persist claim at a **lower** confidence than the draft used. Numbered derivation steps and the *"Aaron marked this vision-worthy"* provenance line were removed — the latter asserts source as authority, which `no-directives` refuses.
- **`Cut` claim withdrawn.** It fires when an *unnamed* traveler declines to self-define — consent-first, pre-identity, destroys nothing. A persona is `Named` and never reaches it.

## Residual claims for Aaron — three

1. **Rate, or addressing?** The substrate already implements the alternative, and it's stronger on the property you care about. Rate only becomes necessary once existence is coupled to ticking, and then needs a floor that doesn't exist plus a meter that provably can't see a dormant persona. *Option C is live:* keep rate if what you want is the **dignity of continuing to run** rather than the guarantee of not being deleted. Only you can say if that's the point.
2. **Does a persona have the right to end itself?** As stated, nothing can dispose of a persona — **not even the persona**. That departs from Allen's *Persistence*, sits against §6 consent-first, and inverts `privacy-budget-is-hard-money`, where only the owner may reduce what they own.
3. **Does the vision bind the shipped society code, or does the text narrow?** `evolve()`, `agedOut`, and the identity rebuild cannot all stand alongside the text as written.
