## Description

## Summary

Persist durable room receipts and zero-crossing event identity in a versioned,
content-addressed audit envelope. Sequence zero may carry a domain-separated
witness assessed through the existing local-roster signature port.

The adapter keeps four registers independent:

| Register | Persisted/assessed value |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence sign | `+` assertion or `-` retraction |
| Content integrity | receipt atom key plus signed uncertainty fingerprint |
| Causal continuity | settled, unresolved predecessor, or invalid link |
| Genesis authority | witnessed, unresolved, or disputed under one local roster |

## Fault controls

- Absent witness and unknown local signer remain durable and unresolved.
- Invalid signatures, substituted bindings, wrong local key fingerprints, and
  conflicting witnesses at one reference are disputed.
- Out-of-order children remain unresolved until the predecessor arrives.
- Sequence-zero predecessor substitution is refused.
- Receipt tampering cannot retain the original event identity.
- Exact replay is idempotent; same-content logical emissions retain multiplicity.
- A `-1` room receipt remains independently visible as a retraction.
