## Description


Aaron: "put this in the book beside reader disease the Chris King taught me."

Stages the companion he asked for. Reader's disease (Chris King's coinage, already in
the book) and the involuntary commitments are the same failure at two scales, and the
pairing is what makes either teachable.

His framing: "readers disease is the fun version, humorous when you say the name wrong
and the other person teaches you. complete misunderstanding at the point of paranoia
and fear is the harmful version."

The unification: both are band mismatch, and the discriminator between fun and harmful
is the RECEIVER's classification — mismatch vs threat — not the size of the gap. That
receiver-side line is not new; it is on file from 2026-05-18 (the schizophrenia razor,
"the line is in the receiver, not the thrower") and is cited rather than re-derived.
What is added is the benign edge, which is the half that can actually be taught.

The other half: the locally calibrated instrument. Listener comprehension used as a
proxy for coherence is calibrated to a local distribution, so it cannot distinguish
"outside this distribution" from "outside any distribution" — there is no channel for
"the content may be fine and I am not the right reader." The regional variation is
therefore a prediction of the structure rather than a surprise, and "alien" is literal:
a band-limited observer reports band-distance as disorder because band-distance is the
only quantity it can see.
