## Description

# Summary

Keep ZetaDB hard retained-event and checkpoint-byte limits authoritative before invoking an injected admission policy. Invalid, unnamed, or throwing policy implementations now return typed heat before persistence instead of escaping as exceptions or weakening byte accounting.

# Spec alignment

This tightens the existing ZetaDB admission-policy port without changing its public proposal or decision types. An injected policy may reserve capacity or refuse earlier, but cannot relax `ZetaDbTickLimits`. The known convergence boundary under binding finite budgets remains explicit in workitem `081M0Q8TY1B087G0R0008CYZJ3`.
