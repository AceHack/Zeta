## Description

D5 lands: the Stump-Dad button, wired end to end on the generator #14553
merged. Click the agent (its screen, or the WHY? chip in the header), get
one sentence; click again, the next reason down; the chain saturates at
"I don't know." and the click past the terminal closes the strip. A cart
switch (cycle jumping backwards) resets the chain.

The spec's two hard constraints, enforced by construction and by test:

- **Generated from the state that actually drove the decision.** The
  predictor assembles `whyContext()` from its own live deciding state —
  the latch's mode and bucket, the learner's posterior means and reward
  count, the self↔adversary relation **stored at the decision site** (not
  recomputed later), explore progress, and the fixation tile with its
  current predictive variance. That object rides the SAME postMessage as
  the frame it explains, and the strip regenerates its sentence every
  frame from that frame's own payload — never a cache, never a string
  table.
- **The chain must reach "I don't know."** — payload-level acceptance
  tests drive the flee scenario to a real latched decision and assert:
  the context IS the deciding state (same mode/bucket/relation/fixation
  the latch used), it survives `structuredClone` (the worker boundary)
  verbatim, the chain generated FROM THE CLONE terminates at the
  terminal, and every non-terminal answer cites a number that
  round-trips to the payload.

Caught live in the headless smoke, and fixed with a value-conditional
frame rather than a shrug: a settled field's fixation rung read "tile 20
keeps surprising me (variance 0.00)" — a sentence contradicting its own
number. It now says what the state says: "My gaze rests on tile 20 —
nothing on screen is surprising me right now (variance 0.00)." A test
pins both branches.

The full chain, clicked live in the built app (15 s in, FLEE latched on
mutual-sim):

1. "I am opening the gap from the big shape — it is 5.39 pixels away and
   the gap is not shrinking."
2. "In this context, hunting has been worth -0.20 to me and fleeing 0.20
   — I take the better one."
3. "Those worths are still my starting guess — I have read 0 score
   changes off the board so far."
4. "My gaze rests on tile 20 — nothing on screen is surprising me right
   now (variance 0.00)."
5. "Why the score should matter to me at all — that is where my reasons
   stop."
6. "I don't know." → next click closes.

Disclosure discipline (spec §8) holds: every rung states what the
agent's own state IS; nothing infers a why about anyone else.
