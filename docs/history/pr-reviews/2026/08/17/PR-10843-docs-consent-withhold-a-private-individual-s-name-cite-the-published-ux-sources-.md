## What

A private individual was **asked directly** whether her name could be used in this repo. She declined: she is **bound by employer confidentiality**. The boundary she and Aaron drew is a **split, not a blanket ban** — the **personal relation may be named; the professional / technical-source attribution may not**.

The repo used her name in exactly the disallowed way: as the named source of the non-biased qualia-elicitation method. This PR removes that attribution and **replaces it with the published sources**, which is where a checkable anchor belonged anyway.

She is not named in this PR, in any commit, or in any edited file.

- **Tullis & Albert, *Measuring the User Experience*** (Morgan Kaufmann, 2008) — Tom Tullis was VP of UX Research at Fidelity Investments (1993–2017, now deceased; UXPA carries an In Memoriam).

- How the method was encountered is now stated without naming anyone: **she encouraged Aaron to read published work by colleagues of hers** — only published material passed, deliberately, to avoid insider information.

**The citation's scope is stated, because it is narrower than the claim.** The old "⚠ author to confirm" flag is resolved as a **checked** anchor: Aaron has read the book and confirms it makes a **measurement-methodology** claim only.
