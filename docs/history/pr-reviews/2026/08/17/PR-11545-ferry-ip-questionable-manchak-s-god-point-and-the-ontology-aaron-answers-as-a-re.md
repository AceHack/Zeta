ferry(ip-questionable): Manchak's God point, and the ontology Aaron answers as a relationalist

Continuation of the Manchak ferry, preserving Aaron's forwarded segment
(~57:28-1:00:34) verbatim and recording three observations with their
registers named.

**The God point.** Manchak's is an event whose past light cone is the
entire universe including its own future, and he EXCLUDES it by
hypothesis because his unknowability results do not survive it. We have
one -- already named as such in
`2026-06-08-staged-coincidence-gan-the-dst-harness-is-the-omniscient-observer.md`.
The load-bearing detail is that it sits OUTSIDE the simulated universe:
inside, no agent has total access, so unknowability holds for agents;
outside, the harness does, which is exactly why replay yields knowledge
no in-universe agent could have. That yields a checkable constraint
rather than a metaphor -- if any in-universe component ever gains
God-point access, the guarantees we rely on inside collapse.

**The ontology.** Manchak declines the substantivalist/relationalist
fork; Aaron does not. "those events are the agent communications and
changes interactions with external interfaces ... everything is pair
wise relative memory between each other" is Leibniz, not Newton: no
independently-existing points, only relations. The GR-to-Zeta mapping
is tabulated and LABELLED AN ANALOGY -- nothing in it is proved, and
what would promote it is naming which structure on the memory relation
plays the metric's role and showing it has a metric's properties.

Three statements of the same position by one person are recorded as
consistent, not corroborated -- one observation, not three
(numerology-vs-number-theory, the too-many-correlations guard).

**A standing correction carried forward.** The MH section of this
document said a continuation's antecedent "could have an infinite
future in it passed through as an immutable value." Measured since:
`Chip8SelfSim.Tests.fs` T4 confirms the immutable/COW half, and T3
-- "the future is simulated within a METERED budget (funded steps
only)" -- refutes the infinite half for shipped code. The route by
which an infinite future could legitimately be carried is aperiodicity
plus undecidability (Berger 1966, Cook 2004), which is under formal
analysis and is not a result.
