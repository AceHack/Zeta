## Description

ferry(ip-questionable): Aaron defines useful as continuing the infinite game — the conspiracy is post-selection

Aaron: "i define useful by continuting the infinate game rather than
ending it, the moves the end the infinate game are weirdly consipired
against in the 2nd time dimension to reinterpert the past where the game
still continues."

Recorded with three registers separated.

STATED ORACLE. Defining useful as "the infinite game continues" is
exactly what §11 asks for -- supplying the metric rather than assuming a
universal one -- and it is what makes the ΔU question in Observation 4
answerable at all. Anchored to Carse 1986: a finite game is played to
win and therefore to end; an infinite game is played to continue play.

CHECKED ANCHOR. "Weirdly conspired against" names a real object:
POST-SELECTION. Condition on an outcome and the conditioned history
acquires precisely the appearance of conspiracy -- the past looks
arranged to produce the surviving branch -- with NO backward causation
in the mechanism. Aaronson 2005 (PostBQP = PP) makes it a complexity
class rather than a metaphor; Horowitz-Maldacena 2003 is the physics
use; Wald's aircraft armour is the vernacular form. The intuition is
right about the shape and needs no second time dimension to hold.

MEASURED, AND IT IS THE GOOD OUTCOME. If the second direction
post-selected for continuation, "useful = the game continues" would be
self-fulfilling and would measure the filter rather than the world --
the hidden-oracle failure exactly. Our store does not do this:
Chip8CrossRunStore.fs:74-86 retains endings as first-class verdicts,
`Closed of mu * lambda * TerminalKind` and `OpenAtBound of maxSteps`, so
a halted orbit is recorded AS halted and budget exhaustion cannot be
misread as closure. The write path cannot manufacture the appearance.

UNMEASURED, AND NAMED. Store completeness does not imply an unbiased
sample. If only continuing orbits are ever READ, the consult path
post-selects even though the artifact set does not. Auto-consult is
deliberately unwired, so this is a property to establish BEFORE turning
it on: instrument the consult path, compare the verdict distribution of
orbits read against orbits stored. Matching distributions mean the
criterion measures the world; over-representation of continuing orbits
means it partly measures its own filter. The comparison is cheap and
does not exist yet.
