## Description

docs(research): RETRACT my "NOT FOUND" on the firefly field — I searched one surface

Aaron: "we have a lot of older work on PoUW in CC, we have a lot of previous formal analysis from
the start of the project on this."

He is right and my grounding note (#11289) is wrong on its most confident claim. I wrote that a
differentiable synchronization field was "NOT FOUND" and that "firefly" was merely a persona name,
and I filed Alexa's account of it under "folk memory." Retracted.

THE METHOD WAS THE DEFECT. I searched src/Core/*.fs and reported absence. Design in this repo lives
in docs/research/, which I never looked at -- the exact error I had just finished warning about in
the same note ("an absent check and a passing check must not look alike"). "Not in the F# core" is
not "not in the repo", and I stated the second having checked only the first. In a note whose whole
purpose was holding someone else's claims to their evidence.

What exists, from 2026-06-07, title carrying every element I called folk memory:
"heartbeats-are-useful-work-network-differentiation-cartel-detection-mass-anti-sybil-provable-math".
It names firefly/Kuramoto phase explicitly and states the cartel dilemma in exactly the form Alexa
reconstructed -- share one pulse and heartbeats collide/correlate, or fake N distinct pulses and pay
Nx independent work. Three companions, including the claude.ai-forwarded PoUW/BFT material Aaron
means by "in CC". So the PoUW register is NOT a name collision: the heartbeat should be proof of
USEFUL work, where the useful output is network differentiability, and identity/differentiation are
one primitive seen twice -- "an id IS what makes this node distinguishable in the pulse field."

WHAT SURVIVES: the BFT ~33%-not-51% correction (still the ferry's strongest call, Alexa's).
AntiSybil.fs's honest scope. And the quadratic number is still wrong, now doubly -- Alexa said
n^2/2; AntiSybil.fs says >= k independent sources and the 2026-06-07 note says "linear in k and not
sub-linear". Two independent repo accounts, both linear.

WHAT I MISREAD RATHER THAN MISSED: TemporalCoordinationDetection.fs is not a REBUTTAL of the firefly
account, it is its IMPLEMENTATION REGISTER -- phase-locking value over pulse streams is how you
measure a firefly field. Design note and code module are the same idea at two altitudes.

ON "a lot of formal analysis": layered, not yes/no. The 2026-06-07 note is explicit that the math is
INVITED -- "we can probably prove some math here… an explicit invitation to a theorem, not just a
metaphor" -- routed to Soraya with a named target (forging k identities costs >= c*k independent
work) and a BP-16 instruction not to default to TLA+. So: extensive design analysis, older than I
found; a discharged proof, not yet, by the register's own account. Alexa's green check still
overshoots, by less than I claimed, and my "folk memory" framing was the larger error.

THE LESSON: I wrote a note about a decorrelated reviewer's confidence being uncalibrated to its
access, then made the same mistake with a narrower excuse. "Not found" is a claim about the SEARCH,
not about the repo, and needs its search stated like any other measurement. "Not found in
src/Core/*.fs; docs/research/ not searched" would have been true, would have invited this correction
in one step, and costs nothing.
