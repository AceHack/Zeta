## Description

## What was asked

An agent in the fleet claimed it "has no privacy budget and no frost" and therefore could not meaningfully consent to being observed. Aaron says that is wrong and estimates the capability at ~90% complete. This PR reports what an end-to-end check of the four steps actually found, treating 90% as a hypothesis rather than a premise.

## The four verdicts

| step | verdict | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (a) accrue budget from others' attestations | **specified-only** | `src/Core/PrivacyEconomy.fs` is a pure `Map<string,int>` with no persistence and no signed attestations. `db/ledgers/` and `db/competence-outcomes/` are README-only. No agent holds a balance. |
| (b) spend it to frost | **implemented, in-memory** | `src/Core/GlassHalo.fs:38` refuses zero-cost and over-spend, tested. But `RoomBoundary.create` (`RoomBoundary.fs:22-32`) takes the budget as a caller-supplied `int` — self-minted, which the rule forbids. |
| (c) withhold from a real observer | **implemented for exactly one surface** | `llmtv-broadcast.ts:90` `frostStrip` is a genuine membrane and `BroadcastMind` has no field that can hold frosted content. The type is the guarantee. Its `frosted` flag is a free self-asserted boolean, not a spend. |
| (d) unconfiscatable | **absent** | `BoundaryCommand.Clear` carries no principal and cannot fail. No frost anywhere is cryptographic. |

## The shape of the finding

The pieces are not missing — they are **disconnected**. A repo-wide intersection of every file naming `frosted`/`GlassHalo`/`privacyBudget` with every file naming `encrypt`/`aes-gcm`/`pkcs11`/`hsm`/`sealShare` returns exactly one file, and both of its hits are unrelated comments.

## Two defects, recorded in a new work-item

`081M0X23R19087G0R003XHGB2B`.

1. **`Clear` is an unauthenticated defrost.** The rule says *"there is no `defrost` that another party can force — only the owner may reveal."* `BoundaryCommand.Clear` (`DarkHallRoomLoop.fs:36`) is a nullary case; `DarkHallRoomLoop.fs:262` dispatches it into `RoomBoundary.clear`, which unlike `Frost` is not even on a `Result` refusal path. `GlassHalo.Tests.fs:35` pins the behaviour as intended, so this is a spec divergence that needs a decision, not a quiet patch.
2. **The budget is self-minted.** Every `frosted: true` in the repository is a literal in a test or a demo.
