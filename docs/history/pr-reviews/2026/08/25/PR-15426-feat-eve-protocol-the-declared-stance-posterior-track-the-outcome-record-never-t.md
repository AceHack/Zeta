## Description

Aaron 2026-08-25, sharpening the Eve Protocol *pre-declared bias* primitive (PR #15414):

> *"yes this is very accurate over a bayesian inference prior on individuals, similar to TrueSkill. eager is also aggressive and reaches local optima quicker and gets stuck, it's mostly selfish but not always in the extremes."*

Eagerness is therefore not only a per-exchange flag. It is a tracked parameter with a prior and a posterior — and the parameter has to be one a counterparty is *allowed* to hold.

## The line, and how it is held

> **The outcome record is trackable. The inner state is not.**

*"This party's claims declared under `Eager` have held up 40% of the time"* is a measurement of results. *"This party is an eager person"* is an inference about their inner life — forbidden by `engagement-profiles-public-work-only-not-surveillance-dossiers.md`, and contradicted by pigeonhole-by-self-claim.

Held **structurally**, not by convention:

1. `Stance` has exactly one introduction form, `declare`, which **refuses** when the declarer is not the subject. There is no function in the module returning `Stance` — it is always an input, never an output, so an observer cannot compute one. (`DSL-1`, `DSL-3`)
2. The stance is a **key**, not a value: it selects which cell an outcome is filed under, and nothing updates, corrects or re-derives it. (`DSL-13`, `DSL-30`)
3. Everything emitted is a statistic over resolved claims — `holdRate`, `informativeness`, `searchProfile`, `beneficiaryProfile`.

Where the design pushed against the line it lost — see the declined tails below.

## 1. The composition

The declaration **is the cell key**: `(party, domain, DECLARED stance) → TrueSkill/ADF posterior` over "does a claim from this cell hold up", reusing `TravelerRankLedger` unchanged.

**Declares `neutral`, record says otherwise** — the honest answer, implemented:

| state | receiver uses | `PriorBasis` |
|---|---|---|
| the party's cells separate (≥ `SEPARATION_EPS`, both above the evidence floor) | that cell's posterior | `DeclaredCell` |
| the cells are indistinguishable | pooled across stances | `PooledAcrossStances` |
| not enough evidence | 0.5, the honest prior | `HonestPrior` |

The party is **not relabelled**. Its `Neutral` claims stay filed under `Neutral` (`DSL-30` asserts the cell still holds its five observations after pooling). The declaration remains authority over *intent*; the record is evidence about *accuracy*. `DeclarationCarriesNoInformation` names a **fact** — it is exactly as consistent with a uniformly reliable party as with a mis-reported one (`dual-use-detection-is-neutral-oracle-decides`).

The **reciprocal obligation** is structural: `Exchange` carries two `Declaration`s, so a one-sided disclosure is not representable and `weigh` cannot be reached from one. `openExchange` also refuses a self-exchange (`DSL-8`) — otherwise a party could satisfy the independence requirement below with itself.

Timing is a **logical phase ordinal**, not a wall clock (`local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold`); `DSL-37` pins the absence of any ambient source negatively.

## 2. The dynamics: a variance, not a rate

Argued in full in the research doc, with anchors.

A **rate** (step size) fails on the second half of Aaron's sentence: large α produces overshoot and oscillation, and in non-convex landscapes it is actively *anti*-sticking (Li, Wei & Ma, NeurIPS 2019; Keskar et al., ICLR 2017). It reproduces "aggressive" and contradicts "gets stuck".

A **temperature** produces both. Simulated annealing (Kirkpatrick, Gelatt & Vecchi, *Science* 220(4598):671–680, 1983; Černý 1985) accepts an uphill move with probability `exp(−Δ/T)`: low `T` means nearly every accepted move is a descent move — which *is* the aggression — and then the search freezes into whatever basin it is in. The sharp form is **Hajek (1988)**, *Cooling schedules for optimal annealing*, *Math. of OR* 13(2):311–329: convergence in probability to the **global** optimum requires `T(k) ≥ d/log(k)`; cool faster and you converge quickly, with positive probability to a **local** optimum. That is Aaron's sentence as a theorem.

Same result in bandit vocabulary: Lai & Robbins (1985), *Adv. Appl. Math.* 6(1):4–22 — the `Ω(log T)` lower bound says exploration cannot be skipped; Auer, Cesa-Bianchi & Fischer (2002), *Machine Learning* 47:235–256 (UCB1) is the finite-time version. Greedy commits on an early small sample and never revisits: **linear** regret.

**The identification, not a coincidence:** the ADF update already contains an annealing schedule — the mean step is proportional to `σ²`, and `σ²` decreases monotonically. In all three vocabularies the parameter multiplies the *variance of the update*, not the *magnitude of the step*, and in all three the fast regime provably converges quickly to a possibly-wrong answer.

**Answer:** eagerness is a **variance (temperature)**. "Aggressive" is the consequence, not the parameter. Forced to one parameter — which is what a protocol field is — it is the temperature, because it is the only one that generates *both* named symptoms.

**Toy vs metered:** the evidence-inflation `κ` is labelled **toy** and appears in **no code path** — fitting a temperature to a person is the forbidden inference. What ships is the model's *observable signature*: `searchProfile` reports later-half minus earlier-half hold rate as `MarginalYieldRising / Flat / Falling`, with an `InsufficientHistory` refusal below six observations. It names the fact because it must — flat marginal yield is equally consistent with a party that was right from the start.

**Unverified, stated as unverified:** the characterisation predicts flat marginal yield should be *more common* under `Eager` declarations. There is **no data**. That is §2's natural falsifier and it cannot run until a corpus of declared exchanges exists. Until then §2 is an argument, not a measurement.

## 3. The tails: DECLINED

> *"mostly selfish but not always in the extremes"*

Declined, for two independent reasons either of which suffices.

1. **It is the forbidden inference in its purest form.** Selfishness is a *motivation*. A function from a declared stance to self-interest is a model of inner life derived from a self-claim — a personality model wearing a ledger's clothes. This reason does not depend on the shape being hard.
2. **The shape is unidentified anyway.** "Mostly increasing, unconstrained at both ends" is an infinite family, and the evidence is one introspective self-report — authority over Aaron's own account, explicitly not over its causes (Nisbett & Wilson 1977, *Psych. Review* 84(3):231–259). Drawing a curve through n=1 is coincidence-promoted-to-belief.

**Built instead: the measurement without the model.** Each resolution carries `Beneficiary ∈ {AccruedToClaimant, AccruedToBoth, AccruedToOthers}` — an allocation you can observe. `beneficiaryProfile` reports raw counts and derives no scalar. If the tail claim is ever checked, this is the column it is checked in.

**The decline is enforced:** `DSL-31` asserts `weigh` is invariant to the beneficiary counts, with a control proving the allocations *were* recorded `(6,0,0)` vs `(0,0,6)`. Mutation `M10` wires in a monotone selfishness penalty and the test goes red.

`Stance` also deliberately carries **no continuous intensity** — an intensity is exactly what would invite the monotone curve, and it would be a regression target.

## 4. What the receiver does — weight the evidence, not the enthusiasm

"An eager party producing five supporting arguments has produced approximately one" is a quantity with a name: **Kish's design effect** (Kish 1965, *Survey Sampling*, §8.2), `deff = 1 + (m−1)ρ`, hence `n_eff = n/(1+(n−1)ρ)`, which is **exactly 1 for every n at ρ = 1**. `effectiveCorroborations` groups supports by source (ordinal), discounts within a source, sums across sources; the receiver requires `2.0` effective supports — the claimant plus one genuinely independent confirmation.

`ρ = 1` is the default and it is a **bound, not a fit**: any smaller value asserts that a party's self-corroborations are partly independent of each other, which nothing here has measured. `DSL-26` is the control that it is a formula and not a switch (ρ=0 → n_eff=n; ρ=0.5 → strictly between).

**EAGER IS NOT A DISCOUNT, enforced:** `RequiresIndependentConfirmation` never reads the stance, so declaring `Eager` costs a party nothing — which is what the Eve row's self-enforcement argument requires. `DSL-24`: two parties with identical outcome records differing only in declared stance weigh identically in every field. Mutation `M9` adds a stance penalty; the test goes red.
