## The first path forward

A ~40-line F# adapter (`ShivaGcCas.fs`, compiled after `ZetaFs.fs`) projecting a `ContentStore`-backed tree into a `ShivaGc` heap, wired first to `ZetaToolStore` — where `FsUnlink` (`ZetaToolStore.fs:89`) and `editLocal` orphan a content node on every call and nothing reclaims. Five falsifiers named; the *sharing* one fails under the most likely wrong implementation. Two honest limits are stated rather than hidden: `DagFs` is flat so `mark` degenerates there, and `DagFs.Tree` is private so a two-line accessor is required.
