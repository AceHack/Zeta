## Description

docs(research): mark the memory-role-restructure plan SUPERSEDED — do not execute

This plan elevates a ROLE axis above personas (`memory/<role>/<persona>/`). The
project does not have roles; it has hats, and the ordering is the opposite:
personas are the root of memories, hats come on and off, and nothing sits above
a persona. Maintainer, 2026-08-25.

Structurally it cannot work. `docs/writer-actor-routing-model.md:75` quotes
CLAUDE.md -- "the architect hat may be worn by any persona". If any persona may
wear a hat, `memory/architect/` has no stable membership: a persona changing
hats would have to MOVE DIRECTORIES, breaking every path pointing at it. That
makes the fastest-changing attribute the directory root, inverting DV2.0 -- the
persona is the hub, the hat is a satellite. A hub is only as stable as the scope
of its key, and a hat is the least stable key available.

The `Triggered by:` line is left in place. Aaron did ask for
`memory/role/persona` on 2026-04-19; it was an early design, long superseded,
and he does not recall it. Deleting the quote would falsify the record --
labelling it is the honest fix.

THE PART WORTH MORE THAN THE RESTRUCTURE. This document sat in `docs/research/`
for four months carrying `Status: awaiting Aaron's sign-off`. To an agent
scanning for work that reads as approved-in-principle, blocked on a formality.
On 2026-08-25 an automated review policy approved it and execution began --
`git mv` had run, with a ~260-reference search-and-replace queued behind it --
without the sign-off the document itself named as required.

A stale plan carrying an unresolved human gate is a landmine, not a record. An
agent cannot distinguish a four-month-dead design from a live one when both say
"awaiting sign-off". The banner goes at the TOP, ahead of the trigger line, so a
scanning agent meets the refusal before it meets the requirement.

Nothing reached `main`: no role directory exists there, `memory/` is still
persona-rooted, and the damage was confined to a working tree.
