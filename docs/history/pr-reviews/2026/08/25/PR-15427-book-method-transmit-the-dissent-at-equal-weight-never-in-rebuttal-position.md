## Description

Aaron 2026-08-25: "I hope to also transmit those who disagree with me. I'm not certain,
only 99%, lol."

WHY IT IS LOAD-BEARING RATHER THAN A COURTESY. The book's purpose is authenticity, not
persuasion — "so my kids know how I think, even if they deem it delusional they can't deny
it's authentic." That purpose fails on exactly one axis: CURATION. It is why the
self-incriminating material carries the weight it does; a record containing only
flattering material is trivially dismissible as curated.

Carrying the dissenters is the strongest available form of the same argument: a record
that includes the people who think the author is wrong, IN THEIR OWN WORDS, UNCORRECTED,
cannot be dismissed as self-serving — because a self-serving author would not have
included them. Dissent is not a concession to fairness. It is the evidence that nothing
was staged.

THE STRUCTURAL TRAP, which is easy to fall into by accident: REBUTTAL POSITION IS A WAY OF
WINNING. If a dissenting account appears after the author's as a response, the FORM scores
it — he holds the frame and the last word, and the disagreement is reduced to an objection
already answered. A win disguised as balance. Equal weight means their account stands on
its own terms, not answering his. Raw vault applied to biography: a single version of the
facts, never a single version of the truth. The CONSENT-LEDGER already does this once, for
the involuntary-commit episode; this generalises it from one episode to the method.

THE 99%, TAKEN LITERALLY. Across ~200 claims held at that confidence, about two should be
wrong and he cannot know which two. That is not an argument to hedge everything downward —
it is the precise argument FOR publishing the critics, since the dissenting accounts are
the mechanism by which those two get found, by readers who can check what he could not.
Certainty at 99% and transmitting your opponents are not in tension; the second is what
makes the first honest instead of decorative.

THE CONSTRAINT IS THE SAME ONE AS NAMING. A dissenting account needs that person's consent
exactly as a name does — and the people whose disagreement would be worth the most are
predictably the ones with the most reason to decline. Where consent is refused, record
that the disagreement EXISTS and could not be carried; never paraphrase it into a form
they did not agree to.

Falsifier stated: a chapter in which every disagreement appears downstream of the author's
account, or in which no dissenting party is quoted at length in their own voice, has
performed balance rather than transmitted dissent.
