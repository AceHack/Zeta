## Description

## Why

A finite `maxEntries` budget currently makes convergence depend on batch arrival order. The database needs an owned policy boundary that can make the retention tradeoff explicit without silently deleting admitted history.

## Change

- Add a pure `ZetaDbRetentionPolicyPort` and guarded evaluator.
- Add an explicit no-forget/backpressure policy that preserves admitted history.
- Add an opt-in canonical-event-ID policy that converges for a shared finite event-count bound.
- Derive refused candidates, duplicate input, displaced history, and typed `forgotten` heat centrally.
- Export the contract from the source-owned ZetaDB surface and record the exact scope in the ZetaId workitem.

This is a planner and law pack. It does not wire retention into durable mutation yet and makes no checkpoint-byte convergence claim.
