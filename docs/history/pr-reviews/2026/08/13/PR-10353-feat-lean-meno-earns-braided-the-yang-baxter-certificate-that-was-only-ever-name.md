## What

`MenoBraidedRMatrix.lean` proved `braidR` is a bijection that is **not involutive** — the *"not symmetric"* half of "braided, not symmetric". It never proved it is a **braiding**. The words "Yang–Baxter" appeared only in docstring prose (`:5`, `:24`, `:28`).

The supporting evidence was thinner than its name implied:

- `tests/Tests.FSharp/BraidRepYangBaxter.Tests.fs` — despite the filename — exercises SWAP / CNOT / Kauffman–Lomonaco and **never touches `braidR`**.
- `tests/Tests.FSharp/BraidR.SectionA24.ConformanceGate.Tests.fs:66` is a single `[<Fact>]` on three fixed words. One point, no generator.

So the load-bearing half rested on a name rather than a proof — a check that did not run, wearing the name of one that did.

## Part 4 — the certificate

States and discharges the set-theoretic Yang–Baxter equation `R₁₂R₂₃R₁₂ = R₂₃R₁₂R₂₃` on `G × G × G`. Coordinates 2–3 are definitional; coordinate 1 is self-distributivity of conjugation, which `group` closes. `conj_selfDistrib` is exposed separately (Joyce 1982; Fenn–Rourke 1992).

With `braidR_not_symmetric_perm3` (Part 3) this is the full earning: `braidR` **is** a braiding and is **not** a symmetry.

## Part 5 — negative control

A true-but-vacuous theorem is worth nothing, so the discrimination is machine-checked and lives in the file:

- **`rfl` alone does not close it** (verified separately) — the statement needs the group axioms.
- **`mut_not_yangBaxter`** — a mutant replacing conjugation `x·y·x⁻¹` with plain multiplication `x·y` is **rejected** by the same equation. Passing YBE is therefore evidence about `braidR` specifically, not about anything of that shape.
- **`braidR_yangBaxter_perm3_exhaustive`** — the positive case re-derived by exhaustive evaluation over all 216 triples of S₃, independent of the `group` tactic. Two routes to one fact; if they ever disagree, one is wrong.
