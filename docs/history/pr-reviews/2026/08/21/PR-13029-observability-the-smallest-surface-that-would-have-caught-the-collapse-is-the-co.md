## Description

**The answer is not a dashboard, and the reasoning for that is the finding.**

The society tick already lands a commit on `main` every 30 minutes, and its subject is `society: evolution tick <timestamp>` — **a clock, not a measurement.** The body is *worse* than empty: it advertises *"score → select → crossover → mutate → replace"* when **only two of five executed, for 202 consecutive ticks.** That is the vacuity class **in the announcement itself.**

`git log` is the highest-traffic human surface here — **378 commits/24h.** Nine appended words would have changed visibly on **2026-08-16T13:43Z, the first tick after the collapse**, then stayed wrong in front of a human **202 more times.**

### The harder half: a dashboard would have been WORSE than nothing

Not neutral — **worse.** A surface nobody opens *looks* like observability, so it **retires the question without answering it.** And the repo already contains that failure: `data/tick-latest.json` is regenerated **51×/day**, and throughout the entire 106-hour collapse it read **`"agents_active": 3`** — **reassuring, and wrong.**

> **Render into surfaces that already have a reader. Do not manufacture readers.**

### The unexpected finding: two population counts that disagree

`agents_active` counts distinct `by` over the **observe-envelope** half of `docs/observe-events/`; the society population counts over the **`society-*`** half of **the same directory.** Disjoint halves, two numbers — **3 and 1** — **and nothing compares them.**

> **So the one number a human could have glanced at during the collapse was the reassuring one.** Not a bug in either producer — two honest counts of two different things wearing the same word. **It needs a name in the glossary before it needs a patch.**

### What's built

`society-readout.ts`. Against the live log: `pop=1 div=0.000 fit=0.651 gen=1 flat=203t/106.9h` with four alarm tokens. Reproduces the collapse timeline exactly (**4→3 at 12:48:24Z, →2 at 13:15:53Z, →1 at 13:43:38Z**).

**Two choices that separate a readout from a decoration:** `nowMs` is **injected, never `Date.now()`** — an ambient clock would make it non-replayable, *the same defect one layer down*; and **a constant series renders all-`_`, never mid-ramp, so flat looks flat.** The tier-2 design carries it further: **the flatline counter counts UP**, so a collapse makes the diff *monotonically louder*. **A committed readout that goes quiet when the system is stuck is silence that looks like calm.**

### Verified — and the re-run is itself the evidence

At `origin/main` it now reads **`flat=203t/106.9h`** against the agent's `200t/105.0h` — **the counter is counting up in real time.** The design property working, not a claim about it. **19 pass / 0 fail / 38 expects.** The disagreement is live: `agents_active = 3` vs `pop=1`.

**Four mutations all bite:** population guard neutered → **3 fail**; flatline counter frozen → **4 fail**; constant series mid-ramp → **1 fail**; alarm token renamed → **3 fail**.

**One defect found in the agent's own work** — two corpus tests **timed out under parallel load.** *A check that times out under load is a check that did not run.* Memoised; green.

The tier-0 one-liner is **not applied** (that workflow was in flight in #13022). The module exports `alarms` as a machine-readable array so the concurrent population falsifier consumes **the same fold** rather than growing a second one.

**Register:** counts metered; *"the commit subject is the smallest sufficient surface"* is an **unmetered design judgement** whose falsifier is cheap and unrun. Thresholds not claimed — except `minPopulation: 2`, non-arbitrary because **below it `evolve()` is provably the identity function.**
