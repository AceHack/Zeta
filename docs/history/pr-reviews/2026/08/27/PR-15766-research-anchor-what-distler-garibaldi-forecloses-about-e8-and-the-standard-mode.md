## What the no-go actually says, at its exact strength

**Distler & Garibaldi, "There is no 'Theory of Everything' inside E8"** (Comm. Math. Phys. 298
(2010) 419–436, arXiv:0905.2658) — read in full, not summarised.

**Theorem 1.3 / 10.1:** no subgroup `SL(2,C)·G` of any real form of E8, or of the realification of
complex E8, satisfies (ToE1) `G` connected compact centralizing `SL(2,C)`, (ToE2) no higher spin,
and (ToE3) `V_{2,1}` a complex representation.

Both halves of the brief I was given were off, in opposite directions:

- **It is stronger than "no three generations".** §11: *"it is impossible to obtain even the
  1-generation Standard Model."* So **"we don't assume three" is no defence** — the number 3 was
  never what it was attacking. That matters directly for a derive-the-constants program.
- **It is narrower than "E8 is ruled out".** (ToE1) requires the **Lorentz group to be inside E8**
  and every field read off `Lie(E8)`. Outside its reach: E8 as a purely internal gauge group, groups
  that are not E8, and **E8 as combinatorics — a root system, a lattice, a code, a generator.**
  That last class is where every line of in-repo E8 code lives.

Independently, and needing no theorem: the `−1` eigenspace in any real form of E8 has dimension
**112 or 128**; three generations need **≥ 180**.

## Chirality and handedness

Four rungs — Lee–Yang/Wu → PVED → Frank/Soai amplification → biological homochirality — each
individually solid, with **two named, quantified gaps**: rung 2 is calculated and never measured,
and the amplification threshold (`~10⁻⁵ J/mol`) exceeds the PVED by **4–7 orders of magnitude**.
Blackmond (2019): *"a relationship between biological homochirality and parity violation energy
difference of enantiomers is not yet supported by either theoretical or experimental findings."*

**Could #15715 ever reach it? No — and the missing object has a name: an energy scale.** Five gates
are listed; we are before the first. That is a finding, not a shrug: it says what to stop looking
for.
