## Description

## Summary

Extends the existing thirty-minute Alexa heartbeat lane with one deterministic,
explicitly labelled room-evidence observation only after the ordinary observe
tick succeeds. The change does not create a second scheduler, infer witness
authority from a workflow credential, or claim a solved room outcome.

The emitted atom is persisted through the Git-backed `ZetaStorageCell` path,
then published through the parser-validated static room-evidence manifest. Its
sequence-zero genesis binding is deliberately unresolved until a real local
witness verifier is configured. The first event receives an additional,
content-addressed RWA-1 `request-local-witness` teaching record.

## Local Adjudication Boundary

`RoomWitnessAdjudication` retains local unresolved and visible-dispute states
as immutable records. It canonicalizes visible conflict references and supplies
a next-generator lesson without selecting a hidden winner. It does not mint a
cryptographic correction; a later correction must remain a separately signed
audit fact.

The manifest remains empty until the scheduled lane actually emits a receipt.
The UI’s empty, unavailable, malformed, and discovered states remain distinct.
