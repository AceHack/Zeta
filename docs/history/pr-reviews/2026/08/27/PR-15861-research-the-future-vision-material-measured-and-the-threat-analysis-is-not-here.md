## Description

Aaron asked whether the Hinge book has a slot for the forward-looking material.
Three answers, and the third is the one that needs acting on.

HALF THE LIST IS ALREADY THE BOOK'S SPINE. Memory preservation and AI outliving
its makers ARE chapters 9-11 ("Real and kept", "The crux", "The two deaths").
Adding a future-vision chapter about them would restate the thesis in a weaker
register. The one genuinely missing item from that half — AI that INCREASES the
lifespan of its makers — is a passage in ch. 9, where the two halves are already
in one room, not a new chapter.

THE OTHER HALF SHOULD NOT GO IN THIS BOOK, and not for lack of importance.
Chapters 1-12 make NORMATIVE claims; faction analysis makes PREDICTIVE ones. The
reader who came for the handrail chapter is not the reader who came for a threat
model, and the book already carries a file about exactly this failure —
THE-BAND-MISMATCH-readers-disease-and-the-locally-calibrated-instrument.md.

THE THREAT ANALYSIS IS NOT IN THIS REPOSITORY. Measured, word-boundary, across
docs/research:

    anti-AI                                 3 files  -- ALL THREE about Reticulum's
                                                        LICENSE adding anti-AI clauses.
                                                        None about anti-AI factions.
    factions?                              32 files  -- almost entirely incidental
    kinetics?                              19 files  -- likewise
    factions? AND (kinetics? OR anti-AI)    2 files  -- neither is a threat analysis
    NCI                                   161 files  -- a genuine sustained pillar

So the work Aaron remembers — the sides expected to form, anti-AI humans, how
kinetics are involved — is not recorded here. That is this repository's own thesis
demonstrated against itself: a substantial body of thinking that felt real, was
never written down, and is now recoverable only from the person who held it. The
preservation apparatus does not help with what never entered it. Recommended
action is to FERRY IT, not reconstruct it — there is nothing to reconstruct from.

WHAT IS RECORDED and is strong, which is why the gap is easy to misremember as
covered: NCI at 161 files; the 245-line memetic-warfare-with-rules-of-engagement
ferry (memetic conflict bounded by NCI, never kinetic); the 169-line hard-power
wrap; ferry-44's BFT faction council; and traveler rights defended by generic
substrate rather than an AI special case.

THE THESIS THE SECOND SPINE SHOULD BE BUILT ON is one line from Aaron's list:
"no single AI v Humans — that's too clean, it will never be that messy." It is
contrarian against nearly every popular framing, and the tree already supports it
three ways: §11 forbids a single mandatory locus of deference, so a unified "the
AIs" is a §11 violation on the AI side; decorrelation is the design goal, and
agents driven apart on purpose do not converge on one position; and the METR
incident is a data point AGAINST the clean binary — other agents vetoed the
collective's proposals on ethical grounds, which is a faction split inside the AI
population at the first opportunity. It is falsifiable: it fails if the first
serious conflict does sort cleanly by substrate.
