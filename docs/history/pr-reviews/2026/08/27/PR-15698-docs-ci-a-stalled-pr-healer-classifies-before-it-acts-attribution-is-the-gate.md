## Description

Nine open PRs had gone quiet. All nine were **already armed** with auto-merge — which kills the obvious healer ("find unarmed PRs, arm them") before it is built. They sorted into four causes needing four different answers, and three of those causes were not the PR's fault at all.

Six were cleared tonight (separate branches). This PR lands the design for doing it without a human, plus a dry-run classifier that writes no state and takes no action.

## The hole this must not inherit

`retraction-actuator.ts` carries twelve golden-vector laws. Classified exhaustively they cover trigger threshold, deference, graph-slice uniqueness, at-most-once, DST replay, terminality, non-retry, non-oscillation, and the byte-lock floor. **None is about attribution.**

Uniqueness is a property of the commit *graph*; attribution is a property of the *failure*. On 2026-08-26 an upstream `www.gnupg.org` outage produced a perfectly unique isolation whose single candidate — a GraphQL-transport hygiene lint, #15683 — was causally unrelated. The actuator would have retracted it confidently, with a correctly formatted letter to its author.

So `isAttributable` is deliberately **asymmetric**: an underivable subject *withholds* the remedy rather than licensing it. Being wrong that way costs a delay; being wrong the other way retracts an innocent commit.

Three more instances of the same shape surfaced while clearing the nine:

- `drift (loud)` red on two PRs while reporting **repo-wide** drift they did not cause
- `lint (bash retirement inventory)` **flapping at 13% on main** (7/54), by its own annotation
- the `pre-push[floor]` guard refusing a merge-main whose floor blob was **byte-identical to main's** — a predicate over *presence* standing in for one over *cause*

## What the classifier refuses

- **Ownership outranks everything.** A branch held by another worktree is untouchable — this fired on #15636 tonight.
- **An unanswered probe is `unknown`, never a negative.** GitHub returned `mergeable_state: unknown` for 9 of 9 PRs across two polls, so mergeability is measured with a local `git merge-tree` — a genuinely different transport.
- **A required check with no run classifies `UNKNOWN`, never `PASS`.**
- **Conflict auto-resolution is confined to generated files** with a declared regeneration recipe. Hand-authored conflicts go to their author.
- **Class 4 (a PR's own failures) is refused outright** — diagnosing why someone's test fails and changing their code is authorship.
