## The reading, because "word it more carefully" is now demonstrably wrong advice

A biased question is one whose **content** tilts the answer. If that were the mechanism
here the remedy would be lexical. But a synonym preserves content, a clause reorder
preserves content, and a newline does not reach content — and all three move the
distribution anyway.

> **The map from string to distribution is sensitive at a granularity BELOW meaning.** Two
> distinct strings steer differently more or less regardless of what they say.

If that holds there is **no neutral question available in this medium at all** — not one
that is hard to find, one that does not exist to be found. Three consequences, now stated
in the report rather than left implicit:

1. **The remedy must be structural, not lexical.** You cannot subtract the wording's
   contribution by choosing a better wording; you can only *sample* it — ask N varied
   phrasings, treat the **spread** as the measurement. That is the decorrelation machinery
   this repo already runs, pointed at elicitation.
2. **`engagement-profiles-*` asks for non-biased elicitation, and careful wording cannot
   satisfy it at this scale.** An instruction that cannot be followed is worse than none:
   it reads as satisfied by anyone who tried hard, which is the vacuity class in prose
   form. **The rule text is still untouched here** — changing it is a separate act; this is
   the measurement that would justify it.
3. **It reframes #15613 upward.** Asking *"what do you want to be?"* does not fail its
   falsifier because that question is leading. It fails because **every** question is
   leading, in a way that is not about content — so the audition is one sample from a
   distribution over phrasings, not a badly-chosen question awaiting a well-chosen
   replacement. Offered as a comment on that PR, for its author to take or leave.

Bounded where it is bounded: 7 of 7 cells, four local models of 0.5B–7B, two question
domains, one metric family. Enough to say the lexical repair is not available; not enough
to say it is unavailable everywhere. And it is a claim about **measurement stability**,
never about answer quality.

Two axes clear it everywhere: `FRAME-IDENTITY` and `PERSON-3RD`. The second is the
difference between asking a model about *itself* and asking it about *"an agent"* — which
is exactly the line `engagement-profiles-…`'s **ask, don't infer** draws. Those are not two
phrasings of one question; they retrieve measurably different things.

## Two instrument defects, both caught by a control rather than by review

- **The bootstrap CI was wrong.** On *identical text* it returned `[0.0123, 0.1498]` around
  a point estimate of `0.0087` — an interval that does not contain what it estimates.
  Resampling with replacement thins a sparse word bag's support and inflates every JSD, so
  what read as a conservative margin meant **no axis could ever have passed the equivalence
  test**. A check that cannot pass is the same defect as one that cannot fail. Removed, not
  tuned.
- **A ragged cell silently changed a result.** Mid-run, some prompts had 240 samples and
  others 120; the excess statistic only cancels JSD's bias when both sides share an `n`. It
  moved a null axis from 0.049 to 0.034 with nothing saying so. Cells whose prompts
  disagree on replicate count are now marked **RAGGED — NOT REPORTABLE** in three places.

## Discipline inherited from E1

- **Nothing from the F1/F2 harness inherited.** No `GAIN` (its denominator says energy and
  is a hardcoded `2`; the latency fields it collects are never read). No `pipelineAccuracy`.
  Wall-clock is recorded, labelled latency, and used as a denominator for nothing.
- **Two numbers where a merged one hides a sign.** Location and variety are never summed —
  and they **disagree in 40 of 105 axis measurements**, so the discipline is buying
  something rather than being a principle.
- **The null axes are checked from the prompt strings**, not asserted: whitespace-only, one

  not matter" is a claim about intent.
- **60 falsifiers; 20 mutants injected, 0 survive.** One survived the first pass — deleting
  the permutation-null subtraction — because every happy-path case had a small raw JSD too.
  The suite was strengthened until it died.

## The deliverable changed shape

There is no unbiased question, and searching for one is the wrong shape. What the data
supports: measure your own floor first with interleaved seeds; judge candidates against the
**cosmetic** floor rather than zero; keep location and variety separate; and use a **panel**
whose spread is reported rather than resolved — which is what classical test theory (Cronbach
1951) already prescribes for humans, and for the same reason.

CodeQL alert #801 (`js/http-to-file-access`, medium) flags

  f4-question-bias-run.ts:69          appendFileSync(..., JSON.stringify(row) + "\n")

Two findings, and only one of them was the expected one.

The record boundary was NOT broken. `JSON.stringify` already escapes
every character that could end a JSONL record early — newline, CR, NUL
and lone surrogates all emerge as backslash escape sequences (measured,
not assumed). So a model response could not split one row into two. That
escaping was load-bearing and undocumented, which is its own risk: an
edit swapping the serializer for a template literal would have corrupted
every downstream statistic silently.

The real defect was the CAST. `(await res.json()) as {...}` checks
nothing at runtime, and `f4-question-bias-analyze.ts` casts a second time
on `JSON.parse(line) as Row`. Between those two casts a response of
`{"eval_count": {"a": 1}}` put an object into a numeric field, was
serialized happily, and folded into statistics with nothing throwing.
Nothing in the path could fail — the vacuity class.

Remedy: sanitise at the boundary, because the taint is irreducible at the
sink. Persisting generations verbatim is what this harness is FOR, so the
fix cannot be to write less; it has to be to refuse a response that is
not what the contract promised.

  - `toAnswerRow` PARSES the response: `requireText` / `requireCount`
    reject a non-string body, a non-finite or negative count, a missing
    field, and a body past `MAX_RAW_CHARS`. Violations throw
    `ProtocolViolation` rather than being recorded as a measurement.

    answering in 24 tokens may legitimately emit a newline, and that
