## The four answers

**Q1 — the site.** Concrete for a *declared finite cover* (Abramsky's event sheaf; the sheaf is assignments-with-projection, and the sub-presheaf `Rᵢ` is what fails to glue). Grothendieck site machinery is explicitly **declined** as more apparatus than the finite case consumes. For **language** the site is written down and labelled **`toy`**: nothing extracts the cover, and a hand-drawn cover makes the framework unfalsifiable — the same three attributes give cyclic or acyclic depending on the carve. The pre-registration experiment that would change that is named.

**Q2 — NO, cleanly, and the reason is worth more than a yes.** Every byte-lock oracle covers the **same** domain, so the cover is *k* copies of one set, the nerve is a full (k−1)-simplex, and the disagreement cochain `d_ij = s_j − s_i` is **by construction** `δ⁰s` — a coboundary. Its H¹ class is **zero whether the oracles agree or not**. Byte-lock passes ⟺ the 0-cochain is a global section, which is **H⁰**. The "which overlap failed" instrument already exists and needs no theory: it is the pairwise diff. *Total redundancy is the H¹ = 0 regime by construction* — a design fact to keep, not a deficiency.

**Q3 — it stops at the flat/local-system case, correctly.** Monodromy *is* a locally constant sheaf, i.e. the degenerate case where local data is the same everywhere. It does not extend to the merge algebra — the 2026-08-17 refutation's **F4** already proved there is no connection there. `anti-babel` needs nothing more. One actionable fragment it is **not** using: a **commutative** reintegration record cannot preserve order-of-divergence (holonomy factors through the abelianization), so if *"each with its path recorded"* means order, the record must be non-commutative.

**Q4 — yes, and this is the deliverable: arity.** No sound check of arity ≤ 2 decides global consistency on a cyclic cover (computed by cases). BFMY restated: **α-acyclic ⟺ gluing arity 2**, which `gyo.ts` already computes in polynomial time. The general question is **undecidable** (Rice 1953, entailment checked), but **arity is a property of a check's signature, not its body**, so it is declared rather than inferred — and runtime read-set tracing is unsound here, named so nobody builds it. The rigorous form of *"the global structure is where hacks happen"* is **Clarkson–Schneider hyperproperties**: **manifesto §13 noninterference is 2-safety**, so every single-run test of it is provably incomplete — and DST paired replay is already the right instrument.
