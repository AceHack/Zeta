## Why extend rather than bend the producers

A coinage arrived at **independently, by separate authors, more than once** is evidence the vocabulary was missing a distinction its users needed — not that its users were careless. And the alternative records something **false**, which is the failure this whole convention exists to prevent:

- `operator-delegated` → `shared` **loses the delegation**
- `autonomous-fail-closed` → `autonomous-fail-open` **claims a reach on error the actor deliberately does not take**

The second is the sharper one. An autonomous actor that *halts* on error claims strictly less than one that continues past it. A vocabulary with only `fail-open` forces the safer actor to overstate itself — the enum was pushing producers toward a *less* truthful block, which is exactly backwards.

## Additions, never renames

## Still closed
