## What

Generalizes the open-weights positioning (#8271) to a **standing design heuristic** (Aaron: *"I never want to have to choose between human welfare and money — I'll come up with a different design if I do"*).

The **presence** of a forced welfare-vs-money tradeoff means the **design is wrong** → redesign until the tradeoff dissolves, don't pick a side. It's **`default-to-both`** applied to welfare-vs-money (refuse the either/or; find where both hold), with **m/acc** as the orientation. Open weights *dissolved* the choice rather than resolving it.

**Honest seam:** the third design isn't *always* available — sometimes the tension is real and the redesign doesn't exist yet. So the invariant is "treat the forced choice as a smell and search hard for the dissolving design *before* accepting the tradeoff," not a guarantee one exists. The **resilience floor** earns its keep here: the potato buys the *runway* to keep searching instead of capitulating under resource pressure.

**Standing-commitment candidate for `docs/WONT-DO.md`** ("Zeta will not ship a design that forces a human-welfare-vs-money choice; the forced choice is a redesign trigger") — **gated**: recorded as positioning pending an explicit "carve it."
