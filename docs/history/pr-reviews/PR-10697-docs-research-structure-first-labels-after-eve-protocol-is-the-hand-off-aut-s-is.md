## Description

docs(research): structure first, labels after — Eve protocol is the hand-off, |Aut(S)| is the residue

Ferries two Aaron observations from 2026-08-14, verbatim. The founding one: "i've thought long
and hard about how first humans communicated without language and how to bootstrap that process
for AI, without inserting the control that comes with asymmetric language, even before AI i
thought about this often in trying to design a new society of life from scratch." He states it
predates the AI work entirely, and the stated purpose is designing a society, not a protocol.

The claim with teeth: whoever supplies the language supplies the categories, and the categories
carry the control. A handed-over vocabulary survives every later negotiation because it fixes
what CAN be said before anything IS said. That is one principle the repo already holds in three
places, not a fourth thing — no-directives (source != authorization), pigeonhole-by-self-claim
(the subject supplies the category), manifesto §3 weight-free. An imposed vocabulary is captured
authority in linguistic form: weight nobody sees, because the medium holds it, not a party.

Peirce's second trichotomy localizes it (CP 2.247-2.249). An icon resembles its object, an index
is causally tied to it, so a receiver can check either against the thing; a symbol is pure
convention, checkable only against a convention someone supplied. Icons and indices bootstrap;
symbols require a prior agreement someone imposed.

The second observation is Aaron's own answer, and it changes the doc's spine: "we have eve
protocol to try to establish this when two imposed vocabs that try to meet in the middle on
algebraic structure then assign labels and translations after the structure first." Structure
first, labels after — and he stated it verbatim three months ago (2026-05-12, in-repo memory):
"Polymorphic diplomacy. So it's basically where you agree on the structure. You agree on the
symbols. And then you assign labels later. And you have to agree on the labels being unweighted,
unbiased. And then you can start diplomacy. It's self-scaffolding." May gave a stance; August
gives the mechanism. Same protocol, Mirror->Beacon compression of his own earlier work.

Why it works, checkably: an algebraic structure is verifiable WITHOUT a shared vocabulary — you
confirm associativity, identity, the group laws by performing them, not by agreeing on names. So
structure is falsifiable-by-the-receiver, and a bad translation breaks the structure visibly. The
structure is the icon, the labels are the symbols, and the ordering is what makes the symbols
non-coercive.

New result (§7, the shadow's derivation, elementary but stated with proof sketches so a reader
can check rather than trust): if Iso(A,B) is non-empty it is a TORSOR under Aut(A), so
|Iso(A,B)| = |Aut(A)|. |Aut| = 1 means the translation is forced — nobody chose it, either party
can compute it alone, and no asymmetry can hide in the labels. |Aut| > 1 means someone must pick,
and that pick is exactly where imposition re-enters; log2|Aut(S)| is the imposition budget in
bits. Corollary: a claim survives the hand-off iff it is Aut-invariant. Honest limit: structure-
first works to the extent the structure is RIGID, and free objects are the WORST case, not the
best — Aut(free monoid on X) = Sym(X), so 3 generators admit 6 translations and the structure
prefers none. That is a real tension with only-the-irreducible-is-primitive and the doc says so:
relations are what buy rigidity. And the convergence worth keeping — pointing adds constants to
the signature, so the relevant group is a pointwise stabiliser. Peirce's index, Tomasello's joint
attention, and Lewis's salience are the same operation: breaking the symmetry is literal, and the
symmetry is Aut(S).

Also lands the synthesis with the visualization thread: a branch-free encoding is an icon in
Peirce's exact sense — no branch means no lookup table means no convention handed to you, and
that is WHY human and AI read the same meaning without either supplying the other a vocabulary.
Goguen & Harrell's semiotic morphisms for information visualization is that argument's existing
formal home. Rendering half is branch shadow/branch-free-visual-encoding-is-the-meaning-junction,
not duplicated here.
