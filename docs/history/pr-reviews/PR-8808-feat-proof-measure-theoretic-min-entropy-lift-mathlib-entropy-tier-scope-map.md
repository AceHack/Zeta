## What

Advances the **measure-theoretic tier** of the math-team handoff (`docs/handoffs/2026-06-19-otto-to-math-team-...md`) rows 1 + 3 — the Mathlib-backed real-distribution versions of the two structural Lean legs on `main`.

### Row 1 (NFT forgery-resistance `H_∞` lift) — REAL Mathlib leg DONE

New `src/Core.Lean4/Lean4/EntropyMeasureTheoretic.lean` (imported from `Lean4.lean`):

- min-entropy `H_∞(D) = −logb 2 (max_x p(x))` over a finite ℝ-valued distribution (`FinDist`, `pmax` via `Finset.sup'`).
- `pmax_product : pmax (A × B) = pmax A * pmax B` — guessing probability factorizes for independent pairs.
- **`Hmin_product : H_∞(A × B) = H_∞(A) + H_∞(B)`** — THE LIFT, as an **exact equality** (stronger than the row-1 `≥` claim), via `Real.logb_mul`. Plus the `≥` corollaries (`Hmin_product_ge`, `Hmin_product_ge_left`) recovering the operational `EntropyFloorLift` shape, and `Hmin_nonneg`.

This is the real-number upgrade of the operational `EntropyFloorLift.lean` (pure-`Nat` guessing-space). Both stand — operational leg untouched (no weaken), cross-checking row 1 (BP-16).

### Row 3 (real Shannon DPI `I(A;U|C) ≥ I(A;f(U)|C)`) — honest-stopped, mapped + skeletoned

Mathlib **v4.30.0-rc1 has no Shannon entropy / mutual-information / conditional-entropy defs** (no `measureEntropy`, `mutualInfo`, `condEntropy`). Present: `Real.negMulLog` (+ `strictConcaveOn_negMulLog`), `binEntropy`/`qaryEntropy`, KullbackLeibler (`klDiv`, chain rule, `strictConvexOn_klFun`) — but **no DPI / kernel monotonicity**. The DPI cannot be *stated* without first defining finite entropy + MI; that is a multi-day Lean job. The exact present/absent lemma names, target statements, and the proof skeleton are in the scoping doc.
