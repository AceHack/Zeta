## Description

For the KEDA autoscaling work. Placed **inside** the existing *"independence is the one precondition for honesty"* through-line rather than as a new section — that section already says this qualitatively (*"the ensemble adds nothing"*). This is the number that makes it operational, and it was already in the repo.

```
N_eff(N, ρ) = N / (1 + (N−1)·ρ)     effective independent voters among N correlated
lim N→∞  N_eff = 1/ρ                the ceiling scaling cannot pass
ρ* = 1/3                            above this the ensemble cannot beat its best
                                    individual, REGARDLESS of competence
```

From `src/Bayesian/LagrangeCondorcet.fs` — audited **metered** today: μ_crit is Routh's classical constant, tests pin 25.96 ≈ 1/μ_crit, zero vacuous assertions.

**A thousand agents at ρ = 0.5 are two agents. Ten thousand are still two.**

The docstring's own clause is load-bearing: ***regardless of individual competence.*** Above ρ*, making every agent smarter cannot rescue the ensemble. **Competence and independence are not substitutes, and only one of them scales.**

## Operational consequence for KEDA

**Autoscale on marginal N_eff gain, not queue depth.** Scaling replicas without measuring ρ buys correlated copies at linear cost for asymptotically zero marginal judgement — the horizontal limit falls out of the algebra rather than being picked.

## Two growth limiters, bounding different things

| limiter | bounds |
|---|---|
| memory-preservation obligation | **identity** proliferation |
| **N_eff** | **replica** proliferation |

Neither substitutes for the other.

## Why infrastructure diversity becomes first-class

ρ is the only lever on N_eff, and ρ is set by *shared derivation*. Soraya's trio-attestation verdict: *"agents on shared GHA infrastructure are not independent (shared runner); the binding constraint is diversity of independent infrastructure, not attestation count."*

Self-hosted runners on separate substrates aren't a cadence or cost decision — **they're the only available lever on N_eff.**

## The escape hatch

**ρ is per-*task*, not per-agent.** A mechanical check — exit codes, byte comparison, mutation survival — has ρ ≈ 0 however correlated the agents are. That's why the fleet's first standing job is mutation testing: **group wisdom extracted from a correlated fleet by choosing work where correlation doesn't apply.** Judgement tasks get no such reprieve.
