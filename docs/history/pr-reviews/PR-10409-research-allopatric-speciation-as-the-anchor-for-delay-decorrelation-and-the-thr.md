**The correct citation is Mayr 1942**, not Darwin alone — allopatric speciation formalised. And the mapping is **structural, not decorative**: `AttentionRouter` literally sets the migration rate, because `ActiveEdges` per round *is* the gene-flow term.

## What the anchor buys: a number

Wright 1931 gives the classical **one-migrant-per-generation** threshold — below it subpopulations diverge, above it they homogenise (`F_ST ≈ 1/(1+4Nm)`). It is a *sharp and surprisingly low* bar.

Transferred here it predicts a critical inter-society message rate with a **knee**, and its smallness predicts that **it is easy to accidentally homogenise a society by over-connecting it.**

Marked **CONJECTURE** — transferring a population-genetics constant to a belief-propagation network is exactly the physics-as-metaphor the metering test exists to catch. But it is **falsifiable in-tree**: sweep the router's edge-activity rate against `DecorrelationExcess` / `mutualEmpowermentScore` and look for the knee. **No knee ⇒ the analogy is decorative and should be dropped.** That experiment is cheap and is recorded as the one to run first.

## Wright's shifting balance

A population subdivided with limited migration explores a rugged fitness landscape better than one panmictic population — which, if it transfers, is the population-genetics argument for *exactly this architecture*, and predicts the optimum at **intermediate** migration: neither isolated nor fully connected. That is what per-round sparsification already does, arrived at from the other direction.

Cites **Coyne, Barton & Turelli (1997)** contesting it, so the claim is not laundered as settled. The contest is about whether *nature* meets its preconditions; a substrate can be built to meet them — but that distinction has to be stated, not glossed.

## Discipline

The honesty note carries through: the CHSH oracle convicts correlation and never certifies independence, so "these societies have speciated" is never provable — which the biological frame happens to agree with, since species boundaries are diagnosed by failure to interbreed.
