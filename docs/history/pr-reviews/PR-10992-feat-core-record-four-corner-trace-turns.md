## Description

## What changed

- add `TraceTurn` with an unbounded logical sequence, received feedback, and the Z-set delta emitted at that turn
- add `RecordedFold` and `foldRecorded` for finite trace batches without retaining an unbounded log in runtime state
- record idempotent feedback turns even when their materialized delta is empty
- add laws proving sequence order, fold equivalence, and append-only correction of earlier interpretations

## Why

Four-corner feedback can reinterpret retained history and emit a present-time `-1` retraction, but it must not rewrite the evidence trail. The explicit logical sequence makes the direction of execution measurable: later corrections append at later turns while the original history and prior turns remain unchanged.
