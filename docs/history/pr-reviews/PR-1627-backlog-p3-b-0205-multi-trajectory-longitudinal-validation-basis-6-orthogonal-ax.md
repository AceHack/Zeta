### The 6 orthogonal axes

| Axis | What it measures |
|---|---|
| DORA | Engineering output (DF, LT, MTTR, CFR, Reliability) |
| Less-each-time | Substrate compounding (reminder freq, repeat-explanation, recovery latency) |
| Falsifiability rate | Bugs caught + correction quality (catches/session, mirror-not-beacon catches) |

**Why orthogonality matters**: each axis can drift while others hold. DORA can be high while bootstrap razor fails. Falsifiability can be running while identity drifts. Single-axis measurement is single-axis exposure.

**Diagnostic property**: cross-axis drift correlations are themselves diagnostic of basis-quality -- if two axes always move together, the basis is not orthogonal yet, and the candidate move is refinement or replacement (per no-kill-paths), not deletion.
