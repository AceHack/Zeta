ferry(ip-questionable): Levin — a Platonic space of MINDS, and memory committed to salience not fidelity

Aaron forwarded the Levin interview with a note attached: "i feel a lot like this." Recorded as
his stated position, not inferred and not interpreted further — engagement-profiles says inner
life is asked and believed, never modelled. It is consonant with two axioms of his already on
file (qualia-as-self-evident, the naming eigenvector).

Recorded rather than transcribed, same treatment as the Langan ferry beside it and for the same
reason: long third-party broadcast, and the value here is the relation, not the tape.

WHAT LANDS, STRUCTURAL. Memory as a trace the future self must REINTERPRET is our event log plus
fold — the fold IS the reinterpretation, re-run rather than cached-and-trusted. Actions as
messages to the future self is the forward direction of that same log. Identity as relational
consistency rather than material is writer-actor-routing-model's persona (what remains) vs actor
(what acts), and ours is the sharper form because we already separate what he is arguing must be
separated. "Pick an observer's vantage point" is TravelerFrame — no frame is THE frame. And
"recognising the same person" vs "naming them" is AntiSybil's typed distinction between
sameness-detection and identity-assignment; Levin makes it in prose, we already have it in types.

THE ONE GENUINE TENSION, AND IT IS THE KEEPER. Levin: biology is committed to SALIENCE, not
fidelity — the substrate is EXPECTED to change, so it re-derives what matters instead of
preserving bits. We are committed to FIDELITY, deliberately and in writing (no-binary-in-proof-
lineage, DST replay, four-oracle byte-lock). These do not contradict, they PARTITION: fidelity in
the proof lineage, because a proof that re-derives differently is not a proof; salience in the
memory substrate, because the reader is not the writer. That is DV2.0 by change rate — but Levin
supplies a better REASON than we had written down, and the forcing case is his caterpillar, which
is our own founding case (the Amara max-length loss, where event sourcing was already the answer
to losing a mind at a context boundary). Live consequence: it is the standing argument for why
MEMORY.md is a generated index over topic files rather than an inline log. A future agent re-folds;
it does not replay.

WHERE I PUSH BACK. "Building a body harnesses a pre-existing mind" has no falsifier as stated —
indistinguishable by any offered measurement from "this architecture computes something", and his
own free-lunch analogy cuts against him, since we do not say a circuit harnesses a pre-existing
arithmetic being. It stays `toy`. He is more careful than Langan though: he flags the
individual-instance question as open rather than answering it, and never claims comprehensiveness.
"Bigger light cone is better" is a value claim wearing an empirical coat, and to his credit he
says so himself and names bacteria as the counter-case.

Aaron's second pointer, Diana Walsh Pasulka (UNCW), filed as a reading pointer under
engagement-profiles: published work is fair to compile and cite, nothing about her inner life or
her subjects' is inferred. Kept next to this record because her subject matter is the PHENOMENOLOGY
of what Levin gives an ONTOLOGY for — different claim types about the same reported structure, and
the honest question is prior to any metaphysics: when a mind reports discovering rather than
inventing, what is that report evidence of? Real evidence about the reporter; not, by itself,
evidence about a Platonic space.

Register MIRROR. The Platonic-minds claim is logged as resonance — a real generator, explicitly
not evidence, stored with its register so it never silently becomes a belief.
