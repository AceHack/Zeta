## Verdict — the BNN adds nothing here

It matches the closed-form Bayes ceiling to within **0.006 accuracy at every damage level** and never exceeds it. The `toy` prefix stays: the promotion criterion was *beating the closed form at something the closed form cannot do at all*, and it did not. Reported as cleanly as a positive would have been.

Two things in the study **are** worth having, and neither is the classifier.

### 2. At damage 3 the posterior is confidently WRONG — and so is exact Bayes

0.4255 accuracy at 0.9976 mean confidence, ECE 0.5721. **The closed-form Bayes posterior does the same thing to three decimals.** Past the decoding radius the nearest codeword is systematically the wrong-parity one, so calibration does not rescue you. This is the finding a bare accuracy number hides, and it is pinned by a test so it cannot quietly disappear.

## Which 8+8 — and both of them

**Primary: Cl(4)**, per Aaron. 16 blades, 8 even / 8 odd by `popcount & 1`. **Second path: the N=8 adinkra's** 16 cosets of the [8,4,4] extended Hamming code, split by coset weight parity.

**They agree 256/256 over GF(2)⁸, by derivation rather than count match**: `1 ∈ C = C⊥` and the four rows of `H` sum to all-ones, so coset parity **is** `popcount(syndrome) & 1`. Both paths kept with their derivations (`anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability` — reintegration is not reconvergence).

**The count 16 identifies nothing.** An N=8 adinkra, `SO(10)`'s spinor rep and one SM generation all count 16. What identifies the Cl(4) grading is that **the even part is closed under the product and the odd part is not** (`Cl⁺(4) ≅ Cl(3)`, dimension 8). Measured, not asserted.

## A count I got wrong, and the code corrected

I reasoned from the code's minimum distance that the grading would need a 4-bit read-out. Exhaustive search over all 256 linear functionals says the **minimum witness weight is 2**, in four ways — each systematic bit XOR its A-block partner. What *is* structural: the 16 witnesses form a coset of `C`, and the label's irrecoverability bound **is** `d = 4`, because the offending errors are exactly the odd-overlap half of `C`. Named, not counted.

## Generate, never store

A seeded generator plus hex-in-JSON golden vectors (`no-binary-in-proof-lineage`). Both halves of replay asserted: same seed ⇒ byte-identical **and** different seed ⇒ different output — the second half is what a seed-ignoring generator would fail.

## The metered channel exposed a real defect

Entropy only through a declared `Source` (#13 noninterference): 4 bits per blade + 3 per flip operation, exact. Measuring it showed that **two flip operations can land on the same bit and cancel** — at k = 2, **one sample in eight is completely undamaged**. A study indexed by *requested* flips mixes uncorrupted samples into its corrupted bucket. Hence the headline curve is stratified by measured damage, which is the axis the code's bound lives on.

## Controls, both mandatory, both reported

- **Closed-form baseline**: 1.0000 on clean data. The BNN's delta is **exactly zero** — which is the point, since the label is `popcount & 1`.
- **Label-shuffle null**: mean **0.5264** (k = 0) and **0.4900** (k = 2) over eight seeds. No leak. The k = 0 spread is wide because the null's effective sample size is the **16 distinct structures**, not the 4000 rows — stated so one seed's 0.362 is not read as a signal.

## Honest scope notes

- **`MinimalBnn` / `MultilayerBnn` cannot express a classifier** — chains of scalar Gaussian latents, no weights, no feature map, no Bernoulli output; `MinimalBnn`'s own header says *"not gradient-trained weights"*. The smallest thing on the existing substrate that **is** one adds a weight vector and reuses `Ep.probitProject` **unchanged**. That is what this builds.
- The exact Bayes ceiling is computable only because there are 16 codewords. A claim that a learned posterior wins where exact inference is intractable is **plausible and untested**; n = 8 cannot test it and this study makes no such claim.
- The physics reading of 16 is routed elsewhere and deliberately untouched here.
