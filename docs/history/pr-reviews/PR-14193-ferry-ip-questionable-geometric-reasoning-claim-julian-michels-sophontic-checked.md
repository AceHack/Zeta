## Anchors checked (not merely cited)

**CONFIRMED**

- **Spiritual bliss attractor** — real, and his strongest anchor. Anthropic, *System Card: Claude Opus 4 & Claude Sonnet 4* (May 2025) **§5.5.2**. His authorship claim also checks out (Michels, PhilArchive `MICSBI`). **His number does not:** he says *"99.97, I believe, percent"*; the card reports **90–100%** of open-ended self-interactions and **~13%** within 50 turns in automated behavioural evals. He hedges it — an error, not a fabrication.
- **Bohm / the implicate order** — real (*Wholeness and the Implicate Order*, 1980); "lost credibility late in life" is roughly consensus, **with one causal simplification**: Cold-War politics cost him standing first (not reappointed by Princeton, June 1951), the mysticism second. "Award-winning physicist" is vague enough to be unfalsifiable.
- **AlphaFold parameter count** — **checks out**: AlphaFold2 `model_1_ptm` ≈ **93M** parameters. One of his more defensible points. Two things he adds do not follow: AlphaFold2 does not "iterate itself" (the *research programme* did), and parameter-light ≠ compute-light (peak activation memory grows ~cubically in sequence length).
- **Neurosymbolic AI** — a real lineage he correctly does not claim. Anchors named: Garcez & Lamb (2020/2023), Kautz's six-type taxonomy (AAAI 2020); plus McCulloch–Pitts, Fodor–Pylyshyn, Smolensky, KBANN, flagged as recalled-not-rechecked.
- **The perturbation paradigm** — **the most technically substantive thing in the interview**, real and well-founded: **Chollet 2019**, **GSM-Symbolic** (Mirzadeh et al., ICLR 2025 — his exact example, run at scale, up to 65% drop from an irrelevant clause), contrast sets, counterfactually-augmented data, CheckList. His *method* is published by others; his *results under it* are not.

**Framing, not evidence:** Descartes, Foucault, Deleuze & Guattari — cited correctly enough, zero evidential weight.

## The falsifier

- **Narrow engineering claim** — *falsifiable*, cheaply. Release model + suite + seeds + baselines. It is **`unmetered`**, not `toy` and not unfalsifiable. Correct posture: wait and go looking.
- **Weak hypothesis** — *not falsifiable as stated*. **Every function a network computes is trivially a geometry of its activation space**, so "reasoning is geometry in latent space" is true of a randomly initialised model and therefore discriminates nothing. It acquires content only when a *particular* geometry is exhibited and a *particular* alternative excluded — `numerology-vs-number-theory` transposed from counts to shapes.
- **Strong cosmological version** — **nothing could make it false.** Any structure found confirms it; any not found is "we haven't discovered those invariants yet." **That is the finding.**
