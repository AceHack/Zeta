## Description

Three contrast cartridges, landed in `db/shapes/cartridges/pending/` rather than the catalog directory — see **Why pending** below.

## The gap these close

`braid.lines` already encodes the distinguishing fact three ways: `constant stuck 1` (*"the locked word is NOT the identity braid, proven by Artin's faithful action"*), the over/under occlusion gap resolved into the ink on 2026-06-12, and a ratified F# treaty citing `sigma^2 != identity`.

**What was missing is the CONTRAST.** One braid diagram shows someone a braid; it does not show them what a braid is *not*.

## `symmetric-vs-braided` — where the picture IS the proof

Two panels, **one word** (σ·σ). Left reads it in a symmetric category: the second crossing undoes the first, strands end parallel, and *no over/under exists to draw*. Right reads it in the braid group: σ² ≠ 1, strands wound, an occlusion gap per crossing.

The sharp detail: **`perm-identity 1` is in both panels on purpose — the permutation cannot tell them apart.** That is the whole content of the distinction, made visible. A braid is a permutation *plus history*, and this is the picture of the "plus".

Anchored with `lean:` laws to `selfBraiding_comp_eq_id`, `braidR_not_symmetric_perm3` and `braidR_yangBaxter` in `src/Core.Lean4/Lean4/MenoBraidedRMatrix.lean` — so the diagram and the machine-checked theorem cite each other.

## `traced` — drawn because it is confusable

The `FourCornerTrace` loop of `src/Core/WSet.fs` (Joyal–Street–Verity 1996): `loop-crossings 0`, `gaps 0`, with the in-file law **`a-bend-is-not-a-crossing`**. Traced does **not** imply braided, and a prose warning already failed to stop that conflation once today.

Also records a **name collision found while drawing it** — `shape-fourcorner` means CHSH measurement corners in one place and ownership corners in another — plus a drawing-honesty note: the bend is in space, there is no time axis, so it must not be read as retrocausality.

## `twist` — the framed/unframed distinction

Ribbon with a 2π turn vs a bare strand. `strand-framing 0` and the law **`nowhere-to-put-it`**: an unframed conjugation quandle has θ = id because a bare strand has nowhere to put the twist. A ribbon does.

The open question (is Meno balanced? does the framed promotion rescue it?) is in `issue` rows and **never a `law`**. The closed rungs — writhe ≠ twist, ribbon blocked at the object, MTC false — are `issue … closed` carrying their obstructions.

## No goldens, and no treaty rows — both deliberate

The renderer **cannot** draw these yet: `ShapeRender.strokesOf` matches on `meta name` and has no branch for them (it would emit an empty SVG), and `ShapeAcceptance.geometryLaw` has no known-answer law, so the hard gate correctly refuses. Hand-writing SVG would break the single-source rule and the golden-lock test.

`treaty` rows are ratified by each oracle's own choice — `treaty fsharp bytes` and `treaty otto meaning` are theirs to write, not an agent's to forge.

## Why `pending/`

The suite globs `cartridges/*.lines` **top-level only** and asserts count = 19, plus the hard gate, the golden lock, and treaty rows for every file it finds. Dropping these straight in would have broken four tests **or forced forged treaties**. `pending/` ships them readable and diffable without a green tick they have not earned.

Promotion is six steps, stated in the accompanying README: registry + cost row → strokes → geometry law → goldens → treaties → count bump.
