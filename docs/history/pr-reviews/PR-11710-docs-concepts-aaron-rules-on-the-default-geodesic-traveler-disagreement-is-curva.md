## The substance

Under §11 the highest-moral-regard **default** is **free fall**, so divergence between travelers is **curvature** — geometry both inhabit — and never a **force** one applied to the other.

That decides what you *do* about divergence. A force has an agent who applied it, so you go looking for who pushed, which is to say for who is at fault. Curvature has no agent, so you look at the geometry both parties are standing in. The named error it forbids: **treating moral disagreement as a force to be resolved is the Newtonian reading of a tidal effect** — general relativity retired exactly that move.

"Default" is what makes §11 a default rather than a mandate: absent a chosen oracle you are in free fall; *choosing* one is leaving the geodesic under thrust. A mandate has no free-fall state.
