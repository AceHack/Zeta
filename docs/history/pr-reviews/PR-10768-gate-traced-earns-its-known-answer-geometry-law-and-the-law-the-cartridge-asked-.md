## The finding this banks

`traced`'s own `geometry-law` issue proposed *"count the drawn crossings and occlusion gaps and
require BOTH to be zero"* and called it **"mechanical and strong"**. It is mechanical, it is
true, and on its own it is worthless: **an empty picture has zero crossings and zero gaps.**
Written as asked, the gate would have been passed by the drawing that does not exist — the
vacuity class, which is the exact thing a known-answer gate exists to refuse.

The law shipped keeps both zero-counts (they are the thesis) and adds what makes them mean
something:

* the figure must **exist** — `wires` distinct *unbroken* wires, `corners` corner markers, one
  closed box the pass-through wire really spans;
* the loop must **return**, and to the corner this cartridge stakes its honesty on —
  TOutFeedback out, TInFeedback back in, **never TOut** (a pipeline is not a trace);
* the return must **bend in space** — it has to leave its own channel height in between, so a
  return drawn flat along the channel it left (the arrow that reads as travelling back along a
  time axis, which `src/Core/WSet.fs` refuses) now fails;
* crossings are counted by **integer segment intersection over the strokes the renderer actually
  emits**, collinear grazing included. A figure whose whole claim is "this wire passes over
  nothing" does not get to call a graze innocent.

Dash and gap are kept apart on purpose and counted separately: a **dash is the sign** (the
`-1` retraction, `WSet.negate`), an **occlusion gap is a broken run** (shape-braid's over/under
memory). Neither may stand in for the other.

## Two findings raised, neither fixed

Both are recorded as **open `issue` lines in the cartridges themselves**, where they will be
read. Changing a file's claims is its author's call, not the gate's.

1. **`traced` — `only-the-ring-corners` crosses two different fours.** The law reads
   `rings-with-a-trace + inverse-free-corners = corners`, adding a split of the four-corner
   **ring family** (ℤ, ℂ, ℝ≥0, Boolean) to a total taken from **FourCornerOwnership**'s
   TIn/TOut/TInFeedback/TOutFeedback. The file's own `differs-from shape-fourcorner` edge records
   that this name collides — and then the law crosses it anyway. It holds by coincidence of
   counts, which is numerology, not an identification. The geometry law does **not** gate on it.
2. **`twist` — the proposed law counts a HALF twist.** Project a flat band: its two edges sit at
   `core ± r·cos(2π·turns·t)` and coincide exactly where `cos = 0`, i.e. **2 × turns** times per
   full turn. One crossing is the Möbius (π) signature — and that file already says *"A half turn
   is not a twist of a band"*. Written as stated, the law would gate the figure the cartridge
   exists to exclude. Verified numerically (0.5→1, 1→2, 1.5→3, 2→4, 3→6), not eyeballed.
