## Description

Continues [#16819](https://github.com/Lucent-Financial-Group/Zeta/pull/16819). Thirty commits; every claim below is measured, and the things that are **not** true are named at the bottom.

## The defects, each hiding the next

**The grammar could not address another agent.** `observe.ts` had 17 `NextAction` kinds and exactly one communication verb — `respond_to_operator`, which addresses the *human*. An agent could work, decompose, explore, rest and rewrite the grammar itself, and had no way to look at a colleague's artifact, answer it, ask for what it was missing, pull anyone into a room, or hand work to someone. *A hierarchy whose members cannot address each other is an org chart drawn over solitary confinement.*

**Reviews and QA had no calendar time.** Only `prioritized_work` was ever booked. Thirteen gates were judged by hats with nothing on their calendars — and `loop-policy.ts` makes the calendar **runtime authority**, so a review with no block is one the reviewing hat's tick *cannot see it is meant to do*. Work was authorised by the schedule and reviews were not.

**Nothing ever asked for a review.** `RequestReview` had a reader building a hat's menu and no writer, so `review_artifact` was a verb no agent could be offered.

**Escalations decided alone.** `RequestDecision` — the family whose policy reads *"authority sits above the hat"* — had zero senders.

**And the deliberation did not survive the process.** `decision_recorded` was an event kind with zero emitters.

## What is here

Five generic peer verbs; `org-observe-bridge` filling them from the organization; `org-drive` closing the loop (state → surface → menu → choice → effect → state); review/QA calendar booking; the review-request writer and the **`scope_holder`** routing kind it needed; `RequestDecision` on escalation; durable facts for signals, anchors, decisions and anchor state.

**No role is hardcoded anywhere.** A TPM assigns because assignment is offered to hats whose reports can take the work; an engineering manager unblocks people because `ReportBlocker` routes to the immediate supervisor; a director decides execution because `RequestDecision` routes up. Encoding "TPMs assign" would freeze one org's shape into the machine — the chart is the shape, and it is data.

## An incident, recorded

Writing the real-adapter test I passed `repoDir` where `gitChangeControl` takes `cwd`. tsc caught it; **`bun test` does not typecheck**, so it ran with `cwd: undefined`, `spawnSync` read that as the current directory, and the adapter **branched, committed and merged in the Zeta repository itself**. Nothing was lost — the branch matched its remote, `main` was untouched, the strays held no unique commits. `gitChangeControl` now refuses at construction without an explicit `cwd`, because *a type only checked by a tool nobody ran before the damage is not a guard.*

## What this does NOT claim

- `end-to-end.test.ts` runs over **simulated** ports and asserts that about itself — evidence about the organization's **wiring**, not about work delivered.
- `ReportRisk` and `SuggestImprovement` still have no senders.
- The drive's `review` effect is deliberately **not applied**: a verdict belongs to the pipeline's gate, where separation of duties and the outcome clamp live. Recording one from the drive would be a second path to a passed gate.
- The runtime produces no artifact histories, so the deliberation and convening paths activate only when a caller supplies them.
