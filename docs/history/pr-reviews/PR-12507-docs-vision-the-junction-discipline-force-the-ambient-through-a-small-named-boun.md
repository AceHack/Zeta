## Description

docs(vision): the junction discipline — force the ambient through a small named boundary

Aaron 2026-08-19, on the through-line connecting Tarski's stratification to Rx's
design decisions: "save this to VISION.md this is almost it in minimal linguistic
form." Recorded at his request, as the minimal statement.

THE IDEA, in one sentence: take the thing that would otherwise be ambient and
unlocatable — effects, failure, time, undecidability — and force it through a small,
named, enumerable junction. You do not escape the thing; you LOCALIZE it. Diffuse
means any statement might be the undecidable one and you cannot route around what you
cannot find. Localized means one named junction — routable around, and routable INTO
on purpose, which is the interesting half.

LINEAGE, CHECKED RATHER THAN CITED. Tarski (1933): truth for `L` is not definable in
`L` but is definable in a metalanguage, so the meta/object junction is precisely where
undecidability is FORCED to live — concentrated at a boundary, not distributed through
the theory. Aaron's move is that hierarchy made an engineering discipline: since the
junction is where it must live, make it small.

Rx is the same idea applied three times, and Aaron names that team's decision as the
lineage he is following: the minimal dual interface (four methods total, DERIVED as
the categorical dual of IEnumerable, so minimal by construction); `OnError` as an
explicit channel rather than ambient stack-unwinding exceptions; and `IScheduler` as
the single door for time and concurrency. Zeta's injected `Source`/`IEffects` is the
third generalized, and §13 noninterference is this discipline stated for entropy.

WHY IT MATTERS — Aaron: "if you get it wrong you get a singularity." The failure mode
is not that the system halts; it is that the system ANSWERS. An unmarked undecidable
gets resolved rather than escalated, and everything derived inherits an unbounded error
with no marker, because at the moment of entry it looked like an ordinary successful
step. It compounds in a self-regenerating architecture: if the generator IS the ECC,
correct localization means regeneration corrects drift and mislocation means it
amplifies — same operation, opposite sign, decided by junction placement.

THE TEST IS CHEAP AND IT CAN FAIL: enumerate where the system actually defers. If that
set is empty, or does not match the junctions we claim to have localized, the junctions
are not marked — only believed to be. `Evidence.AssertedOnly` and the gated classes are
the existing instances; the check is whether the map matches the territory.

REGISTER: no new claim is promoted. The Tarski-stratification result remains work item
081KQGDBJ0008QG0R003NDQTBM — P3, XL, with "might dissolve under closer examination" in
its own acceptance criteria. This section records the discipline and its lineage, not a
theorem.
