## Description

Third of three background tasks. **The verdict inverts the expected direction: §6a should be
UPGRADED, not downgraded.** The install step is **not** dominated by a fixed cost every surface
needs — 99.4% of its apt payload and 100% of its second-largest phase are toolchains most jobs
never touch.

**Method:** 200 gate runs over a 7.36 h window = **5,604 jobs**; 35 raw job logs cut on
`install.sh`'s own progress markers; 60 CodeQL runs to check the `Analyze` claim.

> Recorded honestly: a first pass matched only **two of four** `Install toolchain` naming
> variants and **missed `full-verify`**. A job-name match that silently under-samples is the
> vacuity class in measurement clothing, so it is reported rather than quietly corrected.

## The centrepiece

**Provisioning is a near-constant 136–145 s regardless of what the job does.**

| job | provision | **payload** |
|---|---|---|
| `lint (archive header §33)` | 145 s | **0 s** |
| `lint (no conflict markers)` | 137 s | **0 s** |
| `lint (tick-history order)` | 136 s | **0 s** |
| **`lint (Go)`** | **142 s** | **11 s** |

**`lint (Go)` spends 142 s provisioning to run an 11 s check — 80% of its wall-clock.** Five
hygiene lints spend ~140 s provisioning to run a check that **does not register a single
second**. Per push: **≈ 51.1 minutes, ~51% of the gate's job-seconds.**

## Where it goes

apt is **50.5%** of `install.sh`; the eprover build is **26.9%** — **the E theorem prover is
compiled from an autotools tarball, from scratch, in every single job**, *including
`lint (markdownlint)` and `lint (no conflict markers)`*. **The tarball is cached; the `make` is
not.**

Summing the apt `Get:` lines — **552.7 MB / 160 packages**: proof stack (Agda+GHC) **45.6%**,
WASM/LLVM **31.4%**, R 9.0%, pandoc 4.9%, QEMU 3.3% … **base/other 3.1 MB = 0.6%.** Per push
that is **12.4 GB of apt downloads**; at 574 runs/day, **~7.0 TB/day**.

## The counterfactual is already measured, in this repo

Four gate jobs already opted out of `install.sh` for `setup-bun`, with the reason written into
the YAML (`gate.yml:1415-1417`): *"it needs one 178 MB runtime, and `./tools/setup/install.sh`
provisions the union of every toolchain in the repo."* **Their provisioning is 2.0 s against
140 s.**

Like-for-like: `lint (structural hygiene)`, 6 bun scripts → **35 s total**; `lint (bash
retirement inventory)`, ~9 bun commands → **211 s**. Same repo, same day, same runner image, two
strategies — **so the ~8x is measured, not modelled.**

Six jobs are **demonstrably** bun-only → routing them banks **828 s = 13.8 min/push, measured.**
Twelve further single-language lints add 19–25 min at an **assumed** 15–45 s per-surface cost —
**that half is a model and is flagged as such at every mention.**

## The `exit 124` verdict — resolved in both directions

**Not structurally near the budget:** 0.19% of jobs failed, every one at exactly the 420 s apt
budget, against a healthy apt phase of **54.6 s median / 111.5 s worst = 3.8–7.7x headroom**.
Attempt 1 alone got **247 s** and could not finish ~40 s of dpkg — **a >6x slowdown that never
completed is a stalled mirror.** And the incident is **repo-wide**: the six failures span four
unrelated branches, including **PR #12859**.

**But the union amplifies exposure, and that half is quantified:** P(≥1 of 23 jobs stalls) =
**4.2% modelled, 3.4% observed.** So the note's caution was right **and** a per-surface install
would have prevented these specific ones.

## Two corrections — the second is the interesting direction

1. **§6a overstates the roster.** *"Every one of those per-language jobs runs the same shared
   install step"* is **false for CodeQL** — `codeql.yml:650` gates it to `csharp` only; measured
   **1 of 5** `Analyze` legs.
2. **§6a UNDERSTATES the cost.** It names only the install step, missing the **18 s / 1.49 GB
   cache restore per job**, and the **12.88 GB cache overrun** that converts ~⅓ of jobs into a
   30 s partial re-resolve. `gate.yml:1376` already records the ceiling; **the union cache is
   what pushes it over.**

## Side finding — the escape hatch that saved 8x also dropped the pin

The four `setup-bun` jobs pin the **action** by full SHA but pass **no `bun-version`** — five
call sites — so they run whatever `setup-bun` calls latest while `.mise.toml` pins `bun = "1.3"`.
**Exactly the §1 "fork because there was no seam" pattern, and it argues FOR declared surfaces
rather than against.** Reported rather than fixed: the fix changes the resolved runtime on every
gate job and is not testable from a laptop.

## Recommendation, with its limits

The cheapest honest first move is **not a manifest format** — it is to **declare one surface
(`bun-only`) as data and route the six verified bun-only lints through it**, banking the 13.8
measured minutes and **giving the declaration format a falsifier before anyone designs it.**

Limits stated so they cannot be rounded off: **CI minutes are free here** (public repo) — the
currency is latency and mirror-stall exposure, and **anyone pricing this in money is inventing a
number**. One 7.36 h window; daily counts vary 449–946, so aggregates carry **±40%**. **Nobody
has measured a "Go surface."** macOS shows the **highest** provisioning at 194 s and is
unmeasured here — **a surface design that only fits Linux is parity drift by construction.** And
**parity is the real risk, not the saving**: a surface layer must be **data read by `install.sh`,
never a second installer**, or this measurement will have bought speed with the §24 invariant.
