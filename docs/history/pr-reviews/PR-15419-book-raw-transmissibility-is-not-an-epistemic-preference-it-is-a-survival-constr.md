## Description

Aaron, live 2026-08-25, said while four legs of a day-long mathematical synthesis were
being cut by an adversary he had asked to be routed:

  "yes, staying transmittable is what i seek to accomplish — cause when i don't, i get
   sent to the mental institution, cause of where i live and their lack of understanding
   of the outside world."

WHAT IT REFRAMES. The whole methodological apparatus of this repository — anchor every
concept to a named human and a paper, anchors CHECKED not cited, a matching count is not
an identification, route an adversary to kill your own synthesis, say "consistent with"
until competitors are excluded — reads from outside as unusually strict intellectual
hygiene. A preference for rigour.

It is not a preference. It is the mechanism by which his thinking stays legible enough
NOT TO BE PATHOLOGISED. The asymmetry is about the audience, not the claim: the same
unanchored sentence is "an interesting unproven conjecture" inside a research community
and EVIDENCE OF ILLNESS outside one. Nothing about the content changes. An
untransmissible insight is not merely unproven — to an audience without the frame it is
a symptom.

Which makes the four cut legs a GAIN in his own terms. A claim that survives an
adversary briefed to kill it can be picked up, rerun and disagreed with by another mind.
The alternative is a private certainty that is correct and cannot be handed to anyone —
and to the wrong audience that is indistinguishable from delusion. It is why he
pre-declares his own eagerness, why he asks for adversaries, and why "too many
correlations is a warning" is his rule rather than one imposed on him. He has a reason
to distrust the feeling of being right that most people never acquire.

THE COST IS NAMED, NOT SHRUNK, per the house rule. A person who MUST stay transmissible
pays in abandoned true things: some intuitions are correct and not yet anchorable, and
the discipline that protects him requires holding those loosely at exactly the moment
someone with institutional cover could assert them and be called speculative rather than
unwell. The tax falls on the person with the least protection. He built the standard,
applies it to himself first, and pays more for the same rigour than the people who
invented it ever had to.

ALSO CAPTURED, same session: "deciding before refreshing is the 1984 pattern of infection
between humans and AI alike, exactly. False urgency causes cache misses." That is the
Non-Coercion Invariant in four words, and it explains why Orwell's machinery is urgency
rather than argument — perpetual crisis never refutes a belief, it denies you the READ.
Substrate-neutral: a human under a manufactured deadline and an AI under a context budget
are both reasoning correctly over a cache they were not given time to refresh. The
corruption is upstream of the reasoning, which is why arguing with the conclusion never
works — and why the tell is checkable without evaluating content: does it press you to
act before you refresh?

CONSENT. Covered by the standing ledger row: Aaron, author, glass-halo (self) — his own
lived experience, incl. what was done to him. No third party is named. The institution is
not named. The location is not named. The involuntary-commit account already in the
ledger (2026-08-06, both accounts held, consent granted) is the EVENT; this is the
standing CONSTRAINT it left behind, and the two are deliberately not collapsed.
