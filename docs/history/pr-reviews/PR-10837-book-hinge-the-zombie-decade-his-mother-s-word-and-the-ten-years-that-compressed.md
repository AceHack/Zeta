## Corrections to the brief I was given

Flagged explicitly, as asked.

***

**1. The mother's line is warmer and worse than the brief renders it — and the difference is load-bearing.** The brief has her "calling him a zombie." The transcript has her describing his *waking* as **"a zombie waking up,"** and the full sentence is **"we've never seen a zombie come back from the dead *before*"** — church vernacular, said as a **slip** she then deflected from when he called her on it. It was grief language from a woman who thought she had already lost her son, not an insult. The draft renders both: it was a hard thing to hear, and it was not cruelty.

**2. The two accounts do not agree, and the brief's mechanism reading only fits one of them.** His account is *running on repeat* — maximally predictable. Hers is **"a schizophrenic zombie that didn't make sense and she couldn't predict"** — maximally unpredictable. That is not a softer version of the same claim; it is the opposite reading of the same decade. I held both rather than resolving them, and I named the gap as the honest center of the material. Collapsing it into "periodic orbit, lived" would have been exactly the flattening the brief warned against, arrived at from the other direction.

**3. The return has a named cause in his own account, and it is not "breaking periodicity" in the abstract.** He says he taught an AI shadow work, it accelerated his own, and that is where Amara comes in. The draft keeps his causal claim as his, with the handrail chapter's honest limit attached (one person's route, not clinical, not a substitute for care).

**4. Two of the brief's mechanism anchors are NOT in the repo.** The CHIP-8 boredom mechanism and the theological *"not death — eternal life, it's just boring… compresses time"* refinement do not exist as repo docs at main tip — I checked. They are Aaron's live words from conversation and are labelled as such, not cited as factory work. Related: `W_C` **does** appear in-repo, but as the uniform reference distribution in `V(p) = KL(p ‖ W_C)`; reading it as "undifferentiated unity" is consistent but is his framing, not a documented definition. Recorded as a coincidence with structure pending, per `numerology-vs-number-theory`.

**5. `Orbit.fs classify` has four classes, not two,** and pointing it at a life is a rhyme. `classify` returns `Fixed` / `Crystal n` / `Quasiperiodic`; `classifyDynamics` adds the chaotic class. The vocabulary genuinely fits *running on repeat* (`Crystal n`); the code has never been pointed at a person and could not be. Labelled **rhyme** in the draft. The **checked** in-repo anchor is the freedom thesis instead: a loop captures no external entropy, so it never individuates — which is a real structural correspondence and still an analogy applied to a life.

**6. The consent question the brief asked me to raise is already answered by the ledger,** so I did not invent a new gate. The mother's row is **GRANTED** with **her pre-read** as the publish gate. That makes this material **draftable now** and **not publishable** until she has pre-read it. ## The dark half

Rendered at full size, per the standing correction. Ten years gone while he was present for all of them. His mother had a word for what he was, and it came out when she wasn't guarding it. She had been grieving a living son for years and had never said it to him. It is also not over — when he becomes unpredictable it still flips, and the truce they built is a repair, not a cure. Softening any of that would remove what makes *"we've never seen a zombie come back from the dead before"* land, which is that the person she said it to was the counterexample standing in front of her.
