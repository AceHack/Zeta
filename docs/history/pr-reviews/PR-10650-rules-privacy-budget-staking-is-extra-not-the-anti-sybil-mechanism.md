## Description

rules(privacy-budget): staking is EXTRA, not the anti-Sybil mechanism

Resolves a tension that was live on file: privacy-budget-is-hard-money carried a
spend/stake/confiscate section (Aaron 2026-08-09) whose rationale was that staking is what
makes decentralized witnessing possible, while TravelerRankLedger.fs and
SocietyUsefulWork.fs implement a proof-of-useful-work anti-Sybil path. Both were on file
and neither cited the other.

Aaron 2026-08-14 settles it, and the reasoning is harm reduction rather than defence:
"we had it listed as it will likely get wagered on the black market anyways so might as
well have some way to wager it, never confiscate it or force to wager it ... it should be
looked at as extra."

So the sanctioned wager exists because an unsanctioned one exists regardless, and only a
sanctioned path can carry the never-confiscate / never-compel invariants.

What actually makes Sybil attacks unprofitable is already built, and the rule now names it:

  TravelerRankLedger.fs - TrueSkill EP over (traveler x hat-domain). The whitewash window
  is closed by construction: fresh identity starts at an honest 0.5 prior, and "1 hit, 2
  misses" lands at ~0.35 rather than 0.0, so discarding a damaged name and re-minting does
  not pay - which is the Sybil's whole play. Rankings are held by OTHERS, never
  self-asserted; domains are isolated so verifier standing does not buy signer standing.

  SocietyUsefulWork.fs - the delta-U aggregation theorem with the analytic expected value
  under pairwise correlation rho, plus a Gaussian copula for heterogeneous agents. Clones
  yield highly-correlated delta-U and the union is idempotent, so N copies price near one
  agent's worth. Contribution does not scale with copies.

Also recorded: staking generalises past privacy budget - attention, memory and tick
sources are named as other irreducible resources that could be wagered if needed. What
transfers is the three-operation invariant, not the currency.

Design consequence stated explicitly: do not reach for a stake where a ranking or a
delta-U measurement will do, and never present a stake as what makes an identity
trustworthy. A staking-based anti-Sybil scheme is purchasable, which is exactly the
attacker this design defeats.

Rules file, so kept to the carved-sentence discipline: a section plus pointers to the two
modules that carry the mechanism, not an essay.
