## Part 1 — the survey, and the line

Eight technique families with checked anchors, each with what it measures, what it *cannot* establish, and cost. The line, stated once:

> **Everything read *off* activations is correlational. Only intervention supports a causal claim.**

- **Correlational:** linear probes (Alain & Bengio 2016; and without Hewitt & Liang 2019 control tasks a probe is the vacuity class), nonlinear probes (*weaker*, not stronger — more capacity is more confound), SAEs (reconstruction is not identification — many bases reconstruct equally well), RSA/CKA (and Davari et al. 2022 shows CKA is manipulable), intrinsic dimension, curvature, logit lens (a **LessWrong post, not a paper**) and tuned lens (a *trained* affine probe — inherits the probe confound wholesale).
- **Causal:** activation patching / causal tracing / interchange interventions only.

Two riders that matter: even causal methods are causal about **the run, not the weights**; and **localization is not mechanism** (Hase et al. 2023 — causal-tracing localization does not predict where editing works).

## Part 1b — training-data attribution, honestly

> **For a model whose training corpus you do not have, you cannot do this at all.**

`qwen2.5:7b` has open weights and an **unreleased corpus**, so for the model we can actually run, the answer to *"how does he measure what training data it uses?"* is **you cannot**. Methods are named with their real limits — Grosse et al. 2023 (EK-FAC to 52B, Anthropic's own) and its own finding that influence collapses when key-phrase order flips; Bae et al. 2022 (influence answers a *proximal Bregman objective*, not leave-one-out). And even perfect TDA attributes a **behaviour**, never a **geometry** — that has no established method at all.

**The inversion:** the only clean route is ablation-by-training, which needs corpus control. We *built* our corpus (`db/emus/chip8/orbits/`, content-addressed, DST-replayable). On the question Aaron asked, our position is the strong one.

## Part 3 — GWT verdict: **structural, with a correction**

Baars 1988 (*A Cognitive Theory of Consciousness*); Dehaene, Kerszberg & Changeux, **PNAS 95(24):14529–14534, 1998**.

**Aaron's "j space" is a precise reference, not a metaphor** — Gurnee et al. (2026), *"Verbalizable Representations Form a Global Workspace in Language Models"*, Transformer Circuits / **arXiv:2607.15495**. It introduces the **Jacobian lens**, defines **J-space**, and **cites Baars and Dehaene explicitly**.

Structural because the evidence is causal, not resemblance — five properties tested by swapping J-lens coordinates, including a **selectivity dissociation** (suppressing J-space breaks reasoning, not text continuation).

**The correction, which matters more than the confirmation:**

> **J-space is 6–10% of activation variance.** The latent space is **not** the workspace — a **small sparse privileged subspace** of it is. And **competition/ignition — the mechanism half of GWT — is untested**, by the authors' own statement.

*(The adjacent paper is named so nobody later mis-cites it: Lindsey, "Emergent Introspective Awareness in LLMs", Oct 2025 — introspection, ~20% on Opus 4.1, mentions neither Baars nor Dehaene. Not the paper Aaron meant.)*
