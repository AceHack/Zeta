## What composes now that did not

A prior audit (#15407) found that earning, spending and withholding **all existed and no call edge joined any two of them**. I verified each of its four findings independently before building on them; all four held. This PR adds the edges.

| # | acceptance criterion | before | after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | budget DERIVED from an attestation-backed ledger read | `RoomBoundary.create` took `privacyBudget: int` from the caller — self-minting with extra steps | `create (ledger) (owner) …` reads `PrivacyLedger.balanceOf owner ledger`. An owner nobody attested opens at **0** and can frost nothing. |
| 2 | `Clear` carries an owner principal and can REFUSE | `BoundaryCommand.Clear` was a **nullary** case dispatched into a **total** function — anyone could strip anyone's frost, and the call could not fail | `Clear of principal`, `GlassHalo.clear requester owner v : Result<_,_>`, `RoomBoundary.clear sink requester b : Result<_, Feedback>` with `Feedback.DefrostDenied` + refusal heat |
| 3 | frost DERIVED from a recorded spend | `SourceMind.personal.frosted: boolean` — free and self-asserted; **every `frosted: true` in the repo was a test/demo literal** | `frost?: FrostReceipt`, branded with a module-private `unique symbol`. Only `spend()` can produce one, and only after the debit reached the book. |
| 4 | a test that FAILS when a non-owner defrosts | none | `END TO END: earn, frost, and a NON-OWNER defrost is REFUSED` (F#) + `A NON-OWNER CANNOT DEFROST` (TS) |

New: `src/Core/PrivacyLedger.fs` and `src/Core.TypeScript/ledger/privacy-budget.ts` — the book, with **matching refusal names** across the two oracles. Neither has a `confiscate` case: the absence is the guarantee, the same way `BroadcastMind` has no field that can hold frosted content.

## ⚠️ I CHANGED A PASSING TEST — read this before trusting the diff

`tests/Tests.FSharp/Formal/GlassHalo.Tests.fs:35`, ``clearing is free and returns to the transparent default``, **passed, and I changed it anyway.** That is normally the exact move this repo forbids, so:

**Why it is legitimate here:** the test pinned behaviour that contradicted its own governing rule. `GlassHalo.clear` took no principal, so *anyone could defrost anyone* and the call could not fail — while `.claude/rules/privacy-budget-is-hard-money-earned-by-others.md` says *"there is no `defrost` that another party can force — only the owner may reveal"* and lists **confiscate — anyone else — never** as the one prohibited operation of three. Two surfaces disagreed; the rule is the governing one, so the test moved rather than the rule.

**What is preserved:** clearing is still *free in budget* and still returns to the transparent default. Only the "anyone may do it" half is gone. The reasoning is also recorded in a block comment at the site, so it survives without this PR body.

**Why the gate went on the primitive, not just `RoomBoundary`:** gating the caller while leaving `GlassHalo.clear` free keeps a bypass around the consent check, and a bypass around a consent check is the vacuity class.

## ⚠️ COOPERATIVE, NOT CRYPTOGRAPHIC — do not let the first be described as the second

This gives frost that is **earned, priced and owner-only**. Those are real properties, mechanically enforced against honest and buggy callers, and they did not exist yesterday.

It does **NOT** give **unconfiscatability**:

- an `Attestation` is a **recorded** claim, not a **signed** one — nothing verifies that the named attestor authored the entry;
- any process that can write the book can write entries in **any name**;
- `requester` in a defrost is a **claimed** identity, not a verified one. The gate refuses an honest or buggy non-owner; it does not withstand a caller that lies about who it is.

Unconfiscatability is **hardware-gated** and the hardware is not here: one YubiHSM in hand, SmartCard-HSM not arrived, and the YubiHSM's measured mechanism list contains **no FROST-capable primitive** (#15407). This boundary is stated in the header of both ledger modules, on `GlassHalo.clear`, on `RoomBoundary.clear`, and in the demo's own closing output — not only here, so it cannot be lost when this PR body scrolls away.

## Honest limits — what this does NOT close

- **The F# boundary reads the book but does not write back to it.** `RoomBoundary.create` derives its budget from the ledger (criterion 1), but `RoomBoundary.frost` debits the boundary's **in-memory** `PrivacyBudget` field and does **not** post a `Spend` entry. So an F#-side frost is priced against an earned balance but is not itself recorded. The **TypeScript** path does close the loop — `spend()` writes the debit, which is why the demo's book above ends with a real `"kind":"spend"` line. Closing it in F# means threading the ledger back out of `frost`, which changes `applyBoundaryCommand` and the tick signature; that is a separate slice, not smuggled into this one. Noted here and at the call site rather than left to be discovered.
- **No `stake`.** The rule names three operations; this ships **spend** and refuses **confiscate**. Staking is explicitly *extra* per the rule and is not needed for any acceptance criterion.
- **`PrivacyEconomy.fs` is still unwired.** It remains the pure `Map<string,int>` the audit described. `PrivacyLedger` is a separate, persisted book; reconciling the two is future work and is not claimed here.
