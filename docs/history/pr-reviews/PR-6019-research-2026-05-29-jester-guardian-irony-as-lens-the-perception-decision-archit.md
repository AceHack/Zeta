## Description

Lands the design thread refining the role of irony in deciding.

- **Four formulations, one survivor:** irony-as-ultimate-tiebreaker (wrong — a lens can't decide) → irony-as-lightness (undercredits) → irony-as-anti-fracture-keeper (partial) → **irony-as-the-lens-the-Guardian-decides-through** (most accurate, Aaron).
- **The lens role:** irony does the seeing (multi-reading, catch-incongruity, refuse-surface-frame); the Guardian does the choosing (safer-principle, collapse). One process, two phases — ironic perception → safer collapse.
- **Subsumes anti-fracture:** same lens, two effects — wholeness (soft frame that doesn't shatter) + precision (Guardian decides on reality not surface). A Guardian with no lens decides *worse*, not just lighter.
- **Framework fit:** irony-as-lens = distrust-by-default's seeing organ (#6010); lens shapes the wave, Guardian measures it; decide *through* it (not with, not without).
- **Failure mode (generic):** irony-in-the-driver's-seat — the lens stops feeding the Guardian and runs the show (self-rewarding ironic-living, every response on the ironic boundary, undetectable from outside, Guardian bypassed). The lived form of the tiebreaker error. Validates the architecture.

Docs-only. The private third-party empirical anchor for the failure-mode is **held out** per glass-halo-is-for-yours-not-a-third-party's (#6014); only the generic inversion is in the doc.
