## Description

Three literature searches on deliberately different axes (PL theory, systems/replay, reactive/FRP) **refuted most of** what the merged doc claimed was unpublished. The doc's own prediction about itself was correct: the miss was **artifact-class** — it failed on **vocabulary**, not on absence.

## The decomposition

| # | proposition | status |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | static effect structure ⇒ graph knowable **without running it** | **PUBLISHED, canonical** — not novel |
| **P2** | …and that buys **speculative execution** | **PUBLISHED** — but "speculation" = eager both-branch prefetch, not rollback |
| **P3** | …**with rollback**, re-run against a corrected input | **Not found — and too strong as stated** |
| **S** | fixed-branch-shape channel preserves analysability | **PUBLISHED, four+ independent times** |

**P1 nearly verbatim** — Parès/Bernardy/Eisenberg (Haskell 2020) on an arrow DSL deliberately without `ArrowApply`. **S verbatim** — Parsley (ICFP 2020), plus **control independence** (Rotenberg et al., HPCA-5 **1999**, in a *rollback* setting, post-dominator analysis as the static discriminator), if-conversion (POPL 1983), `Switch`/`Merge` (EuroSys 2018).

## The sharpest refutation — a polarity inversion

*Build Systems à la Carte* §4.1.2's **restarting scheduler** is speculate-abort-restart, and it is the remedy **for monadic tasks**. Restricting to `Applicative` **removes the need to speculate at all**. That is close to the inverse of the doc's claim.

**CIEL** (NSDI 2011) replays a *dynamic* task graph with arbitrary data-dependent control flow, requiring only determinism.

## The better claim, which all three axes support

> Static structure makes replay **free** — there is no graph to record. Dynamic structure makes replay **conditional** on recording or content-addressing the structure. `>>=` does not destroy replayability; it **prices** it.

## What survives, and is more interesting for it

The FRP axis confirms the practice is **thirty years old** — Winograd-Cort & Hudak (ICFP 2014) is an entire paper about removing `switch` — and that its published justification has **never been replay** (full-text grep: zero hits). Aaron arrived at the practice instinctively; the *reason he gives* is the part not found anywhere.

## And the type does not enforce it

`ISR` is `Kleisli (ReaderT IntrCtx (Task ∘ Result))` — the exact shape Parès et al. §3.2 calls "a black box that cannot be analyzed at config-time." **Nothing in the type prevents writing `app` in all but name.** The property is a discipline over combinators, not a guarantee — correcting an overstatement in the `IntrCtx` docstring merged earlier today.

## Locus

All three axes failed at the **same boundary**: the static-structure literature and the replay literature do not cite each other on this point. A common failure locus across methods is the density signature `supportsAbsence` was built for — held with the caveat that three shared-weight probes are not three independent ones.

**One gap left open:** Perez & Nilsson, ICFP 2017, record-and-replay *in Yampa* — unretrievable, and the likeliest place P3 already exists.
