## The measurement

`drift (loud)` on `main` reported `data/platform-drift.json` pinned at run `32816944713`,
older than every one of the 60 runs in its window. The dashboard was serving numbers from

Tracing it found not one wedged lane but **all four telemetry-flush lanes wedged at once,
from three unrelated causes:**

| PR | lane | open | why the head died |
|---|---|---|---|
| #15276 | `drift-sweep` | 06:37Z | 3 lint shards exited **124** in `tools/setup/linux.sh` — the apt budget ran out on a stalled Azure mirror. **The lints never executed.** `gate (required)` is the rollup of those. |
| #15325 | `society` | 08:21Z | `audit-orphaned-archive-refs`: 5 stranded records vs baseline 3 — stranded *because `pr-archive` was itself wedged*. A cascade. |
| #15327 | `pr-archive` | 08:26Z | same audit |
| #15385 | `tick-metrics` | 16:31Z | head cut inside the 10-min window when `main` carried a TypeScript syntax error (`model-benchmark.ts`, broken 16:28Z by #15384, fixed 16:38Z by #15388). Pinned to a `main` that no longer exists. |

Three causes, none of them the lanes' own content. One shared defect.

## The defect

`chooseFlushRoute` asked exactly one question — *is a PR open on this ref* — and if so
buffered, unconditionally and forever.

The immutable-head design is **correct while the head is under test**: replacing it restarts
every required check and would starve a cadence lane whose period is shorter than the gate.
That premise expires the moment a check reaches a terminal non-success conclusion. Nothing
re-runs it, auto-merge never fires, and waiting is no longer backpressure — it is deadlock.

These lanes are **not** in the self-healing `heartbeat/*-flush-*` class. The flusher logs
`is still testing immutable head …` and parks on `-buffer` by design; `heartbeat/drift-sweep`
had not moved since 06:36:55Z, 11h before. Genuinely wedged, not superseded.

## The fix

`chooseFlushRoute` takes a third input, a `HeadVerdict` from `classifyHeadVerdict` over the
head's check-runs (**the check-runs API, latest-per-name — never `statusCheckRollup`, which
under-reports 6 checks against 43 on the same SHA**):

- **`under-test`** — any check queued/running, all completed checks green, or no checks yet.
  Routes to `buffer`. **Behaviour unchanged.**
- **`dead`** — a latest-per-name check concluded `failure` / `timed_out` / `cancelled` /
  `action_required` / `stale`. New route **`supersede`**: force-push the fresh aggregate onto
  the active ref, moving the open PR's head and starting the full gate from scratch on it.

`cancelled` and `timed_out` count as dead deliberately — they are the shapes a check that
**never ran** takes, and treating "did not run" as "still running" is exactly how this
surface went dark.

## No check is widened

The superseding head runs the **same required checks as any other commit**. Nothing is
excluded, relaxed, skipped, or `--admin`-merged. A lane whose *content* genuinely fails now
stays visibly red on a fresh head every tick instead of hiding behind one stale red from
hours ago — and the `::warning` emitted on supersede says precisely that.

`rerun-cancelled-gate.yml` is **untouched**. Widening it to `failure` would convert real reds
into flaky greens, which its own header correctly refuses; the apt-stalled shards concluded
`failure` (step exit 124), so they were outside its scope by design, not by accident.

The probe **fails closed**: an unreadable answer routes to `under-test` (keep buffering), and
the default argument is the safe one, so a caller that cannot answer the verdict never
acquires force-push behaviour.

## Falsifiers

`flush-via-staging.test.ts` — 49 pass. Both halves die under mutation:

- disabling the `headVerdict === "dead"` branch → **1 fail**
- collapsing the terminal-conclusion set to `failure` alone → **2 fail**

Plus the two guards against over-firing: a head with **no** checks is under-test (else every
head is superseded one second after opening), and only the **latest** run per check name
counts (else a just-repaired head still reads dead).
