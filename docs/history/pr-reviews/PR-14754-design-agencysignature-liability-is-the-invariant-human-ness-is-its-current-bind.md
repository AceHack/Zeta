## Description

A design doc. No code changes; `agencysignature-block.ts` is untouched and
Soraya's `Action-Mode` reconciliation (#14584) stands unchanged.

Four answers, measured against origin/main:

1. LAYERING HOLDS BUT IS NOT THE PRIMARY FIX. `Human-Review` carries two
   different objects in one field — an accountability claim (single-valued per
   change) and a review-evidence claim (per-commit, plural). Layering a
   conflated field preserves the confusion. Split first; layer the causal half
   only. Accountability must NOT layer: if it genuinely differs across
   constituents, the commits should not have been squashed together.

2. THE CARRIER ALREADY LAYERS. The concatenating squash CREATES the stack —
   138 of the last 600 commits carry 2-6 complete blocks; one carries 1,082.
   An extra per-block `Layer-Scope:` key parses and validates today with zero
   schema change (run against the shipped parser, control included). The
   carrier does not forbid layering; the READER does. It is a reader change.

3. THE VOCABULARY IS BACKWARDS AND THE CORPUS PROVES IT. Across 15,527 blocks
   in the last 3,000 commits, `Human-Review: not-implied-by-credential` is
   99.15%. The central field spends 99% of its records saying what did not
   happen, phrased as a negation of the thing named in the key. Record instead
   who is accountable, under whose rules, and what decided —
   `no-directives.md` already names the invariant as *blame*, and already says
   human-only is "for now".

4. MIGRATION IS CHEAPER THAN FEARED. v2 exists on main (75 blocks, `Cell`),
   the auditor runs a dual-accept v1|v2 window with a pinned grandfather
   anchor, and no history was rewritten. Additive keys, dual-accept, no
   rewrite. The real cost is producer coverage, and the v1 corpus is
   permanently silent on accountability — it must be recorded as silent, never
   inferred.

Three findings that are not proposals:

* PR #14430's deadlock DISSOLVES with no code change once the accountability
  claim is constant across its three commits — simulated against the shipped
  `detectBlockDisagreement`: `disagreement: null`.
* A 798KB commit on main carries 1,082 blocks with `Human-Review` at
  'not-implied-by-credential' vs 'explicit' vs 'none' vs 'pending'. It is a
  three-key governance disagreement under today's parser and it landed, while
  #14430 (three blocks, one key) has been blocked for a day. The gate is
  strictest on the smallest records. That same commit's reconciliation
  discards 69 claims of human involvement.
* Six commits on main state in prose that their governance fields were copied
  byte-identical to satisfy the agreement check — the one act
  `maintenance-commit-on-another-agents-branch-carries-no-block.md` forbids
  and says the parser can never detect. Those agents were honest and said so;
  the dishonest version is invisible.

Correction: the raw-vault sentence ("a single version of the facts, never a
single version of the truth") is NOT in `dv2-data-split-discipline-activated.md`
— it is in `anti-babel-preserve-reconcilability.md`. CONSENT-LEDGER.md:240
