## Job 2 — I was asked to refute a claim made to Aaron. It partly fails.

The claim being acted on: *"our metered membrane is not decoherence-avoidance but QEC-with-syndrome-extraction — DD and DFS never look at what the environment did; our membrane records every crossing, which is a measurement."*

**Four corrections. Two of them break it.**

1. **The DFS falsifier FIRES.** The claim supplied it: *if a symmetry group can be named, it IS DFS.* It can — reorder/duplicate/delay against the CRDT fold's permutation + idempotence symmetry, under which the folded value is invariant **by construction**. So part of the membrane is genuinely passive avoidance and the **exclusive form is refuted**. The corrected claim is better and has a metered consequence: the membrane is a **hybrid**, and *the meter is needed exactly where the symmetry runs out*. The published framework for that hybrid already exists — **operator quantum error correction** (Kribs–Laflamme–Poulin 2005).

2. **The reasoning is separately invalid.** **Quantum Zeno** measures and is *still* avoidance, so "avoidance = never measures" is false. The working discriminator is **whether the outcome is USED**. Under that repair the *recording* half does land on the QEC side — and **budget refusal is Zeno-shaped**. That distinction pays: Zeno-shaped protection fails **silently by freezing**, QEC-shaped fails **loudly by miscorrection**. So **a membrane whose refusal rate → 1 is not maximally safe, it is maximally vacuous** — and refusal rate is already metered.

3. **A fourth option fits better than either branch: noiseless subsystems** (Knill–Laflamme–Viola 2000). A DFS says *some states* are safe; an NS says *some factor of every state* is safe — and two carved rules already assert the second without citing it: the **persona/actor** split (*"a bus/routing address is not identity"*) and **`local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold`**, whose stated failure mode is literally *leaking the gauge factor into the protected one*.

4. **The syndrome property is real but transfers weakened.** `[gᵢ, L] = 0` gives *both* independence-from-content *and* no back-action; classically there is no collapse to dodge, so only the first half transfers. Its checkable analogue is **§13 noninterference** (Goguen–Meseguer 1982).

### The demarcation, including the line that cannot be drawn

Structural and earnable: the GF(2) algebra (landed above); "the meter observable carries zero mutual information about the protected content"; the gauge/protected decomposition. **Never claimed:** physical decoherence, measurement back-action, a DFS symmetry against a physical environment.

**And one quantity cannot be placed at all — the most useful finding here, because the whole thesis is that the meter draws that line:**

> **The membrane has no distance** — not because it is unmeasured, but because `d` presupposes *sites*, *weight*, and **independent bounded-weight** noise, and our adversary is correlated and adaptive. It would be easy to compute something distance-shaped (channels an attacker must compromise) and quote it as a QEC distance. That number would be real *and would not mean what `d` means*.

**Handed to Soraya as Conjecture Z-M (CONJECTURE tier, not frozen-core)** with a one-observable falsifier and a stated scheme-independence requirement — and stated so that it is a **noninterference proof obligation over a classical system**, with no quantum content, which is exactly what her tools handle.

**The routing Aaron asked for is still correct** — Job 1 is real work with real falsifiers regardless of how the membrane analogy resolves. But the framing that motivated it needed all four corrections and should be carried in the corrected form.
