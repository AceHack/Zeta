# Summary

Aaron named a methodological pattern on 2026-08-16, writing about the shadow's own sampling error:

> *"this is closely related to middle out — noticing the variance difference and what scaling is correct for the problem."*

This note writes it up: **choose the level of description at which the quantity you care about actually varies**, with the operational corollary that **your sampling unit must match your inferential unit**. Registered as a *methodological observation, not a theorem*.

Six candidate instances were checked against artifacts rather than asserted:

| # | instance | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `cancelled` gate runs | **verified, framing corrected** — 27 superseded vs 10 orphan stalls is the real split; the brief's "recency oversamples cancels" explanation is refuted by the checked-in fixture (most recent 8 terminal runs = 5 success / 2 cancelled / 1 failure) |
| 2 | `test (TS suite)` "flakiness" | **verified** — five dated windows, five causes (PR #11111 body) |
| 3 | `supportsAbsence` probe independence | **verified** — #11033 §2–§3 |

| 5 | heartbeat worst-of-N ref age | **verified** — `heartbeat/red-state` is a 6-hourly dashboard lane (`cron: "23 */6 * * *"`), not a per-tick liveness lane |
| 6 | DORA per agent per grant regime | **open** — design intent, not a corrected error; kept and labelled |

Anchor triage per `anchor-to-human-prior-art` (checked, not attached): **Kish 1965 design effect**, **length-biased sampling / inspection paradox**, and **Robinson 1950 ecological fallacy** pass entailment and are cited with the entailment stated. **Renormalization group** and **Wolfram computational irreducibility** are marked analogy/boundary-condition only. **Simpson's paradox** (no sign reversal in any instance) and **Amdahl 1967** (about serial fractions, not sampling units) are **rejected** with reasons in the note.

Per `numerology-vs-number-theory`, the note discloses that all six instances come from one observer, one day, one repo — a correlated sample, so this names a pattern and writes a check rather than claiming a law.
