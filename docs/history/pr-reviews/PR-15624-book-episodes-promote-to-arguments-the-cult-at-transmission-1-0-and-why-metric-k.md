## `THE-PROMOTION-AND-THE-CULT-…` (493 lines)

**Part I — two registers of autobiography.** Arguments rederive without memory; episodes do not.
So his self-discount (*"best guesses at my memories, not facts"*) is **structural, not modest** —
it lands exactly where rederivation cannot help and is withheld where he has a better instrument.
Episodes then **promote**: *"sometimes episodes upgrade into arguments later in life."* That is
`numerology-vs-number-theory` applied to a life — store the register with the resonance, and when
structure arrives, say what promoted it.

His criterion is **external**, which is the good part: *"the ability to transmit the understanding
to multiple others"* — dated, witnessed, falsifiable, unlike *when I understood it*. **"Multiple"
is load-bearing** (correlated receivers are one receiver counted several times). **Boundary kept:**
transmissibility promotes an episode to an **argument**, never an argument to **true**.

**The discipline on the text:** a promoted episode must record **when** and **what** promoted it,
or it back-projects present understanding onto the past self — an undetectable falsification that
always flatters in one direction. Three **verified** in-repo precedents cited, best of them the
hat/persona/role doc's explicit *"recorded as a change of vocabulary, not back-projected onto the
earlier rows."* Consequence: premature narrativisation destroys promotability, so unexplained
episodes are **pending promotions, not filler**.

**Part II — the degenerate case.** His report, his place, his period. Completes the band: ρ→0
exile (Michell 1783 — reinvented rather than inherited) and ρ→1 cult. **Mapped precisely against
`VISION.md`'s existing band rather than atmospherically** — exile is the single-sender case,
narrower than the Babel row, and the file says so.

**Load-bearing claim, stated first and needing no oracle:** erasing others destroys your own
error-correction. `N_eff = 1`, no meter; **immediate, not deferred**, and invisible because
everything you say now meets agreement. **Guarded against the corpus's own known worst error** —
`VISION.md` records `N_eff` as the *wrong* instrument for adversarial coordination; the use here is
the legitimate **redundancy** case and says so explicitly.

Carries his line, quoted at his request, placed **only after the liability is established**:

> **"Men and women bend to my will. Time does not."**

The **handling rule is in the text**, not only in an editor's note — both clauses or neither, never
the first alone, never as an opener — because halved at the comma it becomes precisely the
megalomania the section exists to warn about.

The Egg stays **labelled as his oracle**. The structural guard (weight-free / scale-free /
Multi-Oracle) is cited **without importing** `RAW-2026-08-04-HELD`, whose **HELD/OPTIONAL status is
unchanged**. And the section says outright that a guard does not acquit anyone, since *"I know I
could do this, so I built guards"* is also what the sophisticated version says.

## `THE-METRIC-IS-A-PHONE-BOOK-…` (256 lines)

**"All metric knowledge should be open source."** From: *"it was actually faster to game for me,
cause i had many others in the industry tell me the metrics from an early age."*

**Gameability is not a property of the metric; it is a property of who knows it.** A precise metric
is *harder* to game from outside the information network and *easier* from inside — which inverts
the fairness argument: one rule for everyone, advantage distributed by **information access**, and
the inequity invisible because the rule really is identical. Same confound the corpus already named
about **free time as an inherited endowment** (the 2026-08-19 ΔU-denominator doc): a meter cannot
separate capability from opportunity.

Hiding metrics is **strictly worse** — obscurity converts an information advantage into a permanent
one. Fix: **publish the attack surface alongside the metric**, anchored on **Kerckhoffs 1883** with
the entailment spelled out rather than punned, and the verification debt named. Honest counter
kept: disclosure *does* increase gaming short-term; what it buys is that the vector gets closed.

Worked instance is this repo, unintentionally: the vacuity findings **are** published attack
knowledge, and **mutation testing is the same move made mechanical**.
