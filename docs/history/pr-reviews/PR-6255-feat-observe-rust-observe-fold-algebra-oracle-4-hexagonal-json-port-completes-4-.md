## Hexagonal JSON ingestion (ports & adapters)

Per your 2026-05-31 directive — *we drive the interface; external deps adapt into ours; we never depend on their interfaces*:

- **Port (ours):** `Json` (our AST) + `JsonParser` (our trait). The algebra + mapping depend only on these.
- **`ZetaJsonParser`** — our own small, zero-dependency, idiomatic recursive-descent parser. The **production default** (supply-chain doctrine: zero external deps). Day-one minimal (owned `String`); low-alloc/zero-copy is the eventual goal, not day-one.

Nothing outside the adapter (`from_serde`) ever names a `serde_json` type, so the crate never *depends on* serde's interface — only its implementation, behind our port. Lets serde drop-in-replace ours for systems already on it.
