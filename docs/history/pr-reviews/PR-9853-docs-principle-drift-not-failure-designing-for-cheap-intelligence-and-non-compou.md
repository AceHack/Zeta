## Description

Two connected framings from Aaron, made durable.

**The vocabulary selects a mechanism, not a mood.** Calling a red tip a *failure* imports the corporate frame — incident, blame, therefore a **gate** (prevention by a central authority). Calling it **drift** states what it is: a deviation to be **noticed and healed**. Zeta already owns the algebra (retraction) and the harness laws (#9817: idempotence, closure-as-subset, convergence).

**The inversion:** conventional practice prevents mistakes with expensive review up front; Zeta makes the **low-intelligence contributor the design center** — mistakes cheap to catch, impossible to compound — then spends intelligence only where drift concentrates. Hence: legibility > cleverness; **loud failure > silent competence** (a silent drop is what compounds — cf. today's ZAtom all-or-nothing and the `Meno.Bind` fix).

**Proposed loop (not built):** caught drift → rate per area → model tier / review depth. **Inverted DORA** — optimize how *little* intelligence a task needs. Inputs all exist; the join doesn't. Work-item `081KYYJSRXA…` names two constraints up front: **exposure in the denominator** (else quiet-because-untouched areas get starved) and attribution to **mechanisms, not contributors**.

Boundaries kept honest: non-compounding is a **target**, not an achieved invariant (fails for false doc-comments, which compound by being *read*); no-downtime needs the receipt rung built; the energy claim is directional, not measured. 🤖 Generated with [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code)
