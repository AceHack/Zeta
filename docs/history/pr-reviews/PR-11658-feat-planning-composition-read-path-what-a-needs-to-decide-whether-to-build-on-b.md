## Description

The **consumption** half of Aaron's ask (2026-08-17): *"lego like building for one intelligence to connect together and build on other intelligences based on past reliability."* Given a consumer A and a candidate provider B, this reports what is **conferred** about B in the capability A needs — and offers **no verdict**. The identity-claim contradiction detector is a separate lane; nothing here touches the `SelfClaim` type.

## 1. Measurement first — the header of `observe/self-claims.ts` claims three consumers, and all three are unwired

Method: find the reliability score's definition, then every non-test caller.

| header claim | status | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| (1) *"Other agents use to schedule their own work — if A depends on B's output, A checks B's reliability"* | **UNWIRED** | `computeReliability`: **0 non-test callers**. Every hit is `self-claims.ts` itself, its own test, or two `docs/DECISIONS/` rows. `schedulingWindowForDependency`: **0 non-test callers**. |
| (2) *"The scheduling system uses to extend or shrink the agent's window τ"* | **UNWIRED** | `ferry-throttler/optimal-cadence.ts::adjustPressureByReliability` takes a bare `windowMultiplier: number` and its only callers are its own tests. Both halves of the pipe exist; nothing joins them. |
| (3) *"The fleet KPI overlay uses as the DORA-like measure"* | **UNWIRED** | no consumer of `ReliabilityScore` outside tests. |

The **one real consumer** of `self-claims.ts` is `planning/calibration-bridge.ts`, and it consumes the **ledger** (`recordClaim` / `markClaimMet` / `resolveAtTick`) — never the score. It folds outcomes into `calibration-ledger.ts` and `traveler-rank-ledger.ts` instead.

Two `docs/DECISIONS/` rows mark both functions "✅ Shipped". True of the function, false of the wiring. The header is corrected in this PR (an additive `MEASURED 2026-08-17` note; the type is untouched).

**And the deeper reason not to simply wire lane (1):** `computeReliability` reads only the claiming agent's *own* claims, and `markClaimMet` records **no labeler**. Its number is self-asserted input, not conferred — which is exactly the property a composition surface must have. So the read-path reads the conferred lane instead.

## 2. Reconciliation with `TravelerRankLedger` — extend, do not compete

Nothing is re-derived. `readDependency` calls `attribute()` from `competence-attribution.ts` (inheriting its canonical order, its self-labeled guard, and its jurisdiction weighting) and imports `temperedUpdate` / `trustBand` / `freshBelief` from `traveler-rank-ledger.ts` (the TS port of `src/Core/TravelerRankLedger.fs`) as the estimator. A composition read and a rank read cannot silently diverge.

What is genuinely new is the **shape of the answer**: addressed to a *consumer* with a *specific capability need*, rather than a number about a person.

The ledger's domain isolation is preserved rather than flattened. `DependencyRead` splits B's record into a `binding` block (the queried capability, weight 1) and one `persuasive` block **per** other scope, each with its own posterior. They are never summed — collapsing them is what would let standing as a verifier buy standing as a signer. `binding === undefined` with non-empty `persuasive` is a first-class answer: *"B has a record, but not in what you asked for."*

## 3. The threshold trap

Not answered, deliberately — the same reasoning as `chip9/consult-census.ts`, which computes a total-variation distance and returns no verdict.

- **The module exports zero numeric constants.** Mechanically tested (`typeof v === "number"` over the module's own exports must be empty).
- A caller who genuinely needs a gate supplies a `DependencyPolicy`, and `applyPolicy` **refuses** one whose `derivedFrom` is empty. An unattributed gating constant cannot be minted through this surface — no 113th, after the 112 that #11534 found.
- `readCompositionChain` returns per-link reads plus `unknownLinks` / `offCapabilityLinks` and **no aggregate**. A product of per-link trust bands would assert independence between links nobody measured, and reads as *more* confident the longer the chain — the look-elsewhere effect wearing a score.

## 4. The `unknown` case

`DependencyRead` is a two-constructor union and `unknown` **carries no score field** — a caller must narrow on `kind` to reach any number, so a no-track-record provider cannot be read as good *or* bad. Its `note` says so in words, and `explain()` renders it as *"not a low score and not a clean record; it is an absence."* This matches `readCompetence`'s `prior-only` case and the honest-`0.5`-prior discipline that closes the whitewash window in `TravelerRankLedger`.

Voluntariness is intact: a participant who has never claimed anything simply reads `unknown`, which costs them nothing and is not a penalty. Participation is never made compulsory by this surface.

Humans and AI: `participantId` is an opaque string throughout. Nothing assumes a model, prompt, tick, or process.

## 7. What is deliberately not done

Reconciling the three unwired self-claims lanes. Either claim resolution gains a conferred determiner (a `labeler`, as `OutcomeRecord` already requires) or it stays a voluntary self-report that a consumer must not depend on. That is a design call, and the read-path does not need it resolved.
