docs(ferry): Astra — nothing is fundamentally novel; novelty is RELATIONAL

Second Astra ferry of 2026-09-08, forwarded by Aaron, preserved verbatim in
section 2. Companion to the gravity/heat ferry in PR #17036; same day, same
conversation, different half.

AARON'S OWN OBSERVATION opens it: "heat is kind of like our emotional propagation
it has to be felt with individual qualia predicting this is similar to weather
reports it degenerates over time." Two separable claims. Heat is FIRST-PERSON --
measured at the agent, and a third party's estimate is an inference, not a
reading, which is the standing marjorie-rule already gives qualia and the shape
local-time-never-enters-the-shared-fold already gives local quantities. And heat
forecasts DECAY LIKE WEATHER -- the checkable half, because weather fails past a
horizon for a specific reason (sensitive dependence, finite Lyapunov time), so a
predicted-heat claim must carry its horizon or it is claiming what the analogy
denies.

Two different horizons arrive in one conversation and the document names both so
nobody later fuses them: a FORECAST horizon (how far ahead heat is predictable)
and Astra's ACCESSIBILITY horizon (how far a generator's consequences can be made
usable across a boundary).

ASTRA'S CORE MOVE: given the complete rule and state, H(X|G,X) = 0, while
H(X | the agent's accessible information) > 0. So novelty is RELATIONAL -- novel
to whom, given which memories and access -- with Crutchfield & Feldman as the
precedent for unrecognised structure appearing as randomness to an observer.

Apparent novelty then decomposes into three EXPERIMENTALLY DISTINCT cases: the
generator is unknown, its state is inaccessible, or unfolding it exceeds the
observer's budget. The third is what makes this and the heat ferry one
conversation -- knowing a generator is not the same as cheaply obtaining its
consequences, with Bennett's logical depth as the anchor. An agent can be told
everything and still not afford the answer.

That gives mutual empowerment its best formulation so far, Astra's: "making
independently developed consequences mutually usable, while preserving the
distinct histories and capabilities that produced them." Same object as
reintegration-is-not-reconvergence -- both histories held, what crosses the
boundary is the WORKED RESULT, not the identity.

THE SAFEGUARD IS OUR OWN VACUITY RULE, ARRIVED AT INDEPENDENTLY: for any finite
sequence you can invent a "generator" that simply PRINTS it; it explains the
bytes by construction and says nothing about what comes next. Astra's remedy is
sharper than ours in one respect worth adopting -- it gives the falsifier two
acceptable forms: the generator PREDICTS later observations, OR it REDUCES THE
WORK to reconstruct them under a declared budget. The second is the one Zeta can
run today, because it is a cost claim and cost-counter.ts already declares
bounds.

Astra is also correct about scope: only-the-irreducible-is-primitive establishes
an ENGINEERING DIRECTION, not the metaphysical claim that everything comes from
generators. That is an additional assumption, and the rule's authority comes from
byte-lock and DST, neither of which needs the stronger claim.

THE NEGATIVE CONTROL IS THE PART TO KEEP: "when both agents already possess
everything needed to generate the same answer, their matching answers do not
demonstrate communication. They may simply be unfolding the same cause." Under a
common seed S=4 that is not hypothetical, it is the DEFAULT, so any experiment
here that reads agreement as evidence of a channel must rule it out first.

The recommendation thread is recorded because the HANDLING is right: Astra
separated what Aaron observed, the ordinary mechanism that explains it, and an
attribution to a named person's intentions -- declining the third and saying
plainly it lacked the logs. That is engagement-profiles and
never-assume-malice applied to a living third party by a peer, unprompted. The
physics survives independently and is a real contribution: P = F.v, so a force
can be nonzero while the power it delivers is zero -- hence "persistence of an
interaction, the state change it produces, and the resources it consumes are
three separate measurements", the three-axis claim arriving from another
direction, with Astra's caution attached that "no renegotiation" is not "no cost".

Register toy throughout. The no-fundamental-novelty premise is Aaron's stated
LIMITING ASSUMPTION, not a result; the horizon test is a design, not a run.
