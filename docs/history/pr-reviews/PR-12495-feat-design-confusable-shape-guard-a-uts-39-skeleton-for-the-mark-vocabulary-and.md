## The sharpening

`docs/research/2026-08-14-branch-free-visual-encoding-*.md` layered the ways a rendered surface can
lie and resolved faithfulness to **injectivity of the encoder** `e : data -> geometry`. Its own
table names layer 4 (perception) as **"anchored, unapplied."**

That is right and it is half the composition. The reader never receives `e(x)`; they receive
`p(e(x))`, where `p` is the **perceptual quotient** — everything the eye discards at a glance. So:

> **`p . e` must be injective, not `e`.**

An encoder can be perfectly injective and still deliver two meanings as one percept.

**The frame this sits in:** shapes buy escape from etymological drift (the tower-of-babel failure)
because a mark is a referent that does not route through shared vocabulary. The failure mode does
not disappear — it **relocates** to perceptual collision, and it changes character. A word-level
divergence announces itself; two parties argue, and the argument is the signal. **A confusable mark
produces a confident misread with no hesitation.** That is why it is a spoofing surface rather than
a usability wrinkle, and it is exactly the transfer from Aaron's magic study: a force works because
the spectator is certain they chose freely.

## The criterion

> Two marks that mean different things must differ in a channel that survives the quotient — and
> **which** channel is set by the semantic distance. A difference in **claim class** is carried by
> **base form**; a difference *within* a class may be carried by **fill fraction**; **hue is never a
> channel on its own**; outline style and fill texture are not channels at all at mark size.

Not a preference — it falls out of a capacity bound. The quotient admits ~36 reachable cells, so
**the mark alphabet is scarce**: you cannot escape babel by minting more shapes, because minting more
shapes drives you into the quotient. Base-form separation gets spent on the boundaries that must
never be confused. Anchored to Bertin's nominal/ordered split.

## Register, stated plainly

- **unmetered** — *that `o` and a dotted `o` are confusable to a human at 12px*. That is the

- **refused anchors**, named so nobody promotes them later: **JND/Weber** is defined on a magnitude

## The fix

**The rule, now asserted in tests rather than described in prose:**

> **BASE FORM carries the CLAIM CLASS. FILL FRACTION carries the gradation within a class.**

| member | claim | was | now | skeleton |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| live · stale · cold | observation | ● ◐ ○ | unchanged | circle / full · partial · empty |
| heat | observation | ◆ | unchanged | diamond/full — the alarm should not read as a *degree* |
| unavailable | model | ∅ | unchanged | circle/empty/**struck** |
| unobserved | withheld | ◌ | **□** | square/**empty** |
| sealed | withheld | ◍ | **▨** | square/**partial** |
| frost | withheld | ▨ | **■** | square/**full** |

Withheld fill tracks *how much is actually there*: nothing measured → exists but no operational
content yet → content present and withheld. `frost` is the most-filled mark because it is the only
member with something behind it. `unavailable` ∅ is the one permitted cross-class base-form
sharing, separated by a strike that survives blur — and that exemption is itself pinned by a test.

**This is better than the fix I proposed**, and the difference is recorded rather than quietly
improved: work-item `081M0DN91RK087G0R002X8MBWM` assigned `sealed` U+25A9 and left `sealed`/`frost`
colliding as a within-class **warning**. Ordering the register by fill removes that too — **zero
collisions of any grade**, not zero errors and one warning. The improvement came from taking the
criterion seriously, not from new information.

**A regression I introduced and closed in the same change.** `renderStateText`'s unknown-member
fallback drew `◌` — which, once `unobserved` moved to a square, became **`cold`'s silhouette**. A
failed map lookup would have drawn itself as the observation *"watched, and nothing is there."* It
now renders `◇` (diamond/empty), a base form no member uses, pinned by a test.

**`state-du.test.ts:128` is joined, not replaced.** Both properties are wanted: distinct strings
**and** distinct silhouettes. Added alongside it — skeleton-uniqueness, the base-form rule, the
strike exemption, and the unknown-fallback guard.

**ASCII is now mechanically checked (TIER 3)** — a reassignment that fixes the visual and collides
the fallback has moved the bug. The shipped fallbacks pass, and the check fires on a control for
both exact collision and monospace-confusable interiors (`(!)` vs `(|)`). Worth noting: the ASCII
channel was already the **better-designed** one — a fixed-width frame around a distinct interior
character, injective by construction. The constrained channel forced what the rich one did not. My
first draft called `(!)`/`(/)` *"within tolerance in my judgement"*; judgement is what a guard is
supposed to replace, and the quotient now makes that a checkable claim.

**Surfaces touched** — a partial rename is worse than none, because it leaves two vocabularies:
`state-du.ts`, `state-du.test.ts`, `state-du-css.test.ts` (which now reads
`stateMember("sealed").glyph` instead of hardcoding a mark, so the next reassignment is one file),

## The circuit collision — which finding it is

**Not "the generator emitted the wrong bytes", and not "the catalogue had a duplicate row."** It is
that **a single CHSH corner *is* a coincidence measurement** at those analyzer angles — nothing
distinguished them because there was nothing to distinguish. Minting a second name for one
experiment was the error.

So the repair draws a corner a coincidence measurement *cannot* be: `singletChsh` now renders
**E(a1,b1)** (`a1 = π/2`, `b1 = −π/4`) — the only corner carrying the `−1` coefficient in
`S = E(a0,b0) + E(a0,b1) + E(a1,b0) − E(a1,b1)`, with both analyzers rotated. The angles are the
canonical singlet configuration already pinned at `quantum-observable.test.ts:38`, so the file now
agrees with the oracle's own corner table instead of restating one of its rows twice. Regenerating
changed **only** that golden; the other three came back byte-identical.

**Honest residual, filed not buried** (`081M0DVFPSK087G0R002CRCV6G`): ferry 25 says this SVG is
in-tree *"precisely to draw the gap between Bertlmann's socks and the singlet."* One corner cannot
— the gap is `S = 2` vs `S = 2√2`, which exists only across all four settings, and any *single*
corner is reproducible by a preassigned-bit model. This fix makes the picture honest about being
one corner; it does not make it that falsifier. The real falsifier is a test that computes `S`,
and it already exists.
