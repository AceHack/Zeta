## Description

## What this removes

`.claude/hooks/session-start-cron-verify.ts` printed a **twenty-line notice about the tick loop into every session's context at wake time**. That was the only thing the hook did.

> Aaron 2026-09-09: *"there is no need to have in claude.md or anywhere to loop the agent on startup, most of the time when you loop you just sit there and say nothing to do even when there are 1000s of things to do. this is why we need our harness and such."*

## Two costs, and the second is the real one

- **Cold-start tokens on every wake, by every agent in the fleet.** This is what `rules-are-small-carved-sentences-pointing-to-docs` exists to prevent, and a twenty-line hook banner was the largest single violation of it on the startup surface.
- **It nudged agents toward arming a loop whose observed output is a tick reporting "nothing to do" against a backlog that is manifestly not empty.** A loop that cannot find work in a repo this size is not measuring the repo — it is measuring itself. Routing is the harness's job, not a banner's.

## Deliberately NOT removed

- **The loop registry** (`src/Core.TypeScript/agent-loops/loop-registry.ts`). The exit mechanism it provides is real and unaffected — only the unsolicited advertisement of it is gone.
- **The AGENTS.md heartbeat-via-commit lines.** Those govern what a tick must *produce* once one is running; they are not a startup imperative to arm one.
- **Historical references** under `docs/hygiene-history/` and `docs/history/pr-reviews/`. Those are append-only records of what happened; rewriting them to match the present would falsify the ledger rather than update a pointer.
