## Description

Aaron's live-page report, verified and fixed:

- **"The screen is very small now"** — real regression from D1: the
  overlay wrapper (added so frost/boxes/fixation share the canvas's
  coordinate space) was UNSIZED, and `.screen-container canvas` is
  styled `100%×100%` *of its parent* — so the whole screen collapsed to
  the canvas's intrinsic 64×32. The wrapper now fills the 16:9 box.
  Measured after the fix: canvas 1214×682 inside a 1214×683 container,
  `canvasFillsScreen: true`.
- **"Weird messages when I click it"** — that's D5's WHY chain (the
  spec's Stump-Dad button: click the agent, get one reason; click
  again, the next reason down, bottoming out at "I don't know."). Two
  legibility fixes: the strip now renders BELOW the screen instead of
  inside the fixed 16:9 overflow-hidden flex box it was fighting, and
  it says who is speaking — `🧠 browser-node · why? I am opening the
  gap from the big shape — it is 6.40 pixels away…` — because an
  unlabelled sentence under a game screen reads as a glitch, not as
  the agent answering the question it was asked.
- **"The buttons still go pretty crazy"** — the glow inputs update
  every ~32 ms frame; `.face-btn`'s 100 ms transition relayed the
  flicker and the d-pad/triggers had none at all. All three button
  classes now transition at 250 ms (opacity/transform/box-shadow/
  background), integrating per-frame churn into a pulse without
  changing what the glow shows.

Verified: app tsc rc=0, eslint 0 on the app tree, vite build green;
headless smoke asserts the canvas fills the screen box, the strip is
outside the screen container and hidden until clicked, and the click
renders the labeled answer below the screen.
