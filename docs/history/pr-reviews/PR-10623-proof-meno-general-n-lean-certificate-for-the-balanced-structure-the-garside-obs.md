## Description

## The routing call that made it cheap

The item predicted step 2 (`Delta_{m+n}^2 = (Delta_m^2 (x) Delta_n^2) . c_{n,m} . c_{m,n}`, general `n`) would need Garside normal-form machinery Mathlib does not carry, and pre-authorised a bounded `decide` fallback.

Taking the statement into **Mathlib's `BraidedCategory`** instead of into `B_n` directly moves the problem from braid combinatorics (no Mathlib support) to coherence algebra (excellent Mathlib support). The general-`m,n` result then falls out of the hexagon axioms alone via four Yang-Baxter rewrites. Cost of the wrong call would have been the predicted couple of days for a strictly weaker result.

## Where I stopped, and why

**Not certified:** that a `Twist` INSTANCE exists on `<V>`. That needs the braid groupoid as the free braided monoidal category on one object (Joyal-Street 1993) plus faithfulness of the Artin action (Artin 1925) — neither in Mathlib, and porting the ZSet monoidal category is out of bounds by `MenoBraidedRMatrix`'s standing boundary. So `theta`'s naturality (equivalently centrality of `Delta_n^2` in `B_n`, Chow 1948) is a **field** of `Twist`, assumed rather than derived. Also: the inhabitation witness is symmetric, so it does not exercise `dbl != id`.

Both tracked in the follow-up 081M00EZXN2087G0R003AY3WSJ, which recommends the cheap option (b), a non-symmetric witness, over the expensive option (c).
