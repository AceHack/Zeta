## Description

Routes the gap named in `docs/VISION.md` §"Echolocation over time": decorrelation fails from two opposite ends of the time axis, and only the past end (the S=4 common seed) has an instrument. The future end — **mimetic desire** (Girard) — has none. Routing review; no specs written.

## The headline is a negative, and it is the load-bearing claim

**Mimetic convergence is not identified from observational choice data.** Two agents picking the same target *because it is genuinely most valuable* and two agents picking it *because the other picked it* emit identical observations from different causal structures.

- **Manski 1993**, the reflection problem — endogenous social effects are not identified separately from correlated effects.
- **Shalizi & Thomas 2011** — homophily and contagion are generically confounded, **non-parametrically**. Not fixable by a better estimator or more data.

Our fleet has latent homophily in its strongest possible form: **the S=4 common seed is the textbook unobserved common trait**. So convergence and agreement are **not separable observationally** — stated plainly rather than papered over with a statistic.

## The anchor gap was the real finding

`docs/GLOSSARY.md` already carries Girard's *mechanism*. A repo-wide `rg` returns **zero** hits for Manski, Shalizi, homophily, or "reflection problem". Holding the mechanism literature without the identification literature is exactly how a fleet builds the obvious forward meter and believes it. Added Manski, Shalizi & Thomas, Pearl ch. 3, Kish, and Goguen–Meseguer to `docs/PRIOR-ART-LIST.md`.

## One observable rejected mechanically

"Have agents declare their next target" is the natural design and is **worse than none**: a public declaration board **is** Girard's subject → model edge, built on purpose and pointed at everyone. It creates the coupling, raises the true correlation, then reports the correlation it caused — an instrument whose bias points at its own alarm. Work-item claims are rejected too: a shared priority queue is Manski's *correlated effect*, and a healthy fleet correctly prioritising would score as **maximally mimetic** — a false red on the good case.

## The observable accepted

Not *what they picked* — **how much peer-visibility changes the pick, with the value landscape held fixed.** Identified by construction; the confound is absent rather than adjusted-for.

- **DST gives the counterfactual for free** — the ideal randomised experiment social science cannot run.
- **Ablation is marginal-preserving substitution, not deletion.** Deletion is detectable; the C3 theorem (marginals do not determine the joint — Hoeffding / Fréchet / Sklar) is what makes substitution undetectable by marginals.
- **A placebo arm is mandatory.** Agents are LLMs, and **DST determinism covers the substrate, not the model** — model noise alone yields `m > 0` and would read as mimesis.
