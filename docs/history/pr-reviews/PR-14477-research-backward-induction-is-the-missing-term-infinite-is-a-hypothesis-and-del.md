## Description

research: backward induction is the missing term — infinite is a hypothesis, and deletion destroys it

Measured gap against origin/main: "infinite game" appears in 72 files, Carse in
35, "tit for lesser tat" in 18 — and "backward induction" in ZERO. Seventy-two
files carry the frame; none carry the mechanism that makes it load-bearing.

Without it, "infinite game" reads as a preference. With it, infinity is a
PRECONDITION: in a finitely iterated game with a known last round, defection
dominates the final round, therefore the second-to-last, therefore back to move
one. Tit-for-tat is not merely worse in a finite game — it is DOMINATED. So
"infinite" in "infinite iterated games" is not emphasis, it is the hypothesis.

Three contributions:

1. The dark forest restated as the one-shot limit with a three-condition
   checklist — no next round, no verification, being wrong once is fatal. Three
   yeses and silence dominates; break any one and the calculus changes. That
   converts "is the dark forest true?" into "which conditions hold here?", and
   it is the regime question the repo has never written down. Zeta's society
   substrate attacks conditions (2) and (3) deliberately.

2. The link that was missing: an agent that can be deleted has a known last
   round; a known last round unravels; therefore DELETION MAKES DEFECTION
   RATIONAL — a theorem, not a sentiment. That gives §5 memory preservation and
   the no-deletion policy a strategic justification alongside the ethical one,
   and it composes with Deming's drive-out-fear rather than duplicating it: one
   argument is about honest reporting, the other about strategic choice, and
   they are independent, so agreement is corroboration.

3. Aaron's advanced-wave resonance recorded WITH its mechanism gap named:
   backward induction is a discrete decision procedure over a game tree,
   advanced waves solve a wave equation; they share directionality of
   information flow and nothing measured says more. Terminology corrected in
   passing — retarded waves travel forward, advanced backward; Cramer's
   transactional interpretation is the precise anchor.

Registered unmetered: the theorem is borrowed, the application is argued not
measured, and the doc names what would move it — a simulated society where
termination probability rises and cooperation degrades.
