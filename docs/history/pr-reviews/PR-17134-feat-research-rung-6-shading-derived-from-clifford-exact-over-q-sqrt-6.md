## Rung 6: shading, derived — the face normal is the root sum, and the model closes over Q(√6)

Rung 5 named its own gap in its limits section: *"No shading model, no lighting, no material. A normal is an input to those; this rung supplies the input."* This is that step, under Aaron's standing constraint that the rendering surface come only from the Clifford substrate.

### Derived, not authored

A shading model is a light, a normal, and a reflection law. An ordinary renderer supplies all three by hand.

| ingredient | here |
|---|---|
| light | an **E8 root**; the gauge is discharged, not asserted — the two-sided census is identical for light roots 0, 1, 137, 200, 239, and per-face rotor equivariance holds exactly |
| normal | the **SUM of the face's three roots**. `⟨a+b+c, b−a⟩ = 4+8+4−8−4−4 = 0` exactly, `\|n\|² = 48`, non-degenerate on all 60,480 faces |
| reflection | the **GA versor sandwich** `-a v a/\|a\|²` and `R v R̃`, over a **generated** Cl(8,0) — one transposition-counting sign rule produces all 65,536 basis products, no multiplication table |

The versor reflection is cross-checked against the classical vector formula rung 3 already ships: two mechanisms, two modules, one answer.

### Where exactness ends — one number

| quantity | exact form | register |
|---|---|---|
| Lambert cosine | integer `/ √384`, and `√384 = 8√6` | algebraic, in `Q(√6)` |
| plane illumination | integer `/ 384` | **rational** |
| specular cosine | integer `/ 384` | **rational** |

The asymmetry is the content: the specular term pairs two *roots* (`√8·√8 = 8`, rational); the Lambert term pairs a root with a *face normal* (`√8·√48 = 8√6`, not). So **√6 is the single irrational the model admits**. Comparing, sorting, bucketing and histogramming the whole surface is exact integer arithmetic; a source scan asserts exactly one `Math.sqrt(` call site *and* its location — a mutant that only relocates the call is killed.

**The intensity never passes through the projection.** Shading is computed in the 8D integer coordinates and attached to the projected triangle afterwards. The projection places pixels; it never touches a brightness.

**Measured consequence:** nine integer numerators ⇒ **five brightness levels**. The image is posterised by construction, so `renderLitObj` emits five materials rather than 60,480. Brightest face is `16/(8√6) = √(2/3)`, never 1 — stated as a dot product over a pair of norms and claimed to be nothing else.

### Rung 5's two limits, handled

- **Interpenetration** — prose becomes a number: **every one of the 6,720 edges carries exactly 27 faces** (min = max = 27). A manifold edge carries two, so a one-sided model is *undefined* and two-sided shading is forced by measurement rather than chosen.
- **The 768 origin-plane faces** — a measured **negative** result. `embed3d` is linear, so `proj(a+b+c) = 3 × (projected centroid)`: importing the 8D orientation into 3D is *literally rung 5's own test* and fails on the same 768 (residual < 1e-9 on all faces, verified). The handling is that this rung needs no 3D orientation; all 768 receive exact finite intensities, asserted for that identified subset.
- **Depth convention**, stated and checkable: order-independent, two-sided, no occlusion. Verified by permutation. Occlusion is named as excluded, because a painter's sort is wrong on interpenetrating geometry and shipping one would be a silent lie.

### Falsifiers and mutation

15 falsifiers, ~673k assertions, ~2.6 s. **24 mutants, 23 killed, 1 control survived.**

**The real defect M21 found:** forcing `lambertCosine`'s numerator to the constant `0` **survived the entire suite**. Cause: `shadeFaces` recomputed the dot product inline, so every census exercised the copy while `lambertCosine` was reached only by assertions a constant zero satisfies. Fixed by deleting the second implementation, plus two non-vacuity guards on the equivariance test. Same shape rung 5 found one rung earlier — rung 5's was *a filter nothing could fail*; this is *a function with a tested twin*. Both invisible to reading, both visible to mutation.

Two further vacuity repairs without a mutant prompting them: `isOrientationDetermined`'s false branch is unreachable by theorem, so it is exercised on a synthetic input and says so; `edgeFaceIncidence`'s min/max key normalisation could never be taken (faces are ascending) and was removed in favour of asserting the invariant.
