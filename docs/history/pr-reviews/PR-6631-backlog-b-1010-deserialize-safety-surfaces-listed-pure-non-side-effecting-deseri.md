## What

Captures Aaron's forward directive from the C12 codec-algebra review (#6629):

> *"Serialize with side effects we are going to have safe deserialize surfaces listed eventually for pure non side-effecting stuff, we can backlog deserialize safety as backlog."*

**The asymmetry** the C12 `product` short-circuit surfaced:

| Direction | Trust | Side effects |
|---|---|---|
| **Serialize** (our value → wire) | input trusted | MAY have side effects — `product` short-circuits a doomed encode |
| **Deserialize** (wire → our value) | input often **untrusted** (Eve transport / strangers) | must route to a **pure** subset — decoding attacker bytes through an effectful `Deserialize` is RCE-shaped |

Proposes: a **purity witness** on the codec port, a **registered set** of deserialize-safe surfaces, **routing** so untrusted-wire decode accepts only pure codecs, and **closure** under the C12 algebra (pure∘pure = pure; one effectful component taints).
