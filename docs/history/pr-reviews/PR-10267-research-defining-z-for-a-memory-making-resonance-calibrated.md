## Description

*"Let's make what nobody has done our goal."* — posed properly rather than gestured at. **A well-posed open problem, not a result. Nothing here is implemented.**

## The diagnosis

**Embedding similarity is `y − m_cav` with no denominator** — an unnormalised residual. What's missing is predictive spread: *how surprised should I have been?* Without it, a match one standard deviation out and one five out are indistinguishable at recall time. That **is** the over-correction mechanism.

## What each term becomes

| EP term | memory meaning |
|---|---|
| `y` | the retrieval — free |
| `m_cav` | **what I'd conclude *without* this memory** — the crux |
| `σ²_obs` | how noisy retrieval is at this similarity — estimable from history |

**EP already names the move:** `q_cav = marginal / f_message` is a leave-one-out. **An item that shifts nothing is not evidence, however strongly it resonates.**

## The calibration set is something we'd generate anyway

Storing *"coincidence: 48 matches D₄⊕D₄"* and promoting it when structure arrives yields exactly the (resonance, outcome) pairs needed. **The memory-hygiene practice is the training set for its own calibration** — the strongest practical argument for the labelling discipline beyond it being correct.

## Why it's hard, stated not hidden

**Similarity is model-free; `z` requires a model.** The cavity presupposes a representation of *conclusions* rich enough to recompute. Plus the **reference class problem** (an E8 root count and an organisational echo may not share a noise scale) and **selection bias** (unchased coincidences are censored, not negative — survival analysis, not a ratio).

## Falsification

A flat calibration curve kills it — and that's a result worth having. And **"too many correlations" becomes computable**: several high-`z` observations whose *joint* likelihood under independence is implausible. Your heuristic turns into an independence test.
