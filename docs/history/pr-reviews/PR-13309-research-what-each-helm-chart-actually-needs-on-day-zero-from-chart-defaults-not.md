## Description

## What this is

Aaron asked the inverse of the question `full-ai-cluster/k8s/storage-profiles.json` already answers:

> "1599 GiB why do we need that much? ... when i asked for minimial i was talking about what do we need for our help charts what do they need out the gate. I imagine the largest would be for our coackroach database."

The `minimal` rung was built top-down — pick 200 GiB, cut until it fits, record each cut's cost. This document is bottom-up: per storage-consuming claim, **the chart's own default at our pinned `targetRevision`**, its documented minimum or mechanical floor, what consumes the space and which knob governs it, and a **day-zero figure** with what breaks first.

**Research only. No manifest and no size in `storage-profiles.json` is changed.** Where the findings suggest a row is wrong, the doc says so instead of editing it — the numbers are Aaron's call.

## Method

Eleven of twelve pinned charts were pulled with `helm pull` at the exact `targetRevision` and read directly (MEASURED, file + line cited). Upstream docs are cited by URL (RESEARCHED). Arithmetic shows its inputs (CALCULATED). One input nobody in this repo has measured — the cluster's active-series count — is labelled ASSUMED with the query that would replace it.

## Findings

- **CockroachDB is not the largest.** At the active `large` profile, **Mimir is 350 GiB** across seven PVCs; CockroachDB is **300 GiB** (324 with the duplicate `infra/` tree). The intuition lands one row off.
- **Mimir's 50Gi/50Gi/50Gi is byte-for-byte Grafana's own `large.yaml` preset**, whose header targets "~10M series ... around 660000 samples per second". The bare chart default is **2Gi** for each of the three. We took that preset's disk sizing and none of its compute (it also sets `ingester.replicas: 27`).
- **400 of the 1599 GiB creates no PVC on a fresh sync** — ollama and vllm are manual-sync, and arc's model-cache PVC is orphaned (`081M0JM6SSG087G0R0029X3F6Z`).
- **Day-zero floor derived from chart mechanics: 133 GiB** at `nodeCount: 1`, **209 GiB** with honest 3-node HA replica counts. Both are REQUESTED; CONSUMED on day zero is ~0 because Longhorn is thin-provisioned — but Longhorn's scheduler counts REQUESTED, which is why the floor question is the right one.
- **`infra/cockroachdb` at `8Gi` is below CockroachDB's ballast floor at every rung**, `large` included. The docs require ">= 10 GiB free after creating the ballast file", so that store never gets the emergency reserve the documented full-disk recovery depends on.
- **`ollama` at `minimal: 10Gi` cannot hold a single model this repo names** — `deepseek-coder:33b` is 17.53 GiB and `qwen2.5-coder:32b` (which `weaviate.generative-ollama` requires) is 18.49 GiB, measured from the Ollama registry manifests.
- **Prometheus needs `retentionSize`, not a retention cut.** Its size and its governor are currently decoupled; upstream's own rule is "at most 80-85% of your allocated disk". 15 days at this cluster's scale calculates to ~3.5 GB against 100Gi declared.
- **`oz` pins `ziti-controller` `1.4.5`, a chart version upstream never published** (the 1.x line ends at 1.3.4). That Application cannot resolve its chart.
