**And the defect is wider than the filing said.** Six properties survive that mutant, not two — including `... forward then reverse restores retained wires` and its join twin, which both the work-item and #10942 nominated as the honest replacement that "does the work these two were credited with." Every `ToffoliGateStep` is an involution (no step targets one of its own controls), and the reverse-order composition of involutions is the inverse. So those hold for **every** gate list, the empty one included. They are theorems about the interpreter, not measurements of the circuit — falsifiable only by a malformed gate, never by anything the circuit computes. That correction is recorded in §4.1 of the design doc and on the closed row.

## The definition adopted, and why counting was never going to work

Erasure is not an event **inside** a reversible network. A bijection destroys nothing — which is precisely why any "no bits were erased while running" assertion is unfalsifiable, and why counting map-key deletions was a non-property wearing a data-structure costume.

The quantity Landauer's principle prices sits at the **boundary**: ancilla allocated in a known state and **not returned to it**. Those must be reset before the hardware can run the next operation, and resetting an unknown bit to zero is the irreversible erase that costs kT·ln2 (Landauer 1961). Bennett's compute → copy-out → uncompute schedule (1973, 1989) exists to drive that count to zero.

```
garbage(C) = | { w ∈ Ancilla(C) : final(w) ≠ initial(w) } |
```

measured after the full schedule, over designated ancilla — excluding the caller's operands (retained, not erased) and the output register (carried away, not erased).

## What that measurement says about the shipped model

**`modelWeightMul` / `modelJoinCircuit` do not have zero garbage.** They are keep-all-garbage circuits with no uncompute pass, so every partial product and every carry ends dirty. The zero-erasure claim was not merely unmeasured — **measured correctly, it is false of those artifacts**, and the suite now asserts the positive count as its honest reading.

`modelWeightMulUncomputed` / `modelJoinCircuitUncomputed` (new) carry the Bennett schedule and do measure zero, at 2× gates. That factor of two is the honest price of the schedule, not an inefficiency.

This does not overturn §2 of the design doc. It changes what §2 rests on: "the join uncomputes cleanly" is now a tested property of a **constructed schedule**, not an inference from a model that had no uncompute pass.

**Old: 0 of 5 killed. New: 5 of 5.**

The diagonal is the design, not an accident: **neither new property alone kills more than three.** Zero garbage is satisfied by a circuit that computes nothing; a correct product is satisfied by a circuit that leaves every helper dirty. So they ship as a pair, and the product oracle is computed from the integers rather than from the circuit.

Four of the five mutants are **resident** in the suite as permanent falsifier demonstrations, so the metric cannot quietly revert to a tautology.

Two candidate mutants had to be replaced during construction because they were semantically inert — dropping the last forward gate routes a zero carry into a zero column, and inverting a control that was already firing changes nothing. A mutant a correct circuit is entitled to survive is not evidence. Both were fixed rather than accommodated, and the reason is recorded in the test bodies.

No surviving assertion was weakened. Two were **renamed** with their bodies untouched (`... never erases retained wires` → `... references no unallocated wire`), because what those bodies actually catch is a dangling wire reference, not erasure.

## Anchors page-checked (#10942 flagged both verify-first-before-RTL)

**Cuccaro, Draper, Kutin & Moulton 2004 — supported.** Abstract, verbatim: *"our new adder uses only a single ancillary qubit."* Gate set is *"negations, CNOTs, and Toffoli gates"* — purely classical-reversible, which is what makes it usable in a non-quantum Toffoli network at all. The addend **is** retained: UMA *"restores a_i to A_i and c_i to A_{i−1} and writes s_i to B_i"*, so the map is `(a,b) ↦ (a, a+b)` and §2.3's `(acc, p) ↦ (acc+p, p)` is exactly that. Two clarifications the budget needs: the single ancilla `X` is **in addition to** a high-bit output location `Z`, so "1 ancilla" is not "no extra wires"; and the zero-ancilla variant requires the output bit be pre-initialised to zero. Costs added to the doc: 2n−1 Toffoli, 5n−3 CNOT, 2n−4 NOT, depth 2n+4.

**Frank — citation corrected, claim re-anchored.** *Throwing Computing Into Reverse* is the **print** title; the article is *The Future of Computing Depends on Making It Reversible*, IEEE Spectrum, 25 Aug 2017. More substantively: **the Spectrum article does not state the load-bearing sentence.** It supplies the gap — Landauer's *"17-thousandths of an electron volt"* against present-day CMOS at *"something in the neighborhood of 5,000 electron volts per bit erased"*, floored near 500 eV for improved standard CMOS — which corroborates §8's order-of-magnitude argument but does not assert that reversible *logic* is insufficient. That sentence is re-anchored to **S2LAL (arXiv:2009.00448)**, which states it directly. Register: the Spectrum article was read; **S2LAL is abstract-read only**.
