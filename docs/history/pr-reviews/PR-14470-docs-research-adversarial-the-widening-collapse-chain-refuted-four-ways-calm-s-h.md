## Claim 1 — the widening/collapse chain: **refuted**, four independent ways

The chain: *widening is the support-preserving restriction of `−1`; collapse = a key leaving the support; therefore never-collapse ⟺ the support never shrinks.*

**The decisive one.** The chain's stated support is PR #14302 — *"the EP cavity `Gaussian.( / )` **is** `WeightedSet.subtract`."* That result is real and measured over 20 000 pairs. But read what it is about: `h : Gaussian → WeightedSet<NatCoord, ℝ>` over the **two-element key set `{ν, τ}`** — the *sufficient-statistic index*. And `h(One) = empty` holds *because* `WeightedSet` prunes `Zero`. So in the WeightedSet the homomorphism actually lands in, **an empty support IS the flat message — maximum uncertainty, infinite variance.** The chain reads a key as a surviving *hypothesis*; the licensing theorem indexes keys by *natural-parameter coordinate*. Under its own cited support, "a key leaves the support" denotes the exact opposite of collapse. The #14302 doc even names this error in advance: *"The error was putting the sample space in `'K`."*

**Three more, independently.**

- `SoftValue` calls `WeightedSet.add` / `negate` / `subtract` **zero times** (counted: `ofSeq`×5, `weight`×2, `toSeq`×2, `scale`×2, `singleton`×1, `isEmpty`×1). The group structure that makes the Gaussian cavity safe is dead code with respect to belief. And `BeliefConvergence`'s carrier is `(ℤ, ×)`, which has no inverses at all — a `−1` there is not unimplemented, it is **inexpressible**.
- **Exact zero is reachable, twice, with no adversary** (this was flagged as the most likely refutation; confirmed). `SoftValue.observe` at a 10:1 likelihood ratio deletes a candidate at **step 324** by pure float underflow, **no zero likelihood ever supplied** — last weight before deletion `1e-323`. And `BeliefConvergence.observe` is F#'s **unchecked** `int64` `(*)` (the file opens nothing; `ZSet.fs` reaches for `Checked.(-)` where it cares): `2^32 * 2^32 = 0` in one step, and `sharpen` leaves the non-negative cone in seven, which also silently violates `LyapunovContraction`'s `isInPositiveCone`.
- **`MANIFESTO.md` has no never-collapse text.** Its only "collapse" is about *data models under schema change*. The principle is **Addison Cooper's**, in `CONCEPT-REGISTRY.md`: *"uncertainty is held open, never **prematurely** collapsed."* The gloss deletes the qualifier and the sanctioned exit (`resolve`/`snap`, which `SoftValue` calls *"the ONE legitimate collapse"*). A principle changed meaning without anyone deciding to change it — which is the failure the brief asked to catch.

**And it is not desirable as stated.** A rule "the support never shrinks" would forbid **logical refutation** (`P(H|E)=0` is the *correct* posterior; widening it fabricates uncertainty) and forbid honoring **consent withdrawal** (§6 + privacy-as-hard-money require deletion, not down-weighting). The doc sorts corrections into widenable vs deletion-requiring; those two bins settle it.

**Vocabulary:** collapse-as-support-loss is the **seventh** in-tree meaning, not the fourth — and `SolidGround`'s (*"monotonic — never collapse back into uncertainty"*) points the **opposite way**. Registered `coinage-not-discovery`, with the honest split: the fragment reachable via `scale`-by-`Zero` **is** derivable from the in-tree algebraic meaning (a non-invertible idempotent), but the additive fragment the claim is stated about is a fully invertible group where `Map.remove` is a storage choice, not an act.

## Claim 3 — CALM does not apply, and the third row does not survive

**CALM: borrowed theorem, hypotheses unmet.** Not re-proved — `PRIOR-ART-LIST.md` already carries the correct citation with scope (Hellerstein 2010 conjecture; **Ameloot, Neven & Van den Bussche, JACM 60(2):15, 2013** Cor. 13; model-relativity from Ameloot, Ketsman, Neven & Zinn TODS 2015). The entailment check is what was owed, and **three hypotheses fail**: our objects are numeric folds, not *queries over relational transducer networks*; coordination-freeness is quantified **∃-over-partitions** (we don't choose our partition); and *obliviousness* is violated — `YinYangEnsemble` consults both `Id` (`reseedLeastExperienced`) and `All` (`rhoProxy`, `rhoStar(N)`). The theorem that actually applies to our merges is **Shapiro et al.**'s, not CALM.

**The open question, answered against the third row.** DBSP's exact-delta regime is **an op-based CRDT (CmRDT)** — a well-populated existing row, not a new one. Exactness is not the operative property; the **abelian-group structure** is, and only for the *merge*. An approximate delta with the same algebra would be equally order-free.

Where the coordination actually went — **not into ordering**:

- **Into unbounded memory.** Exactly-once is not irreducible: give each delta a unique id and keep a **G-Set of seen ids** (monotone, CALM-clean); at-least-once then suffices. Coordination is traded for a grow-only set. And that is *precisely* the leak `udp-lossy-transport.ts` already measured — *"no bounded receiver can say otherwise."* The theory's escape hatch and the transport's measured limitation are the same object, reached from opposite ends.
- **Into the read.** Coordination-freedom covers merges, never reads — **Laddad et al., "Keep CALM and CRDT On," PVLDB 16(4):856 (2023)**, already cited in-tree.

**Sub-question resolved too.** *"Unconditioned pushforward ≡ CRDT-mergeable"* is **refuted for CvRDT** — measure pushforward composes by **addition**, which is not idempotent, which is the G-Counter's *problem* and exactly why it is built from per-replica **max**. It **holds for CmRDT**. So the idempotence candidate is the right discriminator at the state-based level; monotonicity is deeper only *after* idempotence is assumed — they are two conditions that fail independently, not one at two depths. **Baquero & Almeida's delta-CRDTs are the middle row done right** (op-sized deltas, join merge), and DBSP's Z-sets are not delta-CRDTs on exactly one bit: merge is `+`, not `⊔`.
