## Description

The experiment promised in `from-otto-tangle-math-reply.md`, sharpened by Soraya's route-B routing. Route B's grading argument established the Cl(3,0) blade-mask sandwich is *not* a W(E8) reflection action; this PR banks **how much it actually is**, in exact integer arithmetic, byte-faithful to `AdinkraCode.fs` / `E8Lattice.fs` / `Cl3.fs`.

**Golden numbers** (asserted in the test):
- Baseline classical reflection: **57,600/57,600** (replication validated against the theorem)
- Exactly **32/240** bridged roots are versor-normed — the 8 single blades + the **only two** weight-4 codewords aligned with Cl(3,0)'s structure: `{e₁,e₂,e₁₃,e₂₃}` and `{S,e₁₂,e₃,e₁₂₃}` (the XOR-closed subalgebra of e₁₂,e₃ and its coset)
- **Each of the 32 preserves all 240 roots** — a perfect symmetry fragment inside the bridge
- The other 208 **quantize**: 160 preserve 0, 32 preserve exactly 64, 16 preserve exactly 128 → total 11,776/57,600 (20.4%)

**Upgrade:** "implements NO W(E8) reflection" (argument) → "implements exactly a 32-element Clifford-aligned fragment, and nothing more" (measurement).

**Minted opens** (questions, not claims): characterize the generated group; explain the {0,64,128,240} quantization (looks provable as a function of support class); F# second-oracle port beside `CliffordE8Roots.fs` (TS module is deliberately self-contained so the port is a genuine second oracle).
