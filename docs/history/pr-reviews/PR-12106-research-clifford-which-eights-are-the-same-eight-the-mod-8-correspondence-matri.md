## Description

## The question

We keep meeting the integer 8. Which of those eights are the SAME eight, joined by a theorem,
and which merely share a digit? Fifteen pairs, a verdict each, the mechanism named in every
cell -- per `.claude/rules/numerology-vs-number-theory.md`.

## The answer

**Items 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 are one eight.** It is the order of the value group of the **Weil index**
of a real quadratic form -- equivalently the Brauer-Wall group `BW(R) = Z/8`.

The **1-3 join was the flagged open question** (is the Clifford eight the quadratic-form
eight?) and it closes in two lines. `W(R) = Z` by signature. The graded Clifford class map and
the Weil index are both homomorphisms out of `Z`, and both send the generator to a generator of
a cyclic group of order 8. A homomorphism out of `Z` is fixed by the image of the generator, so
they are the same map. Milgram Gauss-sum formula is its number-theoretic form.

**Item 5 is a restatement of item 1** (garden-algebra relations ARE Clifford relations at
signature `(0,N)`; the closure is Hurwitz-Radon). **Item 6 is connected but NOT a restatement**
-- Bott proved periodicity with Morse theory on loop spaces, using no Clifford algebra, and
`BW(R) = Z/8` is elementary algebra; ABS identifying them is the theorem.

## THE CLEAN NEGATIVE

**Legendre eight is not the periodicity eight, and it is not a mod-8 condition at all.**
Sharper than I expected going in. The excluded set is not a union of residue classes mod 8:

| `n mod 8` | excluded / total | |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **500 / 500** | the whole class |
| 4 | **125 / 500** | *partial* -- 28, 60, 92, ... but **not** 4 |
| 0 | **40 / 500** | *partial* -- 112, 240, 368, ... but **not** 8 |
| others | 0 / 500 | none |

Two classes are excluded only in PART, because of the `4^a` factor. Contrast items 2 and 3,
where the condition IS literally a union of residue classes. Structural discriminator, machine
checked: `legendreResidueTally(4000).partialResidues` returns `[0, 4]` rather than `[]`.

Group structures differ too: `Z/8` has an element of order 8; the 2-adic square classes
`Q_2*/(Q_2*)^2` have order 8 but **exponent 2** -- `(Z/2)^3`, not isomorphic. And the 8 in
`8b+7` is a **conductor**, not a group order.

## Three corrections to `CliffordPeriodicity.fs` (on #12014 / #12023, not yet on main)

1. **`admitsEvenUnimodularLattice` is stated on the wrong invariant.** It tests dimension mod 8,
   which is false in the indefinite case -- the hyperbolic plane is even unimodular in
   **dimension 2**. The sharp theorem is on the **signature** `p - q`. This *strengthens* the
   result: once stated on `p - q`, items 1 and 3 are about literally the same expression.
2. **The eight is a GRADED phenomenon and `classify` alone cannot witness it.** Computed: the
   ungraded Morita shape takes only **5** distinct values on the 8 clock positions (0 collides
   with 2, 4 with 6, 3 with 7). Pairing with the even subalgebra separates all **8**. The
   ungraded Brauer group of the reals has order 2; the order-8 group is Brauer-Wall, of
   `Z/2`-graded algebras -- which is also exactly why it is the adinkra eight.
3. **Construction A does not reach item 1.** The docstring credits it with joining all three
   eights; it never leaves codes and lattices. The join to item 1 is the Weil index.
