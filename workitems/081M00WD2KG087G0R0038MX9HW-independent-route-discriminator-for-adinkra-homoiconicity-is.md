## The claim to test (Z-hom-1, §B tier — NOT §A)

> An adinkra with chromotopology `(Z/2)^N / C`, `dim C = k`, is **homoiconic** — its vertex module is
> free of rank 1 over the algebra generated by its own edge operators — **iff k = 0 and `d_tau` acts
> as the identity**.

Homoiconic here has a precise algebraic meaning: a **homoiconic pair** is `(A, M, rho)` with `A` a
unital algebra, `M` a left `A`-module, and `rho : A -> M` an isomorphism of `A`-modules. Equivalently
`M` is the **regular representation** of `A`. That is "code lives in the same type as data," literally.

## Two routes that must share no intermediate

**Route S (structure only — never mentions time).** From the graph + dashing build the signed
permutation matrices `L_I, R_I` in `End(R^V)`. Let `A_graph` be the unital associative subalgebra they
generate; compute `d_A = dim A_graph` by row-reducing the span of words in the generators (terminates,
since `A_graph` is a subspace of `End(R^V)`). Test freeness of rank 1: `d_A = |V|` AND some `v` has
trivial annihilator.

**Route T (time only — never mentions freeness).** Form `P = (1/2){Q_I, Q_I}`. Ask whether `P` acts as
an invertible scalar, as a nonzero nilpotent (height shift), or differently per colour I.

## Why this is not the #9713 failure

`#9713` counted down-edges twice and returned `LayeringBToA` unconditionally — the coincidence was
built in. Here the two routes are computed by disjoint procedures and **they are predicted to
disagree at N = 4**: for the N=4 valise (4 bosons + 4 fermions, code `d4`, k = 1), Route T says
`P = id` while Route S says `dim A_graph = 16` (`Cl(0,4) = M_2(H)`) against `|V| = 8`, so the module is
NOT free of rank 1. A harness that cannot produce that disagreement has re-implemented the bug.

At N = 1, `dim Cl(0,1) = 2 = |V|` and the routes genuinely coincide — which is the algebraic reason
the N=1 probe was silent on the fork, independent of the code-path reason already recorded.

## Falsifier

Exhibit an adinkra with k > 0 whose vertex module is free of rank 1 over `A_graph`. One example
refutes Z-hom-1.

## Acceptance

- Both routes implemented independently (ideally by two agents, BP-16 second tool).
- The N=4 disagreement is **observed**, not asserted.
- Mutation check: planting `d_A := |V|` must make the test fail.
- No self-certifying script. Register landing is §B only, after review.
