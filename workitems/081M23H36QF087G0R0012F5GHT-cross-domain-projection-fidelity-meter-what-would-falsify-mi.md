## Why this row exists

Aaron 2026-09-09: _"we want some algebra where mind, body, english, and emotional propagation
can all be projected onto the same space with high fidelity so we can look for and compare
irreducible objects across domains."_

As stated that claim cannot fail: any four finite sets embed into a large enough space, "high
fidelity" names no quantity, and "the same irreducible object" is an identification, which
under `.claude/rules/numerology-vs-number-theory.md` needs invariants rather than a matching
score. This row is the meter that would make it a claim.

Design: `docs/research/2026-09-09-geospatial-reasoning-over-clifford-the-fidelity-meter-is-the-gate-the-shared-structure-is-a-category-not-a-space-and-the-warp-consensus-inventory.md` §2.

## The meter, in two tiers

- **Tier 1 — alignment (necessary, correlational).** Over a paired set whose correspondence is
  external to both embeddings, report **three** statistics: mutual k-NN overlap (Huh et al.
  2024), CKA/RSA (Kornblith et al. 2019; Kriegeskorte et al. 2008), and the generalised shape
  _metric_ (Williams et al. 2021). Three, because ReSi (arXiv 2408.00531) shows they disagree.
- **The three nulls, all required.** Permutation; matched-covariance; and a **random-carrier
  control at equal dimension** — the last decides whether the Clifford carrier is doing any
  work at all, and its absence is how a positive would fake itself.
- **Tier 2 — identification.** Grade, norm class, rank of span, stabiliser, orthogonal
  decomposition. A candidate that matches on score and mismatches on any invariant is reported
  **"aligned, not identified"**, which is a result and not a failure.

- [ ] A **negative is a completed outcome.** "The domains align and Clifford is not why" and
      "aligned, not identified" both close this row.

## Constraints inherited, not negotiable by a result

- **Inner states are asked about, never inferred**
  (`.claude/rules/engagement-profiles-public-work-only-not-surveillance-dossiers.md`). The meter
  may read the substrate's own declared affect quantities; it may not become an instrument that
  infers a person's inner state from their outputs.
- The affect leg's two axes are **not independent** today (`Ctm.Intensity = abs value`,
  `Ctm.Mood = value`); they span a cone. The resemblance to Russell's circumplex is registered
  in the doc as a **coincidence with three promotion conditions**, and must not be cited as an
  identification.
