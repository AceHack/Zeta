# Meno balanced structure: Lean certificate for theta = Garside full twist Delta_n^2

## Verdict this item implements (Soraya, 2026-08-14)

`<V>` — the braided monoidal subcategory generated by `MenoBraided.braidR` — **IS
balanced**, and the balanced structure is **unique**. The twist is the Garside full
twist:

```text
theta_{V^n} = rho(Delta_n^2)      Delta_n = (s1)(s2 s1)...(s_{n-1}...s1)
```

`theta_V = rho(Delta_1^2) = id` is **not** a degeneracy and **not** a contradiction.
Two prior reviews read the balanced axiom as `theta_{A(x)B} = theta_A (x) theta_B`,
which would indeed force `c^2 = id`. The actual axiom is

```text
theta_{A(x)B} = (theta_A (x) theta_B) . c_{B,A} . c_{A,B}
```

so `theta_V = id` forces `theta_{V(x)V} = c^2 = s1^2`, NOT `c^2 = id`. The `id` value
on the generator is *forced* (B_1 is trivial) and *correct*.

## The job

Port the CHECKED-for-`n<=7` result to a general-`n` Lean 4 certificate under
`src/Core.Lean4/Lean4/`, reachable from the `Lean4` root (orphan guard —
`lean-orphan-modules.ts`).

Statement to discharge, in the same set-theoretic style as `MenoBraidedRMatrix.lean`
(do NOT port the ZSet monoidal category into Lean):

1. naturality of `theta` in `<V>` reduces to **centrality in `B_n`**, because
   `Hom_{<V>}(V^n, V^n) = rho(B_n)` and `rho` is faithful (Artin 1925)
2. `Delta_{m+n}^2 = (Delta_m^2 (x) Delta_n^2) . c_{n,m} . c_{m,n}` (Garside 1969)
3. therefore `<V>` is balanced, and `theta_1 = id` forces the whole family

**Honest cost note.** Step 2 in general `n` needs Garside normal-form machinery that
Mathlib does not carry. If that is more than a couple of days, land the bounded
version instead (`decide` over `n <= 5` on a concrete faithful model) and mark the
general case CONJECTURE-pending — a bounded machine-check plus the literature anchor
beats an unbuilt general proof.

## Anchors

- Joyal & Street 1993, *Braided Tensor Categories* (Adv. Math. 102) — braided /
  balanced definitions; the braid groupoid as the free braided monoidal category on
  one object
- Garside 1969, *The braid group and other groups* — the element `Delta`, the full
  twist `Delta^2`
- Chow 1948 — `Z(B_n) = <Delta^2>` for `n >= 3`
- Artin 1925 — faithfulness of the action on `F_n` (what `Braid.equal` runs on)
- Kassel, *Quantum Groups* XIII — twist / balanced / ribbon ladder

## Routing (rejected alternatives, and why)

- **Z3 / SMT over `S3`, `S4`, `Q8`** — REJECTED. It answers a different question.
  `theta` must be a morphism of `<V>`, whose homs are exactly `rho(B_n)`; since `rho`
  is faithful the problem is central elements of `B_n`, not set-maps `G^n -> G^n`.
  Encoding free-group rewriting into SMT-LIB is roughly a week of work producing a
  less trustworthy artefact than `Braid.equal`, which we already ship and test.
- **FsCheck** — not primary. The property space is a small finite grid (`m`,`n`), so
  exhaustive beats random; a generator would only re-sample the same 21 cases.
