Gosset 421, lit by a root

Nothing on this page was authored as geometry. The 240 vertices, the 6,720 edges, the 60,480 triangles and the five brightness levels are all computed in your browser by the same Clifford/E8 modules the test suite runs.

Renderer

Exposure is authored, and is the only authored number in the picture. The surface is roughly 700 triangle-layers deep along a chord (total triangle area ÷ silhouette area ≈ 738), so an order-independent accumulation with no exposure saturates every interior pixel to white. It is a property of your eye, not of the polytope.

Light gauge

0

The light direction is an E8 root. Which root is a gauge: the Weyl group is transitive on roots, so the face census below is identical for all 240 choices while the picture turns. Move the slider and watch the table not move.

Derived shading levels

Five levels, not a gradient. The two-sided Lambert numerator is an integer taking {0,4,8,12,16} over the whole surface; brightness is that integer over √384 = 8√6. The shader evaluates no lighting — it indexes a 5-entry palette.

Occlusion

Every edge carries 27 faces, so there is no inside and a painter's sort over primitives is undefined here. Per-sample nearest hit is not — which is what the z-buffer above and this single-threaded BVH tracer both compute.
